### §2312. National activities

#### (a) <sup>1</sup> Information

From amounts available under section 2303(c) of this title, the Secretary shall implement a uniform data collection system to collect information from the States. The information to be collected shall include—

&emsp;(1) the number of displaced homemakers served,

&emsp;(2) the race, age, and sex of displaced homemakers,

&emsp;(3) the number of dependents of displaced homemakers,

&emsp;(4) the amount of income of displaced homemakers,

&emsp;(5) the range of services identified during the assessment process as necessary for displaced homemakers, and

&emsp;(6) the services received by displaced homemakers and appropriate outcomes, including type of training and education received, andtype of job and wage-at-placement for displaced homemakers placed.

(Pub. L. 101–554, §13, Nov. 15, 1990, 104 Stat. 2756.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. No subsec. (b) has been enacted.</i>
