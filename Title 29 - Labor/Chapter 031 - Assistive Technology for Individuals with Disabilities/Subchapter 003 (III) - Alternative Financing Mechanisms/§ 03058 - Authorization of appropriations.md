### §3058. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter $10,000,000 for fiscal year 1999 and such sums as may be necessary for fiscal year 2000.

#### (b) Reservation

Of the amounts appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for a fiscal year, the Secretary shall reserve 2 percent for the purpose of providing information and technical assistance to States under section 3056 of this title.

(Pub. L. 105–394, title III, §308, Nov. 13, 1998, 112 Stat. 3661.)

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in section 3052 of this title.
