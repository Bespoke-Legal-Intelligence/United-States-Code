### §795m. Eligibility

An individual shall be eligible under this part to receive supported employment services authorized under this chapter if—

&emsp;(1) the individual is eligible for vocational rehabilitation services;

&emsp;(2) the individual is determined to be an individual with the most severe disabilities; and

&emsp;(3) a comprehensive assessment of rehabilitation needs of the individual provided under section 722(b)(1)(A) of this title, including an evaluation of rehabilitation, career, and job needs, identifies supported employment as the appropriate rehabilitation objective for the individual.

(Pub. L. 93–112, title VI, §634, as added Pub. L. 102–569, title VI, §621(a), Oct. 29, 1992, 106 Stat. 4440.)
