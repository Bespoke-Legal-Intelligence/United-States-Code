### §796b. Eligibility for receipt of services

Services may be provided under this part to any individual with a severe disability, as defined in section 706(15)(B) of this title.

(Pub. L. 93–112, title VII, §703, as added Pub. L. 102–569, title VII, §701(2), Oct. 29, 1992, 106 Stat. 4444.)
