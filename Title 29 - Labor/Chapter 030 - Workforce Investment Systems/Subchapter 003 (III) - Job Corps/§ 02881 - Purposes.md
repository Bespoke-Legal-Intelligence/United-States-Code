### §2881. Purposes

The purposes of this subchapter are—

&emsp;(1) to maintain a national Job Corps program, carried out in partnership with States and communities, to assist eligible youth who need and can benefit from an intensive program, operated in a group setting in residential and nonresidential centers, to become more responsible, employable, and productive citizens;

&emsp;(2) to set forth standards and procedures for selecting individuals as enrollees in the Job Corps;

&emsp;(3) to authorize the establishment of Job Corps centers in which enrollees will participate in intensive programs of activities described in this subchapter; and

&emsp;(4) to prescribe various other powers, duties, and responsibilities incident to the operation and continuing development of the Job Corps.

(Pub. L. 105–220, title I, §141, Aug. 7, 1998, 112 Stat. 1006.)
