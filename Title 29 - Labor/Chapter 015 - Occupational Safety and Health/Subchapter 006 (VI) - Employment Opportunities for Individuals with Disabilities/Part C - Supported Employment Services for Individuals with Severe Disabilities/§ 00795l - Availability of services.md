### §795<i>l</i>. Availability of services

Funds provided under this part may be used to provide supported employment services to individuals who are eligible under this part. Funds provided under this part, subchapter I of this chapter, or subsection (b) or (c) of section 777a of this title may not be used to provide extended services to individuals who are eligible under this part or subchapter I of this chapter.

(Pub. L. 93–112, title VI, §633, as added Pub. L. 102–569, title VI, §621(a), Oct. 29, 1992, 106 Stat. 4440; amended Pub. L. 103–73, title I, §113, Aug. 11, 1993, 107 Stat. 728.)
