### §2245. Products of universal design

The Secretary may make grants to commercial or other enterprises and institutions of higher education for the research and development of products of universal design. In awarding such grants, the Secretary shall give preference to enterprises that are owned or operated by individuals with disabilities.

(Pub. L. 100–407, title II, §215, as added Pub. L. 103–218, title II, §202, Mar. 9, 1994, 108 Stat. 90.)
