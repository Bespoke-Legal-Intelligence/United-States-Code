### §1630. Purpose

It is the purpose of programs assisted under this part—

&emsp;(1) to enhance the basic educational skills of youth;

&emsp;(2) to encourage school completion or enrollment in supplementary or alternative school programs;

&emsp;(3) to provide eligible youth with exposure to the world of work; and

&emsp;(4) to enhance the citizenship skills of youth.

(Pub. L. 97–300, title II, §251, as added Pub. L. 102–367, title II, §204, Sept. 7, 1992, 106 Stat. 1061.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Pub. L. 105–220, title I, §199(b)(2), (c)(2)(B), Aug. 7, 1998, 112 Stat. 1059, provided that this section is repealed effective July 1, 2000.</i>
