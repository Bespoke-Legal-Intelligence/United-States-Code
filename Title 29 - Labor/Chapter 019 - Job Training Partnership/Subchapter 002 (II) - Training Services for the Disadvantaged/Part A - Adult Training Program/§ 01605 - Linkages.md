### §1605. Linkages

#### (a) In general

In conducting the program assisted under this part, service delivery areas shall establish appropriate linkages with other Federal programs. Such programs shall include, where feasible, programs assisted under—

&emsp;(1) the Adult Education Act; <sup>1</sup>

&emsp;(2) the Carl D. Perkins Vocational and Applied Technology Education Act (20 U.S.C. 2301 et seq.);

&emsp;(3) the Wagner-Peyser Act (29 U.S.C. 49 et seq.);

&emsp;(4) the portions of title IV of the Social Security Act \[42 U.S.C. 601 et seq.\] relating to work activities;

&emsp;(5) the employment program established under section 6(d)(4) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 2015(d)(4));

&emsp;(6) the National Apprenticeship Act (29 U.S.C. 50 et seq.);

&emsp;(7) the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 701 et seq.);

&emsp;(8) title V of the Older Americans Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 3056 et seq.);

&emsp;(9) chapter 2 of title II of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2271 et seq.);

&emsp;(10) the Stewart B. McKinney Homeless Assistance Act (Public Law 100–77; 101 Stat. 482) \[42 U.S.C. 11301 et seq.\];

&emsp;(11) the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437 et seq.);

&emsp;(12) the National Literacy Act of 1991 <sup>1</sup> (Public Law 102–73);

&emsp;(13) the Head Start Act (42 U.S.C. 9831 et seq.) (for purposes of child care services); and

&emsp;(14) any other provisions of this chapter.

#### (b) Other appropriate linkages

In addition to the linkages required under subsection (a) of this section, each service delivery area receiving financial assistance under this part shall establish other appropriate linkages to enhance the provision of services under this part. Such linkages may be established with local educational agencies, local service agencies, public housing agencies, community-based organizations, business and labor organizations, volunteer groups working with disadvantaged adults, and other training, education, employment, economic development, and social service programs.

(Pub. L. 97–300, title II, §205, as added Pub. L. 102–367, title II, §203, Sept. 7, 1992, 106 Stat. 1060; amended Pub. L. 104–193, title I, §110(n)(7), Aug. 22, 1996, 110 Stat. 2174.)
