### §1534. Identification of additional imposed requirements

If a State or service delivery area imposes a requirement, including a rule, regulation, policy, or performance standard, relating to the administration and operation of programs funded by this chapter (including requirements based on State or service delivery area interpretation of any Federal law, regulation, or guideline) the State or area shall identify the requirement as a State- or service delivery area-imposed requirement.

(Pub. L. 97–300, title I, §124, Oct. 13, 1982, 96 Stat. 1341; Pub. L. 99–496, §15(f), Oct. 16, 1986, 100 Stat. 1266; Pub. L. 102–367, title I, §123, Sept. 7, 1992, 106 Stat. 1041.)

#### Amendments

1986—Subsec. (b). Pub. L. 99–496 inserted “, including veterans organizations” after “organizations”.
