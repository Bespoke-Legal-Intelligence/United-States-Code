### §49a. Definitions

For purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “chief elected official or officials” has the same meaning given that term under the Job Training Partnership Act \[29 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.\];

&emsp;(2) the term “private industry council” has the same meaning given that term under the Job Training Partnership Act \[29 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.\];

&emsp;(3) the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Labor;

&emsp;(4) the term “service delivery area” has the same meaning given that term under the Job Training Partnership Act \[29 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.\]; and

&emsp;(5) the term “State” means any of the several States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Virgin Islands.

(June 6, 1933, ch. 49, §2, 48 Stat. 114; Oct. 13, 1982, Pub. L. 97–300, title VI, §601(a), formerly title V, §501(a), 96 Stat. 1392; renumbered title VI, §601(a), Nov. 7, 1988, Pub. L. 100–628, title VII, §712(a)(1), (2), 102 Stat. 3248.)
