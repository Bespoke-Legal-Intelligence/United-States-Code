### §451. Procedure for consideration of alleged violations

The procedure for consideration of alleged violations of part A of subchapter II consists of—

&emsp;(1) counseling and mediation as provided in section 452; and

&emsp;(2) election, as provided in section 453, of either—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an administrative proceeding as provided in section 453(1) and judicial review as provided in section 1296 of title 28; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a civil action in a district court of the United States as provided in section 1346(g) of title 28.

(Added Pub. L. 104–331, §2(a), Oct. 26, 1996, 110 Stat. 4067.)
