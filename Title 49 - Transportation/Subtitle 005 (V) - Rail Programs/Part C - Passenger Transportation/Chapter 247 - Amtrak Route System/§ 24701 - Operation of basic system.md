### §24701. Operation of basic system

(a)

By Amtrak.—Amtrak shall provide intercity rail passenger transportation within the basic system unless the transportation is provided by—

&emsp;(1) a rail carrier with which Amtrak did not make a contract under section 401(a) of the Rail Passenger Service Act; or

&emsp;(2) a regional transportation authority under contract with Amtrak.

(b)

By Others With Consent of Amtrak.—Except as provided in section 24306 of this title, a person may provide intercity rail passenger transportation over a route over which Amtrak provides scheduled intercity rail passenger transportation under a contract under section 401(a) of the Act only with the consent of Amtrak.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(e), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 923.)

#### References in Text

Section 401(a) of the Rail Passenger Service Act, referred to in subsecs. (a)(1) and (b), is section 401(a) of Pub. L. 91–518, which was classified to section 561(a) of Title 45, Railroads, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 103–272, §7(b), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 1379.
