### §70120. Regulations

(a)

In General.—The Secretary of Transportation, within 9 months after the date of the enactment of this section, shall issue regulations to carry out this chapter that include—

&emsp;(1) guidelines for industry and State governments to obtain sufficient insurance coverage for potential damages to third parties;

&emsp;(2) procedures for requesting and obtaining licenses to launch a commercial launch vehicle;

&emsp;(3) procedures for requesting and obtaining operator licenses for launch;

&emsp;(4) procedures for requesting and obtaining launch site operator licenses; and

&emsp;(5) procedures for the application of government indemnification.

(b)

Reentry.—The Secretary of Transportation, within 6 months after the date of the enactment of this section, shall issue a notice of proposed rulemaking to carry out this chapter that includes—

&emsp;(1) procedures for requesting and obtaining licenses to reenter a reentry vehicle;

&emsp;(2) procedures for requesting and obtaining operator licenses for reentry; and

&emsp;(3) procedures for requesting and obtaining reentry site operator licenses.

(Added Pub. L. 105–303, title I, §102(a)(16), Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2850.)

#### References in Text

The date of the enactment of this section, referred to in text, is the date of enactment of Pub. L. 105–303, which was approved Oct. 28, 1998.
