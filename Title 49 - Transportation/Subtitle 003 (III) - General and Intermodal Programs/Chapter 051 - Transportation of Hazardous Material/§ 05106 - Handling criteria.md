### §5106. Handling criteria

The Secretary of Transportation may prescribe criteria for handling hazardous material, including—

&emsp;(1) a minimum number of personnel;

&emsp;(2) minimum levels of training and qualifications for personnel;

&emsp;(3) the kind and frequency of inspections;

&emsp;(4) equipment for detecting, warning of, and controlling risks posed by the hazardous material;

&emsp;(5) specifications for the use of equipment and facilities used in handling and transporting the hazardous material; and

&emsp;(6) a system of monitoring safety procedures for transporting the hazardous material.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(d), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 763.)
