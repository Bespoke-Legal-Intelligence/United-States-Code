### §5322. Human resource programs

The Secretary of Transportation may undertake, or make grants and contracts for, programs that address human resource needs as they apply to mass transportation activities. A program may include—

&emsp;(1) an employment training program;

&emsp;(2) an outreach program to increase minority and female employment in mass transportation activities;

&emsp;(3) research on mass transportation personnel and training needs; and

&emsp;(4) training and assistance for minority business opportunities.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(d), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 820.)
