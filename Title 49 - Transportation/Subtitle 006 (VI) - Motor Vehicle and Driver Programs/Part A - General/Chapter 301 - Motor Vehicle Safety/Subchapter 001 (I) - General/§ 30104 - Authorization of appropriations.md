### §30104. Authorization of appropriations

The following amounts may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation for the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration to carry out this chapter:

&emsp;(1) $71,333,436 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1993.

&emsp;(2) $74,044,106 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1994.

&emsp;(3) $76,857,782 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(e), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 944.)
