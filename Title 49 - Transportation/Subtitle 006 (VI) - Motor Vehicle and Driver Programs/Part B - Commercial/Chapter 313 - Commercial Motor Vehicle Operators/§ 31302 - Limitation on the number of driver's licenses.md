### §31302. Limitation on the number of driver's licenses

An individual operating a commercial motor vehicle may have only one driver's license at any time, except during the 10-day period beginning on the date the individual is issued a driver's license.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(e), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 1015.)
