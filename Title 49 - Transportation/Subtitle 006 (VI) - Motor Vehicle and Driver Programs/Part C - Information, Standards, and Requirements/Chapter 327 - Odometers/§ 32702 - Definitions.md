### §32702. Definitions

In this chapter—

&emsp;(1) “auction company” means a person taking possession of a motor vehicle owned by another to sell at an auction.

&emsp;(2) “dealer” means a person that sold at least 5 motor vehicles during the prior 12 months to buyers that in good faith bought the vehicles other than for resale.

&emsp;(3) “distributor” means a person that sold at least 5 motor vehicles during the prior 12 months for resale.

&emsp;(4) “leased motor vehicle” means a motor vehicle leased to a person for at least 4 months by a lessor that leased at least 5 vehicles during the prior 12 months.

&emsp;(5) “odometer” means an instrument for measuring and recording the distance a motorvehicle is driven, but does not include an auxiliary instrument designed to be reset by the operator of the vehicle to record mileage of a trip.

&emsp;(6) “repair” and “replace” mean to restore to a sound working condition by replacing any part of an odometer or by correcting any inoperative part of an odometer.

&emsp;(7) “title” means the certificate of title or other document issued by the State indicating ownership.

&emsp;(8) “transfer” means to change ownership by sale, gift, or any other means.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(e), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 1048; Pub. L. 104–287, §5(61), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3394.)
