### §47501. Definitions

In this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) “airport” means a public-use airport as defined in section 47102 of this title.

&emsp;(2) “airport operator” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) for an airport serving air carriers that have certificates from the Secretary of Transportation, any person holding an airport operating certificate issued under section 44706 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) for any other airport, the person operating the airport.

(Pub. L. 103–272, §1(e), July 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 1284.)
