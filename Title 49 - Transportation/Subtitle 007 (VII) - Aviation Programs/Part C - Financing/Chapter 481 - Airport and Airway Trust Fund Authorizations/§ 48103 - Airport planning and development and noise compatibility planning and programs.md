### §48103. Airport planning and development and noise compatibility planning and programs

The total amounts which shall be available after September 30, 1998, to the Secretary of Transportation out of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund established under section 9502 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 9502) to make grants for airport planning and airport development under section 47104 of this title, airport noise compatibility planning under section 47505(a)(2) of this title, and carrying out noise compatibility programs under section 47504(c) of this title shall be—

&emsp;(1) $2,410,000,000 for fiscal year 1999;

&emsp;(2) $2,475,000,000 for fiscal year 2000;

&emsp;(3) $3,200,000,000 for fiscal year 2001;

&emsp;(4) $3,300,000,000 for fiscal year 2002; and

&emsp;(5) $3,400,000,000 for fiscal year 2003.

> Such sums shall remain available until expended.

#### Notice of Grants

Pub. L. 106–181, title I, §159, Apr. 5, 2000, 114 Stat. 90, provided that:

“(a)

Timely Announcement.—The Secretary \[of Transportation\] shall announce a grant to be made with funds made available under section 48103 of title 49, United States Code, in a timely fashion after receiving necessary documentation concerning the grant from the Administrator \[of the Federal Aviation Administration\].

“(b)

Notice to Committees.—If the Secretary provides any committee of Congress advance notice of a grant to be made with funds made available under section 48103 of title 49, United States Code, the Secretary shall provide, on the same date, such notice to the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate.”
