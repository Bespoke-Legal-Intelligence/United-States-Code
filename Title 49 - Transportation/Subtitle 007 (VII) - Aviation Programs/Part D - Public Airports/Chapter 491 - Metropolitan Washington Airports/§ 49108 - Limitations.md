### §49108. Limitations

After October 1, 2001, the Secretary of Transportation may not approve an application of the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority—

&emsp;(1) for an airport development project grant under subchapter I of chapter 471 of this title; or

&emsp;(2) to impose a passenger facility fee under section 40117 of this title.

(Added Pub. L. 105–102, §2(26), Nov. 20, 1997, 111 Stat. 2213.)
