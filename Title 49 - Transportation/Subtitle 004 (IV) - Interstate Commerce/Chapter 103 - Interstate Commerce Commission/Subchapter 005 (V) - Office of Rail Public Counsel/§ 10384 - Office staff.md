### §10384. Office staff

The Director of the Office of Rail Public Counsel may—

&emsp;(1) appoint and fix the pay of employees of the Office; and

&emsp;(2) procure under section 3109 of title 5 the temporary or intermittent services of experts and consultants.

(Pub. L. 95–473, Oct. 17, 1978, 92 Stat. 1358.)
