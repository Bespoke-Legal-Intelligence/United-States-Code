### §10524. Transportation furthering a primary business

(a) The Interstate Commerce Commission does not have jurisdiction under this subchapter over the transportation of property by motor vehicle when—

&emsp;(1) the property is transported by a person engaged in a business other than transportation; and

&emsp;(2) the transportation is within the scope of, and furthers a primary business (other than transportation) of the person.

(b) The Commission does not have jurisdiction under this subchapter over transportation of property by motor vehicle for compensation provided by a person who is a member of a corporate family for other members of such corporate family if—

&emsp;(1) the parent corporation notifies the Commission of its intent or one of its subsidiaries’ intent to provide the transportation;

&emsp;(2) the notice contains a list of participating subsidiaries and an affidavit that the parent corporation owns directly or indirectly a 100 percent interest in each of the subsidiaries;

&emsp;(3) the Commission publishes the notice in the Federal Register within 30 days of receipt; and

&emsp;(4) a copy of the notice is carried in the cab of all vehicles conducting the transportation.

(c) In this section, “corporate family” means a group of corporations consisting of a parent corporation and all subsidiaries in which the parent corporation owns directly or indirectly a 100 percent interest.

(Pub. L. 95–473, Oct. 17, 1978, 92 Stat. 1363; Pub. L. 96–296, §9, July 1, 1980, 94 Stat. 798.)
