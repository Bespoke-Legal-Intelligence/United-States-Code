### §433. Objects and purposes of corporation

The objects and purposes of the corporation shall be—

&emsp;(1) to hold annually a national conference on citizenship on or about “Citizenship Day”, September 17;

&emsp;(2) to assist in the development of more dynamic procedures for making citizenship more effective, including the promotion and encouragement of local, State, and regional citizenship conferences; and

&emsp;(3) to indicate the ways and means by which various organizations may contribute concretely to the development of a more active, alert, enlightened, conscientious, and progressive citizenry in our country.

(Aug. 13, 1953, ch. 427, §3, 67 Stat. 562.)
