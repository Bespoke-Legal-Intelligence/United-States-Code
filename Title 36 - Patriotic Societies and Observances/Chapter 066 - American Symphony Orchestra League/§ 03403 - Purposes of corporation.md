### §3403. Purposes of corporation

The purposes of the corporation shall be to—

&emsp;(1) serve as a coordinating, research and educational agency and clearinghouse for symphony orchestras in order to help strengthen the work in their local communities;

&emsp;(2) assist in the formation of new symphony orchestras;

&emsp;(3) through suitable means, encourage and recognize the work of America's musicians, conductors, and composers; and

&emsp;(4) aid the expansion of the musical and cultural life of the United States through suitable educational and service activities.

(Pub. L. 87–817, §3, Oct. 15, 1962, 76 Stat. 930.)
