### §5006. Board of directors

Except as provided in section 5008 of this title, the composition of the board of directors of thecorporation and the responsibilities of such board shall be as provided in the articles of incorporation of the corporation and in conformity with the laws of the State in which it is incorporated.

(Pub. L. 102–484, div. A, title XVIII, §1806, Oct. 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 2582.)
