### §5015. Failure to comply with restrictions or provisions

The charter granted by this chapter shall expire if the corporation fails to comply with—

&emsp;(1) any restriction or other provision of this chapter;

&emsp;(2) any provision of its bylaws or articles of incorporation; or

&emsp;(3) any provision of the laws of the District of Columbia that apply to corporations such as the corporation recognized under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 102–484, div. A, title XVIII, §1816, Oct. 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 2583.)
