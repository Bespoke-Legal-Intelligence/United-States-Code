### §883. Purposes of corporation

The purposes of the corporation shall be to aid and assist individuals throughout the United States of America in the solution of their social and economic problems, and assist in theirhealth, educational and character development; to promote the use of the techniques of such assistance developed by the corporation, by other lay and professional agencies and workers, to receive, invest, and disburse funds and to hold property for the purposes of the corporation.

(Pub. L. 85–870, §3, Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1691; Pub. L. 95–167, §1(b), Nov. 11, 1977, 91 Stat. 1347.)

#### Amendments

1977—Pub. L. 95–167 substituted “individuals” for “boys” and struck out “and Canada” after “United States of America”.
