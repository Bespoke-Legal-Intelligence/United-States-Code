### §117. Exclusive right to name, seal, emblems, and badges

The said corporation shall have the sole and exclusive right to have and to use, in carryingout its purposes, the name “Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States” and the sole and exclusive right to the use of its corporate seal, emblems, and badges as adopted by said corporation.

(May 28, 1936, ch. 471, §7, 49 Stat. 1391.)
