### §1201. Corporation created

The following persons:

&emsp;Honorable Fred Schwengel, Washington, District of Columbia;

&emsp;Honorable Marguerite Stitt Church, Evanston, Illinois;

&emsp;Doctor Melvin M. Payne, Washington, District of Columbia;

&emsp;Carl Haverlin, Northridge, California;

&emsp;Doctor Walter Rundell, College Park, Maryland;

&emsp;Victor M. Birely, Washington, District of Columbia;

&emsp;Arthur B. Hanson, Esquire, Washington, District of Columbia;

&emsp;Mrs. Adlai Stevenson III, Hanover, Illinois;

&emsp;Mrs. Florian Thayn, Washington, District of Columbia;

> and their successors are hereby created and declared to be a body corporate of the District of Columbia having the name “United States Capitol Historical Society” (hereinafter the <sup>1</sup> corporation”). The said corporation shall have perpetual existence and the powers, limitations, and restrictions herein contained.

(Pub. L. 95–493, §1, Oct. 20, 1978, 92 Stat. 1643.)
