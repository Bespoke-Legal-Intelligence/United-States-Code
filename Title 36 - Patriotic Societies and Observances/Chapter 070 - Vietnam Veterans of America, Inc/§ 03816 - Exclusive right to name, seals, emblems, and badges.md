### §3816. Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges

The corporation shall have the sole and exclusive right to use the name “The Vietnam Veterans of America, Inc.”, “Vietnam Veterans of America, Inc.”, and “Vietnam Veterans ofAmerica”, and such seals, emblems, and badges as the corporation may lawfully adopt. Nothing in this section shall be construed to interfere or conflict with established or vested rights.

(Pub. L. 99–318, §17, May 23, 1986, 100 Stat. 476.)
