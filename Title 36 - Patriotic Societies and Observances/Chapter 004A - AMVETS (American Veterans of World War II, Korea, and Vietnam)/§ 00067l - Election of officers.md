### §67<i>l</i>. Election of officers

The following national officers of the said corporation shall be elected by the chosen delegatesthereof in annual national conventions, each official delegate casting one vote, to wit: National commander and seven national vice commanders, one of whom shall be a woman; finance officer, adjutant, judge advocate, and provost marshal.

(July 23, 1947, ch. 298, §13, 61 Stat. 408.)
