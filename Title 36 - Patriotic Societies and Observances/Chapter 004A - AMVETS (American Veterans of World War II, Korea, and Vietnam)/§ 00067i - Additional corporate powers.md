### §67i. Additional corporate powers

The corporation shall have power to—

&emsp;(1) Have succession by its corporate name;

&emsp;(2) Choose such officers, representatives, and agents as are necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation;

&emsp;(3) Contract and be contracted with;

&emsp;(4) Transfer and convey all real or personal property;

&emsp;(5) Borrow money for the purposes of the corporation, issue bonds therefor, and secure same by mortgage subject in every case to all applicable provisions of Federal or State laws.

(July 23, 1947, ch. 298, §10, 61 Stat. 407.)
