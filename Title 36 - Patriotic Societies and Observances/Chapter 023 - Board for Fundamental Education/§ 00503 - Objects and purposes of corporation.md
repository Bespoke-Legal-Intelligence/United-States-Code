### §503. Objects and purposes of corporation

The objects and purposes of the corporation shall be to foster the development of fundamental education through programs and projects such as—

&emsp;(1) giving citizens (children, youth, and adults) opportunity to acquire the understandings and skills necessary to relate the resources of the community to the needs and interests of the community.

&emsp;(2) demonstrating programs of fundamental education and measuring results.

&emsp;(3) training men and women as leaders in fundamental education by providing internships and other experiences.

(July 19, 1954, ch. 536, §3, 68 Stat. 490.)
