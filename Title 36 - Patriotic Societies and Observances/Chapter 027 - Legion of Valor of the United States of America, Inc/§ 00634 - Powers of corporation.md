### §634. Powers of corporation

The corporation shall have power—

&emsp;(1) to have succession by its corporate name;

&emsp;(2) to sue and be sued, complain and defend in any court of competent jurisdiction;

&emsp;(3) to adopt, use, and alter a corporate seal;

&emsp;(4) to charge and collect membership dues;

&emsp;(5) to adopt, amend, and alter a constitution and bylaws, not inconsistent with the laws of the United States or any State in which the corporation is to operate, for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;

&emsp;(6) to contract and be contracted with;

&emsp;(7) to take by lease, gift, purchase, grant, devise, or bequest from any private corporation, association, partnership, firm, or individual and to hold any property, real, personal, or mixed, necessary or convenient for attaining the objects and carrying into effect the purposes of the corporation, subject, however, to applicable provisions of law of any State (A) governing the amount or kind of property which may be held by, or (B) otherwise limiting or controlling the ownership of property by, a corporation operating in such State;

&emsp;(8) to transfer, convey, lease, sublease, encumber, and otherwise alienate real, personal or mixed property; and

&emsp;(9) to borrow money for the purposes of the corporation, issue bonds therefor, and securethe same by mortgage, deed of trust, pledge, or otherwise, subject in every case to all applicable provisions of Federal and State laws.

(Aug. 4, 1955, ch. 546, §4, 69 Stat. 487.)
