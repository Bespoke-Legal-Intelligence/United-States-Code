### §224. Powers of corporation

The corporation shall have perpetual succession and power—

&emsp;(1) to sue and be sued;

&emsp;(2) to acquire, hold, lease, and dispose of such real and personal property as may be necessary to carry out the corporate object and purpose;

&emsp;(3) to accept gifts, legacies, and devises in furtherance of the corporate object and purpose;

&emsp;(4) to adopt and alter a corporate seal;

&emsp;(5) to adopt and alter a constitution and bylaws not inconsistent with the laws of the United States or of any State;

&emsp;(6) to establish, regulate, and discontinue subordinate departmental subdivisions and local chapters;

&emsp;(7) to adopt and alter emblems and badges;

&emsp;(8) to publish a newspaper, magazine, or other publications; and

&emsp;(9) to do any and all acts and things necessary and proper to carry out the object and purpose of the corporation.

(June 30, 1950, ch. 431, §4, 64 Stat. 313.)
