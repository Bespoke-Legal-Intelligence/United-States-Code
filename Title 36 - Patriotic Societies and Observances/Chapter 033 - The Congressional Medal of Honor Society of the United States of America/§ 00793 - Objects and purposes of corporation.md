### §793. Objects and purposes of corporation

The objects and purposes of the corporation are as follows:

&emsp;(1) To form a bond of friendship and comradeship among all holders of the Congressional Medal of Honor.

&emsp;(2) To protect, uphold, and preserve the dignity and honor of the medal at all times and on all occasions.

&emsp;(3) To protect the name of the medal, and individual holders of the medal from exploitation.

&emsp;(4) To provide appropriate aid to all persons to whom the medal has been awarded, their widows or their children.

&emsp;(5) To serve our country in peace as we did in war.

&emsp;(6) To inspire and stimulate our youth to become worthy citizens of our country.

&emsp;(7) To foster and perpetuate Americanism.

(Pub. L. 85–642, §3, Aug. 14, 1958, 72 Stat. 598.)
