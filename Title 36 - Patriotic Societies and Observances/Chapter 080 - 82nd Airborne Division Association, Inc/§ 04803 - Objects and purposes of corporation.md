### §4803. Objects and purposes of corporation

The objects and purposes of the corporation are those provided in its articles of incorporation and shall include—

&emsp;(1) perpetuating the memory of members of the 82nd Airborne Division who fought and died for this Nation;

&emsp;(2) furthering the common bond between retired and active members of the 82nd Airborne Division;

&emsp;(3) providing educational assistance in the form of college scholarships and grants to the qualified children of current and former members of the 82nd Airborne Division;

&emsp;(4) promoting civic and patriotic activities; and

&emsp;(5) promoting the indispensable role of airborne defense to the national security of the United States.

(Pub. L. 101–510, div. A, title XVI, §1603, Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1736.)
