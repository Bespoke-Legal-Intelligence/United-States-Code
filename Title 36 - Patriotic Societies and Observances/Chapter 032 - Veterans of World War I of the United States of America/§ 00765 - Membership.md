### §765. Membership

Eligibility for membership in the corporation and the rights, privileges, and designation ofclasses of members shall be determined as the constitution and bylaws of the corporation may provide but in no case shall eligibility for all classes of membership include persons who did not serve honorably in the armed forces of the United States during the period beginning April 6, 1917 and ending November 11, 1918.

(Pub. L. 85–530, §5, July 18, 1958, 72 Stat. 372.)
