### §3308. Annual report

Said corporation shall on or before the 1st day of January in each year make and transmit to Congress a report of its proceedings for the preceding calendar year: <i>Provided, however</i>, That said report shall not be printed as a public document.

(July 5, 1935, ch. 373, §8, 49 Stat. 459; Aug. 30, 1964, Pub. L. 88–504, §4(4), 78 Stat. 636.)

#### Amendments

1964—Pub. L. 88–504 struck out before colon “including a full and complete report of its receipts and expenditures”.
