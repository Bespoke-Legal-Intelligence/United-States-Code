### §2103. Objects and purposes of corporation

The objects and purposes of the corporation are those provided in its articles of incorporation and shall include—

&emsp;(1) encouragement of fraternity for the common good;

&emsp;(2) fostering patriotism and loyalty;

&emsp;(3) assistance to widows and orphans of deceased ex-prisoners of war;

&emsp;(4) assistance to ex-prisoners of war who have been injured or handicapped as a result of their service;

&emsp;(5) maintenance of allegiance to the United States of America;

&emsp;(6) preservation and defense of the United States from all of her enemies; and

&emsp;(7) maintenance of historical records.

(Pub. L. 97–234, §3, Aug. 10, 1982, 96 Stat. 261.)
