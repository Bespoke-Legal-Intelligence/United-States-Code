### §1004. Powers of corporation

The corporation shall have power—

&emsp;(1) to have succession by its corporate name;

&emsp;(2) to sue and be sued, complain and defend in any court of competent jurisdiction;

&emsp;(3) to adopt, use, and alter a corporate seal;

&emsp;(4) to choose such officers, as the corporation may require;

&emsp;(5) to adopt, amend, and alter a constitution and bylaws; not inconsistent with the laws ofthe United States or any State in which the corporation is to operate, for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;

&emsp;(6) to contract and be contracted with;

&emsp;(7) to take by lease, gift, purchase, grants, devise, or bequest from any public body or agency or any private corporation, association, partnership, firm, or individual and to hold absolutely or in trust for any of the purposes of the corporation any property, real, personal, or mixed, necessary or convenient for attaining the objects and carrying into effect the purposes of the corporation, subject, however, to applicable provisions of law of any State, (A) governing the amount or kind of property which may be held by, or (B) otherwise limiting or controlling the ownership of property by, a corporation operating in such State; and

&emsp;(8) to transfer, convey, lease, sublease, encumber, and otherwise alienate real, personal, or mixed property.

(Pub. L. 87–650, §4, Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 503.)
