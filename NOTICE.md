# Notice

**Unofficial. Not legal advice.** This repository is an unofficial arrangement of the United States Code. The
official text is published by the U.S. Government Publishing Office (GPO) on govinfo
(https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/uscode) and by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel of the U.S. House of
Representatives (https://uscode.house.gov). Check the official sources before relying on anything here.

## Source

Every edition is GPO's annual edition of the United States Code as served by govinfo: 1994 to 2024, with "laws in
effect as of" dates from 4 January 1995 to 6 January 2025. Each title file was checked on arrival against the SHA-256
digest and size in GPO's own provenance record for that package (premis.xml), then converted to Markdown with the text
unchanged. Title-years govinfo holds no copy of are listed in `GAPS.md`.

## Statute text: not claimed

The text of the United States Code is a work of the United States Government. Copyright protection under title 17 is
not available for any work of the United States Government (17 U.S.C. &sect; 105), and no claim is made to it here. The
House report on &sect; 105 (H.R. Rep. No. 94-1476) says the provision is not intended to affect protection of these works
abroad. See `LICENSES/LicenseRef-US-Government-Work.txt`.
