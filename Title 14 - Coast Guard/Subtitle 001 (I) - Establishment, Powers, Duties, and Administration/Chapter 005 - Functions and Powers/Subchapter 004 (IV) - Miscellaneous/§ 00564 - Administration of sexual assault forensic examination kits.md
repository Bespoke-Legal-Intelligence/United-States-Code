### §564. Administration of sexual assault forensic examination kits

(a)

Sexual Assault Forensic Exam Procedure.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—Before embarking on any prescheduled voyage, a Coast Guard vessel shall have in place a written operating procedure that ensures that an embarked victim of sexual assault shall have access to a sexual assault forensic examination—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) as soon as possible after the victim requests an examination; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) that is treated with the same level of urgency as emergency medical care.

&emsp;(2)

Requirements.—The written operating procedure required by paragraph (1),<sup>1</sup> shall, at a minimum, account for—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the health, safety, and privacy of a victim of sexual assault;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the proximity of ashore or afloat medical facilities, including coordination as necessary with the Department of Defense, including other military departments (as defined in section 101 of title 10);

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the availability of aeromedical evacuation;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the operational capabilities of the vessel concerned;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) the qualifications of medical personnel onboard;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) coordination with law enforcement and the preservation of evidence;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) the means of accessing a sexual assault forensic examination and medical care with a restricted report of sexual assault;

&emsp;&emsp;(H) the availability of nonprescription pregnancy prophylactics; and

&emsp;&emsp;(I) other unique military considerations.

(Added Pub. L. 117–263, div. K, title CXII, §11272(a), Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 4066.)
