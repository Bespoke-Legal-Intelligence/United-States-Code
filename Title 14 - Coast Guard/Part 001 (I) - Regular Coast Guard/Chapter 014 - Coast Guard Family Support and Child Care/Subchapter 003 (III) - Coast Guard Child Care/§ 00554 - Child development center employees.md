### §554. Child development center employees

(a)

Training.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—The Commandant shall establish a training program for Coast Guard child development center employees and satisfactory completion of the training program shall be a condition of employment for each employee of a Coast Guard child development center.

&emsp;(2)

Timing for new hires.—The Commandant shall require each employee of a Coast Guard child development center to complete the training program established under paragraph (1) not later than 6 months after the date on which the employee is hired.

&emsp;(3)

Minimum requirements.—The training program established under paragraph (1) shall include, at a minimum, instruction with respect to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) early childhood development;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) activities and disciplinary techniques appropriate to children of different ages;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) child abuse and neglect prevention and detection; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) cardiopulmonary resuscitation and other emergency medical procedures.

&emsp;(4)

Use of department of defense programs.—The Commandant may use Department of Defense training programs, on a reimbursable or nonreimbursable basis, for purposes of this subsection.

(b)

Training and Curriculum Specialists.—

&emsp;(1)

Specialist required.—The Commandant shall require that at least 1 employee at each Coast Guard child development center be a specialist in training and curriculum development with appropriate credentials and experience.

&emsp;(2)

Duties.—The duties of the specialist described in paragraph (1) shall include—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) special teaching activities;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) daily oversight and instruction of other child care employees;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) daily assistance in the preparation of lesson plans;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) assisting with child abuse and neglect prevention and detection; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) advising the director of the center on the performance of the other child care employees.

&emsp;(3)

Competitive service.—Each specialist described in paragraph (1) shall be an employee in a competitive service position.

(Added Pub. L. 113–281, title II, §214(a), Dec. 18, 2014, 128 Stat. 3032.)
