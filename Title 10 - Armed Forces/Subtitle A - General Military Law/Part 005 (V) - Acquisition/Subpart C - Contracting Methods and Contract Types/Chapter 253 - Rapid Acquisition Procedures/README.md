#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### Prior Provisions

A prior chapter 253 "EMERGENCY AND RAPID ACQUISITIONS", as added by Pub. L. 115–232, div. A, title VIII, §801(a), Aug. 13, 2018, 132 Stat. 1828, and consisting of reserved section 3601, was repealed by Pub. L. 116–283, div. A, title XVIII, §1824(a), Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4205.

#### Amendments

<b>2024</b>—Pub. L. 118–159, div. A, title VIII, §§804(a), 805(a), 861(a), Dec. 23, 2024, 138 Stat. 1966, 1969, 1999, added items 3602 to 3604. Amendments were made pursuant to operation of section 102 of this title.

<b>2022</b>—Pub. L. 117–263, div. A, title VIII, §804(a), Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 2696, amended chapter analysis generally, substituting "RAPID ACQUISITION PROCEDURES" for "\[Reserved\]" in heading and item 3601 for former items for subchapters I and II, which were both "\[Reserved\]".

<b>2021</b>—Pub. L. 117–81, div. A, title XVII, §1701(i)(5)(B)(i), Dec. 27, 2021, 135 Stat. 2141, amendedPub. L. 116–283, div. A, title XVIII, §1824(a), Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4205, which added this analysis, by substituting "\[Reserved\]" for "RAPID ACQUISITION PROCEDURES" in chapter heading.
