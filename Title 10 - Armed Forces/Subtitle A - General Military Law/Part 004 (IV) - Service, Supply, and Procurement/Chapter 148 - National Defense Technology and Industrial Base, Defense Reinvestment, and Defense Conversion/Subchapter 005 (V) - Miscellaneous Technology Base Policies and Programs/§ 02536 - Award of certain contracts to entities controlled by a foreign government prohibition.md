### §2536. Award of certain contracts to entities controlled by a foreign government: prohibition

(a)

In General.—A Department of Defense contract or Department of Energy contract under a national security program may not be awarded to an entity controlled by a foreign government if it is necessary for that entity to be given access to information in a proscribed category of information in order to perform the contract.

(b)

Waiver Authority.—The Secretary concerned may waive the application of subsection (a) to a contract award if the Secretary concerned determines that the waiver is essential to the national security interests of the United States.

(c)

Definitions.—In this section:

&emsp;(1) The term “entity controlled by a foreign government” includes—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any domestic or foreign organization or corporation that is effectively owned or controlled by a foreign government; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any individual acting on behalf of a foreign government,

> &emsp;as determined by the Secretary concerned. Such term does not include an organization or corporation that is owned, but is not controlled, either directly or indirectly, by a foreign government if the ownership of that organization or corporation by that foreign government was effective before October 23, 1992.

&emsp;(2) The term “proscribed category of information” means a category of information that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) with respect to Department of Defense contracts—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) includes special access information;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) is determined by the Secretary of Defense to include information the disclosure of which to an entity controlled by a foreign government is not in the national security interests of the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) is defined in regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense for the purposes of this section; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) with respect to Department of Energy contracts—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) is determined by the Secretary of Energy to include information described in subparagraph (A)(ii); and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) is defined in regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Energy for the purposes of this section.

&emsp;(3) The term “Secretary concerned” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Secretary of Defense, with respect to Department of Defense contracts; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Secretary of Energy, with respect to Department of Energy contracts.
