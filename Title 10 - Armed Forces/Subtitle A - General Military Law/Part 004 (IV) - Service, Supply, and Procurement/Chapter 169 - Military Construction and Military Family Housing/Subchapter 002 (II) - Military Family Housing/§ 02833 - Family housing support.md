### §2833. Family housing support

Amounts authorized by law for support of military family housing include amounts for—

&emsp;(1) operating expenses;

&emsp;(2) leasing expenses;

&emsp;(3) maintenance of real property expenses;

&emsp;(4) payments of principal and interest on mortgage debts incurred; and

&emsp;(5) payments of mortgage insurance premiums authorized under section 222 of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715m).

(Added Pub. L. 99–167, title VIII, §804(b)(1), Dec. 3, 1985, 99 Stat. 987.)
