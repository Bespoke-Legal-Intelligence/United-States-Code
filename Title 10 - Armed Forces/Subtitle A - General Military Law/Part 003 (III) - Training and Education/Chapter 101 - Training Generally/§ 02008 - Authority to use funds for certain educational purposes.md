### §2008. Authority to use funds for certain educational purposes

Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense may be used to carry out section 10 of the Act of September 23, 1950 (20 U.S.C. 640), relating to impact aid authorization.

(Added Pub. L. 98–525, title XIV, §1401(g)(1), Oct. 19, 1984, 98 Stat. 2618.)
