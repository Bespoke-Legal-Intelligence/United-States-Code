### §2126. Members of the program: service credit

Service performed while a member of the program shall not be counted—

&emsp;(1) in determining eligibility for retirement other than by reason of a physical disability incurred while on active duty as a member of the program; or

&emsp;(2) in computing years of service creditable under section 205 of title 37.

(Added Pub. L. 92–426, §2(a), Sept. 21, 1972, 86 Stat. 718; amended Pub. L. 96–513, title V, §501(22), Dec. 12, 1980, 94 Stat. 2908.)
