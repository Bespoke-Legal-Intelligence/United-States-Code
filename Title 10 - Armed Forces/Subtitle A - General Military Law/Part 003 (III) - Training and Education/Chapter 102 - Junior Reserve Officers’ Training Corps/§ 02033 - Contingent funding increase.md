### §2033. Contingent funding increase

If for any fiscal year the amount appropriated for the National Guard Challenge Program under section 509 of title 32 is in excess of $62,500,000, the Secretary of Defense shall (notwithstanding any other provision of law) make the amount in excess of $62,500,000 available for the Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps program under section 2031 of this title, and such excess amount may not be used for any other purpose.

(Added Pub. L. 106–65, div. A, title V, §547(a)(1), Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 608.)

#### Effective Date

Pub. L. 106–65, div. A, title V, §547(b), Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 609, provided that: “Section 2033 of title 10, United States Code, as added by subsection (a), shall apply only with respect to funds appropriated for fiscal years after fiscal year 1999.”
