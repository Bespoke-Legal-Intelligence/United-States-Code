### §2151. Definitions

(a)

Joint Professional Military Education.—Joint professional military education consists of the rigorous and thorough instruction and examination of officers of the armedforces in an environment designed to promote a theoretical and practical in-depth understanding of joint matters and, specifically, of the subject matter covered. The subject matter to be covered by joint professional military education shall include at least the following:

&emsp;(1) National Military Strategy.

&emsp;(2) Joint planning at all levels of war.

&emsp;(3) Joint doctrine.

&emsp;(4) Joint command and control.

&emsp;(5) Joint force and joint requirements development.

(b)

Other Definitions.—In this chapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “senior level service school” means any of the following:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) The Army War College.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) The College of Naval Warfare.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) The Air War College.

&emsp;&emsp;(D) The Marine Corps War College.

&emsp;(2) The term “intermediate level service school” means any of the following:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) The United States Army Command and General Staff College.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) The College of Naval Command and Staff.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) The Air Command and Staff College.

&emsp;&emsp;(D) The Marine Corps Command and Staff College.

(Added Pub. L. 108–375, div. A, title V, §532(a)(2), Oct. 28, 2004, 118 Stat. 1897.)
