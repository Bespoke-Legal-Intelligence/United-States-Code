### §649j. Other administrative authorities

(a) <sup>1</sup>

In General.—The following provisions of this title shall apply to officers in competitive categories of officers designated for purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) Section 638b, relating to voluntary retirement incentives.

&emsp;(2) Section 639, relating to continuation on active duty to complete disciplinary action.

&emsp;(3) Section 640, relating to deferment of retirement or separation for medical reasons.

(Added Pub. L. 115–232, div. A, title V, §507(a)(1), Aug. 13, 2018, 132 Stat. 1748.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. There is no subsec. (b).</i>
