### \[§1624. Repealed. Pub. L. 101–510, div. A, title XII, §1207(c)(4), Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1665; Pub. L. 102–190, div. A, title X, §1061(a)(26)(C)(i), Dec. 5, 1991, 105 Stat. 1474\]

Section, added Pub. L. 99–145, title IX, §924(a)(1), Nov. 8, 1985, 99 Stat. 698, required a training program for quality assurance personnel.

#### Effective Date of Repeal

Section 1207(c)(4) of Pub. L. 101–510 and section 1061(a)(26)(C)(i) of Pub. L. 102–190 provided that the repeal of this section is effective Oct. 1, 1993.
