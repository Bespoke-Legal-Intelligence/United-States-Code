### §1112. Assets of Fund

There shall be deposited into the Fund the following, which shall constitute the assets of the Fund:

&emsp;(1) Amounts paid into the Fund under section 1116 of this title.

&emsp;(2) Any amount appropriated to the Fund.

&emsp;(3) Any return on investment of the assets of the Fund.

(Added Pub. L. 106–398, §1 \[\[div. A\], title VII, §713(a)(1)\], Oct. 30, 2000, 114 Stat. 1654, 1654A–180.)
