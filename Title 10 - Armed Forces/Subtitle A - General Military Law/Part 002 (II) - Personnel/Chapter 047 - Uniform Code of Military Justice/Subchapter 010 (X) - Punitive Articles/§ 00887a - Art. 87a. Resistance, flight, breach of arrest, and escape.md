### §887a. Art. 87a. Resistance, flight, breach of arrest, and escape

Any person subject to this chapter who—

&emsp;(1) resists apprehension;

&emsp;(2) flees from apprehension;

&emsp;(3) breaks arrest; or

&emsp;(4) escapes from custody or confinement;

> shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 69, §895; Pub. L. 104–106, div. A, title XI, §1112(a), Feb. 10, 1996, 110Stat. 461; renumbered §887a, Pub. L. 114–328, div. E, title LX, §5401(2), Dec. 23, 2016, 130 Stat. 2938.)
