### §898. Art. 98. Noncompliance with procedural rules

Any person subject to this chapter who—

&emsp;(1) is responsible for unnecessary delay in the disposition of any case of a person accused of an offense under this chapter; or

&emsp;(2) knowingly and intentionally fails to enforce or comply with any provision of this chapter regulating the proceedings before, during, or after trial of an accused;

> shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 69.)
