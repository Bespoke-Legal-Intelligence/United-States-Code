### §928b. Art. 128b. Domestic violence <sup>1</sup>

Any person who—

&emsp;(1) commits a violent offense against a spouse, an intimate partner, or an immediate family member of that person;

&emsp;(2) with intent to threaten or intimidate a spouse, an intimate partner, or an immediate family member of that person—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) commits an offense under this chapter against any person; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) commits an offense under this chapter against any property, including an animal;

&emsp;(3) with intent to threaten or intimidate a spouse, an intimate partner, or an immediate family member of that person, violates a protection order;

&emsp;(4) with intent to commit a violent offense against a spouse, an intimate partner, or an immediate family member of that person, violates a protection order; or

&emsp;(5) assaults a spouse, an intimate partner, or an immediate family member of that person by strangling or suffocating;

> shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.

(Added Pub. L. 115–232, div. A, title V, §532(a)(1), Aug. 13, 2018, 132 Stat. 1759.)
