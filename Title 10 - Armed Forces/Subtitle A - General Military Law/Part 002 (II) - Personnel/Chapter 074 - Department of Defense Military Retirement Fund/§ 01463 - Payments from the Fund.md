### §1463. Payments from the Fund

(a) There shall be paid from the Fund—

&emsp;(1) retired pay payable to members on the retired lists of the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps;

&emsp;(2) retired pay payable under chapter 67 <sup>1</sup> of this title to former members of the armed forces (other than retired pay payable by the Secretary of Transportation);

&emsp;(3) retainer pay payable to members of the Fleet Reserve and Fleet Marine Corps Reserve;

&emsp;(4) benefits payable under programs under the jurisdiction of the Department of Defense that provide annuities for survivors of members and former members of the armed forces, including chapter 73 of this title, section 4 of Public Law 92–425, and section 5 of Public Law 96–402; and

&emsp;(5) amounts payable under section 1408(h) of this title.

(b) The assets of the Fund are hereby made available for payments under subsection (a).

#### References in Text

Chapter 67 of this title, referred to in subsec. (a)(2), was transferred to part II of subtitle E of this title, renumbered as chapter 1223, and amended generally by Pub. L. 103–337, div. A, title XVI, §1662(j)(1), Oct. 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 2998. A new chapter 67 (§1331) of this title was added by section 1662(j)(7) of Pub. L. 103–337.
