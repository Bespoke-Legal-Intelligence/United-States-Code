### §518. Temporary enlistments

Temporary enlistments may be made only in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, orCoast Guard, as the case may be, without specification of component.

(Added Pub. L. 90–235, §2(a)(1)(B), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 755.)
