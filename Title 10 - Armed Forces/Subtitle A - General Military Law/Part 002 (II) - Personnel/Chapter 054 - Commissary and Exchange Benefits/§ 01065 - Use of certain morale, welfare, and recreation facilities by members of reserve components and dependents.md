### §1065. Use of certain morale, welfare, and recreation facilities by members of reserve components and dependents

(a)

Unrestricted Use Required.—Members of the Selected Reserve in good standing (as determined by the Secretary concerned) and members who would be eligible for retired pay under chapter 67 <sup>1</sup> of this title but for the fact that the member is under 60 years of age, and the dependents of such members, shall be permitted to use the exchange stores and other revenue generating facilities operated by nonappropriated fund activities of the Department of Defense for the morale, welfare, and recreation of members of the Armed Forces. Such use shall be permitted on the same basis as members on active duty.

(b)

Eligibility to Use Authorized.—Subject to such regulations as the Secretary of Defense may prescribe, members of the Ready Reserve (other than members of the Selected Reserve) may be permitted to use the facilities referred to in subsection (a) on the same basis as members serving on active duty.

(Added Pub. L. 101–510, div. A, title III, §321(c), Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1528.)

#### References in Text

Chapter 67 of this title, referred to in subsec. (a), was transferred to part II of subtitle E of this title, renumbered as chapter 1223, and amended generally by Pub. L. 103–337, div. A, title XVI, §1662(j)(1), Oct. 5, 1994, 108 Stat. 2998. A new chapter 67 (§1331) of this title was added by section 1662(j)(7) of Pub. L. 103–337.

#### Effective Date

Section effective 120 days after Nov. 5, 1990, see section 321(e)(1) of Pub. L. 101–510, set out as a note under section 1064 of this title.

#### Regulations

For authority of Secretary of Defense to prescribe regulations necessary for proper administration of section, see section 321(e)(2) of Pub. L. 101–510, set out as a note under section 1064 of this title.
