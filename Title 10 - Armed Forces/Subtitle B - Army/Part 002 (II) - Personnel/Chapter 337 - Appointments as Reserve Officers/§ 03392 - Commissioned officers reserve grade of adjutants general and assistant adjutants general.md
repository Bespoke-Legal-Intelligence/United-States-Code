### §3392. Commissioned officers: reserve grade of adjutants general and assistant adjutants general

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the adjutant general or an assistant adjutant general of a State or Territory, Puerto Rico, or the District of Columbia may, upon being extended Federal recognition, be appointed as a reserve officer of the Army as of the date on which he is so recognized.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(80)(E), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1479; amended Pub. L. 100–456, div. A, title XII, §1234(a)(1), Sept. 29, 1988, 102 Stat. 2059.)
