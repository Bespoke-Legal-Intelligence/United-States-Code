### §3357. Commissioned officers; Army Reserve: for service in Army Nurse Corps or Army Medical Specialist Corps

To be eligible for an appointment as a reserve officer for service in the Army Reserve in theArmy Nurse Corps or the Army Medical Specialist Corps, a person must be at least 21 years of age.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 194; Sept. 2, 1958, Pub. L. 85–861, §1(60), (80)(D), 72 Stat. 1462, 1468.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Section repealed effective Oct. 1, 1996, see note set out preceding section 3351 of this title.</i>
