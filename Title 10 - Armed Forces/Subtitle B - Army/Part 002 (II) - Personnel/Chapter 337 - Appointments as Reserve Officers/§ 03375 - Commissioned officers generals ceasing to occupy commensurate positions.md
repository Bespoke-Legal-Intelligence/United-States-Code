### §3375. Commissioned officers: generals ceasing to occupy commensurate positions

Within 30 days after an officer in a reserve general officer grade ceases to occupy a position commensurate with his grade or with a higher grade, the Secretary of the Army shall, as the officer elects—

&emsp;(1) transfer him in grade to the Retired Reserve, if he is qualified and applies therefor;

&emsp;(2) transfer him in grade to the inactive status list of the Standby Reserve, if he is qualified;

&emsp;(3) discharge him from his reserve appointment and, if he is qualified and applies therefor, appoint him in the reserve grade held by him as a reserve officer before his appointment in a general officer grade; or

&emsp;(4) discharge him from his reserve appointment.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(80)(E), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1475.)
