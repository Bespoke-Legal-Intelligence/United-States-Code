### §3395. During war

In addition to appointments in time of war under chapter 339 of this title, appointments of reserve officers may be made in time of war.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 195.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Section repealed effective Oct. 1, 1996, see note set out preceding section 3351 of this title.</i>
