### §3067. Army Medical Department

There is an Army Medical Department in the Army. The Army Medical Department consists of—

&emsp;(1) the Surgeon General;

&emsp;(2) the Assistant Surgeons General;

&emsp;(3) the Medical Corps;

&emsp;(4) the Dental Corps;

&emsp;(5) the Veterinary Corps;

&emsp;(6) the Medical Service Corps;

&emsp;(7) the Army Nurse Corps; and

&emsp;(8) the Army Medical Specialist Corps.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 168; Sept. 2, 1958, Pub. L. 85–861, §1(60), 72 Stat. 1462; June 4, 1968, Pub. L. 90–329, 82 Stat. 170.)
