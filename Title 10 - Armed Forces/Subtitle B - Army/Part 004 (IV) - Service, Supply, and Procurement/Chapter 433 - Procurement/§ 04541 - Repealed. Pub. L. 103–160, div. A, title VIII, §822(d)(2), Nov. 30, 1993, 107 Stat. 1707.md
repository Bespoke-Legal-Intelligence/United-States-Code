### \[§4541. Repealed. Pub. L. 103–160, div. A, title VIII, §822(d)(2), Nov. 30, 1993, 107 Stat. 1707\]

Section, added Pub. L. 97–258, §2(b)(9)(B), Sept. 13, 1982, 96 Stat. 1056, authorized Secretary of the Army to accept gratuitous services of officers of the Army Reserve. See section 10212 of this title.
