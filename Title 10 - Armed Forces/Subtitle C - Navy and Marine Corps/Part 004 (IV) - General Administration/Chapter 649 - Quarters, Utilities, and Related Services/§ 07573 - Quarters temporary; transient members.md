### §7573. Quarters: temporary; transient members

Temporary quarters may be furnished on a rental basis to transient members of the naval service with their dependents, for periods not exceeding 60 days, without loss of entitlement to basic allowance for quarters.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 468.)
