### §5894. Selection boards: oath of members

Each member of a selection board convened under this chapter shall swear that he will, without prejudice or partiality, and having in view both the special fitness of officers and the efficiency of the naval service, perform the duties imposed on him as a member of the board.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(133), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1501.)
