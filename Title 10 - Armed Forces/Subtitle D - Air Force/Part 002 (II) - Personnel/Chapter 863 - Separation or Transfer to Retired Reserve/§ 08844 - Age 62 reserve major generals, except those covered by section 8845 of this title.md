### §8844. Age 62: reserve major generals, except those covered by section 8845 of this title

Unless retired, transferred to the Retired Reserve, or discharged at an earlier date, each commissioned officer whose reserve grade is major general, except an officer covered by section 8845 of this title, shall, on the last day of the month in which he becomes 62 years of age—

&emsp;(1) be transferred to the Retired Reserve, if he is qualified and applies therefor; or

&emsp;(2) if he is not qualified or does not apply therefor, be discharged from his reserve appointment.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(192), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1535; amended Pub. L. 86–559, §1(68), June 30, 1960, 74 Stat. 279.)
