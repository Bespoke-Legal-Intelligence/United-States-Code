### §8845. Age 64: Chief of National Guard Bureau; adjutants general

Unless retired, transferred to the Retired Reserve, or discharged at an earlier date, a reserve officer of the Air Force who is Chief of the National Guard Bureau or adjutant general of a State or Territory, Puerto Rico, or the District of Columbia shall, on the last day of the month in which he becomes 64 years of age—

&emsp;(1) be transferred to the Retired Reserve, if he is qualified and applies therefor; or

&emsp;(2) if he is not qualified or does not apply therefor, be discharged from his reserve appointment.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(192), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1535; amended Pub. L. 86–559, §1(69), June 30, 1960, 74 Stat. 279; Pub. L. 100–456, div. A, title XII, §1234(a)(1), Sept. 29, 1988, 102 Stat. 2059.)
