### §8920. More than thirty years: professors of the United States Air Force Academy

The Secretary of the Air Force may retire any permanent professor of the United States Air Force Academy who has more than 30 years of service as a commissioned officer.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 551.)
