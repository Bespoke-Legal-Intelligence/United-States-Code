### §8281. Commissioned officer grades

The commissioned grades in the Regular Air Force are:

&emsp;(1) Major general.

&emsp;(2) Brigadier general.

&emsp;(3) Colonel.

&emsp;(4) Lieutenant colonel.

&emsp;(5) Major.

&emsp;(6) Captain.

&emsp;(7) First lieutenant.

&emsp;(8) Second lieutenant.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 507.)
