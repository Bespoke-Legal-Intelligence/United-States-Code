### §8393. Commissioned officers: sea or foreign service not to be required for promotion

Sea or foreign service may not be made a condition to the promotion of reserve commissioned officers in reserve grades.

(Added Pub. L. 85–861, §1(178)(C), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1531.)
