#### Amendments

<b>2018</b>—Pub. L. 115–232, div. A, title VIII, §806(d)(1), Aug. 13, 2018, 132 Stat. 1833, redesignated chapter 953 of this title as this chapter.

<b>2008</b>—Pub. L. 110–181, div. A, title III, §375(c)(2)(B), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 84, struck out items 9832 "Property accountability: regulations" and 9836 "Individual equipment: unauthorized disposition".

<b>2006</b>—Pub. L. 109–163, div. A, title VI, §683(c)(2), (3), Jan. 6, 2006, 119 Stat. 3324, struck out "enlisted" before "members" in item 9837.

<b>2002</b>—Pub. L. 107–314, div. A, title X, §1006(c)(2), Dec. 2, 2002, 116 Stat. 2633, struck out item 9835 "Reports of survey".

<b>1982</b>—Pub. L. 97–258, §2(b)(14)(A), Sept. 13, 1982, 96 Stat. 1058, added items 9841 and 9842.

<b>1980</b>—Pub. L. 96–513, title V, §514(22)(C), Dec. 12, 1980, 94 Stat. 2937, substituted "remission or cancellation of indebtedness of enlisted members" for "deductions from pay" in item 9837.

<b>1962</b>—Pub. L. 87–480, §1(5), June 8, 1962, 76 Stat. 94, struck out item 9833 "Accountability for public money; disbursing officers; agent officers".
