### §9341. Faculty and other officers: leaves of absence

The Superintendent of the Academy may grant a leave of absence for the period of thesuspension of the ordinary academic studies, without deduction of pay or allowances, to a professor, assistant professor, instructor, or other officer of the Academy.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 563.)
