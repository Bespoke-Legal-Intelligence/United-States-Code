### §9303. Aviation cadets and aviation students: schools

The Secretary of the Air Force shall establish and maintain—

&emsp;(1) one or more schools for the training and instruction of aviation cadets; and

&emsp;(2) courses of instruction for aviation students at one or more established flying schools.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 560.)
