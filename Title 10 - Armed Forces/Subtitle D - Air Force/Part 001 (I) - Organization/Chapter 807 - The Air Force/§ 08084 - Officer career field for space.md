### §8084. Officer career field for space

The Secretary of the Air Force shall establish and implement policies and procedures to develop a career field for officers in the Air Force with technical competence in space-related matters to have the capabilty <sup>1</sup> to—

&emsp;(1) develop space doctrine and concepts of space operations;

&emsp;(2) develop space systems; and

&emsp;(3) operate space systems.

(Added Pub. L. 107–107, div. A, title IX, §912(a), Dec. 28, 2001, 115 Stat. 1196.)
