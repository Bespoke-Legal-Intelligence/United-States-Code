### §12551. Prohibition of use of Air Force Reserve AGR personnel for Air Force base security functions

(a)

Limitation.—The Secretary of the Air Force may not use members of the Air Force Reserve who are AGR personnel for the performance of force protection, base security, or security police functions at an Air Force facility in the United States.

(b)

AGR Personnel Defined.—In this section, the term “AGR personnel” means members of the Air Force Reserve who are on active duty (other than for training) in connection with organizing, administering, recruiting, instructing, or training the Air Force Reserve.

(Added Pub. L. 105–85, div. A, title V, §515(a), Nov. 18, 1997, 111 Stat. 1732.)
