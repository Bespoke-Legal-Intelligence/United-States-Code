### §4514. Authority to require reports by enterprises

#### (a) Special reports and reports of financial condition

#### (1) Financial condition

&emsp;The Director may require an enterprise to submit reports of financial condition and operations (in addition to the annual and quarterly reports required under section 1723a(k) of this title and section 1456(c) of this title).

#### (2) Special reports

&emsp;The Director may also require an enterprise to submit special reports whenever, in the judgment of the Director, such reports are necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

#### (3) Limitation

&emsp;The Director may not require the inclusion, in any report pursuant to paragraph (1) or (2), of any information that is not reasonably obtainable by the enterprise.

#### (4) Notice and declaration

&emsp;The Director shall notify the enterprise, a reasonable period in advance of the date for submission of any report under this subsection, of any specific information to be contained in the report and the date for the submission of the report. Each report under this subsection shall contain a declaration by the president, vice president, treasurer, or any other officer designated by the board of directors of the enterprise to make such declaration, that the report is true and correct to the best of such officer's knowledge and belief.

#### (b) Capital distributions

The Director may require an enterprise to submit a report to the Director after the declaration of any capital distribution by the enterprise and before making the capital distribution. The report shall be made in such form and under such circumstances and shall contain such information as the Director shall require.

(Pub. L. 102–550, title XIII, §1314, Oct. 28, 1992, 106 Stat. 3946.)

#### References in Text
