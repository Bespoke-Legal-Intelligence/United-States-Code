### §5403. Fines

The Postal Service may impose or remit fines on carriers transporting mail by air on routesextending beyond the borders of the United States for—

&emsp;(1) unreasonable or unnecessary delay to mail; and

&emsp;(2) other delinquencies in the transportation of the mail.

(Pub. L. 91–375, Aug. 12, 1970, 84 Stat. 773.)

#### <b>Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries</b>

#### Effective Date

Section effective July 1, 1971, pursuant to Resolution No. 71–9 of the Board of Governors. See section 15(a) of Pub. L. 91–375, set out as a note preceding section 101 of this title.
