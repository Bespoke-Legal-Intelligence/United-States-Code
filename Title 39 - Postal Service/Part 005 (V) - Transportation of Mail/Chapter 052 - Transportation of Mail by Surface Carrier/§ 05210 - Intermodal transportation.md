### §5210. Intermodal transportation

The Postal Service may permit a carrier to perform mail transportation by any form of transportation it deems appropriate at rates orcompensation not exceeding those allowable for similar service by the designated form of transportation.

(Pub. L. 91–375, Aug. 12, 1970, 84 Stat. 771.)
