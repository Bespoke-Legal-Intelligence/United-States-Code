### §5003. Establishment of post roads

The following are post roads:

&emsp;(1) the waters of the United States, during the time the mail is carried thereon;

&emsp;(2) railroads or parts of railroads and air routes in operation;

&emsp;(3) canals, during the time the mail is carried thereon;

&emsp;(4) public roads, highways, and toll roads during the time the mail is carried thereon; and

&emsp;(5) letter-carrier routes established for the collection and delivery of mail.

(Pub. L. 91–375, Aug. 12, 1970, 84 Stat. 766.)

#### <b>Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries</b>

#### Effective Date

Section effective July 1, 1971, pursuant to Resolution No. 71–9 of the Board of Governors. See section 15(a) of Pub. L. 91–375, set out as a note preceding section 101 of this title.
