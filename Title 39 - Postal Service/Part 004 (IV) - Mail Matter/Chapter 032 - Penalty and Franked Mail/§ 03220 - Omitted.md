### \[§3220. Omitted\]

#### Codification

Section, added Pub. L. 99–87, §1(a)(1), Aug. 9, 1985, 99 Stat. 290, which related to use of official mail in the location and recovery of missing children, was omitted in view of termination of amendment enacting this section by section 5 of Pub. L. 99–87, as amended. See Termination Date note below.

#### Termination Date

Section 5 of Pub. L. 99–87, as amended by Pub. L. 100–202, §101(m) \[title VI, §627(a)\], Dec. 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 1329–390, 1329–430; Pub. L. 102–514, §1(2), Oct. 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 3371; Pub. L. 105–126, §1(2), Dec. 1, 1997, 111 Stat. 2542, provided that: “The amendments made by section 1 \[enacting this section and amending sections 3201 and 3204 of this title and section 733 of Title 44, PublicPrinting and Documents\] and any guidelines, rules, or regulations prescribed to carry out such amendments shall cease to be effective after December 31, 2002.”
