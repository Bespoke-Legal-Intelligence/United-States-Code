### §300112. Office of the Ombudsman

(a)

Establishment.—The corporation shall establish an Office of the Ombudsman with such duties and responsibilities as may be provided inthe bylaws or a resolution of the board of governors.

(b)

Report.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—The Office of the Ombudsman shall submit annually to the appropriate Congressional committees a report concerning any trends and systemic matters that the Office of the Ombudsman has identified as confronting the corporation.

&emsp;(2)

Appropriate congressional committees.—For purposes of paragraph (1), the appropriate Congressional committees are the following committees of Congress:

&emsp;&emsp;(A)

Senate committees.—The appropriate Congressional committees of the Senate are—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the Committee on Finance;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the Committee on Foreign Relations;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) the Committee on the Judiciary.

&emsp;&emsp;(B)

House committees.—The appropriate Congressional committees of the House of Representatives are—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the Committee on Energy and Commerce;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the Committee on Foreign Affairs;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) the Committee on Homeland Security;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) the Committee on the Judiciary; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) the Committee on Ways and Means.

(Added Pub. L. 110–26, §11(a), May 11, 2007, 121 Stat. 110.)
