### §702. Definition

For purposes of this chapter, “Federal land management agency” includes—

&emsp;(1) the Forest Service of the Department of Agriculture;

&emsp;(2) the Bureau of Land Management of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;(3) the National Park Service of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;(4) the Fish and Wildlife Service of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;(5) the Bureau of Reclamation of the Department of the Interior; and

&emsp;(6) the Army Corps of Engineers.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1267.)
