### §701. Findings

Congress finds that—

&emsp;(1) Federal lands, parks, recreation areas, and waterways provide recreational opportunities for millions of Americans each year;

&emsp;(2) Federal lands administered by Federal land management agencies contain valuable wildlife, scenery, natural and historic features, and other resources which may be damaged by litter and misuse;

&emsp;(3) it is in the best interest of the United States and its citizens to maintain and preserve the beauty, safety, and availability of these Federal lands;

&emsp;(4) these Federal land management agencies have been designated as the caretakers of these Federal lands and are responsible for maintaining and preserving those areas and facilities;

&emsp;(5) there is great value in volunteer involvement in maintaining and preserving Federal lands for recreational use;

&emsp;(6) the Federal land management agencies should be concerned with promoting a sense of pride and ownership among citizens toward these lands;

&emsp;(7) the use of citizen volunteers in a national cleanup effort promotes these goals and encourages the thoughtful use of these Federal lands and facilities;

&emsp;(8) the positive impact of annual cleanup events held at various recreation sites has already been proven by steadily declining levels of litter at these sites; and

&emsp;(9) a national program for cleaning and maintaining Federal lands using volunteers will save millions of tax dollars.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1266.)
