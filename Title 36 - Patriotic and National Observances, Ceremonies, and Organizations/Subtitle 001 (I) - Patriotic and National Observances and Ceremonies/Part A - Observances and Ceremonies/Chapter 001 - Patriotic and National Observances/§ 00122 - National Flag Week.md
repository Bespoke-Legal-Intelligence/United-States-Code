### §122. National Flag Week

The President is requested to issue each year a proclamation—

&emsp;(1) designating the week in which June 14 falls as National Flag Week; and

&emsp;(2) calling on citizens to display the flag of the United States during National Flag Week.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1259.)
