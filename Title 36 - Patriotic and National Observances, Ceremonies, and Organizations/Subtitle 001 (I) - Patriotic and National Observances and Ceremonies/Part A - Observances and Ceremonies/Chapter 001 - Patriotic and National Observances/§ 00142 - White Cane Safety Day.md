### §142. White Cane Safety Day

The President may issue each year a proclamation—

&emsp;(1) designating October 15 as White Cane Safety Day; and

&emsp;(2) calling on the people of the United States to observe White Cane Safety Day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1262.)
