### §133. National Transportation Week

The President is requested to issue each year a proclamation—

&emsp;(1) designating the week that includes the third Friday of May as National Transportation Week; and

&emsp;(2) inviting the people of the United States to observe National Transportation Week with appropriate ceremonies and activities as a tribute to the men and women who, night and day, move goods and individuals throughout the United States.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1260.)
