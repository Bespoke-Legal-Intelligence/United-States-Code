### §40502. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are—

&emsp;(1) to form a bond of friendship and comradeship among all holders of the Medal of Honor as presented by Congress;

&emsp;(2) to protect, uphold, and preserve the dignity and honor of the medal at all times and on all occasions;

&emsp;(3) to protect the name of the medal and individual holders of the medal from exploitation;

&emsp;(4) to provide appropriate aid to all persons to whom the medal has been awarded, their widows, or their children;

&emsp;(5) to serve our country in peace as in war;

&emsp;(6) to inspire and stimulate our youth to become worthy citizens of our country; and

&emsp;(7) to foster and perpetuate Americanism.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1333.)
