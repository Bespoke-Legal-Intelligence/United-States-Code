### §151902. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are to raise from private sources, administer, and dispersefunds for medical education, and in carrying out those purposes, to take other appropriate action to promote—

&emsp;(1) the interpretation of the needs of medical education to the American public;

&emsp;(2) the encouragement of the growth, development, and advancement of constantly improving standards and methods in the education and training of all medical personnel in the United States; and

&emsp;(3) the preservation of academic freedom in the institutions of medical education.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1408.)
