### §230509. Duty to maintain tax-exempt status

The corporation shall maintain its status as an organization exempt from taxation under theInternal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 1 et seq.).

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1487.)
