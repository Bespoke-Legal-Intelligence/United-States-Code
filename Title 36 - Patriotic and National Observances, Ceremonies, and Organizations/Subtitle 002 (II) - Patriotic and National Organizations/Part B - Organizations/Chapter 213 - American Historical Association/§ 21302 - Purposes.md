### §21302. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are—

&emsp;(1) to promote historical studies;

&emsp;(2) to collect and preserve historical manuscripts; and

&emsp;(3) other kindred purposes in the interest of American history and of history in America.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1294.)
