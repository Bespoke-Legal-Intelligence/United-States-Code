### §230102. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are fraternal, patriotic, historical, charitable, and educational, and are—

&emsp;(1) to preserve and strengthen comradeship among its members;

&emsp;(2) to assist worthy comrades;

&emsp;(3) to perpetuate the memory and history of our dead, and to assist their surviving spouses and orphans;

&emsp;(4) to maintain true allegiance to the Government of the United States, and fidelity to its Constitution and laws;

&emsp;(5) to foster true patriotism;

&emsp;(6) to maintain and extend the institutions of American freedom; and

&emsp;(7) to preserve and defend the United States from all enemies.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1481; Pub. L. 107–242, §2, Oct. 16, 2002, 116 Stat. 1497; Pub. L. 113–199, §1(b), Dec. 4, 2014, 128 Stat. 2058.)
