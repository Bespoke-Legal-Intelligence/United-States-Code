### §30702. Purpose

The purpose of the corporation is to foster the development of fundamental education through programs and projects such as—

&emsp;(1) giving citizens (children, youth, and adults) an opportunity to acquire the understandings and skills necessary to relate the resources of the community to the needs and interests of the community;

&emsp;(2) demonstrating programs of fundamental education and measuring results; and

&emsp;(3) training men and women as leaders in fundamental education by providing internships and other experiences.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1322.)
