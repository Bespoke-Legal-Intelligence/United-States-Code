### §154102. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are, through organization and cooperation with the Department of the Navy—

&emsp;(1) to encourage and aid American young people to develop an interest and skill in basic seamanship and in its naval adaptations;

&emsp;(2) to train them in seagoing skills; and

&emsp;(3) to teach them patriotism, courage, self-reliance, and kindred virtues.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1432.)
