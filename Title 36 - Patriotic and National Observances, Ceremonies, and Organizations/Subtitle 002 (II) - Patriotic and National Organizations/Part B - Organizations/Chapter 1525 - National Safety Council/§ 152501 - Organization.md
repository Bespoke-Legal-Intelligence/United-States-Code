### §152501. Organization

(a)

Federal Charter.—National Safety Council (in this chapter, the "corporation") is a federally chartered corporation.

(b)

Perpetual Existence.—Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1415.)
