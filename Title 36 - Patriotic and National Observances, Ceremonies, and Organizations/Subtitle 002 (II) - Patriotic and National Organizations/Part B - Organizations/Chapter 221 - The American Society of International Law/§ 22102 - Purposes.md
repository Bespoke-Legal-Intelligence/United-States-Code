### §22102. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are—

&emsp;(1) to foster the study of international law; and

&emsp;(2) to promote the establishment and maintenance of international relations on the basis of law and justice.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1300.)
