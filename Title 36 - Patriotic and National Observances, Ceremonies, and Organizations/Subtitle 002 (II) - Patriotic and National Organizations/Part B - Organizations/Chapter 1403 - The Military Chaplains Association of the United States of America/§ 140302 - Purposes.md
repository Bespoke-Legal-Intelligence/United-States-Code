### §140302. Purposes

The purposes of the corporation are—

&emsp;(1) to safeguard and strengthen the forces of faith and morality of our Nation;

&emsp;(2) to perpetuate and deepen the bonds of understanding and friendship of our military service;

&emsp;(3) to preserve our spiritual influence and interest in all members and veterans of the Armed Forces;

&emsp;(4) to uphold the Constitution of the United States; and

&emsp;(5) to promote justice, peace, and good will.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1380.)
