### §30504. Membership

An individual is eligible for membership in the corporation if—

&emsp;(1) she is a mother, adoptive mother, or stepmother (who has given a mother's care at least since the stepchild was age 13) of a son or daughter who—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is serving in the Armed Forces; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) has served in, or has been honorably discharged from, the Armed Forces in World War II or the Korean hostilities; and

&emsp;(2) she is living in the United States.

(Pub. L. 105–225, Aug. 12, 1998, 112 Stat. 1320.)
