### §374. Regulations

This subchapter and subchapter II shall be administered under regulations prescribed by—

&emsp;(1) the Secretary of Defense, with respect to the armed forces under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of Defense;

&emsp;(2) the Secretary of Homeland Security, with respect to the Coast Guard when it is not operating as a service in the Navy;

&emsp;(3) the Secretary of Health and Human Services, with respect to the commissioned corps of the Public Health Service; and

&emsp;(4) the Secretary of Commerce, with respect to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

(Added Pub. L. 110–181, div. A, title VI, §661(a)(2), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 178.)
