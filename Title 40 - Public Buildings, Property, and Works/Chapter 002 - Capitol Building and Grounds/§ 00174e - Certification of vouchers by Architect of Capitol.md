### §174e. Certification of vouchers by Architect of Capitol

It shall not be a duty of the Architect of the Capitol to certify any pay roll or other vouchercovering any expenditure from any appropriation for the Senate Office Building, or for any other building or activity, unless the obligation involved was incurred by him or under his direction.

(June 8, 1942, ch. 396, 56 Stat. 343.)
