### §193<i>l</i>. Concerts on grounds

Nothing in sections 193a to 193k, 207a, 212a, 212a–2, and 212a–3, of this title shall be construed to prohibit the giving of concerts in the United States Capitol Grounds, at such times as will not interfere with the Congress, by any band in the service of the United States, when and as authorized by the Architect of the Capitol.

(July 31, 1946, ch. 707, §13, 60 Stat. 720.)
