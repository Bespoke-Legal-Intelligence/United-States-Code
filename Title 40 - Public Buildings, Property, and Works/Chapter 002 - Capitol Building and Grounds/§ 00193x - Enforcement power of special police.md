### §193x. Enforcement power of special police

The special police provided for in section 193n of this title are authorized to enforce concurrently with the United States Park Police the laws and regulation applicable to the National Capital Parks, and to make arrests for violations of sections 193<i>o</i> to 193q of this title, within the several areas located within the exterior boundaries of the face of the curb lines of the squares within which the aforementioned buildings are located.

(Oct. 24, 1951, ch. 559, §11, as added Aug. 1, 1964, Pub. L. 88–391, §4, 78 Stat. 366.)
