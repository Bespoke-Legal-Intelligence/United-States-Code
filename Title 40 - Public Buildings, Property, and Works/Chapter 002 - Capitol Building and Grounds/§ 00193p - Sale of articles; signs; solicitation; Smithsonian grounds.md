### §193p. Sale of articles; signs; solicitation; Smithsonian grounds

It shall be unlawful for anyone other than an authorized employee or concessionaire to offeror expose any article for sale within the specified buildings or grounds; or to display any sign, placard, or other form of advertisement; or to solicit alms, subscriptions, or contributions therein.

(Oct. 24, 1951, ch. 559, §3, 65 Stat. 634.)
