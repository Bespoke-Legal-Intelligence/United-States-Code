### §762. Definitions

As used in sections 762 to 762d of this title—

&emsp;(1) The term “TDD” means a Telecommunications Device for the Deaf, a machine which employs graphic communications in the transmission of coded signals through the nationwide telecommunications system.

&emsp;(2) The term “Federal agency” has the meaning given such term by section 472(b) of this title.

(Pub. L. 100–542, §2, Oct. 28, 1988, 102 Stat. 2721.)

#### Short Title

Section 1 of Pub. L. 100–542 provided that: “This Act \[enacting this section and sections 762a to 762d of this title\] may be cited as the ‘Telecommunications Accessibility Enhancement Act of 1988’.”
