### §6132. Sale of articles, signs, and solicitation in Supreme Court Building and grounds

It is unlawful—

&emsp;(1) to offer or expose any article for sale in the Supreme Court Building or grounds;

&emsp;(2) to display a sign, placard, or other form of advertisement in the Building or grounds; or

&emsp;(3) to solicit fares, alms, subscriptions, or contributions in the Building or grounds.

(Pub. L. 107–217, Aug. 21, 2002, 116 Stat. 1183.)
