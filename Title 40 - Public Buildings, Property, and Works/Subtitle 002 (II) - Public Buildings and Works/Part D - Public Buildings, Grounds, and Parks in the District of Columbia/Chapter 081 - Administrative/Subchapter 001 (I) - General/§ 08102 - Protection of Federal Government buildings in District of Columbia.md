### §8102. Protection of Federal Government buildings in District of Columbia

The Attorney General and the Secretary of the Treasury may prohibit—

&emsp;(1) a vehicle from parking or standing on a street or roadway adjacent to a building in the District of Columbia—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) at least partly owned or possessed by, or leased to, the Federal Government; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) used by law enforcement authorities subject to their jurisdiction; and

&emsp;(2) a person or entity from conducting business on property immediately adjacent to a building described in paragraph (1).

(Pub. L. 107–217, Aug. 21, 2002, 116 Stat. 1205.)
