### §15736. Southern New England Regional Commission

The region of the Southern New England Regional Commission shall include the following counties:

&emsp;(1)

Rhode island.—Each county in the State of Rhode Island.

&emsp;(2)

Connecticut.—The counties of Hartford, Middlesex, New Haven, New London, Tolland, and Windham in the State of Connecticut.

&emsp;(3)

Massachusetts.—Each county in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

(Added Pub. L. 118–272, div. B, title II, §2250(b)(1), Jan. 4, 2025, 138 Stat. 3210.)
