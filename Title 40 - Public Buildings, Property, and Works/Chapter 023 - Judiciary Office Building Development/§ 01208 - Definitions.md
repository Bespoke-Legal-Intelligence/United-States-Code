### §1208. Definitions

As used in this chapter—

#### (1) Architect

&emsp;The term “Architect” means the Architect of the Capitol.

#### (2) Chief Justice

&emsp;The term “Chief Justice” means the Chief Justice of the United States or his designee; except that in any case in which there is a vacancy of the office of the Chief Justice of the United States, the most senior associate justice of the Supreme Court shall be treated asthe Chief Justice of the United States for purposes of this chapter until such time as such vacancy is filled.

#### (3) Commission

&emsp;The term “Commission” means the Commission for the Judiciary Office Building established by section 1206 of this title.

(Pub. L. 100–480, §10, Oct. 7, 1988, 102 Stat. 2335.)
