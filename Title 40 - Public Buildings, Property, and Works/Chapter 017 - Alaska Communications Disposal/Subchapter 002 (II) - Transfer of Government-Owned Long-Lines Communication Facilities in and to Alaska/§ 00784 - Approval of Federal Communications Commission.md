### §784. Approval of Federal Communications Commission

Transfers under this subchapter do not require the approval of the Federal Communications Commission except to the extent that the approval of the Federal Communications Commission is necessary under section 783(3) of this title.

(Pub. L. 90–135, title II, §204, Nov. 14, 1967, 81 Stat. 443.)
