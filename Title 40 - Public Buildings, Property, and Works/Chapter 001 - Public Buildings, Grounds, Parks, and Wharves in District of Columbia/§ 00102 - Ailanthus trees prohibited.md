### §102. Ailanthus trees prohibited

No more ailantus <sup>1</sup> trees shall be purchased for or planted in the public grounds.

(R.S. §1830.)

#### Codification

R.S. §1830 derived from act Mar. 3, 1853, ch. 97, §1, 10 Stat. 207.

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “ailanthus”.</i>
