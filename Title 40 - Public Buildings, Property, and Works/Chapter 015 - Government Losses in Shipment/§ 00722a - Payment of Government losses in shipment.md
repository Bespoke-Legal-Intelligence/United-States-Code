### §722a. Payment of Government losses in shipment

Beginning in fiscal year 1995 and thereafter, there are appropriated such sums as may be necessary to make payments for the replacement of valuables, or the value thereof, lost, destroyed, or damaged in the course of shipments effected pursuant to section 721 of this title.

(Pub. L. 103–329, title I, Sept. 30, 1994, 108 Stat. 2387.)

#### Codification

Section enacted as part of the Treasury, Postal Service and General Government Appropriations Act, 1995, and not as part of the Government Losses in Shipment Act which comprises this chapter.
