### §17502. Monitoring system

The head of each executive agency shall designate one office, officer, or employee of the agency—

&emsp;(1) to establish and operate a central monitoring system for the motor vehicle operations of the agency, related activities, and related reporting requirements; and

&emsp;(2) provide oversight of those operations, activities, and requirements.

(Pub. L. 107–217, Aug. 21, 2002, 116 Stat. 1283.)
