### §1308. Exclusive rights

The owner of a design protected under this chapter has the exclusive right to—

&emsp;(1) make, have made, or import, for sale or for use in trade, any useful article embodying that design; and

&emsp;(2) sell or distribute for sale or for use in trade any useful article embodying that design.

(Added Pub. L. 105–304, title V, §502, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2908.)
