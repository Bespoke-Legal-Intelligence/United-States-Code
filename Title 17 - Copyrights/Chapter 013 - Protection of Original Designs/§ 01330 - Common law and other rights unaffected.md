### §1330. Common law and other rights unaffected

Nothing in this chapter shall annul or limit—

&emsp;(1) common law or other rights or remedies, if any, available to or held by any person with respect to a design which has not been registered under this chapter; or

&emsp;(2) any right under the trademark laws or any right protected against unfair competition.

(Added Pub. L. 105–304, title V, §502, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2916.)
