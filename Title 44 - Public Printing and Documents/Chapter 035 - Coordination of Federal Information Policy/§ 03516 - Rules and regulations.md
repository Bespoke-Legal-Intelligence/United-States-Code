### §3516. Rules and regulations

The Director shall promulgate rules, regulations, or procedures necessary to exercise the authority provided by this chapter.

(Added Pub. L. 96–511, §2(a), Dec. 11, 1980, 94 Stat. 2824.)

#### Prior Provisions

Provisions similar to those comprising this section were contained in former section 3510 prior to the general amendment of this chapter by Pub. L. 96–511, §2(a).
