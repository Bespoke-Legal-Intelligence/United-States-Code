### §88b–3. Membership of Page Board

#### (a) Appointed and designated members

The Page Board shall consist of—

&emsp;(1) two Members of the House appointed by the Speaker and one Member of the House appointed by the minority leader; and

&emsp;(2) the Clerk and the Sergeant at Arms of the House.

#### (b) “Member of the House” defined

As used in sections 88b–2 to 88b–4 of this title, the term “Member of the House” means a Representative in, and a Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress.

(Pub. L. 97–377, title I, §127, Dec. 21, 1982, 96 Stat. 1914; Pub. L. 104–186, title II, §204(37), Aug. 20, 1996, 110 Stat. 1735; Pub. L. 105–275, title I, §101(a), Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2438.)

#### Codification

Section is based on section 2 of House Resolution No. 611, Ninety-seventh Congress, Nov. 30, 1982, which was enacted into permanent law by Pub. L. 97–377.

#### Amendments

<b>1998</b>—Subsec. (a)(3). Pub. L. 105–275 inserted “and” at end of par. (1), substituted a period for “; and” at end of par. (2), and struck out par. (3) which read as follows: “the Architect of the Capitol.”

<b>1996</b>—Subsec. (a)(2). Pub. L. 104–186 substituted “Clerk and the Sergeant” for “Clerk, Doorkeeper, and Sergeant”.

#### Effective Date of 1998 Amendment

Pub. L. 105–275, title I, §101(b), Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2438, provided that: “The amendment made by subsection (a) \[amending this section\] shall apply with respect to the One Hundred Sixth Congress and each succeeding Congress.”
