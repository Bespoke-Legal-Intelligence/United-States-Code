### §60e–1d. Withholding of charitable contributions; definitions

For purposes of section 60e–1c of this title—

&emsp;(1) the term “charitable organizations” means national voluntary health and welfare agencies designated by the Director of the Office of Personnel Management pursuant to Executive Order 10927, dated March 19, 1961;

&emsp;(2) the term “Clerk” means the Clerk of the House of Representatives;

&emsp;(3) the term “employee” means any employee of the House of Representatives whose compensation is disbursed by the Clerk.

(Pub. L. 95–391, title I, §111, Sept. 30, 1978, 92 Stat. 777; 1978 Reorg. Plan No. 2, §102, eff. Jan. 1, 1979, 43 F.R. 36037, 92 Stat. 3783.)

#### References in Text

Executive Order 10927, dated March 18, 1961, referred to in par. (1), was revoked, and is covered, by Ex. Ord. No. 12353, Mar. 23, 1982, 47 F.R. 12785.

#### Codification

Section is based on section 2 of House Resolution No. 12, Ninety-fifth Congress, August 5, 1977, which was enacted into permanent law by Pub. L. 95–391.

#### Transfer of Functions
