### §1436. Use of frequent flyer miles

#### (a) Limitation on use of travel awards; exception

#### (1) In general

&emsp;Except as provided in paragraph (2), notwithstanding any other provision of law, or any rule, regulation, or other authority, any travel award that accrues by reason of official travel of a Member, officer, or employee of the Senate shall be considered the property of the office for which the travel was performed and may not be converted to personal use.

#### (2) Travel between the Washington metropolitan area and a home State

&emsp;Paragraph (1) shall not apply to any travel award relating to air transportation for aMember of the Senate, the spouse of that Member, or a son or daughter of that Member, between the Washington metropolitan area and the State of that Member.

#### (b) Regulations

The Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate shall have authority to prescribe regulations to carry out this section.

#### (c) Definitions

As used in this section—

&emsp;(1) the term “travel award” means any frequent flyer, free, or discounted travel, or other travel benefit, whether awarded by coupon, membership, or otherwise; and

&emsp;(2) the term “official travel” means travel engaged in the course of official business of the Senate.

(Pub. L. 104–1, title V, §507, Jan. 23, 1995, 109 Stat. 43; Pub. L. 105–275, title I, §12, Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2436.)

#### Amendments

1998—Subsec. (a). Pub. L. 105–275 designated existing provisions as par. (1), inserted heading, substituted “Except as provided in paragraph (2), notwithstanding” for “Notwithstanding”, and added par. (2).
