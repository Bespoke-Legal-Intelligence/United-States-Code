### §1502. Definitions

For purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) except as provided in section 1555 of this title, the terms defined under section 658 of this title shall have the meanings as so defined; and

&emsp;(2) the term “Director” means the Director of the Congressional Budget Office.

(Pub. L. 104–4, §3, Mar. 22, 1995, 109 Stat. 49.)
