### §1202. Discriminatory practices prohibited

All personnel actions affecting employees of the Senate shall be made free from any discrimination based on—

&emsp;(1) race, color, religion, sex, or national origin, within the meaning of section 2000e–16 of title 42;

&emsp;(2) age, within the meaning of section 633a of title 29; or

&emsp;(3) handicap or disability, within the meaning of section 791 of title 29 and sections 12112 to 12114 of title 42.

(Pub. L. 102–166, title III, §302, Nov. 21, 1991, 105 Stat. 1088.)
