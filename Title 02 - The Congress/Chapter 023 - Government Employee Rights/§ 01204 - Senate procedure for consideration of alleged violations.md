### §1204. Senate procedure for consideration of alleged violations

The Senate procedure for consideration of alleged violations consists of 4 steps as follows:

&emsp;(1) Step I, counseling, as set forth in section 1205 of this title.

&emsp;(2) Step II, mediation, as set forth in section 1206 of this title.

&emsp;(3) Step III, formal complaint and hearing by a hearing board, as set forth in section 1207 of this title.

&emsp;(4) Step IV, review of a hearing board decision, as set forth in section 1208 or 1209 of this title.

(Pub. L. 102–166, title III, §304, Nov. 21, 1991, 105 Stat. 1090.)
