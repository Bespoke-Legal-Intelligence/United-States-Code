### §88c–2. Academic year and summer term for page program

The page program shall consist of the two terms of the academic year plus a summer term.

(Pub. L. 98–367, title I, §103, July 17, 1984, 98 Stat. 479.)

#### Codification

Section is based on section 2 of House Resolution No. 234, Ninety-eighth Congress, June 29, 1983, which was enacted into permanent law by Pub. L. 98–367.

#### Effective Date

Section effective June 29, 1983, see note set out under section 88c–1 of this title.

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 88c–1, 88c–4 of this title.
