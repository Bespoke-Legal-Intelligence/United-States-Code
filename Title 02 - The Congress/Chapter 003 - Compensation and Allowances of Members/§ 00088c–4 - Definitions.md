### §88c–4. Definitions

As used in sections 88c–1 to 88c–4 of this title, the term—

&emsp;(1) “academic year” means a regular school year, consisting of two terms;

&emsp;(2) “page” means a page of the House of Representatives, but such term does not include a full time, permanent employee of the House of Representatives with supervisory responsibility for pages; and

&emsp;(3) “congressional page” means a page of the House of Representatives or the Senate.

(Pub. L. 98–367, title I, §103, July 17, 1984, 98 Stat. 479.)

#### Codification

Section is based on section 4 of House Resolution No. 234, Ninety-eighth Congress, June 29, 1983, which was enacted into permanent law by Pub. L. 98–367.

#### Effective Date

Section effective June 29, 1983, see note set out under section 88c–1 of this title.
