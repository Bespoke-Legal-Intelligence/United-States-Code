### §179j. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Librarian such sums as are necessary to carryout the provisions of sections 179 to 179k of this title, but in no fiscal year shall such sum exceed $250,000.

(Pub. L. 102–307, title II, §212, June 26, 1992, 106 Stat. 272.)

#### Termination of Section

<i>For termination of section, see section 179k of this title.</i>

This section is referred to in sections 179, 179g, 179h, 179i, 179k of this title.
