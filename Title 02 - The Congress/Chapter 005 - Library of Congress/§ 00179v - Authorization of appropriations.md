### §179v. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Librarian such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of sections 179<i>l</i> to 179w of this title, but in no fiscal year shall such sum exceed $250,000.

(Pub. L. 104–285, title I, §112, Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3382.)

#### Termination of Section

<i>For termination of section, see section 179w of this title.</i>

This section is referred to in sections 179<i>l</i>, 179s, 179t, 179u, 179w of this title.
