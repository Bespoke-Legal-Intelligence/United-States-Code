### §172. Definitions

As used in sections 171 to 175 of this title—

&emsp;(1) the term Center means the Center for the Book;

&emsp;(2) the term Librarian means the Librarian of Congress.

(Pub. L. 95–129, §2, Oct. 13, 1977, 91 Stat. 1151.)
