### §167c. Injuries to Library property

It shall be unlawful to step or climb upon, remove, or in any way injure any statue, seat, wall fountain, or other erection or achitectural <sup>1</sup> feature, or any tree, shrub, plant, or turf in the Library of Congress buildings or grounds.

(Aug. 4, 1950, ch. 561, §4, 64 Stat. 411.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Pub. L. 110–161, div. H, title I, §1004(d)(2)(B), (4), Dec. 26, 2007, 121 Stat. 2234, and Pub. L. 110–178, §4(b)(2), (d), Jan. 7, 2008, 121 Stat. 2552, provided that, effective Oct. 1, 2009, this section is repealed.</i>
