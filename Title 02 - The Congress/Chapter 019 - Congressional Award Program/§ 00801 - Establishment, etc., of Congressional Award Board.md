### §801. Establishment, etc., of Congressional Award Board

There is established a board to be known as the Congressional Award Board (hereinafter inthis chapter referred to as the “Board”), which shall be responsible for administering the Congressional Award Program described under section 802 of this title. The Board shall not be an agency or instrumentality of the United States, and the United States is not liable for any obligation or liability incurred by the Board.

(Pub. L. 96–114, §2, Nov. 16, 1979, 93 Stat. 851.)
