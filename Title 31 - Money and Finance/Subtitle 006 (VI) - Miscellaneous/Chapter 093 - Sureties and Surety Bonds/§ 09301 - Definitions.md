### §9301. Definitions

In this chapter—

&emsp;(1) “person” means an individual, a trust, an estate, a partnership, and a corporation.

&emsp;(2) “Government obligation” means a public debt obligation of the United States Government and an obligation whose principal and interest is unconditionally guaranteed by the Government.

(Pub. L. 97–258, Sept. 13, 1982, 96 Stat. 1046.)
