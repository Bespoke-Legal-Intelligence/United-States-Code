### §6307. Interpretative guidelines and exemptions

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget may—

&emsp;(1) issue supplementary interpretative guidelines to promote consistent and efficient use of procurement contracts, grant agreements, and cooperative agreements; and

&emsp;(2) exempt a transaction or program of an executive agency from this chapter.

(Pub. L. 97–258, Sept. 13, 1982, 96 Stat. 1005.)
