### §3564. Management improvement programs

In accordance with guidance provided by the Director of the Office of Management and Budget under section 3561 of this title, the head of an executive agency required to carry out a program under such section 3561 may carry out a program for improving management processes within the executive agency—

&emsp;(1) to address problems that contribute directly to the occurrence of errors in the paying of contractors of the executive agency; or

&emsp;(2) to improve the recovery of overpayments due to the agency.

(Added Pub. L. 107–107, div. A, title VIII, §831(a)(1), Dec. 28, 2001, 115 Stat. 1188.)
