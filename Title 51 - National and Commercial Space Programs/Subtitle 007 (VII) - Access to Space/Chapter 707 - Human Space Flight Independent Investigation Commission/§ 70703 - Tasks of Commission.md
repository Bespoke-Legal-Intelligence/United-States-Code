### §70703. Tasks of Commission

A Commission established pursuant to this chapter shall, to the extent possible, undertake the following tasks:

&emsp;(1)

Investigation.—Investigate the incident.

&emsp;(2)

Cause.—Determine the cause of the incident.

&emsp;(3)

Contributing factors.—Identify all contributing factors to the cause of the incident.

&emsp;(4)

Recommendations.—Make recommendations for corrective actions.

&emsp;(5)

Additional findings or recommendations.—Provide any additional findings or recommendations deemed by the Commission to be important, whether or not they are related to the specific incident under investigation.

&emsp;(6)

Report.—Prepare a report to Congress, the President, and the public.

(Pub. L. 111–314, §3, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3432.)
