### §70702. Establishment of Commission

(a)

Establishment.—The President shall establish an independent, nonpartisan Commission within the executive branch to investigate any incident that results in the loss of—

&emsp;(1) a space shuttle;

&emsp;(2) the International Space Station or its operational viability;

&emsp;(3) any other United States space vehicle carrying humans that is owned by the Federal Government or that is being used pursuant to a contract with the Federal Government; or

&emsp;(4) a crew member or passenger of any space vehicle described in this subsection.

(b)

Deadline for Establishment.—The President shall establish a Commission within 7 days after an incident specified in subsection (a).

(Pub. L. 111–314, §3, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3432.)
