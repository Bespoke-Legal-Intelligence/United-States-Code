### §70701. Definitions

In this chapter:

&emsp;(1)

Commission.—The term “Commission” means a Commission established under this chapter.

&emsp;(2)

Incident.—The term “incident” means either an accident or a deliberate act.

(Pub. L. 111–314, §3, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3432.)
