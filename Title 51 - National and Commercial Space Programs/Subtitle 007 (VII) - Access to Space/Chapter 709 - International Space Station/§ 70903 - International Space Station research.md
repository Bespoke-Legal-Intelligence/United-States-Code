### §70903. International Space Station research

The Administrator shall—

&emsp;(1) carry out a program of microgravity research consistent with section 40904 of this title; and

&emsp;(2) consider the need for a life sciences centrifuge and any associated holding facilities.

(Pub. L. 111–314, §3, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3436.)
