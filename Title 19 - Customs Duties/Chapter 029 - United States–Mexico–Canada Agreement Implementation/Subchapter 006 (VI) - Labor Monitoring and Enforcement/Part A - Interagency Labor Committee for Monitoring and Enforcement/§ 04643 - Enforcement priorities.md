### §4643. Enforcement priorities

The Interagency Labor Committee shall—

&emsp;(1) review the list of priority sectors under Annex 31–A of the USMCA and suggest to USTR additional sectors for review by the USMCA countries as appropriate;

&emsp;(2) establish and annually update a list of priority subsectors within such priority sectors to be the focus of the enforcement efforts of the Committee, the first of which shall consist of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) auto assembly;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) auto parts;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) aerospace;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) industrial bakeries;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) electronics;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) call centers;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) mining; and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) steel and aluminum; and

&emsp;(3) review priority facilities within such priority subsectors for monitoring and enforcement.

(Pub. L. 116–113, title VII, §713, Jan. 29, 2020, 134 Stat. 82.)
