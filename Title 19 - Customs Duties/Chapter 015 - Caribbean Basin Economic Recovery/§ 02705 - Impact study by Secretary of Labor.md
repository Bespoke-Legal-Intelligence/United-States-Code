### §2705. Impact study by Secretary of Labor

The Secretary of Labor, in consulation <sup>1</sup> with other appropriate Federal agencies, shall undertake a continuing review and analysis of the impact which the implementation of the provisions of this chapter have with respect to United States labor; and shall make an annual written report to Congress on the results of such review and analysis.

(Pub. L. 98–67, title II, §216, Aug. 5, 1983, 97 Stat. 394.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “consultation”.</i>
