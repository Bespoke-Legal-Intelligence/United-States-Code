### §2374. Rule of construction

Nothing in this part prevents a worker from receiving trade adjustment assistance under part 2 of this subchapter at the same time the worker is receiving assistance in any manner from—

&emsp;(1) a community receiving a community grant under subpart A;

&emsp;(2) an eligible institution receiving a Community College and Career Training Grant under subpart B; or

&emsp;(3) an eligible partnership receiving a Sector Partnership Grant under subpart C.

(Pub. L. 93–618, title II, §279C, as added Pub. L. 111–5, div. B, title I, §1872(a), Feb. 17, 2009, 123 Stat. 413.)
