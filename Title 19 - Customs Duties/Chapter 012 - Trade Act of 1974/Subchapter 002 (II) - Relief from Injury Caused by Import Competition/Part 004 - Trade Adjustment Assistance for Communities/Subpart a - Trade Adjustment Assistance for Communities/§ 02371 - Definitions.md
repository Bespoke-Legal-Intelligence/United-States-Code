### §2371. Definitions

In this subpart:

#### (1) Agricultural commodity producer

&emsp;The term “agricultural commodity producer” has the meaning given that term in section 2401 of this title.

#### (2) Community

&emsp;The term “community” means a city, county, or other political subdivision of a State or a consortium of political subdivisions of a State.

#### (3) Community impacted by trade

&emsp;The term “community impacted by trade” means a community described in section 2371b(b)(2) of this title.

#### (4) Eligible community

&emsp;The term “eligible community” means a community that the Secretary has determined under section 2371b(b)(1) of this title is eligible to apply for assistance under this subpart.

#### (5) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.

(Pub. L. 93–618, title II, §271, as added Pub. L. 111–5, div. B, title I, §1872(a), Feb. 17, 2009, 123 Stat. 401.)
