### §3706. Sub-Saharan Africa defined

For purposes of this chapter, the terms "sub-Saharan Africa", "sub-Saharan African country", "country in sub-Saharan Africa", and "countries in sub-Saharan Africa" refer to the following or any successor political entities:

&emsp;Republic of Angola (Angola).

&emsp;Republic of Benin (Benin).

&emsp;Republic of Botswana (Botswana).

&emsp;Burkina Faso (Burkina).

&emsp;Republic of Burundi (Burundi).

&emsp;Republic of Cameroon (Cameroon).

&emsp;Republic of Cape Verde (Cape Verde).

&emsp;Central African Republic.

&emsp;Republic of Chad (Chad).

&emsp;Federal Islamic Republic of the Comoros (Comoros).

&emsp;Democratic Republic of Congo.

&emsp;Republic of the Congo (Congo).

&emsp;Republic of Côte d'Ivoire (Côte d'Ivoire).

&emsp;Republic of Djibouti (Djibouti).

&emsp;Republic of Equatorial Guinea (Equatorial Guinea).

&emsp;State of Eritrea (Eritrea).

&emsp;Ethiopia.

&emsp;Gabonese Republic (Gabon).

&emsp;Republic of the Gambia (Gambia).

&emsp;Republic of Ghana (Ghana).

&emsp;Republic of Guinea (Guinea).

&emsp;Republic of Guinea-Bissau (Guinea-Bissau).

&emsp;Republic of Kenya (Kenya).

&emsp;Kingdom of Lesotho (Lesotho).

&emsp;Republic of Liberia (Liberia).

&emsp;Republic of Madagascar (Madagascar).

&emsp;Republic of Malawi (Malawi).

&emsp;Republic of Mali (Mali).

&emsp;Islamic Republic of Mauritania (Mauritania).

&emsp;Republic of Mauritius (Mauritius).

&emsp;Republic of Mozambique (Mozambique).

&emsp;Republic of Namibia (Namibia).

&emsp;Republic of Niger (Niger).

&emsp;Federal Republic of Nigeria (Nigeria).

&emsp;Republic of Rwanda (Rwanda).

&emsp;Democratic Republic of Sao Tomé and Principe (Sao Tomé and Principe).

&emsp;Republic of Senegal (Senegal).

&emsp;Republic of Seychelles (Seychelles).

&emsp;Republic of Sierra Leone (Sierra Leone).

&emsp;Somalia.

&emsp;Republic of South Africa (South Africa).

&emsp;Republic of South Sudan (South Sudan).

&emsp;Republic of Sudan (Sudan).

&emsp;Kingdom of Swaziland (Swaziland).

&emsp;United Republic of Tanzania (Tanzania).

&emsp;Republic of Togo (Togo).

&emsp;Republic of Uganda (Uganda).

&emsp;Republic of Zambia (Zambia).

&emsp;Republic of Zimbabwe (Zimbabwe).

(Pub. L. 106–200, title I, §107, May 18, 2000, 114 Stat. 256; Pub. L. 112–163, §1(b), Aug. 10, 2012, 126 Stat. 1274.)
