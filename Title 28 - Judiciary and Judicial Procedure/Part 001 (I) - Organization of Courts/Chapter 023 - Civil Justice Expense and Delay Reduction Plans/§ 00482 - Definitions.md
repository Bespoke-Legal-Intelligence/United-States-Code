### §482. Definitions

As used in this chapter, the term “judicial officer” means a United States district court judge or a United States magistrate.

(Added Pub. L. 101–650, title I, §103(a), Dec. 1, 1990, 104 Stat. 5096.)

#### Change of Name

Reference to United States magistrate or to magistrate deemed to refer to United States magistrate judge pursuant to section 321 of Pub. L. 101–650, set out as a note under section 631 of this title.
