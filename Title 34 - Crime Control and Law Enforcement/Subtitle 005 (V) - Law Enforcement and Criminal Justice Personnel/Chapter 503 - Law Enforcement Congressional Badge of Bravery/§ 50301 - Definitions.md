### §50301. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Federal agency head

&emsp;The term "Federal agency head" means the head of any executive, legislative, or judicial branch Government entity that employs Federal law enforcement officers.

#### (2) Federal Board

&emsp;The term "Federal Board" means the Federal Law Enforcement Congressional Badge of Bravery Board established under section 50313(a) of this title.

#### (3) Federal Board members

&emsp;The term "Federal Board members" means the members of the Federal Board appointed under section 50313(c) of this title.

#### (4) Federal Law Enforcement Badge

&emsp;The term "Federal Law Enforcement Badge" means the Federal Law Enforcement Congressional Badge of Bravery described in section 50311 of this title.

#### (5) Federal law enforcement officer

&emsp;The term "Federal law enforcement officer"—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) means a Federal employee—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) who has statutory authority to make arrests or apprehensions;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) who is authorized by the agency of the employee to carry firearms; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) whose duties are primarily—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) engagement in or supervision of the prevention, detection, investigation, or prosecution of, or the incarceration of any person for, any violation of law; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) the protection of Federal, State, local, or foreign government officials against threats to personal safety; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) includes a law enforcement officer employed by the Amtrak Police Department or Federal Reserve.

#### (6) Office

&emsp;The term "Office" means the Congressional Badge of Bravery Office established under section 50331(a) of this title.

#### (7) State and Local Board

&emsp;The term "State and Local Board" means the State and Local Law Enforcement Congressional Badge of Bravery Board established under section 50323(a) of this title.

#### (8) State and Local Board members

&emsp;The term "State and Local Board members" means the members of the State and Local Board appointed under section 50323(c) of this title.

#### (9) State and Local Law Enforcement Badge

&emsp;The term "State and Local Law Enforcement Badge" means the State and Local Law Enforcement Congressional Badge of Bravery described in section 50321 of this title.

#### (10) State or local agency head

&emsp;The term "State or local agency head" means the head of any executive, legislative, or judicial branch entity of a State or local government that employs State or local law enforcement officers.

#### (11) State or local law enforcement officer

&emsp;The term "State or local law enforcement officer" means an employee of a State or local government—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) who has statutory authority to make arrests or apprehensions;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) who is authorized by the agency of the employee to carry firearms; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) whose duties are primarily—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) engagement in or supervision of the prevention, detection, investigation, orprosecution of, or the incarceration of any person for, any violation of law; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the protection of Federal, State, local, or foreign government officials against threats to personal safety.

(Pub. L. 110–298, §2, July 31, 2008, 122 Stat. 2985.)

#### Codification

Section was formerly classified to section 15231 of Title 42, The Public Health and Welfare, prior to editorial reclassification and renumbering as this section.
