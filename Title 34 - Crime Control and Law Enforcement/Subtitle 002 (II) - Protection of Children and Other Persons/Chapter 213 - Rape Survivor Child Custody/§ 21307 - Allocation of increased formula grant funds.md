### §21307. Allocation of increased formula grant funds

The Attorney General shall allocate an increase in the amount provided to a State under the covered formula grants under this chapter such that—

&emsp;(1) 25 percent <sup>1</sup> the amount of the increase is provided under the program described in section 21301(1)(A) of this title; and

&emsp;(2) 75 percent <sup>1</sup> the amount of the increase is provided under the program described in section 21301(1)(B) of this title.

(Pub. L. 114–22, title IV, §408, May 29, 2015, 129 Stat. 258.)

#### Codification
