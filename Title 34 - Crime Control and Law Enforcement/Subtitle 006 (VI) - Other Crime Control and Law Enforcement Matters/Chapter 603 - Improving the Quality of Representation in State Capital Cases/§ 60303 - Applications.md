### §60303. Applications

#### (a) In general

The Attorney General shall establish a process through which a State may apply for a grant under this chapter.

#### (b) Application

#### (1) In general

&emsp;A State desiring a grant under this chapter shall submit an application to the Attorney General at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Attorney General may reasonably require.

#### (2) Contents

&emsp;Each application submitted under paragraph (1) shall contain—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a certification by an appropriate officer of the State that the State authorizes capital punishment under its laws and conducts, or will conduct, prosecutions in which capital punishment is sought;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a description of the communities to be served by the grant, including the nature of existing capital defender services and capital prosecution programs within such communities;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a long-term statewide strategy and detailed implementation plan that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) reflects consultation with the judiciary, the organized bar, and State and local prosecutor and defender organizations; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) establishes as a priority improvement in the quality of trial-level representation of indigents charged with capital crimes and trial-level prosecution of capital crimes;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) in the case of a State that employs a statutory procedure described in section 60301(e)(1)(C) of this title, a certification by an appropriate officer of the State that the State is in substantial compliance with the requirements of the applicable State statute; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) assurances that Federal funds received under this chapter shall be—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) used to supplement and not supplant non-Federal funds that would otherwise be available for activities funded under this chapter; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) allocated in accordance with section 60306(b) of this title.

(Pub. L. 108–405, title IV, §423, Oct. 30, 2004, 118 Stat. 2288.)
