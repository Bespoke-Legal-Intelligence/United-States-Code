### §703. Employee sanctions and remedies

A grantee or contractor shall, within 30 days after receiving notice from an employee of a conviction pursuant to section 701(a)(1)(D)(ii) or 702(a)(1)(D)(ii) of this title—

&emsp;(1) take appropriate personnel action against such employee up to and including termination; or

&emsp;(2) require such employee to satisfactorily participate in a drug abuse assistance or rehabilitation program approved for such purposes by a Federal, State, or local health, law enforcement, or other appropriate agency.

(Pub. L. 100–690, title V, §5154, Nov. 18, 1988, 102 Stat. 4307.)
