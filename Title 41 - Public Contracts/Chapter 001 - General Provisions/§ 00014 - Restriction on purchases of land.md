### §14. Restriction on purchases of land

No land shall be purchased on account of the United States, except under a law authorizing such purchase.

(R.S. §3736.)

#### Codification

R.S. §3736 derived from act May 1, 1820, ch. 52, §7, 3 Stat. 568.
