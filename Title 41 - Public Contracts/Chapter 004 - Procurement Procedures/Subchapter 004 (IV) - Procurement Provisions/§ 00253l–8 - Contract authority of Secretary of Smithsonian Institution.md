### §253<i>l</i>–8. Contract authority of Secretary of Smithsonian Institution

#### (a) In general

The Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution may—

&emsp;(1) enter into multi-year contracts for the acquisition of property and services under the authority of section 254c of this title; and

&emsp;(2) enter into contracts for the acquisition of severable services for a period that begins inone fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year under the authority of section 253<i>l</i> of this title.

#### (b) Effective date

This section shall apply to contracts entered into on or after August 15, 2003.

(Pub. L. 108–72, §4, Aug. 15, 2003, 117 Stat. 889.)

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of title III of act June 30, 1949, ch. 288, 63 Stat. 393, which comprises this subchapter.
