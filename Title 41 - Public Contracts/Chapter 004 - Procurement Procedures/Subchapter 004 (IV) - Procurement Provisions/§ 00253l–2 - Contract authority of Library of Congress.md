### §253<i>l</i>–2. Contract authority of Library of Congress

The Library of Congress may use available funds, now and hereafter, to enter into contracts for the lease or acquisition of severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year and to enter into multi-year contracts for the acquisition of property and services pursuant to sections 253<i>l</i> and 254c of this title, respectively.

(Pub. L. 106–57, title II, §207, Sept. 29, 1999, 113 Stat. 423.)

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of title III of act June 30, 1949, ch. 288, 63 Stat. 393, which comprises this subchapter.
