### §253<i>l</i>–4. Contract authority of Congressional Budget Office

Beginning on December 21, 2000, and hereafter, the Congressional Budget Office may use available funds to enter into contracts for the procurement of severable services for a period that begins in one fiscal year and ends in the next fiscal year and may enter into multi-year contracts for the acquisition of property and services, to the same extent as executive agencies under the authority of section <sup>1</sup> 253<i>l</i> and 254c, respectively, of this title.

(Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(2) \[title I, §110\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–108.)

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of title III of act June 30, 1949, ch. 288, 63 Stat. 393, which comprises this subchapter.

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “sections”.</i>
