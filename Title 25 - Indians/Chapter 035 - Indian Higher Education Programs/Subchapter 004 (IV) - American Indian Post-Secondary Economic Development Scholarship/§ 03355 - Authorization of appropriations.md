### §3355. Authorization of appropriations

For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this subchapter, there are authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000 for fiscal year 1993 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years.

(Pub. L. 102–325, title XIII, §1365, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 815.)
