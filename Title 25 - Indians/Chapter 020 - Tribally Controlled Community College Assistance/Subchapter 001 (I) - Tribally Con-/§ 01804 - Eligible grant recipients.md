### §1804. Eligible grant recipients

To be eligible for assistance under this subchapter, a tribally controlled community college must be one which—

&emsp;(1) is governed by a board of directors or board of trustees a majority of which are Indians;

&emsp;(2) demonstrates adherence to stated goals, a philosophy, or a plan of operation which is directed to meet the needs of Indians; and

&emsp;(3) if in operation for more than one year, has students a majority of whom are Indians.

(Pub. L. 95–471, title I, §103, Oct. 17, 1978, 92 Stat. 1326.)
