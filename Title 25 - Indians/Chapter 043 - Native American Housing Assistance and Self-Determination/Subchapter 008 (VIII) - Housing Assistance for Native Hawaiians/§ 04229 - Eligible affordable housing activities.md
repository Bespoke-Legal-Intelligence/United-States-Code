### §4229. Eligible affordable housing activities

#### (a) In general

Affordable housing activities under this section are activities conducted in accordance with the requirements of section 4230 of this title to—

&emsp;(1) develop or to support affordable housing for rental or homeownership; or

&emsp;(2) provide housing services with respect to affordable housing, through the activities described in subsection (b) of this section.

#### (b) Activities

The activities described in this subsection are the following:

#### (1) Development

&emsp;The acquisition, new construction, reconstruction, or moderate or substantial rehabilitation of affordable housing, which may include—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) real property acquisition;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) site improvement;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the development of utilities and utility services;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) conversion;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) demolition;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) financing;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) administration and planning; and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) other related activities.

#### (2) Housing services

&emsp;The provision of housing-related services for affordable housing, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) housing counseling in connection with rental or homeownership assistance;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the establishment and support of resident organizations and resident management corporations;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) energy auditing;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) activities related to the provisions of self-sufficiency and other services; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) other services related to assisting owners, tenants, contractors, and other entities participating or seeking to participate in other housing activities assisted pursuant to this section.

#### (3) Housing management services

&emsp;The provision of management services for affordable housing, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the preparation of work specifications;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) loan processing;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) inspections;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) tenant selection;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) management of tenant-based rental assistance; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) management of affordable housing projects.

#### (4) Crime prevention and safety activities

&emsp;The provision of safety, security, and law enforcement measures and activities appropriate to protect residents of affordable housing from crime.

#### (5) Model activities

&emsp;Housing activities under model programs that are—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) designed to carry out the purposes of this subchapter; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) specifically approved by the Secretary as appropriate for the purpose referred to in subparagraph (A).
