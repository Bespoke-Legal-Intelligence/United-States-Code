### §714. Definitions

For the purposes of this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) “Tribe” means the Confederated Tribes of Coos, Lower Umpqua, and Siuslaw Indians;

&emsp;(2) “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior or his authorized representative;

&emsp;(3) “Interim Council” means the tribal council of the Confederated Tribes of Coos, Lower Umpqua, and Siuslaw Indians, which serves pursuant to section 714c of this title; and

&emsp;(4) “member” used with respect to the Tribe means a person enrolled on the membership roll of the Tribe provided for in section 714b of this title.

(Pub. L. 98–481, §2, Oct. 17, 1984, 98 Stat. 2250.)

#### Short Title

Pub. L. 98–481, §1, Oct. 17, 1984, 98 Stat. 2250, provided: “That this Act \[enacting this subchapter\] may be cited as the ‘Coos, Lower Umpqua, and Siuslaw Restoration Act’.”
