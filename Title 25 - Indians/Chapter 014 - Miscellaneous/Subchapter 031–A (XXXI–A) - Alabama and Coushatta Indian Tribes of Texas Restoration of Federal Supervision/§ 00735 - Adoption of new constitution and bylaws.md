### §735. Adoption of new constitution and bylaws

Upon written request of the tribal council,<sup>1</sup> the Secretary shall hold an election for themembers of the tribe for the purpose of adopting a new constitution and bylaws in accordance with section 476 of this title.

(Pub. L. 100–89, title II, §205, Aug. 18, 1987, 101 Stat. 671.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “Tribal Council,”.</i>
