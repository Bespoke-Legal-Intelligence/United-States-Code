### §1300b–12. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter—

&emsp;(a) "Band" means the Texas Band of Kickapoo Indians, a subgroup of the Kickapoo Tribe of Oklahoma;

&emsp;(b) "Tribe" means the Kickapoo Tribe of Oklahoma; and

&emsp;(c) "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 97–429, §3, Jan. 8, 1983, 96 Stat. 2269.)
