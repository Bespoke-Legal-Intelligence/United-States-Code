### §1300n–1. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term "Tribe" means the Indians of the Graton Rancheria of California.

&emsp;(2) The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

&emsp;(3) The term "Interim Tribal Council" means the governing body of the Tribe specified in section 1300n–5 of this title.

&emsp;(4) The term "member" means an individual who meets the membership criteria under section 1300n–4(b) of this title.

&emsp;(5) The term "State" means the State of California.

&emsp;(6) The term "reservation" means those lands acquired and held in trust by the Secretary for the benefit of the Tribe.

&emsp;(7) The term "service area" means the counties of Marin and Sonoma, in the State of California.

(Pub. L. 106–568, title XIV, §1403, Dec. 27, 2000, 114 Stat. 2939.)
