### §5632. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) BIA

&emsp;The term "BIA" means the Bureau of Indian Affairs.

#### (2) Department

&emsp;The term "Department" means the Department of the Interior.

#### (3) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

#### (4) Under Secretary

&emsp;The term "Under Secretary" means the Under Secretary for Indian Affairs established under section 5633(a) of this title.

(Pub. L. 114–178, title III, §302, June 22, 2016, 130 Stat. 439.)
