### §5612. Establishment of demonstration project; selection of participating Indian tribes

#### (a) In general

The Secretary shall establish and carry out an Indian trust asset management demonstration project, in accordance with this subchapter.

#### (b) Selection of participating Indian tribes

#### (1) In general

&emsp;An Indian tribe shall be eligible to participate in the project if—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Indian tribe submits to the Secretary an application under subsection (c); and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Secretary approves the application of the Indian tribe.

#### (2) Notice

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall provide a written notice to each Indian tribe approved to participate in the project.

#### (B) Contents

&emsp;&emsp;A notice under subparagraph (A) shall include—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a statement that the application of the Indian tribe has been approved by the Secretary; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a requirement that the Indian tribe shall submit to the Secretary a proposed Indian trust asset management plan in accordance with section 5613 of this title.

#### (c) Application

#### (1) In general

&emsp;To be eligible to participate in the project, an Indian tribe shall submit to the Secretarya written application in accordance with paragraph (2).

#### (2) Requirements

&emsp;The Secretary shall consider an application under this subsection only if the application—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) includes a copy of a resolution or other appropriate action by the governing body of the Indian tribe, as determined by the Secretary, in support of or authorizing the application;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) is received by the Secretary after June 22, 2016; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) states that the Indian tribe is requesting to participate in the project.

#### (d) Duration

The project—

&emsp;(1) shall remain in effect for a period of 10 years after June 22, 2016; but

&emsp;(2) may be extended at the discretion of the Secretary.

(Pub. L. 114–178, title II, §203, June 22, 2016, 130 Stat. 433.)
