### §1621w. Authorization of appropriations

Except as provided in sections 1621h(m), 1621j, 1621<i>l</i>, 1621m(b)(5), 1621n, and 1621<i>o</i> of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each fiscal year through fiscal year 2000 to carry out this subchapter.

(Pub. L. 94–437, title II, §224, as added Pub. L. 102–573, title II, §217(a), Oct. 29, 1992, 106 Stat. 4559.)
