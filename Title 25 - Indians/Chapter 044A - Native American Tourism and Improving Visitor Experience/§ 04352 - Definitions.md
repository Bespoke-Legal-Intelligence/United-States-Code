### §4352. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Agency

&emsp;The term "agency" has the meaning given the term in section 551 of title 5.

#### (2) Indian tribe

&emsp;The term "Indian tribe" has the meaning given the term in section 5304 of this title.

#### (3) Native Hawaiian organization

&emsp;The term "Native Hawaiian organization" means a nonprofit organization—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) that serves the interests of Native Hawaiians;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) in which Native Hawaiians serve in substantive and policymaking positions; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) that is recognized for having expertise in Native Hawaiian culture and heritage, including tourism.

#### (4) Tribal organization

&emsp;The term "tribal organization" has the meaning given the term in section 5304 of this title.

(Pub. L. 114–221, §3, Sept. 23, 2016, 130 Stat. 848.)
