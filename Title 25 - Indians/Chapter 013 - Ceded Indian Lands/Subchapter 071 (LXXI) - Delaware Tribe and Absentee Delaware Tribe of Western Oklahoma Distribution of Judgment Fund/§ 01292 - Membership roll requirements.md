### §1292. Membership roll requirements

The Secretary of the Interior shall prepare a roll of all persons who meet the following requirements:

&emsp;(a) they were born on or prior to and were living on October 3, 1972; and

&emsp;(b) they are citizens of the United States; and

&emsp;(c)(1) their name or the name of a lineal ancestor appears on the Delaware Indian per capita payroll approved by the Secretary on April 20, 1906, or

&emsp;(2) their name or the name of a lineal ancestor is on or is eligible to be on the constructed base census roll as of 1940 of the Absentee Delaware Tribe of Western Oklahoma, approved by the Secretary.

(Pub. L. 92–456, §2, Oct. 3, 1972, 86 Stat. 762.)
