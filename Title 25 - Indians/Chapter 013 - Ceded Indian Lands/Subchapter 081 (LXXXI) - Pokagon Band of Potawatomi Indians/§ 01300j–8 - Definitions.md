### §1300j–8. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “Band” means the Pokagon Band of Potawatomi Indians;

&emsp;(2) the term “member” means those individuals eligible for enrollment in the Band pursuant to section 1300j–3 of this title; and

&emsp;(3) the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 103–323, §10, formerly §9, Sept. 21, 1994, 108 Stat. 2155; renumbered §10, Pub. L. 103–435, §20(1), Nov. 2, 1994, 108 Stat. 4574.)
