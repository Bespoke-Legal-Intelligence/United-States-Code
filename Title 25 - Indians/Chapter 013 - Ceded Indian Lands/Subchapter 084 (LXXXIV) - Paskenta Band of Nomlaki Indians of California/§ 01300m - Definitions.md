### §1300m. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “Tribe” means the Paskenta Band of Nomlaki Indians of the Paskenta Rancheria of California.

&emsp;(2) The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

&emsp;(3) The term “Interim Council” means the governing body of the Tribe specified in section 1300m–5 of this title.

&emsp;(4) The term “member” means an individual who meets the membership criteria under section 1300m–4(b) of this title.

&emsp;(5) The term “State” means the State of California.

&emsp;(6) The term “reservation” means those lands acquired and held in trust by the Secretary for the benefit of the Tribe pursuant to section 1300m–3 of this title.

&emsp;(7) The term “service area” means the counties of Tehama and Glenn, in the State of California.

(Pub. L. 103–454, title III, §302, Nov. 2, 1994, 108 Stat. 4793.)

#### Short Title

Section 301 of title III of Pub. L. 103–454 provided that: “This title \[enacting this subchapter\] may be cited as the ‘Paskenta Band Restoration Act’.”
