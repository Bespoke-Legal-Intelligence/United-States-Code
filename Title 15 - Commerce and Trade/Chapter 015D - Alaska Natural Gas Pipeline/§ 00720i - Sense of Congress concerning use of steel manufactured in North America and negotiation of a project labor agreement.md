### §720i. Sense of Congress concerning use of steel manufactured in North America and negotiation of a project labor agreement

It is the sense of Congress that—

&emsp;(1) an Alaska natural gas transportation project would provide significant economic benefits to the United States and Canada; and

&emsp;(2) to maximize those benefits, the sponsors of the Alaska natural gas transportation project should make every effort to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) use steel that is manufactured in North America; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) negotiate a project labor agreement to expedite construction of the pipeline.

(Pub. L. 108–324, div. C, §111, Oct. 13, 2004, 118 Stat. 1263.)
