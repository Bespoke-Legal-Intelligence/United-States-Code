### §7461. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) Director

&emsp;The term "Director" means the Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

#### (2) Institute

&emsp;The term "Institute" means the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

(Pub. L. 113–274, title V, §501, Dec. 18, 2014, 128 Stat. 2986.)
