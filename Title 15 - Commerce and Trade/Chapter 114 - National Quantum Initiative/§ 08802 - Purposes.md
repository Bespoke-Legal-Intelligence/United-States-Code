### §8802. Purposes

The purpose of this chapter is to ensure the continued leadership of the United States in quantum information science and its technology applications by—

&emsp;(1) supporting research, development, demonstration, and application of quantum information science and technology—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to expand the number of researchers, educators, and students with training in quantum information science and technology to develop a workforce pipeline;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to promote the development and inclusion of multidisciplinary curriculum and research opportunities for quantum information science at the undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral level;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) to address basic research knowledge gaps, including computational research gaps;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) to promote the further development of facilities and centers available for quantuminformation science and technology research, testing and education; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) to stimulate research on and promote more rapid development of quantum-based technologies;

&emsp;(2) improving the interagency planning and coordination of Federal research and development of quantum information science and technology;

&emsp;(3) maximizing the effectiveness of the Federal Government's quantum information science and technology research, development, and demonstration programs;

&emsp;(4) promoting collaboration among the Federal Government, Federal laboratories, industry, and universities; and

&emsp;(5) promoting the development of international standards for quantum information science and technology security—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to facilitate technology innovation and private sector commercialization; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to meet economic and national security goals.

(Pub. L. 115–368, §3, Dec. 21, 2018, 132 Stat. 5093.)
