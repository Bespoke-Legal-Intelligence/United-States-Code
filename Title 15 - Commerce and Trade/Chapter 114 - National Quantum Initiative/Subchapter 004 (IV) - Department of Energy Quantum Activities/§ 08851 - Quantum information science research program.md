### §8851. Quantum information science research program

#### (a) In general

The Secretary of Energy shall carry out a basic research program on quantum information science.

#### (b) Program components

In carrying out the program under subsection (a), the Secretary of Energy shall—

&emsp;(1) formulate goals for quantum information science research to be supported by the Department of Energy;

&emsp;(2) leverage the collective body of knowledge from existing quantum information science research;

&emsp;(3) provide research experiences and training for additional undergraduate and graduate students in quantum information science, including in the fields of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) quantum information theory;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) quantum physics;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) quantum computational science;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) applied mathematics and algorithm development;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) quantum networking;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) quantum sensing and detection; and

&emsp;&emsp;(G) materials science and engineering;

&emsp;(4) coordinate research efforts funded through existing programs across the Department of Energy, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Nanoscale Science Research Centers;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Energy Frontier Research Centers;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Energy Innovation Hubs;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the National Laboratories;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) the Advanced Research Projects Agency; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) the National Quantum Information Science Research Centers; and

&emsp;(5) coordinate with other Federal departments and agencies, research communities, and potential users of information produced under this section.

(Pub. L. 115–368, title IV, §401, Dec. 21, 2018, 132 Stat. 5101.)
