### SUBCHAPTER I–A—FAIR TRADE IN AUTOMOTIVE PARTS

#### Codification

This subchapter was enacted as part of title XXXVIII of div. C of the Strom Thurmond National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1999, known as the Fair Trade in Automotive Parts Act of 1998, and not as part of the Export Enhancement Act of 1988 which enacted this chapter.
