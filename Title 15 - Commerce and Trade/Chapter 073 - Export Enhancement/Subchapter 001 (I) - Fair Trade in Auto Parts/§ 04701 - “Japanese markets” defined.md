### §4701. “Japanese markets” defined

For purposes of this subchapter, the term “Japanese markets” refers to markets, including those in the United States and Japan, where automotive parts and accessories, both original equipment and aftermarket, are purchased for use in the manufacture or repair of Japanese automobiles.

(Pub. L. 100–418, title II, §2122, Aug. 23, 1988, 102 Stat. 1325.)
