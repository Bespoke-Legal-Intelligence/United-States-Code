### §2936. Scientific assessment

On a periodic basis (not less frequently than every 4 years), the Council, through the Committee, shall prepare and submit to the President and the Congress an assessment which—

&emsp;(1) integrates, evaluates, and interprets the findings of the Program and discusses the scientific uncertainties associated with such findings;

&emsp;(2) analyzes the effects of global change on the natural environment, agriculture, energy production and use, land and water resources, transportation, human health and welfare, human social systems, and biological diversity; and

&emsp;(3) analyzes current trends in global change, both human-inducted<sup>1</sup> and natural, and projects major trends for the subsequent 25 to 100 years.

(Pub. L. 101 606, title I, §106, Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 3101.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i>So in original. Probably should be “human-induced”.</i>
