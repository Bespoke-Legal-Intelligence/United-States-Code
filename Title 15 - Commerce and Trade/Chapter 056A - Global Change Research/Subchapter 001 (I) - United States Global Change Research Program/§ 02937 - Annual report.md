### §2937. Annual report

#### (a) General

Each year at the time of submission to the Congress of the President's budget, the Chairman of the Council shall submit to the Congress a report on the activities conducted by the Committee pursuant to this subchapter, including—

&emsp;(1) a summary of the achievements of the Program during the period covered by the report and of priorities for future global change research;

&emsp;(2) an analysis of the progress made toward achieving the goals of the Plan;

&emsp;(3) expenditures required by each agency or department for carrying out its portion of the Program, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the amounts spent during the fiscal year most recently ended;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the amounts expected to be spent during the current fiscal year; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the amounts requested for the fiscal year for which the budget is being submitted.

#### (b) Recommendations

The report required by subsection (b)<sup>1</sup> of this section shall include recommendations by the President concerning—

&emsp;(1) changes in agency or department roles needed to improve implementation of the Plan; and

&emsp;(2) additional legislation which may be required to achieve the purposes of this subchapter.

(Pub. L. 101 606, title I, §107, Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 3101.)
