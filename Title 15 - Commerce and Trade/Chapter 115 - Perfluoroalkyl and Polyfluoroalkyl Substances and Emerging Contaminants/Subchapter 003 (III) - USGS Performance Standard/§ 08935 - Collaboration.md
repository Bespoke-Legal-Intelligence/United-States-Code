### §8935. Collaboration

In carrying out this subchapter, the Director shall collaborate with—

&emsp;(1) appropriate Federal and State regulators;

&emsp;(2) institutions of higher education;

&emsp;(3) research institutions; and

&emsp;(4) other expert stakeholders.

(Pub. L. 116–92, div. F, title LXXIII, §7335, Dec. 20, 2019, 133 Stat. 2283.)
