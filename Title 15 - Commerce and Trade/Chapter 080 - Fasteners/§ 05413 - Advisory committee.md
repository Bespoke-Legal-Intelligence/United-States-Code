### §5413. Advisory committee

Within 90 days after November 16, 1990, the Secretary shall appoint an advisory committee consisting of representatives of fastener manufacturers, importers, distributors, end-users, independent laboratories, and standards organizations. The Secretary and Director shall consult with the advisory committee—

&emsp;(1) prior to promulgating any regulations under this chapter; and

&emsp;(2) in such other matters related to fasteners as the Secretary may determine.

(Pub. L. 101 592, §14, Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 2952.)

#### Termination of Advisory Committees

Advisory committees established after Jan. 5, 1973, to terminate not later than the expiration of the 2-year period beginning on the date of their establishment, unless, in the case of a committee established by the President or an officer of the Federal Government, such committee is renewed by appropriate action prior to the expiration of such 2-year period, or in the case of a committee established by the Congress, its duration is otherwise provided by law. See section 14 of Pub. L. 92 463, Oct. 6, 1972, 86 Stat. 770, 776, set out in the Appendix to Title 5, Government Organization and Employees.
