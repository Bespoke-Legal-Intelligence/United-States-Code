### §5412. Regulations

The Secretary shall issue such regulations as may be necessary to implement this chapter.

(Pub. L. 101–592, §13, Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 2952; Pub. L. 104–113, §11(i), Mar. 7, 1996, 110 Stat. 782.)

#### Amendments

1996—Pub. L. 104–113 struck out “within 180 days after November 16, 1990,” after “Secretary shall”.
