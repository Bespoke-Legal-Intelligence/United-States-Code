### §78d–8. Annual financial controls audit

#### (a) Reports of Commission

#### (1) Annual reports required

&emsp;Not later than 6 months after the end of each fiscal year, the Commission shall publish and submit to Congress a report that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) describes the responsibility of the management of the Commission for establishing and maintaining an adequate internal control structure and procedures for financial reporting; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) contains an assessment of the effectiveness of the internal control structure and procedures for financial reporting of the Commission during that fiscal year.

#### (2) Attestation

&emsp;The reports required under paragraph (1) shall be attested to by the Chairman and chief financial officer of the Commission.

#### (b) Report by Comptroller General

#### (1) Report required

&emsp;Not later than 6 months after the end of the first fiscal year after July 21, 2010, the Comptroller General of the United States shall submit a report to Congress that assesses—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the effectiveness of the internal control structure and procedures of the Commission for financial reporting; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the assessment of the Commission under subsection (a)(1)(B).

#### (2) Attestation

&emsp;The Comptroller General shall attest to, and report on, the assessment made by the Commission under subsection (a).

#### (c) Reimbursements for cost of reports

#### (1) Reimbursements required

&emsp;The Commission shall reimburse the Government Accountability Office for the full cost of making the reports under subsection (b), as billed therefor by the Comptroller General.

#### (2) Crediting and use of reimbursements

&emsp;Such reimbursements shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) be credited to the appropriation account "Salaries and Expenses, Government Accountability Office" current when the payment is received; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) remain available until expended.

(Pub. L. 111–203, title IX, §963, July 21, 2010, 124 Stat. 1910.)

#### Codification

Section was enacted as part of the Investor Protection and Securities Reform Act of 2010 and also as part of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, and not as part of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 which comprises this chapter.

#### Effective Date

Section effective 1 day after July 21, 2010, except as otherwise provided, see section 4 of Pub. L. 111–203, setout as a note under section 5301 of Title 12, Banks and Banking.

#### Definition

For definition of "Commission" as used in this section, see section 5301 of Title 12, Banks and Banking.
