### §7108. Definitions

For purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “Administration” means the Small Business Administration;

&emsp;(2) the term “Administrator” means the Administrator of the Small Business Administration;

&emsp;(3) the term “control” means exercising the power to make policy decisions concerning a business;

&emsp;(4) the term “Council” means the National Women's Business Council, established under section 7105 of this title;

&emsp;(5) the term “Interagency Committee” means the Interagency Committee on Women's Business Enterprise, established under section 7101 of this title;

&emsp;(6) the term “operate” means being actively involved in the day-to-day management of a business;

&emsp;(7) the term “women's business enterprise” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a business or businesses owned by a woman or a group of women; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the establishment, maintenance, or development of a business or businesses by a woman or a group of women; and

&emsp;(8) the term “women-owned business” means a small business which a woman or a group of women—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) control and operate; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) own not less than 51 percent of the business.

(Pub. L. 100–533, title IV, §408, as added Pub. L. 103–403, title IV, §413, Oct. 22, 1994, 108 Stat. 4197.)
