### §7407. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Commerce for the National Institute of Standards and Technology—

&emsp;(1) for activities under section 278h of this title—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $25,000,000 for fiscal year 2003;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $40,000,000 for fiscal year 2004;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $55,000,000 for fiscal year 2005;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) $70,000,000 for fiscal year 2006;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) $85,000,000 for fiscal year 2007; and

&emsp;(2) for activities under section 278g–3(f) of this title—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $6,000,000 for fiscal year 2003;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $6,200,000 for fiscal year 2004;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $6,400,000 for fiscal year 2005;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) $6,600,000 for fiscal year 2006; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) $6,800,000 for fiscal year 2007.

(Pub. L. 107–305, §11, Nov. 27, 2002, 116 Stat. 2379.)
