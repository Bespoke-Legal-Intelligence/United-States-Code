### §5632. Availability of federally gathered unenhanced data

#### (a) General rule

All unenhanced land remote sensing data gathered and owned by the United States Government, including unenhanced data gathered under the technology demonstration program carried out pursuant to section 5633 of this title, shall be made available to users in a timely fashion.

#### (b) Protection for commercial data distributor

The President shall seek to ensure that unenhanced data gathered under the technology demonstration program carried out pursuant to section 5633 of this title shall, to the extent practicable, be made available on terms that would not adversely effect <sup>1</sup> the commercialmarket for unenhanced data gathered by the Landsat 6 spacecraft.

(Pub. L. 102–555, title III, §302, Oct. 28, 1992, 106 Stat. 4174.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “affect”.</i>
