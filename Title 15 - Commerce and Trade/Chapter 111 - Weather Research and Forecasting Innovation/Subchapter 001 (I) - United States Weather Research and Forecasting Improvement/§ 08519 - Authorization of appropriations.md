### §8519. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Office of Oceanic and Atmospheric Research to carry out this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) $136,516,000 for fiscal year 2019, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $85,758,000 is authorized for weather laboratories and cooperative institutes;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $30,758,000 is authorized for weather and air chemistry research programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $20,000,000 is authorized for the joint technology transfer initiative described in section 8512(b)(4) of this title;

&emsp;(2) $148,154,000 for fiscal year 2020, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $87,258,000 is authorized for weather laboratories and cooperative institutes;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $40,896,000 is authorized for weather and air chemistry research programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $20,000,000 is authorized for the joint technology transfer initiative described in section 8512(b)(4) of this title;

&emsp;(3) $150,154,000 for fiscal year 2021, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $88,758,000 is authorized for weather laboratories and cooperative institutes;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $41,396,000 is authorized for weather and air chemistry research programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $20,000,000 is authorized for the joint technology transfer initiative described in section 8512(b)(4) of this title;

&emsp;(4) $152,154,000 for fiscal year 2022, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $90,258,000 is authorized for weather laboratories and cooperative institutes;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $41,896,000 is authorized for weather and air chemistry research programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $20,000,000 is authorized for the joint technology transfer initiative described in section 8512(b)(4) of this title; and

&emsp;(5) $154,154,000 for fiscal year 2023, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $91,758,000 is authorized for weather laboratories and cooperative institutes;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $42,396,000 is authorized for weather and air chemistry research programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $20,000,000 is authorized for the joint technology transfer initiative described in section 8512(b)(4) of this title.

#### (b) Limitation

No additional funds are authorized to carry out this subchapter and the amendments made by this title.<sup>1</sup>

(Pub. L. 115–25, title I, §110, Apr. 18, 2017, 131 Stat. 98; Pub. L. 115–423, §3(b), Jan. 7, 2019, 132 Stat. 5455.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### References in Text

This subchapter, referred to in text, was in the original "this title", meaning title I of Pub. L. 115–25, which enacted this subchapter and amended provisions formerly set out as a note under section 313 of this title, which is now classified to section 8520 of this title. For complete classification of title I to the Code, see Tables.

The amendments made by this title, referred to in subsec. (b), mean the amendments made by title I of Pub. L. 115–25, which amended provisions formerly set out as a note under section 313 of this title and which is now classified to section 8520 of this title.

#### Amendments

<b>2019</b>—Pub. L. 115–423 amended section generally. Prior to amendment, section related to authorization of appropriations for fiscal years 2017 and 2018.
