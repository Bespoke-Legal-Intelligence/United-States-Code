### §8548. Weather enterprise outreach

#### (a) In general

The Under Secretary may establish mechanisms for outreach to the weather enterprise—

&emsp;(1) to assess the weather forecasts and forecast products provided by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration; and

&emsp;(2) to determine the highest priority weather forecast needs of the community described in subsection (b).

#### (b) Outreach community

In conducting outreach under subsection (a), the Under Secretary shall contact leading experts and innovators from relevant stakeholders, including the representatives from the following:

&emsp;(1) State or local emergency management agencies.

&emsp;(2) State agriculture agencies.

&emsp;(3) Indian tribes (as defined in section 5304 of title 25) and Native Hawaiians (as defined in section 7517 of title 20).

&emsp;(4) The private aerospace industry.

&emsp;(5) The private earth observing industry.

&emsp;(6) The operational forecasting community.

&emsp;(7) The academic community.

&emsp;(8) Professional societies that focus on meteorology.

&emsp;(9) Such other stakeholder groups as the Under Secretary considers appropriate.

(Pub. L. 115–25, title IV, §412, Apr. 18, 2017, 131 Stat. 113.)
