### §78hh. Effective date

This chapter shall become effective on July 1, 1934, except that sections 78f and 78<i>l</i>(b to e) of this title shall become effective on September 1, 1934; and sections 78e, 78g, 78h, 78i(a)(6), 78j, 78k, 78<i>l</i>(a), 78m, 78n, 78<i>o</i>, 78p, 78q, 78r, 78s, and 78dd of this title shall become effective on October 1, 1934.

(June 6, 1934, ch. 404, title I, §34, 48 Stat. 905.)

#### References in Text

This chapter, referred to in text, was in the original “This Act”. See References in Text note set out under section 78a of this title.
