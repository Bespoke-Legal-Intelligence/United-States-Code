### §9522. Purpose

The purpose of the MBDA Business Center Program shall be to create a national network of public-private partnerships that—

&emsp;(1) assist minority business enterprises in—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) accessing capital, contracts, and grants; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) creating and maintaining jobs;

&emsp;(2) provide counseling and mentoring to minority business enterprises; and

&emsp;(3) facilitate the growth of minority business enterprises by promoting trade.

(Pub. L. 117–58, div. K, title I, §100112, Nov. 15, 2021, 135 Stat. 1451.)
