### §7305. Additional powers

In order to support Teams in carrying out this chapter, the Director may—

&emsp;(1) procure the temporary or intermittent services of experts or consultants under section 3109 of title 5;

&emsp;(2) request the use, when appropriate, of available services, equipment, personnel, andfacilities of a department, agency, or instrumentality of the United States Government on a reimbursable or other basis;

&emsp;(3) confer with employees and request the use of services, records, and facilities of State and local governmental authorities;

&emsp;(4) accept voluntary and uncompensated services;

&emsp;(5) accept and use gifts of money and other property, to the extent provided in advance in appropriations Acts;

&emsp;(6) make contracts with nonprofit entities to carry out studies related to purpose, functions, and authorities of the Teams; and

&emsp;(7) provide nongovernmental members of the Team reasonable compensation for time spent carrying out activities under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 107–231, §6, Oct. 1, 2002, 116 Stat. 1474.)
