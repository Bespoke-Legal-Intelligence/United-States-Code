### §7307. National Construction Safety Team report

Not later than 90 days after completing an investigation, a Team shall issue a public report which includes—

&emsp;(1) an analysis of the likely technical cause or causes of the building failure investigated;

&emsp;(2) any technical recommendations for changes to or the establishment of evacuation and emergency response procedures;

&emsp;(3) any recommended specific improvements to building standards, codes, and practices; and

&emsp;(4) recommendations for research and other appropriate actions needed to help prevent future building failures.

(Pub. L. 107–231, §8, Oct. 1, 2002, 116 Stat. 1475.)
