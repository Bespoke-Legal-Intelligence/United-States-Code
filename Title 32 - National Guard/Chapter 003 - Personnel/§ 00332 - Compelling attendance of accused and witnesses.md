### §332. Compelling attendance of accused and witnesses

In the National Guard not in Federal service, the president of a court-martial or a summary court officer may—

&emsp;(1) issue a warrant for the arrest of any accused person who, having been served with a warrant and a copy of the charges, disobeys a written order by the convening authority to appear before the court;

&emsp;(2) issue subpenas duces tecum and other subpenas;

&emsp;(3) enforce by attachment the attendance or witnesses and the production of books and papers; and

&emsp;(4) sentence for refusal to be sworn or to answer, as provided in actions before civil courts.

(Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 609.)
