### §31h. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out sections 31a to 31h of this title—

&emsp;(1) $28,000,000 for fiscal year 1999;

&emsp;(2) $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2000;

&emsp;(3) $37,000,000 for fiscal year 2001;

&emsp;(4) $43,000,000 for fiscal year 2002;

&emsp;(5) $50,000,000 for fiscal year 2003;

&emsp;(6) $57,000,000 for fiscal year 2004; and

&emsp;(7) $64,000,000 for fiscal year 2005.

#### (b) Allocation of appropriations

Of any amounts appropriated for any fiscal year in excess of the amount appropriated for fiscal year 2000—

&emsp;(1) 48 percent shall be available for the State component; and

&emsp;(2) 2 percent shall be available for the education component.

(Pub. L. 102–285, §9, as added Pub. L. 106–148, §9, Dec. 9, 1999, 113 Stat. 1724.)

#### Prior Provisions

A prior section 31h, Pub. L. 102–285, §9, May 18, 1992, 106 Stat. 171; Pub. L. 105–36, §3(g), Aug. 5, 1997, 111 Stat. 1111, authorized appropriations for the national cooperative geologic mapping program, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 106–148, §9, Dec. 9, 1999, 113 Stat. 1724.

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 31a, 31b of this title.
