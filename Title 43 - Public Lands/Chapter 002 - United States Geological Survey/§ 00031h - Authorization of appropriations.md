### §31h. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the national cooperative geologic mapping program under sections 31a to 31h of this title—

&emsp;(1) $26,000,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(2) $28,000,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(3) $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.

#### (b) Allocation of appropriated funds

#### (1) In general

&emsp;Of the amount of funds that are appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for any fiscal year up to the amount that is equal to the amount appropriated to carry out the national cooperative geologic mapping program for fiscal year 1996—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 20 percent shall be allocated to State mapping activities; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 2 percent shall be allocated to educational mapping activities.

#### (2) Increased appropriations

&emsp;Of the amount of funds that are appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for any fiscal year up to the amount that exceeds the amount appropriated to carry out the national cooperative geologic mapping program for fiscal year 1996—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) for fiscal year 1998—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) 75 percent shall be allocated for Federal mapping and support mapping activities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) 23 percent shall be allocated for State mapping activities; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) 2 percent shall be allocated for educational mapping activities;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) for fiscal year 1999—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) 74 percent shall be allocated for Federal mapping and support mapping activities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) 24 percent shall be allocated for State mapping activities; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) 2 percent shall be allocated for educational mapping activities; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) for fiscal year 2000—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) 73 percent shall be allocated for Federal mapping and support mapping activities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) 25 percent shall be allocated for State mapping activities; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) 2 percent shall be allocated for educational mapping activities.

(Pub. L. 102–285, §9, May 18, 1992, 106 Stat. 171; Pub. L. 105–36, §3(g), Aug. 5, 1997, 111 Stat. 1111.)

#### Amendments

1997—Pub. L. 105–36 reenacted section catchline without change and amended text generally. Prior to amendment, text read as follows: “There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out sections 31a to 31h of this title the following:

&emsp;“(1) For Federal mapping activities under sections 31a to 31h of this title, $12,500,000 for fiscal year 1993, $14,000,000 for fiscal year 1994, $16,000,000 for fiscal year 1995, and $18,000,000 for fiscal year 1996.

&emsp;“(2) For Federal support activities under sections 31a to 31h of this title, $9,500,000 for fiscal year 1993, $10,000,000 for fiscal year 1994, $10,500,000 for fiscal year 1995, and $11,000,000 for fiscal year 1996.

&emsp;“(3) For State mapping activities under sections 31a to 31h of this title, $15,000,000 for fiscal year 1993, $18,000,000 for fiscal year 1994, $21,000,000 for fiscal year 1995, and $25,000,000 for fiscal year 1996.

&emsp;“(4) For educational support activities under sections 31a to 31h of this title, $500,000 for fiscal year 1993, $750,000 for fiscal year 1994, $1,000,000 for fiscal year 1995, and $1,500,000 for fiscal year 1996.”
