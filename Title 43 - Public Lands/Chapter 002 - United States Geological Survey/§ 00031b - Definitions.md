### §31b. Definitions

As used in sections 31a to 31h of this title:

&emsp;(1) The term “advisory committee” means the advisory committee established under section 31d of this title.

&emsp;(2) The term “Director” means the Director of the United States Geological Survey.

&emsp;(3) The term “geologic mapping program” means the National Cooperative Geologic Mapping Program established by section 31c(a) of this title.

&emsp;(4) The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

&emsp;(5) The term “Survey” means the United States Geological Survey.

(Pub. L. 102–285, §3, May 18, 1992, 106 Stat. 167.)
