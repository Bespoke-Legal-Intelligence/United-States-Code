### §390h–12g. Albuquerque Metropolitan Area Water Reclamation and Reuse Study

#### (a) Authorization

The Secretary, in cooperation with the city of Albuquerque, New Mexico, is authorized to participate in the Albuquerque Metropolitan Area Water Reclamation and Reuse Study to reclaim and reuse industrial and municipal wastewater and reclaim and use naturally impaired ground water in the Albuquerque metropolitan area.

#### (b) Cost share

The Federal share of the cost of a project described in subsection (a) of this section shall not exceed 25 percent of the total cost.

#### (c) Limitation

The Secretary shall not provide funds for the operation or maintenance of a project described in subsection (a) of this section.

(Pub. L. 102–575, title XVI, §1621, as added Pub. L. 104–266, §2(a)(2), Oct. 9, 1996, 110 Stat. 3292.)
