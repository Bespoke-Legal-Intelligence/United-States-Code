United States Government work: not subject to copyright in the United States

The text of the United States Code in this repository is a work of the United States Government. Under 17 U.S.C.
section 105, copyright protection under title 17 is not available for any work of the United States Government. No
copyright is claimed in that text here. The House report on section 105 (H.R. Rep. No. 94-1476) says the provision is not
intended to have any effect on protection of these works abroad.
