### §7368. Expiration of authority

No corporation may be established under this subchapter after December 31, 1992.

(Added Pub. L. 100–322, title II, §204(a), May 20, 1988, 102 Stat. 512, §4168; renumbered §7368, Pub. L. 102–40, title IV, §401(a)(4)(B), May 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 221; Pub. L. 102–291, §3(b), May 20, 1992, 106 Stat. 179.)

#### Amendments

1992—Pub. L. 102–291 substituted “December 31, 1992” for “September 30, 1991”.

1991—Pub. L. 102–40 renumbered section 4168 of this title as this section.

#### Effective Date of 1992 Amendment

Amendment by Pub. L. 102–291 effective Oct. 1, 1991, see section 3(c) of Pub. L. 102–291, set out as a note under section 7361 of this title.
