### §7367. Report to Congress

Not later than February 1, 1991, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report on the experience through the end of fiscal year 1990 under this subchapter. The report shall include such recommendations as the Secretary considers appropriate.

(Added Pub. L. 100–322, title II, §204(a), May 20, 1988, 102 Stat. 512, §4167; renumbered §7367 and amended Pub. L. 102–40, title IV, §§401(a)(4)(B), 403(a)(1), May 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 221, 239.)

#### Amendments

1991—Pub. L. 102–40 renumbered section 4167 of this title as this section and substituted “Secretary” for “Administrator” in two places.
