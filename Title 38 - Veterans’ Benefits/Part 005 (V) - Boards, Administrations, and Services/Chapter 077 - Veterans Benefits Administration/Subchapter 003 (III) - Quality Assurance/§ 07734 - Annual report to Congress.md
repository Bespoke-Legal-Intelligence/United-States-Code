### §7734. Annual report to Congress

The Secretary shall include in the annual report to the Congress required by section 529 of this title a report on the quality assurance activities carried out under this subchapter. Each such report shall include—

&emsp;(1) an appraisal of the quality of services provided by the Veterans Benefits Administration, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the number of decisions reviewed;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a summary of the findings on the decisions reviewed;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the number of full-time equivalent employees assigned to quality assurance in each division or entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) specific documentation of compliance with the standards for independence and internal control required by section 7731(b) of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) actions taken to improve the quality of services provided and the results obtained;

&emsp;(2) information with respect to the accuracy of decisions, including trends in that information; and

&emsp;(3) such other information as the Secretary considers appropriate.

(Added Pub. L. 106–117, §801(a)(1), Nov. 30, 1999, 113 Stat. 1585.)
