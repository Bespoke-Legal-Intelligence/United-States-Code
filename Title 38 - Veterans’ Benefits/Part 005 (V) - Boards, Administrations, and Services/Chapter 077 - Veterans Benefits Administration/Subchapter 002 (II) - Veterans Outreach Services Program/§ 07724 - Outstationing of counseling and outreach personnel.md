### §7724. Outstationing of counseling and outreach personnel

The Secretary may station employees of the Department at locations other than Departmentoffices, including educational institutions, to provide counseling and other assistance regarding benefits under this title to veterans and other persons eligible for benefits under this title and to provide outreach services under this subchapter.

(Added Pub. L. 102–83, §2(b), Aug. 6, 1991, 105 Stat. 401.)

#### Prior Provisions

Provisions similar to those in this section were contained in section 243 of this title prior to repeal by Pub. L. 102–83, §2(a).
