### §7283. Disposition of fees

Except for amounts received pursuant to section 7285 of this title, all fees received by the Court of Veterans Appeals shall be covered into the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts.

(Added Pub. L. 100–687, div. A, title III, §301(a), Nov. 18, 1988, 102 Stat. 4118, §4083; renumbered §7283 and amended Pub. L. 102–40, title IV, §402(b)(1), (d)(1), May 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 238, 239.)

#### Amendments

1991—Pub. L. 102–40 renumbered section 4083 of this title as this section and substituted “7285” for “4085”.
