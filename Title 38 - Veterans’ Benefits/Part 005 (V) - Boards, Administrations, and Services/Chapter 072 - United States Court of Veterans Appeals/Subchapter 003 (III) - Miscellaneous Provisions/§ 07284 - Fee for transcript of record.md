### §7284. Fee for transcript of record

The Court of Veterans Appeals may fix a fee, not in excess of the fee authorized by law to becharged and collected therefor by the clerks of the district courts, for comparing, or for preparing and comparing, a transcript of the record of any proceeding before the Court, or for copying any record, entry, or other paper and the comparison and certification thereof.

(Added Pub. L. 100–687, div. A, title III, §301(a), Nov. 18, 1988, 102 Stat. 4118, §4084; renumbered §7284, Pub. L. 102–40, title IV, §402(b)(1), May 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 238.)

#### Amendments

1991—Pub. L. 102–40 renumbered section 4084 of this title as this section.
