### §5310. Payment of benefits for month of death

If, in accordance with the provisions of section 5110(d) of this title, a surviving spouse is entitled to death benefits under chapter 11, 13, or 15 of this title for the month in which a veteran's death occurs, the amount of such death benefits for that month shall be not less than the amount of benefits the veteran would have received under chapter 11 or 15 of this title for that month but for the death of the veteran.

(Added Pub. L. 87–825, §4(a), Oct. 15, 1962, 76 Stat. 950, §3110; amended Pub. L. 98–160, title VII, §703(3), Nov. 21, 1983, 97 Stat. 1010; renumbered §5310 and amended Pub. L. 102–40, title IV, §402(b)(1), (d)(1), May 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 238, 239.)

#### Amendments

1991—Pub. L. 102–40 renumbered section 3110 of this title as this section and substituted “5110(d)” for “3010(d)”.

1983—Pub. L. 98–160 substituted “surviving spouse” for “widow”, and “the death of the veteran” for “his death”.
