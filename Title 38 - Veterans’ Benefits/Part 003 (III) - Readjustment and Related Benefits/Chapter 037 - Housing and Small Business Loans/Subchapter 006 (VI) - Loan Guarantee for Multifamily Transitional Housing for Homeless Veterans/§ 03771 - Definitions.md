### §3771. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “veteran” has the meaning given such term by paragraph (2) of section 101.

&emsp;(2) The term “homeless veteran” means a veteran who is a homeless individual.

&emsp;(3) The term “homeless individual” has the meaning given such term by section 103 of the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 11302).

(Added Pub. L. 105–368, title VI, §601(a), Nov. 11, 1998, 112 Stat. 3342; amended Pub. L. 106–400, §2, Oct. 30, 2000, 114 Stat. 1675.)

#### Amendments

2000—Par. (3). Pub. L. 106–400 substituted “McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act” for “Stewart B. McKinney Homeless Assistance Act”.
