### §2002. Definitions

In this chapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “homeless veteran” means a veteran who is homeless (as that term is defined in section 103(a) of the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 11302(a)).<sup>1</sup>

&emsp;(2) The term “grant and per diem provider” means an entity in receipt of a grant under section 2011 or 2012 of this title.

(Added Pub. L. 107–95, §5(a)(1), Dec. 21, 2001, 115 Stat. 905.)

#### Prior Provisions
