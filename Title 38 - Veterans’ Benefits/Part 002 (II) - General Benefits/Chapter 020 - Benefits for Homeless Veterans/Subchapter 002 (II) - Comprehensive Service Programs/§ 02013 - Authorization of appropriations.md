### §2013. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter amounts as follows:

&emsp;(1) $60,000,000 for fiscal year 2002.

&emsp;(2) $75,000,000 for fiscal year 2003.

&emsp;(3) $75,000,000 for fiscal year 2004.

&emsp;(4) $75,000,000 for fiscal year 2005.

(Added Pub. L. 107–95, §5(a)(1), Dec. 21, 2001, 115 Stat. 909.)
