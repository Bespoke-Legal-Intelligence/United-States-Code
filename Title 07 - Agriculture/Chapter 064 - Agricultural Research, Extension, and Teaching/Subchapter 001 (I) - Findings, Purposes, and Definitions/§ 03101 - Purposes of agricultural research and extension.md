### §3101. Purposes of agricultural research and extension

Subject to the varying conditions and needs of States, Federally funded agricultural research and extension programs shall be designed to, among other things, accomplish the following—

&emsp;(1) continue to satisfy human food and fiber needs;

&emsp;(2) enhance the long-term viability and competitiveness of the food production and agricultural system of the United States within the global economy;

&emsp;(3) expand economic opportunities in rural America and enhance the quality of life for farmers, rural citizens, and society as a whole;

&emsp;(4) improve the productivity of the American agricultural system and develop new agricultural crops and new uses for agricultural commodities;

&emsp;(5) develop information and systems to enhance the environment and the natural resource base upon which a sustainable agricultural economy depends; or

&emsp;(6) enhance human health—

&emsp;&emsp;(1) by fostering the availability and affordability of a safe, wholesome, and nutritious food supply that meets the needs and preferences of the consumer; and

&emsp;&emsp;(2) by assisting farmers and other rural residents in the detection and prevention of health and safety concerns.

(Pub. L. 95–113, title XIV, §1402, as added Pub. L. 101–624, title XVI, §1602(a), Nov. 28, 1990, 104 Stat. 3705.)
