### §8201. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Eligible orchardist

&emsp;The term “eligible orchardist” means a person that produces annual crops from trees for commercial purposes.

#### (2) Natural disaster

&emsp;The term “natural disaster” means plant disease, insect infestation, drought, fire, freeze, flood, earthquake, lightning, and other occurrence, as determined by the Secretary.

#### (3) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.

#### (4) Tree

&emsp;The term “tree” includes a tree, bush, and vine.

(Pub. L. 107–171, title X, §10201, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 490.)
