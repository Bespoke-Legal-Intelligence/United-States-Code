### §8108. Continuation of bioenergy program

#### (a) Definitions

In this section:

#### (1) Bioenergy

&emsp;The term “bioenergy” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) biodiesel; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) fuel grade ethanol.

#### (2) Biodiesel

&emsp;The term “biodiesel” means a monoalkyl ester that meets the requirements of an appropriate American Society for Testing and Materials standard.

#### (3) Eligible commodity

&emsp;The term “eligible commodity” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) wheat, corn, grain sorghum, barley, oats, rice, soybeans, sunflower seed, rapeseed, canola, safflower, flaxseed, mustard, crambe, sesame seed, and cottonseed;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a cellulosic commodity (such as hybrid poplar and switch grass);

&emsp;&emsp;(C) fats, oils, and greases (including recycled fats, oils, and greases) derived from an agricultural product; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) any animal byproduct (in addition to oils, fats, and greases) that may be used to produce bioenergy, as determined by the Secretary.

#### (4) Eligible producer

&emsp;The term “eligible producer” means a producer that uses an eligible commodity to produce bioenergy.

#### (b) Bioenergy Program

#### (1) Continuation

&emsp;The Secretary shall continue the program under part 1424 of title 7, Code of Federal Regulations (or any successor regulation), under which the Secretary makes payments to eligible producers to encourage increased purchases of eligible commodities for the purpose of expanding production of such bioenergy and supporting new production capacity for such bioenergy.

#### (2) Contracts

&emsp;To be eligible to receive a payment, an eligible producer shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) enter into a contract with the Secretary to increase bioenergy production for 1 or more fiscal years; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) submit to the Secretary such records as the Secretary may require as evidence of increased purchase and use of eligible commodities for the production of bioenergy.

#### (3) Payment

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;Under the program, the Secretary shall make payments to eligible producers, based on the quantity of bioenergy produced by the eligible producer during a fiscal year that exceeds the quantity of bioenergy produced by the eligible producer during the preceding fiscal year.

#### (B) Payment rate

#### (i) Producers of less than 65,000,000 gallons

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;An eligible producer that produces less than 65,000,000 gallons of bioenergy shall be reimbursed 1 feedstock unit for every 2.5 feedstock units of eligible commodity used for increased production.

#### (ii) Producers of 65,000,000 or more gallons

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;An eligible producer that produces 65,000,000 or more gallons of bioenergy shall be reimbursed 1 feedstock unit for every 3.5 feedstock units of eligible commodity used for increased production.

#### (C) Quarterly payments

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall make payments to an eligible producer for each quarter of the fiscal year.

#### (4) Proration

&emsp;If the amount made available for a fiscal year under subsection (c) of this section is insufficient to allow the payment of the amount of the payments that eligible producers (thatapply for the payments) otherwise would receive under this subsection, the Secretary shall prorate the amount of the funds among all such eligible producers.

#### (5) Overpayments

&emsp;If the total amount of payments that an eligible producer receives for a fiscal year under this section exceeds the amount that the eligible producer should have received under this subsection, the eligible producer shall repay the amount of the overpayment to the Secretary, with interest (as determined by the Secretary).

#### (6) Limitation

&emsp;No eligible producer shall receive more than 5 percent of the total amount made available under subsection (c) of this section for a fiscal year.

#### (7) Other requirements

&emsp;To be eligible to receive a payment under this subsection, an eligible producer shall meet other requirements of Federal law (including regulations) applicable to the production of bioenergy.

#### (c) Funding

Of the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation, the Secretary shall use to carry out this section—

&emsp;(1) not more than $150,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2003 through 2006; and

&emsp;(2) $0 for fiscal year 2007.

(Pub. L. 107–171, title IX, §9010, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 485.)
