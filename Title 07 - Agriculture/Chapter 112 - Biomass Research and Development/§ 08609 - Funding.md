### §8609. Funding

#### (a) Funding

Of funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation, the Secretary shall make available to carry out this chapter—

&emsp;(1) $5,000,000 for fiscal year 2002; and

&emsp;(2) $14,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2003 through 2007;

> to remain available until expended.

#### (b) Authorization of appropriations

In addition to amounts transferred under subsection (a), there are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $200,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2006 through 2015.

(Pub. L. 106–224, title III, §310, as added Pub. L. 107–171, title IX, §9008(a)(3), May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 483; amended Pub. L. 108–148, title II, §201(b), Dec. 3, 2003, 117 Stat. 1901; Pub. L. 109–58, title IX, §941(g), Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 878.)
