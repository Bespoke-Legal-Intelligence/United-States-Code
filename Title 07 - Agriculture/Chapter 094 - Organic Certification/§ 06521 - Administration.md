### §6521. Administration

#### (a) Regulations

Not later than 540 days after November 28, 1990, the Secretary shall issue proposed regulations to carry out this chapter.

#### (b) Assistance to State

#### (1) Technical and other assistance

&emsp;The Secretary shall provide technical, administrative, and Extension Service assistance to assist States in the implementation of an organic certification program under this chapter.

#### (2) Financial assistance

&emsp;The Secretary may provide financial assistance to any State that implements an organic certification program under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 101–624, title XXI, §2122, Nov. 28, 1990, 104 Stat. 3951.)
