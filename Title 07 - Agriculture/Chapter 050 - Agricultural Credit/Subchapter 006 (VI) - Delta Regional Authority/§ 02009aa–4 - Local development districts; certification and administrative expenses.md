### §2009aa–4. Local development districts; certification and administrative expenses

#### (a) Definition of local development district

In this section, the term “local development district” means an entity that—

&emsp;(1) is—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a planning district in existence on December 21, 2000, that is recognized by the Economic Development Administration of the Department of Commerce; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) where an entity described in subparagraph (A) does not exist—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) organized and operated in a manner that ensures broad-based community participation and an effective opportunity for other nonprofit groups to contribute to the development and implementation of programs in the region;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) governed by a policy board with at least a simple majority of members consisting of elected officials or employees of a general purpose unit of local government who have been appointed to represent the government;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) certified to the Authority as having a charter or authority that includes the economic development of counties or parts of counties or other political subdivisions within the region—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) by the Governor of each State in which the entity is located; or

> &emsp;(II) by the State officer designated by the appropriate State law to make the certification; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv)(I) a nonprofit incorporated body organized or chartered under the law of the State in which the entity is located;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) a nonprofit agency or instrumentality of a State or local government;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) a public organization established before December 21, 2000, under State law for creation of multi-jurisdictional, area-wide planning organizations; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) a nonprofit association or combination of bodies, agencies, and instrumentalities described in subclauses (I) through (III); and

&emsp;(2) has not, as certified by the Federal cochairperson—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) inappropriately used Federal grant funds from any Federal source; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) appointed an officer who, during the period in which another entity inappropriately used Federal grant funds from any Federal source, was an officer of the other entity.

#### (b) Grants to local development districts

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Authority may make grants for administrative expenses under this section.

#### (2) Conditions for grants

#### (A) Maximum amount

&emsp;&emsp;The amount of any grant awarded under paragraph (1) shall not exceed 80 percent of the administrative expenses of the local development district receiving the grant.

#### (B) Maximum period

&emsp;&emsp;No grant described in paragraph (1) shall be awarded to a State agency certified as a local development district for a period greater than 3 years.

#### (C) Local share

&emsp;&emsp;The contributions of a local development district for administrative expenses may be in cash or in kind, fairly evaluated, including space, equipment, and services.

#### (c) Duties of local development districts

A local development district shall—

&emsp;(1) operate as a lead organization serving multicounty areas in the region at the local level; and

&emsp;(2) serve as a liaison between State and local governments, nonprofit organizations (including community-based groups and educational institutions), the business community, and citizens that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) are involved in multijurisdictional planning;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) provide technical assistance to local jurisdictions and potential grantees; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) provide leadership and civic development assistance.

(Pub. L. 87–128, title III, §382E, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(4) \[div. B, title V, §503\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–276.)
