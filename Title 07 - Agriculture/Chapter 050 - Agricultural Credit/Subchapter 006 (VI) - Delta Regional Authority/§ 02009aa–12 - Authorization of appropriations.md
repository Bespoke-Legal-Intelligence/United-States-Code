### §2009aa–12. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Authority to carry out this subchapter $30,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2001 through 2002, to remain available until expended.

#### (b) Administrative expenses

Not more than 5 percent of the amount appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for a fiscal year shall be used for administrative expenses of the Authority.

(Pub. L. 87–128, title III, §382M, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(4) \[div. B, title V, §503\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–280.)
