### §2009aa–13. Termination of authority

This subchapter and the authority provided under this subchapter expire on October 1, 2007.

(Pub. L. 87–128, title III, §382N, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(4) \[div. B, title V, §503\], Dec. 21,2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–281; amended Pub. L. 107–171, title VI, §6027(h), May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 374.)

#### Amendments

2002—Pub. L. 107–171 substituted “2007” for “2002”.
