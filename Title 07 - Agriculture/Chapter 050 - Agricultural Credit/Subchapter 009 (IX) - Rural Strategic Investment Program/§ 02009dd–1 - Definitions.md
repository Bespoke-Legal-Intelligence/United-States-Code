### §2009dd–1. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) Benchmark

&emsp;The term “benchmark” means an annual set of strategies and goals of a Regional Board established for the purpose of measuring performance in meeting the regional plan of the Regional Board.

#### (2) Conference

&emsp;The term “Conference” means the National Conference on Rural America conducted under section 2009dd–7 of this title.

#### (3) Eligible area

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “eligible area” means a nonmetropolitan county (as defined by the Secretary) that has a population of 50,000 inhabitants or less.

#### (B) Inclusion

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Subject to clause (ii), the term “eligible area” includes an unincorporated or other area of a county that has a population of more than 50,000 inhabitants if the unincorporated area or other area is adjacent to an eligible rural area described in subparagraph (A).

#### (ii) Participation

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;An area described in clause (i) may be represented on a Regional Board.

#### (C) Exclusion

&emsp;&emsp;The term “eligible area” does not include any area designated by the Secretary as a rural empowerment zone or rural enterprise community.

#### (4) Innovation grant

&emsp;The term “innovation grant” means an innovation grant made by the National Board to a Regional Board under section 2009dd–6 of this title.

#### (5) National Board

&emsp;The term “National Board” means the National Board on Rural America established under section 2009dd–3(a) of this title.

#### (6) National plan

&emsp;The term “national plan” means a national strategic investment plan of the NationalBoard developed under section 2009dd–3(d)(3) of this title.

#### (7) Planning grant

&emsp;The term “planning grant” means a regional strategic investment planning grant made by the National Board to a Regional Board under section 2009dd–5 of this title.

#### (8) Program

&emsp;The term “program” means the rural strategic investment program established under this subchapter.

#### (9) Region

&emsp;The term “region” means the eligible areas that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) are under the jurisdiction of a Regional Board; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) meet criteria established by the National Board not later than 1 year after May 13, 2002.

#### (10) Regional Board

&emsp;The term “Regional Board” means a Regional Investment Board certified under section 2009dd–2(a) of this title.

#### (11) Regional plan

&emsp;The term “regional plan” means a regional strategic investment plan of a Regional Board developed under section 2009dd–2(b)(3)(B) of this title.

(Pub. L. 87–128, title III, §385B, as added Pub. L. 107–171, title VI, §6030, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 402.)
