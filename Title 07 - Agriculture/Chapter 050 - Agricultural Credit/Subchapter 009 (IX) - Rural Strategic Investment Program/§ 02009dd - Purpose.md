### §2009dd. Purpose

The purpose of this subchapter is to establish a rural strategic investment program—

&emsp;(1) to provide rural communities with flexible resources to develop comprehensive, collaborative, and locally-based strategic planning processes; and

&emsp;(2) to implement innovative community and economic development strategies that optimize regional competitive advantages.

(Pub. L. 87–128, title III, §385A, as added Pub. L. 107–171, title VI, §6030, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 402.)
