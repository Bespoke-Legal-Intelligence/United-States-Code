### §2204f. Fund for Rural America

#### (a) In general

There is established in the Treasury of the United States an account to be known as the Fund for Rural America (referred to in this section as the “Account”) to provide funds for activities described in subsection (c) of this section.

#### (b) Funding

#### (1) In general

&emsp;On October 1, 1998, and each October 1 thereafter through October 1, 2002, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the Secretary of the Treasury shall transfer $60,000,000 to the Account.

#### (2) Entitlement

&emsp;The Secretary of Agriculture (referred to in this section as the “Secretary”)—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) shall be entitled to receive the funds transferred to the Account under paragraph (1);

&emsp;&emsp;(B) shall accept the funds; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) shall use the funds to carry out this section.

#### (3) Purposes

&emsp;Subject to subsection (d) of this section, of the amounts transferred to the Account for a fiscal year, the Secretary shall make available—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) for activities described in subsection (c)(1) of this section, not less than 1/3 and not more than 2/3 of the funds in the Account; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) for activities described in subsection (c)(2) of this section, all funds in the Account not made available by the Secretaryfor activities described in subsection (c)(1) of this section.

#### (c) Activities

#### (1) Rural development

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may use the funds in the Account for a rural development activity—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) authorized under the Housing Act of 1949 \[42 U.S.C. 1441 et seq.\] for—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) direct loans to low-income borrowers under section 502 (42 U.S.C. 1472);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) loans for financial assistance for housing for domestic farm laborers under section 514 (42 U.S.C. 1484);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) financial assistance for housing for domestic farm laborers under section 516 (42 U.S.C. 1486);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) payments for elderly who are not now receiving rental assistance under section 521 (42 U.S.C. 1490a);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(V) grants and contracts for mutual and self-help housing under section 523(b)(1)(A) (42 U.S.C. 1490c(b)(1)(A)); or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VI) grants for rural housing preservation under section 533 (42 U.S.C. 1490m); or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) conducted under any rural development program, including a program authorized under—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1921 et seq.);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) subtitle G of title XVI \[7 U.S.C. 5901 et seq.\] and title XXIII of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) title V of the Rural Development Act of 1971 (7 U.S.C. 2661 et seq.); or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) section 1323(b) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (Public Law 99–198; 7 U.S.C. 1932 note).

#### (B) Limitation on programs funded

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may not expend funds made available to carry out activities described in subparagraph (A) for any activity that did not receive appropriations for fiscal year 1995. Funds expended under this section for any program purpose shall be spent in accordance with and subject to the applicable program limitations, restrictions, and priorities found in the underlying program authority and this Act.

#### (C) Limitation on housing assistance

&emsp;&emsp;Not more than 20 percent of the funds made available to carry out activities described in subparagraph (A) shall be made available to carry out activities described in subparagraph (A)(i).

#### (D) Disclosure of allocation

&emsp;&emsp;For any fiscal year, the Secretary shall not disclose the allocation of funds under this section for any activity described in subparagraph (A) until the date that is 1 day after the date of enactment of legislation authorizing appropriations for the Department of Agriculture for any period in the fiscal year.

#### (2) Research

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may use the funds in the Account for research, extension, and education grants to—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) increase international competitiveness, efficiency, and farm profitability;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) reduce economic and health risks;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) conserve and enhance natural resources;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) develop new crops, new crop uses, and new agricultural applications of biotechnology;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) enhance animal agricultural resources;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) preserve plant and animal germplasm;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) increase economic opportunities in farming and rural communities; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) expand locally-owned value-added processing.

#### (B) Eligible grantee

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may make a grant under this paragraph to—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a Federal research agency;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a national laboratory;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) a college or university or a research foundation maintained by a college or university;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) a private research organization with an established and demonstrated capacity to perform research or technology transfer; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) a State agricultural experiment station.

#### (C) Use of grant

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;A grant made under this paragraph may be used by a grantee for 1 or more of the following uses:

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) Outcome-oriented research at the discovery end of the spectrum to provide breakthrough results.

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) Exploratory and advanced development and technology with well-identified outcomes.

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) A national, regional, or multi-State program oriented primarily toward extension programs and education programs demonstrating and supporting the competitiveness of United States agriculture.

#### (ii) Smaller institutions

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Of the amounts made available for activities described in this paragraph, not less than 15 percent shall be awarded to colleges, universities, or research foundations eligible for a grant under subparagraph (B)(iii) that rank in the lowest 1/3 of such colleges, universities, and foundations on the basis of Federal research funds received under a provision of law other than this section.

#### (D) Administration

#### (i) Priority

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;In administering this paragraph, the Secretary shall—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) establish criteria for allocating grants based on the priorities in subparagraph (A) and in consultation with the National Agricultural Research, Extension, Education, and Economics Advisory Board established under section 3123 of this title;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) seek and accept proposals for grants;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) determine the relevance and merit of proposals through a system of peer review and review by the National Agricultural Research, Extension, Education, and Economics Advisory Board; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) award grants on the basis of merit, quality, and relevance to advancing the purposes of federally supported agricultural research, extension, and education provided in section 3101 of this title.

#### (ii) Competitive basis

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;A grant under this paragraph shall be awarded on a competitive basis.

#### (iii) Terms

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;A grant under this paragraph shall have a term that does not exceed 5 years.

#### (iv) Matching funds

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;As a condition of making a grant under this paragraph, the Secretary shall require the funding of the grant with equal matching funds from a non-Federal source if the grant is—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) for applied research that is commodity-specific; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) not of national scope.

#### (v) Delegation

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall administer this section through the Cooperative State Research, Education, and Extension Service of the Department of Agriculture.

#### (vi) Availability of funds

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Funds shall be available for obligation under this paragraph for a 2-year period.

#### (vii) Administrative costs

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may use not more than 4 percent of the funds made available for activities described in this paragraph for administrative costs incurred by the Secretary in carrying out this paragraph.

#### (viii) Buildings

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Funds made available for activities described in this paragraph shall not be used for the construction of a new building or the acquisition, expansion, remodeling, or alteration of an existing building (including site grading and improvement and architect fees).

#### (d) Limitations

Amounts in the Account may not be used for an activity described in subsection (c) of this section for a fiscal year if the program funding level for the fiscal year for the activity is less than 90 percent of the amount appropriated for the activity for fiscal year 1996, adjusted for inflation.
