### §2284. Annual reports

The Secretary shall annually transmit to the appropriate committees of Congress having legislative jurisdiction or oversight with respect to the agency within the Department of Agriculture that provides support services to an advisory committee, and to the Library of Congress—

&emsp;(1) a copy of the report concerning that advisory committee prepared in compliance with section 6(c) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 U.S.C. App.);

&emsp;(2) a list of the members of that advisory committee which shall specify the principal place of residence, persons or companies by whom they are employed, and other major sources of income, as defined by the Secretary, of each member; and

&emsp;(3) a statement of the amount of expenses incurred in connection with advisory committee meetings by any member of an advisory committee for which reimbursement was received from any source other than the United States or the member's employer.

(Pub. L. 95–113, title XVIII, §1804, Sept. 29, 1977, 91 Stat. 1041; Pub. L. 97–98, title XI, §1116(a), Dec. 22, 1981, 95 Stat. 1270.)

#### References in Text

Section 6(c) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act, referred to in par. (1), is section 6(c) of Pub. L. 92–463, which is set out in the Appendix to Title 5, Government Organization and Employees.

#### Amendments

1981—Pub. L. 97–98 substituted provision relating to transmission of an annual report for provision relating to additional duties of the Secretary of Agriculture.

#### Effective Date of 1981 Amendment

Amendment by Pub. L. 97–98 effective Dec. 22, 1981, see section 1801 of Pub. L. 97–98, set out as an Effective Date note under section 4301 of this title.
