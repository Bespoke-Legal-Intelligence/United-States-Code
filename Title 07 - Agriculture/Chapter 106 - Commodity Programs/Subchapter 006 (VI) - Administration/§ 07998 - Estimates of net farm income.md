### §7998. Estimates of net farm income

In each issuance of projections of net farm income, the Secretary shall include (as determined by the Secretary)—

&emsp;(1) an estimate of the net farm income earned by commercial producers in the United States; and

&emsp;(2) an estimate of the net farm income attributable to commercial producers of each of the following:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) Livestock.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) Loan commodities.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) Agricultural commodities other than loan commodities.

(Pub. L. 107–171, title I, §1615, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 222.)
