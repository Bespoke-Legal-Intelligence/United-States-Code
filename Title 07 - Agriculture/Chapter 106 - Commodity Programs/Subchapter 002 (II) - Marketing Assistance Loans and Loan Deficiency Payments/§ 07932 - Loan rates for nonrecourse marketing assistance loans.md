### §7932. Loan rates for nonrecourse marketing assistance loans

#### (a) 2002 and 2003 crop years

For purposes of the 2002 and 2003 crop years, the loan rate for a marketing assistance loan under section 7931 of this title for a loan commodity shall be equal to the following:

&emsp;(1) In the case of wheat, $2.80 per bushel.

&emsp;(2) In the case of corn, $1.98 per bushel.

&emsp;(3) In the case of grain sorghum, $1.98 per bushel.

&emsp;(4) In the case of barley, $1.88 per bushel.

&emsp;(5) In the case of oats, $1.35 per bushel.

&emsp;(6) In the case of upland cotton, $0.52 per pound.

&emsp;(7) In the case of extra long staple cotton, $0.7977 per pound.

&emsp;(8) In the case of rice, $6.50 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(9) In the case of soybeans, $5.00 per bushel.

&emsp;(10) In the case of other oilseeds, $0.0960 per pound.

&emsp;(11) In the case of graded wool, $1.00 per pound.

&emsp;(12) In the case of nongraded wool, $0.40 per pound.

&emsp;(13) In the case of mohair, $4.20 per pound.

&emsp;(14) In the case of honey, $0.60 per pound.

&emsp;(15) In the case of dry peas, $6.33 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(16) In the case of lentils, $11.94 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(17) In the case of small chickpeas, $7.56 per hundredweight.

#### (b) 2004 through 2007 crop years

For purposes of the 2004 through 2007 crop years, the loan rate for a marketing assistanceloan under section 7931 of this title for a loan commodity shall be equal to the following:

&emsp;(1) In the case of wheat, $2.75 per bushel.

&emsp;(2) In the case of corn, $1.95 per bushel.

&emsp;(3) In the case of grain sorghum, $1.95 per bushel.

&emsp;(4) In the case of barley, $1.85 per bushel.

&emsp;(5) In the case of oats, $1.33 per bushel.

&emsp;(6) In the case of upland cotton, $0.52 per pound.

&emsp;(7) In the case of extra long staple cotton, $0.7977 per pound.

&emsp;(8) In the case of rice, $6.50 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(9) In the case of soybeans, $5.00 per bushel.

&emsp;(10) In the case of other oilseeds, $0.0930 per pound.

&emsp;(11) In the case of graded wool, $1.00 per pound.

&emsp;(12) In the case of nongraded wool, $0.40 per pound.

&emsp;(13) In the case of mohair, $4.20 per pound.

&emsp;(14) In the case of honey, $0.60 per pound.

&emsp;(15) In the case of dry peas, $6.22 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(16) In the case of lentils, $11.72 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(17) In the case of small chickpeas, $7.43 per hundredweight.

(Pub. L. 107–171, title I, §1202, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 155.)
