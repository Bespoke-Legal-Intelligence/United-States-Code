### §9032. Loan rates for nonrecourse marketing assistance loans

#### (a) In general

For purposes of each of the 2014 through 2018 crop years, the loan rate for a marketing assistance loan under section 9031 of this title for a loan commodity shall be equal to the following:

&emsp;(1) In the case of wheat, $2.94 per bushel.

&emsp;(2) In the case of corn, $1.95 per bushel.

&emsp;(3) In the case of grain sorghum, $1.95 per bushel.

&emsp;(4) In the case of barley, $1.95 per bushel.

&emsp;(5) In the case of oats, $1.39 per bushel.

&emsp;(6) In the case of base quality of upland cotton, for each of the 2014 through 2018 crop years, the simple average of the adjusted prevailing world price for the 2 immediately preceding marketing years, as determined by the Secretary and announced October 1 preceding the next domestic plantings, but in no case less than $0.45 per pound or more than $0.52 per pound.

&emsp;(7) In the case of extra long staple cotton, $0.7977 per pound.

&emsp;(8) In the case of long grain rice, $6.50 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(9) In the case of medium grain rice, $6.50 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(10) In the case of soybeans, $5.00 per bushel.

&emsp;(11) In the case of other oilseeds, $10.09 per hundredweight for each of the following kinds of oilseeds:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) Sunflower seed.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) Rapeseed.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) Canola.

&emsp;&emsp;(D) Safflower.

&emsp;&emsp;(E) Flaxseed.

&emsp;&emsp;(F) Mustard seed.

&emsp;&emsp;(G) Crambe.

&emsp;&emsp;(H) Sesame seed.

&emsp;&emsp;(I) Other oilseeds designated by the Secretary.

&emsp;(12) In the case of dry peas, $5.40 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(13) In the case of lentils, $11.28 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(14) In the case of small chickpeas, $7.43 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(15) In the case of large chickpeas, $11.28 per hundredweight.

&emsp;(16) In the case of graded wool, $1.15 per pound.

&emsp;(17) In the case of nongraded wool, $0.40 per pound.

&emsp;(18) In the case of mohair, $4.20 per pound.

&emsp;(19) In the case of honey, $0.69 per pound.

&emsp;(20) In the case of peanuts, $355 per ton.

#### (b) Single county loan rate for other oilseeds

The Secretary shall establish a single loan rate in each county for each kind of other oilseeds described in subsection (a)(11).

(Pub. L. 113–79, title I, §1202, Feb. 7, 2014, 128 Stat. 675.)
