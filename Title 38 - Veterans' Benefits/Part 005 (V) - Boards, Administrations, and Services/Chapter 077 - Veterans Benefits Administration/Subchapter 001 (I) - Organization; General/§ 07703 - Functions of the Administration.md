### §7703. Functions of the Administration

The Veterans Benefits Administration is responsible for the administration of the following programs of the Department:

&emsp;(1) Compensation and pension programs.

&emsp;(2) Vocational rehabilitation and educational assistance programs.

&emsp;(3) Veterans' housing loan programs.

&emsp;(4) Veterans' and servicemembers' life insurance programs.

&emsp;(5) Outreach programs and other veterans' services programs.

(Added Pub. L. 102–83, §2(b), Aug. 6, 1991, 105 Stat. 399.)
