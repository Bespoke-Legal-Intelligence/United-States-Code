### §51505. Annual payments for maintenance and support

(a)

Payment Agreements.—The Secretary of Transportation may make an agreement (effective for not more than 4 years) with the following academies to provide annual payments to those academies for their maintenance and support:

&emsp;(1) One State maritime academy in each State that satisfies section 51506(a) of this title.

&emsp;(2) Each regional maritime academy that satisfies section 51506(a) of this title.

(b)

Payments.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—Subject to paragraph (2), an annual payment to an academy under subsection (a) shall be at least equal to the amount given to the academy for its maintenance and support by the State in which it is located, or, for a regional maritime academy, by all States cooperating to sponsor the academy.

&emsp;(2)

Maximum.—The amount under paragraph (1) may not be more than $25,000. However, if the academy satisfies section 51506(b) of this title, the amount shall be—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $100,000 for a State maritime academy; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $200,000 for a regional maritime academy.

(Pub. L. 109–304, §8(b), Oct. 6, 2006, 120 Stat. 1578; Pub. L. 109–163, div. C, title XXXV, §3502(a), Jan. 6, 2006, 119 Stat. 3547.)
