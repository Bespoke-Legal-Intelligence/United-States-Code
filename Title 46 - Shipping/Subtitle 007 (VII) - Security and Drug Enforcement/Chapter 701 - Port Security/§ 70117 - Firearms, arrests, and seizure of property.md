### §70117. Firearms, arrests, and seizure of property

Subject to guidelines approved by the Secretary, members of the Coast Guard may, in the performance of official duties—

&emsp;(1) carry a firearm; and

&emsp;(2) while at a facility—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) make an arrest without warrant for any offense against the United States committed in their presence; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) seize property as otherwise provided by law.

(Added Pub. L. 108–293, title VIII, §801(a), Aug. 9, 2004, 118 Stat. 1078, §70118; renumbered §70117, Pub. L. 109–241, title IX, §901(<i>l</i>)(1), July 11, 2006, 120 Stat. 565; Pub. L. 109–304, §15(33)(A), Oct. 6, 2006, 120 Stat. 1705.)
