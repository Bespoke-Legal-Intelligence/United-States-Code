### §3509. Medical standards

The owner of a vessel to which section 3507 applies shall ensure that—

&emsp;(1) a physician is always present and available to treat any passengers who may be on board the vessel in the event of an emergency situation;

&emsp;(2) the vessel is in compliance with the Health Care Guidelines for Cruise Ship Medical Facilities established by the American College of Emergency Physicians; and

&emsp;(3) the initial safety briefing given to the passengers on board the vessel includes—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the location of the vessel's medical facilities; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the appropriate steps passengers should follow during a medical emergency.

(Added Pub. L. 116–283, div. G, title LVXXXIII \[LXXXIII\], §8322(a), Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4702.)
