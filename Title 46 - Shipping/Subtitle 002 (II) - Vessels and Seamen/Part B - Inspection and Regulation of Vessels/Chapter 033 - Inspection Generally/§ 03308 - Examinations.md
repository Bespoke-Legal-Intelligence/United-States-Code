### §3308. Examinations

In addition to inspections required by section 3307 of this title, the Secretary shall examine or have examined—

&emsp;(1) each vessel subject to inspection at proper times to ensure compliance with law and regulations; and

&emsp;(2) crewmember accommodations on each vessel subject to inspection at least once a month or when the vessel enters United States ports to ensure that the accommodations are—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) of the size required by law and regulations;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) properly ventilated and in a clean and sanitary condition; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) equipped with proper plumbing and mechanical appliances required by law and regulations, and the appliances are in good working condition.

(Pub. L. 98–89, Aug. 26, 1983, 97 Stat. 514; Pub. L. 104–324, title VI, §603(c), Oct. 19, 1996, 110 Stat. 3930.)
