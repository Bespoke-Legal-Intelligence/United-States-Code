### §7316. Lifeboatmen

To qualify for an endorsement as lifeboatman, an applicant must provide satisfactory proof that the applicant—

&emsp;(1) has the service or training required by regulation;

&emsp;(2) is qualified professionally as demonstrated by examination; and

&emsp;(3) is qualified professionally by actual demonstration.

(Pub. L. 98–89, Aug. 26, 1983, 97 Stat. 544.)
