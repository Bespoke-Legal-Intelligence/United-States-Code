### §2108. Refund of penalties

The Secretary may refund or remit a civil penalty collected under this subtitle if—

&emsp;(1) application has been made for refund or remission of the penalty within one year from the date of payment; and

&emsp;(2) the Secretary finds that the penalty was unlawfully, improperly, or excessively imposed.

(Pub. L. 98–89, Aug. 26, 1983, 97 Stat. 507.)
