### §15104. National Merchant Mariner Medical Advisory Committee

(a)

Establishment.—There is established a National Merchant Mariner Medical AdvisoryCommittee (in this section referred to as the "Committee").

(b)

Function.—The Committee shall advise the Secretary on matters relating to—

&emsp;(1) medical certification determinations for the issuance of licenses, certification of registry, and merchant mariners' documents with respect to merchant mariners;

&emsp;(2) medical standards and guidelines for the physical qualifications of operators of commercial vessels;

&emsp;(3) medical examiner education; and

&emsp;(4) medical research.

(c)

Membership.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—The Committee shall consist of 14 members appointed by the Secretary in accordance with this section and section 15109 of this chapter.

&emsp;(2)

Expertise.—Each member of the Committee shall have particular expertise, knowledge, and experience in matters relating to the function of the Committee.

&emsp;(3)

Representation.—Members of the Committee shall be appointed as follows:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 9 shall represent health-care professionals and have particular expertise, knowledge, and experience regarding the medical examinations of merchant mariners or occupational medicine.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) 5 shall represent professional mariners and have particular expertise, knowledge, and experience in occupational requirements for mariners.

(Added Pub. L. 115–282, title VI, §601(a), Dec. 4, 2018, 132 Stat. 4283.)
