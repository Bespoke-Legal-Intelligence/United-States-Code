### §15108. National Towing Safety Advisory Committee

(a)

Establishment.—There is established a National Towing Safety Advisory Committee (in this section referred to as the "Committee").

(b)

Function.—The Committee shall advise the Secretary on matters relating to shallow-draft inland navigation, coastal waterway navigation, and towing safety.

(c)

Membership.—

&emsp;(1)

In general.—The Committee shall consist of 18 members appointed by the Secretary in accordance with this section and section 15109 of this chapter.

&emsp;(2)

Expertise.—Each member of the Committee shall have particular expertise, knowledge, and experience in matters relating to the function of the Committee.

&emsp;(3)

Representation.—Members of the Committee shall be appointed as follows:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 7 members shall represent the barge and towing industry, reflecting a regional geographic balance.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) 1 member shall represent the offshore mineral and oil supply vessel industry.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) 1 member shall represent masters and pilots of towing vessels who hold active licenses and have experience on the Western Rivers and the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway.

&emsp;&emsp;(D) 1 member shall represent masters of towing vessels in offshore service who hold active licenses.

&emsp;&emsp;(E) 1 member shall represent masters of active ship-docking or harbor towing vessels.

&emsp;&emsp;(F) 1 member shall represent licensed and unlicensed towing vessel engineers with formal training and experience.

&emsp;&emsp;(G) 2 members shall represent port districts, authorities, or terminal operators.

&emsp;&emsp;(H) 2 members shall represent shippers and, of the 2, 1 shall be engaged in the shipment of oil or hazardous materials by barge.

&emsp;&emsp;(I) 2 members shall represent the general public.

(Added Pub. L. 115–282, title VI, §601(a), Dec. 4, 2018, 132 Stat. 4285.)
