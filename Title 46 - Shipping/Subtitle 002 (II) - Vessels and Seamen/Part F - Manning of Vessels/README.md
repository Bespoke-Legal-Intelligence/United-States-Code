Part F—Manning of Vessels

#### Historical and Revision Notes

Part F provides for the manning of vessels including requirements generally for the number of individualsrequired, qualifications and conditions of employment, and duties; for masters and other licenses and registered individuals; for pilots; for unlicensed personnel; for small vessels; for tank vessels; and for pilotage on the Great Lakes. The Committee intends that all manning provisions throughout this subtitle will be interpreted to be consistent with one another.

#### Part Referred to in Other Sections

This part is referred to in sections 2101, 2113, 7302, 12306, 14305 of this title.
