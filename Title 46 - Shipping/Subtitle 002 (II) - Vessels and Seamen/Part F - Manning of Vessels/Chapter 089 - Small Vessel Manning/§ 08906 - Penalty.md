### §8906. Penalty

An owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, or individual in charge of a vesseloperated in violation of this chapter or a regulation prescribed under this chapter is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of $1,000. The vessel also is liable in rem for the penalty.

(Pub. L. 98–89, Aug. 26, 1983, 97 Stat. 556.)
