### §1608. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Advanced communications service

&emsp;The term "advanced communications service" has the meaning given the term "advanced telecommunications capability" in section 1302 of this title.

#### (2) Appropriate national security agency

&emsp;The term "appropriate national security agency" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Department of Homeland Security;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Department of Defense;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Office of the Director of National Intelligence;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the National Security Agency; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

#### (3) Commission

&emsp;The term "Commission" means the Federal Communications Commission.

#### (4) Communications equipment or service

&emsp;The term "communications equipment or service" means any equipment or service that is essential to the provision of advanced communications service.

#### (5) Covered communications equipment or service

&emsp;The term "covered communications equipment or service" means any communications equipment or service that is on the list published by the Commission under section 1601(a) of this title.

#### (6) Customers

&emsp;The term "customers" means, with respect to a provider of advanced communications service—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the customers of such provider; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the customers of any affiliate (as defined in section 153 of this title) of such provider.

#### (7) Executive branch interagency body

&emsp;The term "executive branch interagency body" means an interagency body established in the executive branch.

#### (8) Person

&emsp;The term "person" means an individual or entity.

#### (9) Program

&emsp;The term "Program" means the Secure and Trusted Communications Networks Reimbursement Program established under section 1603(a) of this title.

#### (10) Provider of advanced communications service

&emsp;The term "provider of advanced communications service"—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) means a person who provides advanced communications service to United States customers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) includes—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) accredited public or private noncommercial educational institutions, providing their own facilities-based educational broadband service, as defined in section 27.4 of title 47, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor regulation; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) health care providers and libraries providing advanced communications service.

#### (11) Recipient

&emsp;The term "recipient" means any provider of advanced communications service the application of which for a reimbursement under the Program has been approved by the Commission, regardless of whether the provider has received reimbursement funds.

#### (12) Reimbursement funds

&emsp;The term "reimbursement funds" means any reimbursement received under the Program.

(Pub. L. 116–124, §9, Mar. 12, 2020, 134 Stat. 169; Pub. L. 116–260, div. N, title IX, §901(2), Dec. 27, 2020, 134 Stat. 2121.)
