### §12899a. Program authority

The Secretary may make—

&emsp;(1) planning grants to enable applicants to develop Youthbuild programs; and

&emsp;(2) implementation grants to enable applicants to carry out Youthbuild programs.

(Pub. L. 101–625, title IV, §452, as added Pub. L. 102–550, title I, §164, Oct. 28, 1992, 106 Stat. 3723.)
