### §2297b–15. Working Capital Account

There shall be established within the Corporation a Working Capital Account in which theCorporation may retain all revenue necessary for legitimate business expenses, or investments, related to carrying out its purposes.

(Aug. 1, 1946, ch. 724, title II, §1316, as added Oct. 24, 1992, Pub. L. 102–486, title IX, §901, 106 Stat. 2934.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Section repealed effective as of date on which 100 percent of ownership of United States Enrichment Corporation has been transferred to private investors, see section 3116(a)(1) of Pub. L. 104–134, set out as a note under section 2297 of this title.</i>
