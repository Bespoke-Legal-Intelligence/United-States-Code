### §2297e–2. Predeployment activities by United States Enrichment Corporation

The Corporation may begin activities necessary to prepare AVLIS or alternative technologies for uranium enrichment for commercialization including—

&emsp;(1) completion of preapplication activities with the Nuclear Regulatory Commission;

&emsp;(2) preparation of a transition plan to move AVLIS or alternative technologies for uranium enrichment from the laboratory to the marketplace;

&emsp;(3) confirmation of technical performance;

&emsp;(4) validation of economic projections;

&emsp;(5) completion of feasibility and risk studies;

&emsp;(6) initiation of preliminary plant design and engineering; and

&emsp;(7) site selection, site characterization, and environmental documentation activities on the basis of site evaluations and recommendations prepared for the Department by the Argonne National Laboratory.

(Aug. 1, 1946, ch. 724, title II, §1603, as added Oct. 24, 1992, Pub. L. 102–486, title IX, §901, 106 Stat. 2940.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Section repealed effective as of date on which 100 percent of ownership of United States Enrichment Corporation has been transferred to private investors, see section 3116(a)(1) of Pub. L. 104–134, set out as a note under section 2297 of this title.</i>
