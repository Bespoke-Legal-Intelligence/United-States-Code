### §16198. Smart energy and water efficiency pilot program

#### (a) Definitions

In this section:

#### (1) Eligible entity

&emsp;The term "eligible entity" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a utility;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a municipality;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a water district;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) an Indian Tribe or Alaska Native village; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) any other authority that provides water, wastewater, or water reuse services.

#### (2) Smart energy and water efficiency pilot program

&emsp;The term "smart energy and water efficiency pilot program" or "pilot program" means the pilot program established under subsection (b).

#### (b) Smart energy and water efficiency pilot program

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Secretary shall establish and carry out a smart energy and water efficiency pilot program in accordance with this section.

#### (2) Purpose

&emsp;The purpose of the smart energy and water efficiency pilot program is to award grants to eligible entities to demonstrate unique, advanced, or innovative technology-based solutions that will—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) improve the net energy balance of water, wastewater, and water reuse systems;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) improve the net energy balance of water, wastewater, and water reuse systems to help communities across the United States make measurable progress in conserving water, saving energy, and reducing costs;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) support the implementation of innovative and unique processes and the installation of established advanced automated systems that provide real-time data on energy and water; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) improve energy-water conservation and quality and predictive maintenance through technologies that utilize internetconnected technologies, including sensors, intelligent gateways, and security embedded in hardware.

#### (3) Project selection

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall make competitive, merit-reviewed grants under the pilot program to not less than 3, but not more than 5, eligible entities.

#### (B) Selection criteria

&emsp;&emsp;In selecting an eligible entity to receive a grant under the pilot program, the Secretary shall consider—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) energy and cost savings;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the uniqueness, commercial viability, and reliability of the technology to be used;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) the degree to which the project integrates next-generation sensors software, analytics, and management tools;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) the anticipated cost-effectiveness of the pilot project through measurable energy savings, water savings or reuse, and infrastructure costs averted;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) whether the technology can be deployed in a variety of geographic regions and the degree to which the technology can be implemented in a wide range of applications ranging in scale from small towns to large cities, including Tribal communities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) whether the technology has been successfully deployed elsewhere;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) whether the technology was sourced from a manufacturer based in the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) whether the project will be completed in 5 years or less.

#### (C) Applications

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Subject to clause (ii), an eligible entity seeking a grant under the pilot program shall submit to the Secretary an application at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Secretary determines to be necessary.

#### (ii) Contents

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;An application under clause (i) shall, at a minimum, include—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) a description of the project;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) a description of the technology to be used in the project;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) the anticipated results, including energy and water savings, of the project;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) a comprehensive budget for the project;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(V) the names of the project lead organization and any partners;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VI) the number of users to be served by the project;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VII) a description of the ways in which the proposal would meet performance measures established by the Secretary; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VIII) any other information that the Secretary determines to be necessary to complete the review and selection of a grant recipient.

#### (4) Administration

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;Not later than 1 year after December 27, 2020, the Secretary shall select grant recipients under this section.

#### (B) Evaluations

#### (i) Annual evaluations

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall annually carry out an evaluation of each project for which a grant is provided under this section that meets performance measures and benchmarks developed by the Secretary, consistent with the purposes of this section.

#### (ii) Requirements

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Consistent with the performance measures and benchmarks developed under clause (i), in carrying out an evaluation under that clause, the Secretary shall—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) evaluate the progress and impact of the project; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) assess the degree to which the project is meeting the goals of the pilot program.

#### (C) Technical and policy assistance

&emsp;&emsp;On the request of a grant recipient, the Secretary shall provide technical and policy assistance.

#### (D) Best practices

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall make available to the public through the Internet and other means the Secretary considers to be appropriate—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a copy of each evaluation carried out under subparagraph (B); and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a description of any best practices identified by the Secretary as a result of those evaluations.

#### (E) Report to Congress

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall submit to Congress a report containing the results of each evaluation carried out under subparagraph (B).

#### (c) Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this section $15,000,000, to remain available until expended.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title IX, §918, as added Pub. L. 116–260, div. Z, title I, §1014(a), Dec. 27, 2020, 134 Stat. 2451.)
