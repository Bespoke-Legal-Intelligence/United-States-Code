### §16051. Joint flexible fuel/hybrid vehicle commercialization initiative

#### (a) Definitions

In this section:

#### (1) Eligible entity

&emsp;The term "eligible entity" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a for-profit corporation;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a nonprofit corporation; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) an institution of higher education.

#### (2) Program

&emsp;The term "program" means a program established under subsection (b).

#### (b) Establishment

The Secretary shall establish a program to improve technologies for the commercialization of—

&emsp;(1) a combination hybrid/flexible fuel vehicle; or

&emsp;(2) a plug-in hybrid/flexible fuel vehicle.

#### (c) Grants

In carrying out the program, the Secretary shall provide grants that give preference to proposals that—

&emsp;(1) achieve the greatest reduction in miles per gallon of petroleum fuel consumption;

&emsp;(2) achieve not less than 250 miles per gallon of petroleum fuel consumption; and

&emsp;(3) have the greatest potential of commercialization to the general public within 5 years.

#### (d) Verification

Not later than 90 days after August 8, 2005, the Secretary shall publish in the Federal Register procedures to verify—

&emsp;(1) the hybrid/flexible fuel vehicle technologies to be demonstrated; and

&emsp;(2) that grants are administered in accordance with this section.

#### (e) Report

Not later than 260 days after August 8, 2005, and annually thereafter, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report that—

&emsp;(1) identifies the grant recipients;

&emsp;(2) describes the technologies to be funded under the program;

&emsp;(3) assesses the feasibility of the technologies described in paragraph (2) in meeting the goals described in subsection (c);

&emsp;(4) identifies applications submitted for the program that were not funded; and

&emsp;(5) makes recommendations for Federal legislation to achieve commercialization of the technology demonstrated.

#### (f) Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section, to remain available until expended—

&emsp;(1) $3,000,000 for fiscal year 2006;

&emsp;(2) $7,000,000 for fiscal year 2007;

&emsp;(3) $10,000,000 for fiscal year 2008; and

&emsp;(4) $20,000,000 for fiscal year 2009.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title VII, §706, Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 817.)
