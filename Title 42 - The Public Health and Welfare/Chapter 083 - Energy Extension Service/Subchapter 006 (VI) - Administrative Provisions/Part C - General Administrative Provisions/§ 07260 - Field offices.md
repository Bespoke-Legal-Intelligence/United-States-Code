### §7260. Field offices

The Secretary is authorized to establish, alter, consolidate or discontinue and to maintain suchState, regional, district, local or other field offices as he may deem to be necessary to carry out functions vested in him.

(Pub. L. 95–91, title VI, §650, Aug. 4, 1977, 91 Stat. 601.)
