### §7218. Sanctions

#### (a) Civil penalty

Any individual who violates section 7212 of this title shall be subject to a civil penalty, assessed by the Secretary in accordance with applicable law or by any district court of the United States, not to exceed $10,000 for each violation.

#### (b) Effect on operation of sections 207 and 208 of title 18

Nothing in this title shall be deemed to limit the operation of section 207 or section 208 of title 18. Nor shall any waiver issued pursuant to section 7212(c) of this title constitute a waiver of the requirements of such provision.

(Pub. L. 95–91, title VI, §603, formerly §608, Aug. 4, 1977, 91 Stat. 596; renumbered §603 and amended, Pub. L. 103–160, div. C, title XXXI, §3161(c)(1)(D), (E), Nov. 30, 1993, 107 Stat. 1958.)

#### Prior Provisions
