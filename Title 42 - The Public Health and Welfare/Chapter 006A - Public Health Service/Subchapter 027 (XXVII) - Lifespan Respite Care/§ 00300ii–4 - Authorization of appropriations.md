### §300ii–4. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2007;

&emsp;(2) $40,000,000 for fiscal year 2008;

&emsp;(3) $53,330,000 for fiscal year 2009;

&emsp;(4) $71,110,000 for fiscal year 2010; and

&emsp;(5) $94,810,000 for fiscal year 2011.

(July 1, 1944, ch. 373, title XXIX, §2905, as added Pub. L. 109–442, §2, Dec. 21, 2006, 120 Stat. 3296.)
