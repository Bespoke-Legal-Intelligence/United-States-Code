### §300mm–2. Education and outreach

The WTC Program Administrator shall institute a program that provides education and outreach on the existence and availability of services under the WTC Program. The outreach and education program—

&emsp;(1) shall include—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the establishment of a public Web site with information about the WTC Program;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) meetings with potentially eligible populations;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) development and dissemination of outreach materials informing people about the program; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the establishment of phone information services; and

&emsp;(2) shall be conducted in a manner intended—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to reach all affected populations; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to include materials for culturally and linguistically diverse populations.

(July 1, 1944, ch. 373, title XXXIII, §3303, as added Pub. L. 111–347, title I, §101, Jan. 2, 2011, 124 Stat. 3629.)
