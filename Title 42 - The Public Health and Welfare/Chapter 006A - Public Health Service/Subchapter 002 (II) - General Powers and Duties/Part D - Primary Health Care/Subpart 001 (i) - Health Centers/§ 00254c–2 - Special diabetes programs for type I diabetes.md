### §254c–2. Special diabetes programs for type I diabetes

#### (a) In general

The Secretary, directly or through grants, shall provide for research into the prevention and cure of Type <sup>1</sup> I diabetes.

#### (b) Funding

#### (1) Transferred funds

&emsp;Notwithstanding section 1397dd(a) of this title, from the amounts appropriated in such section for each of fiscal years 1998 through 2002, $30,000,000 is hereby transferred and made available in such fiscal year for grants under this section.

#### (2) Appropriations

&emsp;For the purpose of making grants under this section, there is appropriated, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $70,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2001 and 2002 (which shall be combined with amounts transferred under paragraph (1) for each such fiscal years);

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $100,000,000 for fiscal year 2003; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $150,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2004 through 2008.
