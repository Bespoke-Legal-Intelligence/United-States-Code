### §17154. Use of funds

An eligible entity may use a grant received under this part to carry out activities to achieve the purposes of the program, including—

&emsp;(1) development and implementation of an energy efficiency and conservation strategy under section 17155(b) of this title;

&emsp;(2) retaining technical consultant services to assist the eligible entity in the development of such a strategy, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) formulation of energy efficiency, energy conservation, and energy usage goals;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) identification of strategies to achieve those goals—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) through efforts to increase energy efficiency and reduce energy consumption; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) by encouraging behavioral changes among the population served by the eligible entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) development of methods to measure progress in achieving the goals;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) development and publication of annual reports to the population served by the eligible entity describing—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the strategies and goals; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the progress made in achieving the strategies and goals during the preceding calendar year; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) other services to assist in the implementation of the energy efficiency and conservation strategy;

&emsp;(3) conducting residential and commercial building energy audits;

&emsp;(4) establishment of financial incentive programs for energy efficiency improvements;

&emsp;(5) the provision of grants to nonprofit organizations and governmental agencies for the purpose of performing energy efficiency retrofits;

&emsp;(6) development and implementation of energy efficiency and conservation programs for buildings and facilities within the jurisdiction of the eligible entity, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) design and operation of the programs;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) identifying the most effective methods for achieving maximum participation and efficiency rates;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) public education;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) measurement and verification protocols; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) identification of energy efficient technologies;

&emsp;(7) development and implementation of programs to conserve energy used in transportation, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) use of flex time by employers;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) satellite work centers;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) development and promotion of zoning guidelines or requirements that promote energy efficient development;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) development of infrastructure, such as bike lanes and pathways and pedestrian walkways;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) synchronization of traffic signals; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) other measures that increase energy efficiency and decrease energy consumption;

&emsp;(8) development and implementation of building codes and inspection services to promote building energy efficiency;

&emsp;(9) application and implementation of energy distribution technologies that significantly increase energy efficiency, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) distributed resources; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) district heating and cooling systems;

&emsp;(10) activities to increase participation and efficiency rates for material conservation programs, including source reduction, recycling, and recycled content procurement programs that lead to increases in energy efficiency;

&emsp;(11) the purchase and implementation of technologies to reduce, capture, and, to the maximum extent practicable, use methane and other greenhouse gases generated by landfills or similar sources;

&emsp;(12) replacement of traffic signals and street lighting with energy efficient lighting technologies, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) light emitting diodes; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any other technology of equal or greater energy efficiency;

&emsp;(13) development, implementation, and installation on or in any government building of the eligible entity of onsite renewable energy technology that generates electricity from renewable resources, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) solar energy;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) wind energy;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) fuel cells; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) biomass; and

&emsp;(14) any other appropriate activity, as determined by the Secretary, in consultation with—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Secretary of Transportation; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development.

(Pub. L. 110–140, title V, §544, Dec. 19, 2007, 121 Stat. 1669.)
