### §17152. Energy Efficiency and Conservation Block Grant Program

#### (a) Establishment

The Secretary shall establish a program, to be known as the "Energy Efficiency and Conservation Block Grant Program", under which the Secretary shall provide grants to eligible entities in accordance with this part.

#### (b) Purpose

The purpose of the program shall be to assist eligible entities in implementing strategies—

&emsp;(1) to reduce fossil fuel emissions created as a result of activities within the jurisdictions of eligible entities in a manner that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is environmentally sustainable; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to the maximum extent practicable, maximizes benefits for local and regional communities;

&emsp;(2) to reduce the total energy use of the eligible entities; and

&emsp;(3) to improve energy efficiency in—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the transportation sector;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the building sector; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) other appropriate sectors.

(Pub. L. 110–140, title V, §542, Dec. 19, 2007, 121 Stat. 1668.)
