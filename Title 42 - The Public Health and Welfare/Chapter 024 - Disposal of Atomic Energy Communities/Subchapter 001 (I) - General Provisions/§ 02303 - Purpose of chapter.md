### §2303. Purpose of chapter

It is the purpose of this chapter to effectuate the policies set forth above by providing for—

&emsp;(a) the maintenance of conditions which will not impede the recruitment and retention of personnel essential to the atomic energy program;

&emsp;(b) the obligation of the United States to contribute to the support of municipal functions in a manner commensurate with—

&emsp;&emsp;(1) the fiscal problems peculiar to the communities by reason of their construction as national defense installations, and

&emsp;&emsp;(2) the municipal and other burdens imposed on the governmental or other entities at the communities by the United States in its operations at or near the communities;

&emsp;(c) the opportunity for the residents of the communities to assume the obligations and privileges of local self-government; and

&emsp;(d) the encouragement of the construction of new homes at the communities.

(Aug. 4, 1955, ch. 543, ch. 1, §13, 69 Stat. 472.)
