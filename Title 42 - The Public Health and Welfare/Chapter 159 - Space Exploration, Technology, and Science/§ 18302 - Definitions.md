### §18302. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Administrator

&emsp;The term "Administrator" means the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

#### (2) Appropriate committees of Congress

&emsp;The term "appropriate committees of Congress" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Committee on Science <sup>1</sup> of the House of Representatives.

#### (3) Cis-lunar space

&emsp;The term "cis-lunar space" means the region of space from the Earth out to and including the region around the surface of the Moon.

#### (4) Deep space

&emsp;The term "deep space" means the region of space beyond cis-lunar space.

#### (5) ISS

&emsp;The term "ISS" means the International Space Station.

#### (6) NASA

&emsp;The term "NASA" means the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

#### (7) Near-Earth space

&emsp;The term "near-Earth space" means the region of space that includes low-Earth orbit and extends out to and includes geo-synchronous orbit.

#### (8) NOAA

&emsp;The term "NOAA" means the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

#### (9) OSTP

&emsp;The term "OSTP" means the Office of Science and Technology Policy.

#### (10) Space Launch System

&emsp;The term "Space Launch System" means the follow-on government-owned civil launch system developed, managed, and operated by NASA to serve as a key component to expand human presence beyond low-Earth orbit.

(Pub. L. 111–267, §3, Oct. 11, 2010, 124 Stat. 2808.)
