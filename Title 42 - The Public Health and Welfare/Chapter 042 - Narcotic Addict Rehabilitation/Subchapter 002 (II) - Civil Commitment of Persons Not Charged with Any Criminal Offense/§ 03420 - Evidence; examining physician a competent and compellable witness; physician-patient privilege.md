### §3420. Evidence; examining physician a competent and compellable witness; physician-patient privilege

Any physician conducting an examination under this subchapter shall be a competent and compellable witness at any hearing or other proceeding conducted pursuant to this subchapter and the physician-patient privilege shall not be applicable.

(Pub. L. 89–793, title III, §310, Nov. 8, 1966, 80 Stat. 1447.)
