### §3422. Commitment to hospital of the Service dependent upon certification of availability of facilities or personnel for treatment

Notwithstanding any other provision of this subchapter, no patient shall be committed to a hospital of the Service under this subchapter if the Surgeon General certifies that adequate facilities or personnel for treatment of such patient are unavailable.

(Pub. L. 89–793, title III, §312, Nov. 8, 1966, 80 Stat. 1447.)
