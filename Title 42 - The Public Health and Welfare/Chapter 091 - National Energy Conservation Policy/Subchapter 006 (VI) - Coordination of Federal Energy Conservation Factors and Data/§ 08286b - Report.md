### §8286b. Report

The President shall report to the Congress on January 1, 1981, and annually thereafter, with respect to—

&emsp;(1) the activities which have been carried out under this subchapter; and

&emsp;(2) other efforts which are being carried out to coordinate the various Federal energy conservation programs.

(Pub. L. 96–294, title V, §597, June 30, 1980, 94 Stat. 762.)

#### Delegation of Functions

Memorandum of President of the United States, June 23, 1993, 58 F.R. 34519, provided:

Memorandum for the Secretary of Energy

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and the laws of the United States, including section 301 of title 3 of the United States Code, I hereby delegate to you the authority to transmit to the Congress the annual report describing the activities of the Federal Government as required by subtitle H, title V of the Energy Security Act (Public Law 96–294; 42 U.S.C. 8286, <i>et seq</i>.).

You are authorized and directed to publish this memorandum in the Federal Register.

William J. Clinton.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
