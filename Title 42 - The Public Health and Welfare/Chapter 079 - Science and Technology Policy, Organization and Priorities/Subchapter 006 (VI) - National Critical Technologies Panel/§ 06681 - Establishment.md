### §6681. Establishment

The Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy shall establish within that office <sup>1</sup> a National Critical Technologies Panel (hereinafter in this subchapter referred to as the “panel”). The panel shall prepare the biennial national critical technologies report required by section 6683 of this title.

(Pub. L. 94–282, title VI, §601, as added Pub. L. 101–189, div. A, title VIII, §841(a)(1), Nov. 29, 1989, 103 Stat. 1511.)
