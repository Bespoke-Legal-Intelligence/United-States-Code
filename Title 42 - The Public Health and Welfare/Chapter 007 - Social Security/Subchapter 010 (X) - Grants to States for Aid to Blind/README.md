### SUBCHAPTER X—GRANTS TO STATES FOR AID TO BLIND

#### Repeal of Subchapter X of This Chapter; Inapplicability of Repeal to Puerto Rico, Guam, and Virgin Islands

<i>Pub. L. 92–603, title III, §303(a), (b), Oct. 30, 1972, 86 Stat. 1484, provided that this subchapter is repealed effective Jan. 1, 1974, except with respect to Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Virgin Islands.</i>

#### Subchapter Referred to in Other Sections

This subchapter is referred to in sections 428, 671, 1301, 1306a, 1308, 1309, 1311, 1315, 1316, 1318, 1319, 1320b–2,1320b–3, 1320b–7, 1382, 1382c, 1395v, 1396a, 1396b, 1396d of this title; title 7 sections 2012, 2014; title 8 section 1255a; title 26 section 6103; title 29 section 802.
