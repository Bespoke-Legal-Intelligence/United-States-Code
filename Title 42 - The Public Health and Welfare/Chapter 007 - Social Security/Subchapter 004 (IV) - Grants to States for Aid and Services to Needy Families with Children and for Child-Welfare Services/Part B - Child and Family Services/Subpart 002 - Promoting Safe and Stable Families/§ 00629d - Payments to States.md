### §629d. Payments to States

#### (a) Entitlement

Each State that has a plan approved under section 629b of this title shall be entitled to payment of the lesser of—

&emsp;(1) 75 percent of the total expenditures by the State for activities under the plan during the fiscal year or the immediately succeeding fiscal year; or

&emsp;(2) the allotment of the State under section 629c of this title for the fiscal year.

#### (b) Prohibitions

#### (1) No use of other Federal funds for State match

&emsp;Each State receiving an amount paid under subsection (a) of this section may not expend any Federal funds to meet the costs of services under the State plan under section 629b of this title not covered by the amount so paid.

#### (2) Availability of funds

&emsp;A State may not expend any amount paid under subsection (a) of this section for any fiscal year after the end of the immediately succeeding fiscal year.

#### (c) Direct payments to tribal organizations of Indian tribes

The Secretary shall pay any amount to which an Indian tribe is entitled under this section directly to the tribal organization of the Indian tribe.
