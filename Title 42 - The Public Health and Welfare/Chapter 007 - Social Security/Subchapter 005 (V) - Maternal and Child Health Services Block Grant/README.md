### SUBCHAPTER V—MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH SERVICES BLOCK GRANT

#### Amendments

1981—Pub. L. 97–35, title XXI, §2192(a), Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 818, substituted “MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH SERVICES BLOCK GRANT” for “MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH AND CRIPPLED CHILDREN'S SERVICES” as the heading of title V of the Social Security Act \[this subchapter\] as part of the general revision of this subchapter.

#### Subchapter Referred to in Other Sections

This subchapter is referred to in sections 247b–1, 247b–4a, 280c–6, 300w–3, 300z–5, 300ff–71, 671, 1301, 1320a–3, 1320a–7, 1320a–7a, 1320b–19, 1382d, 1396a, 1396b, 1396n, 1396r–1, 1396r–4, 1397d, 14402 of this title; title 7 section 3178; title 8 section 1255a; title 20 sections 1412, 1440.
