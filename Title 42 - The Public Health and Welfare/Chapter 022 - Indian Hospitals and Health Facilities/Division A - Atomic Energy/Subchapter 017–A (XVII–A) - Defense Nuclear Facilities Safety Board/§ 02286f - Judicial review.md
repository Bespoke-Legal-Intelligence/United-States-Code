### §2286f. Judicial review

Chapter 7 of title 5 shall apply to the activities of the Board under this subchapter.

(Aug. 1, 1946, ch. 724, title I, §317, as added Sept. 29, 1988, Pub. L. 100–456, div. A, title XIV, §1441(a)(1), 102 Stat. 2083; renumbered title I, Oct. 24, 1992, Pub. L. 102–486, title IX, §902(a)(8), 106 Stat. 2944.)
