### §17702. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Administrator

&emsp;The term “Administrator” means the Administrator of NASA.

#### (2) NASA

&emsp;The term “NASA” means the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

#### (3) NOAA

&emsp;The term “NOAA” means the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

#### (4) OSTP

&emsp;The term “OSTP” means the Office of Science and Technology Policy.

(Pub. L. 110–422, §3, Oct. 15, 2008, 122 Stat. 4782.)

#### References in Text

This chapter, referred to in text, was in the original “this Act”, meaning Pub. L. 110–422, Oct. 15, 2008, 122 Stat. 4779, which is classified principally to this chapter. For complete classification of this Act to the Code, see Short Title note set out under section 17701 of this title and Tables.
