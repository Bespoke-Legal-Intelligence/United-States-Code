### §6250e. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to implement this part.

(Pub. L. 94–163, title I, §186, as added Pub. L. 106–469, title II, §201(a)(3), Nov. 9, 2000, 114 Stat. 2036; amended Pub. L. 108–7, div. F, title III, §339(a)(2), Feb. 20, 2003, 117 Stat. 278.)

#### Amendments

2003—Pub. L. 108–7 struck out “for fiscal years 2001, 2002, and 2003” after “appropriated”.
