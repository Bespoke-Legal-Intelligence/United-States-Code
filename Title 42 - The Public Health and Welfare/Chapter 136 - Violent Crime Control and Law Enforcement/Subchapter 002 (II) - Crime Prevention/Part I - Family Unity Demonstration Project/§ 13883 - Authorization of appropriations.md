### §13883. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) Authorization

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part—

&emsp;(1) $3,600,000 for fiscal year 1996;

&emsp;(2) $3,600,000 for fiscal year 1997;

&emsp;(3) $3,600,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(4) $3,600,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(5) $5,400,000 for fiscal year 2000.

#### (b) Availability of appropriations

Of the amount appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for any fiscal year—

&emsp;(1) 90 percent shall be available to carry out subpart 1; and

&emsp;(2) 10 percent shall be available to carry out subpart 2.

(Pub. L. 103–322, title III, §31904, Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1894.)

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 13901, 14214 of this title.
