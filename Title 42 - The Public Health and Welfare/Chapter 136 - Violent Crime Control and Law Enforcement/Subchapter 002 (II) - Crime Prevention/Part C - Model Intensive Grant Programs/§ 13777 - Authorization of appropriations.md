### §13777. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part—

&emsp;(1) $100,000,000 for fiscal year 1996;

&emsp;(2) $125,100,000 for fiscal year 1997;

&emsp;(3) $125,100,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(4) $125,100,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(5) $150,200,000 for fiscal year 2000.

(Pub. L. 103–322, title III, §30307, Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1846.)
