### §13802. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under section 13801 of this title—

&emsp;(1) $5,400,000 for fiscal year 1996;

&emsp;(2) $6,300,000 for fiscal year 1997;

&emsp;(3) $7,200,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(4) $8,100,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(5) $9,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.

(Pub. L. 103–322, title III, §30702, Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1856.)
