### §13812. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under section 13811 of this title—

&emsp;(1) $2,000,000 for fiscal year 1996;

&emsp;(2) $4,000,000 for fiscal year 1997;

&emsp;(3) $5,000,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(4) $6,000,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(5) $7,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.

(Pub. L. 103–322, title III, §30802, Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1858.)
