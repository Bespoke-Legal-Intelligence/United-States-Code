### §13709. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part—

&emsp;(1) $175,000,000 for fiscal year 1995;

&emsp;(2) $750,000,000 for fiscal year 1996;

&emsp;(3) $1,000,000,000 for fiscal year 1997;

&emsp;(4) $1,900,000,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(5) $2,000,000,000 for fiscal year 1999; and

&emsp;(6) $2,070,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.

(Pub. L. 103–322, title II, §20109, Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1818.)
