### §9811. Limitation on assistance

No financial assistance shall be provided under this part unless the Secretary determines that—

&emsp;(1) any cooperative association receiving assistance has a minimum of fifteen active members, a majority of which are low-income rural persons;

&emsp;(2) adequate technical assistance is made available and committed to the programs being supported;

&emsp;(3) such financial assistance will materially further the purposes of this part; and

&emsp;(4) the applicant is fulfilling or will fulfill a need for services, supplies, or facilities which is otherwise not being met.

(Pub. L. 97–35, title VI, §622, Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 494.)
