### §16981. Pilot program for monitoring sexual offenders

#### (a) Sex offender monitoring program

#### (1) Grants authorized

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Attorney General is authorized to award grants (referred to as “Jessica Lunsford and Sarah Lunde Grants”) to States, local governments, and Indian tribal governments to assist in—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) carrying out programs to outfit sex offenders with electronic monitoring units; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the employment of law enforcement officials necessary to carry out such programs.

#### (B) Duration

&emsp;&emsp;The Attorney General shall award grants under this section for a period not to exceed 3 years.

#### (C) Minimum standards

&emsp;&emsp;The electronic monitoring units used in the pilot program shall at a minimum—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) provide a tracking device for each offender that contains a central processing unit with global positioning system; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) permit continuous monitoring of offenders 24 hours a day.

#### (2) Application

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;Each State, local government, or Indian tribal government desiring a grant under this section shall submit an application to the Attorney General at such time, in such manner, and accompanied by such information as the Attorney General may reasonably require.

#### (B) Contents

&emsp;&emsp;Each application submitted pursuant to subparagraph (A) shall—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) describe the activities for which assistance under this section is sought; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) provide such additional assurances as the Attorney General determines to be essential to ensure compliance with the requirements of this section.

#### (b) Innovation

In making grants under this section, the Attorney General shall ensure that different approaches to monitoring are funded to allow an assessment of effectiveness.

#### (c) Authorization of appropriations

#### (1) In general

&emsp;There are authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 2007 through 2009 to carry out this section.

#### (2) Report

&emsp;Not later than September 1, 2010, the Attorney General shall report to Congress—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) assessing the effectiveness and value of this section;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) comparing the cost effectiveness of the electronic monitoring to reduce sex offenses compared to other alternatives; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) making recommendations for continuing funding and the appropriate levels for such funding.

(Pub. L. 109–248, title VI, §621, July 27, 2006, 120 Stat. 633; Pub. L. 110–400, §4(a), Oct. 13, 2008, 122 Stat. 4227.)

#### Amendments

2008—Subsec. (a)(1)(C). Pub. L. 110–400, §4(a), amended subpar. (C) generally. Prior to amendment, subpar. (C) set minimum standards for electronic monitoring units used in the pilot program.

#### Effective Date of 2008 Amendment

Pub. L. 110–400, §4(b), Oct. 13, 2008, 122 Stat. 4228, provided that: “The amendment made by subsection (a) \[amending this section\] shall apply to grants provided on or after the date of the enactment of this Act \[Oct. 13, 2008\].”
