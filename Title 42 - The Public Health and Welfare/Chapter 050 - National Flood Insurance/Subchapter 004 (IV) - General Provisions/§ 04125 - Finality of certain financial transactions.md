### §4125. Finality of certain financial transactions

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law—

&emsp;(1) any financial transaction authorized to be carried out under this chapter, and

&emsp;(2) any payment authorized to be made or to be received in connection with any such financial transaction,

> shall be final and conclusive upon all officers of the Government.

(Pub. L. 90–448, title XIII, §1374, Aug. 1, 1968, 82 Stat. 589.)
