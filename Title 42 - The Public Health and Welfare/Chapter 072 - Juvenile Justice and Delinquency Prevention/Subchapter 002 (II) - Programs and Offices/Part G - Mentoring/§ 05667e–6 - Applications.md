### §5667e–6. Applications

An application for assistance under this part shall include—

&emsp;(1) information on the youth expected to be served by the program;

&emsp;(2) a provision for a mechanism for matching youth with mentors based on the needs of the youth;

&emsp;(3) an assurance that no mentor will be assigned to more than one youth, so as to ensure a one-to-one relationship;

&emsp;(4) an assurance that projects operated in secondary schools will provide youth with a variety of experiences and support, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an opportunity to spend time in a work environment and, when possible, participate in the work environment;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) an opportunity to witness the job skills that will be required for youth to obtain employment upon graduation;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) assistance with homework assignments; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) exposure to experiences that youth might not otherwise encounter;

&emsp;(5) an assurance that projects operated in elementary schools will provide youth with—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) academic assistance;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) exposure to new experiences and activities that youth might not encounter on their own; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) emotional support;

&emsp;(6) an assurance that projects will be monitored to ensure that each youth benefits from a mentor relationship, with provision for a new mentor assignment if the relationship is not beneficial to the youth;

&emsp;(7) the method by which mentors and youth will be recruited to the project;

&emsp;(8) the method by which prospective mentors will be screened; and

&emsp;(9) the training that will be provided to mentors.

(Pub. L. 93–415, title II, §288F, as added Pub. L. 102–586, §2(i)(1)(C), Nov. 4, 1992, 106 Stat. 5011.)
