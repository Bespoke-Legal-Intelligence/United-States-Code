### §5667e–1. Definitions

For purposes of this part—

&emsp;(1) the term “at-risk youth” means a youth at risk of educational failure or dropping out of school or involvement in delinquent activities; and

&emsp;(2) the term “mentor” means a person who works with an at-risk youth on a one-to-one basis, establishing a supportive relationship with the youth and providing the youth with academic assistance and exposure to new experiences that enhance the youth's ability to become a responsible citizen.

(Pub. L. 93–415, title II, §288A, as added Pub. L. 102–586, §2(i)(1)(C), Nov. 4, 1992, 106 Stat. 5009.)
