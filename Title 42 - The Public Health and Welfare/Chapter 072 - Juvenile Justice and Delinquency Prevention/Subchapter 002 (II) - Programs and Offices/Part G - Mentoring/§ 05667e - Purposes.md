### §5667e. Purposes

The purposes of this part are—

&emsp;(1) to reduce juvenile delinquency and gang participation;

&emsp;(2) to improve academic performance; and

&emsp;(3) to reduce the dropout rate,

> through the use of mentors for at-risk youth.

(Pub. L. 93–415, title II, §288, as added Pub. L. 102–586, §2(i)(1)(C), Nov. 4, 1992, 106 Stat. 5009.)
