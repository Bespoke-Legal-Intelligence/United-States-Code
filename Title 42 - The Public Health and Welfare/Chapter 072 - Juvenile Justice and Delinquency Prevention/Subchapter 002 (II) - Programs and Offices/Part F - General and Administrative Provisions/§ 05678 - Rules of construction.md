### §5678. Rules of construction

Nothing in this subchapter or subchapter I of this chapter shall be construed—

&emsp;(1) to prevent financial assistance from being awarded through grants under this subchapter to any otherwise eligible organization; or

&emsp;(2) to modify or affect any Federal or State law relating to collective bargaining rights of employees.

(Pub. L. 93–415, title II, §299G, as added Pub. L. 107–273, div. C, title II, §12217, Nov. 2, 2002, 116 Stat. 1893.)

#### Effective Date

Section effective Nov. 2, 2002, and applicable only with respect to fiscal years beginning after Sept. 30, 2002, see section 12223 of Pub. L. 107–273, set out as an Effective Date of 2002 Amendment note under section 5601 of this title.
