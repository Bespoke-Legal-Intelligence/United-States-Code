### §5116g. National network for community-based family resource programs

The Secretary may allocate such sums as may be necessary from the amount provided under the State allotment to support the activities of the lead entity in the State—

&emsp;(1) to create, operate and maintain a peer review process;

&emsp;(2) to create, operate and maintain an information clearinghouse;

&emsp;(3) to fund a yearly symposium on State system change efforts that result from the operation of the Statewide networks of community-based, prevention-focused, family resource and support programs;

&emsp;(4) to create, operate and maintain a computerized communication system between lead entities; and

&emsp;(5) to fund State-to-State technical assistance through bi-annual conferences.

(Pub. L. 93–247, title II, §208, as added Pub. L. 104–235, title I, §121, Oct. 3, 1996, 110 Stat. 3086.)
