### SUBCHAPTER III—COMMUNITY-BASED FAMILY RESOURCE AND SUPPORT GRANTS

#### Codification

Subchapter is comprised of title II of the Child Abuse Prevention and Treatment Act, Pub. L. 93–247. Title I of that Act is classified to subchapter I (§5101 et seq.) of this chapter.

#### Subchapter Referred to in Other Sections

This subchapter is referred to in sections 5101, 5105, 5106a, 5106d, 5106e, 5106i of this title; title 31 section 6703.
