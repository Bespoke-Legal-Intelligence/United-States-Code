### §5116e. Local program requirements

#### (a) In general

Grants made under this subchapter shall be used to develop, implement, operate, expand and enhance community-based, prevention-focused, family resource and support programs that—

&emsp;(1) assess community assets and needs through a planning process that involves parents and local public agencies, local nonprofit organizations, and private sector representatives;

&emsp;(2) develop a strategy to provide, over time, a continuum of preventive, family centered services to children and families, especially to young parents and parents with young children, through public-private partnerships;

&emsp;(3) provide—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) core family resource and support services such as—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) parent education, mutual support and self help, and leadership services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) outreach services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) community and social service referrals; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) follow-up services;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) other core services, which must be provided or arranged for through contracts or agreements with other local agencies, including all forms of respite care services to the extent practicable; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) access to optional services, including—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) referral to and counseling for adoption services for individuals interested in adopting a child or relinquishing their child for adoption;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) child care, early childhood development and intervention services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) referral to services and supports to meet the additional needs of families with children with disabilities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) referral to job readiness services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) referral to educational services, such as scholastic tutoring, literacy training, and General Educational Degree services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) self-sufficiency and life management skills training;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) community referral services, including early developmental screening of children; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) peer counseling;

&emsp;(4) develop leadership roles for the meaningful involvement of parents in the development, operation, evaluation, and oversight of the programs and services;

&emsp;(5) provide leadership in mobilizing local public and private resources to support theprovision of needed family resource and support program services; and

&emsp;(6) participate with other community-based, prevention-focused, family resource and support program grantees in the development, operation and expansion of the Statewide network.

#### (b) Priority

In awarding local grants under this subchapter, a lead entity shall give priority to effective community-based programs serving low income communities and those serving young parents or parents with young children, including community-based family resource and support programs.
