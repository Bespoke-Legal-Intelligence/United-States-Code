### §3750. Name of programs

The grant programs established under this subchapter shall be known as the “EdwardByrne Memorial State and Local Law Enforcement Assistance Programs”.

(Pub. L. 90–351, title I, §500, as added Pub. L. 100–690, title VI, §6091(a), Nov. 18, 1988, 102 Stat. 4329.)
