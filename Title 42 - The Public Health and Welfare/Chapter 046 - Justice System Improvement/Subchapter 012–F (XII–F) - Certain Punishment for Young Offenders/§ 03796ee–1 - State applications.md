### §3796ee–1. State applications

#### (a) In general

#### (1) Submission of application

&emsp;To request a grant under this subchapter, the chief executive of a State shall submit anapplication to the Attorney General in such form and containing such information as the Attorney General may reasonably require.

#### (2) Assurances

&emsp;An application under paragraph (1) shall include assurances that Federal funds received under this subchapter shall be used to supplement, not supplant, non-Federal funds that would otherwise be available for activities funded under this subchapter.

#### (b) State office

The office designated under section 3757 of this title—

&emsp;(1) shall prepare the application as required under subsection (a) of this section; and

&emsp;(2) shall administer grant funds received under this subchapter, including review of spending, processing, progress, financial reporting, technical assistance, grant adjustments, accounting, auditing, and fund disbursement.

(Pub. L. 90–351, title I, §1802, as added Pub. L. 103–322, title II, §20201(a)(3), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1820.)
