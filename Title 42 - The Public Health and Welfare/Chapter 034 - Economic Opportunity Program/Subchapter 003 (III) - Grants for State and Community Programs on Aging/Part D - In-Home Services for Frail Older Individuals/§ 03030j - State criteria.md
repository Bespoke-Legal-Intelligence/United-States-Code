### §3030j. State criteria

The State agency shall develop eligibility criteria for providing in-home services to frailolder individuals which shall take into account—

&emsp;(1) age;

&emsp;(2) greatest economic need;

&emsp;(3) noneconomic factors contributing to the frail condition; and

&emsp;(4) noneconomic and nonhealth factors contributing to the need for such services.

(Pub. L. 89–73, title III, §343, as added Pub. L. 100–175, title I, §140(d), Nov. 29, 1987, 101 Stat. 945.)
