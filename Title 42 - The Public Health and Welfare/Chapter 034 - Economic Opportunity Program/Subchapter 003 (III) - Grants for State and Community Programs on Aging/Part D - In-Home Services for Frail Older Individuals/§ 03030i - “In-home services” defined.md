### §3030i. “In-home services” defined

For purposes of this part, the term “in-home services” includes—

&emsp;(1) homemaker and home health aides;

&emsp;(2) visiting and telephone reassurance;

&emsp;(3) chore maintenance;

&emsp;(4) in-home respite care for families, and adult day care as a respite service for families;

&emsp;(5) minor modification of homes that is necessary to facilitate the ability of older individuals to remain at home and that is not available under other programs, except that not more than $150 per client may be expended under this part for such modification;

&emsp;(6) personal care services; and

&emsp;(7) other in-home services as defined—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) by the State agency in the State plan submitted in accordance with section 3027 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) by the area agency on aging in the area plan submitted in accordance with section 3026 of this title.

(Pub. L. 89–73, title III, §342, as added Pub. L. 100–175, title I, §140(d), Nov. 29, 1987, 101 Stat. 945; amended Pub. L. 102–375, title I, §102(b)(7), title III, §318, Sept. 30, 1992, 106 Stat. 1201, 1241.)

#### Amendments

1992—Pub. L. 102–375 amended section catchline, struck out par. (1) designation, redesignated subpars (A) to (E) as pars. (1) to (5), respectively, and realigned their margins, struck out former par. (2) which defined “frail”, and added pars. (6) and (7).
