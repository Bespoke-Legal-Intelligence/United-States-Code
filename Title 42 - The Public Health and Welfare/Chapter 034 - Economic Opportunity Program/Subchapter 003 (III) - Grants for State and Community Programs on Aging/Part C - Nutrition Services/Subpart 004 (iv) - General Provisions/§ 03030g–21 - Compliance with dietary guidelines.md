### §3030g–21. Compliance with dietary guidelines

A State that establishes and operates a nutrition project under this part shall ensure that the meals provided through the project—

&emsp;(1) comply with the Dietary Guidelines for Americans, published by the Secretary and the Secretary of Agriculture; and

&emsp;(2) provide to each participating older individual—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a minimum of 331/3 percent of the daily recommended dietary allowances as established by the Food and Nutrition Board of the National Research Council of the National Academy of Sciences, if the project provides 1 meal per day;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a minimum of 662/3 percent of the allowances if the project provides 2 meals per day; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) 100 percent of the allowances if the project provides 3 meals per day.

(Pub. L. 89–73, title III, §339, as added Pub. L. 102–375, title III, §317, Sept. 30, 1992, 106 Stat. 1241.)
