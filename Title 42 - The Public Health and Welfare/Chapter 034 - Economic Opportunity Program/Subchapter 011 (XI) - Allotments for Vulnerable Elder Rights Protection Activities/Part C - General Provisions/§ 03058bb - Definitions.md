### §3058bb. Definitions

As used in this subchapter:

#### (1) Elder right

&emsp;The term “elder right” means a right of an older individual.

#### (2) Vulnerable elder rights protection activity

&emsp;The term “vulnerable elder rights protection activity” means an activity funded under subpart II, III, IV, or V of this subchapter.<sup>1</sup>

(Pub. L. 89–73, title VII, §761, as added Pub. L. 102–375, title VII, §707, Sept. 30, 1992, 106 Stat. 1291.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “part A of this subchapter.”</i>
