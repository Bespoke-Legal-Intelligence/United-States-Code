### §16252. Education

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Architect of the Capitol shall establish in the Capitol Complex a program of public education regarding use by the Architect of the Capitol of biobased products.

#### (2) Purposes

&emsp;The purposes of the program shall be—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to establish the Capitol Complex as a showcase for the existence and benefits of biobased products; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to provide access to further information on biobased products to occupants and visitors.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title IX, §943(c), Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 881.)
