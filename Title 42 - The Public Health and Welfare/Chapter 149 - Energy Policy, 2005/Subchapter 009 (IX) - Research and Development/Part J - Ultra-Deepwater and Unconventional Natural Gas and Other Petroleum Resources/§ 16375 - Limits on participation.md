### §16375. Limits on participation

An entity shall be eligible to receive an award under this part only if the Secretary finds—

&emsp;(1) that the entity's participation in the program under this part would be in the economic interest of the United States; and

&emsp;(2) that either—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the entity is a United States-owned entity organized under the laws of the United States; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the entity is organized under the laws of the United States and has a parent entity organized under the laws of a country that affords—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) to United States-owned entities opportunities, comparable to those afforded to any other entity, to participate in any cooperative research venture similar to those authorized under this part;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) to United States-owned entities local investment opportunities comparable to those afforded to any other entity; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) adequate and effective protection for the intellectual property rights of United States-owned entities.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title IX, §999E, Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 923.)
