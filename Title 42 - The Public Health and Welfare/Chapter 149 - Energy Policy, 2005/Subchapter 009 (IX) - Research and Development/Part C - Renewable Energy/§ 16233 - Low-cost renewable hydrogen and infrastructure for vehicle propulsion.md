### §16233. Low-cost renewable hydrogen and infrastructure for vehicle propulsion

The Secretary shall—

&emsp;(1) establish a research, development, and demonstration program to determine the feasibility of using hydrogen propulsion in light-weight vehicles and the integration of the associated hydrogen production infrastructure using off-the-shelf components; and

&emsp;(2) identify universities and institutions that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) have expertise in researching and testing vehicles fueled by hydrogen, methane, and other fuels;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) have expertise in integrating off-the-shelf components to minimize cost; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) within 2 years can test a vehicle based on an existing commercially available platform with a curb weight of not less than 2,000 pounds before modifications, that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) operates solely on hydrogen;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) qualifies as a light-duty passenger vehicle; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) uses hydrogen produced from water using only solar energy.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title IX, §933, Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 872.)
