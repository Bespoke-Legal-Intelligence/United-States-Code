### §16132. National grant and loan programs

#### (a) In general

The Administrator shall use 70 percent of the funds made available to carry out this part for each fiscal year to provide grants and low-cost revolving loans, as determined by the Administrator, on a competitive basis, to eligible entities to achieve significant reductions in diesel emissions in terms of—

&emsp;(1) tons of pollution produced; and

&emsp;(2) diesel emissions exposure, particularly from fleets operating in areas designated by the Administrator as poor air quality areas.

#### (b) Distribution

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Administrator shall distribute funds made available for a fiscal year under this part in accordance with this section.

#### (2) Fleets

&emsp;The Administrator shall provide not less than 50 percent of funds available for a fiscal year under this section to eligible entities for the benefit of public fleets.

#### (3) Engine configurations and technologies

#### (A) Certified engine configurations and verified technologies

&emsp;&emsp;The Administrator shall provide not less than 90 percent of funds available for a fiscal year under this section to eligible entities for projects using—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a certified engine configuration; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a verified technology.

#### (B) Emerging technologies

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The Administrator shall provide not more than 10 percent of funds available for a fiscal year under this section to eligible entities for the development and commercialization of emerging technologies.

#### (ii) Application and test plan

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;To receive funds under clause (i), a manufacturer, in consultation with an eligible entity, shall submit for verification to the Administrator or the California Air Resources Board a test plan for the emerging technology, together with the application under subsection (c).

#### (c) Applications

#### (1) In general

&emsp;To receive a grant or loan under this section, an eligible entity shall submit to the Administrator an application at a time, in a manner, and including such information as the Administrator may require.

#### (2) Inclusions

&emsp;An application under this subsection shall include—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a description of the air quality of the area served by the eligible entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the quantity of air pollution produced by the diesel fleets in the area served by the eligible entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a description of the project proposed by the eligible entity, including—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) any certified engine configuration, verified technology, or emerging technology to be used or funded by the eligible entity; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the means by which the project will achieve a significant reduction in diesel emissions;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) an evaluation (using methodology approved by the Administrator or the National Academy of Sciences) of the quantifiable and unquantifiable benefits of the emissions reductions of the proposed project;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) an estimate of the cost of the proposed project;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) a description of the age and expected lifetime control of the equipment used or funded by the eligible entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) a description of the diesel fuel available in the areas to be served by the eligible entity, including the sulfur content of the fuel; and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) provisions for the monitoring and verification of the project.

#### (3) Priority

&emsp;In providing a grant or loan under this section, the Administrator shall give priority to proposed projects that, as determined by the Administrator—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) maximize public health benefits;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) are the most cost-effective;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) serve areas—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) with the highest population density;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) that are poor air quality areas, including areas identified by the Administrator as—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) in nonattainment or maintenance of national ambient air quality standards for a criteria pollutant;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) Federal Class I areas; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) areas with toxic air pollutant concerns;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) that receive a disproportionate quantity of air pollution from a <sup>1</sup> diesel fleets, including truckstops, ports, rail yards, terminals, and distribution centers; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) that use a community-based multistakeholder collaborative process to reduce toxic emissions;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) include a certified engine configuration, verified technology, or emerging technology that has a long expected useful life;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) will maximize the useful life of any certified engine configuration, verified technology, or emerging technology used or funded by the eligible entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) conserve diesel fuel; and

&emsp;&emsp;(G) use diesel fuel with a sulfur content of less than or equal to 15 parts per million, as the Administrator determines to be appropriate.

#### (d) Use of funds

#### (1) In general

&emsp;An eligible entity may use a grant or loan provided under this section to fund the costs of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a retrofit technology (including any incremental costs of a repowered or new diesel engine) that significantly reduces emissions through development and implementation of a certified engine configuration, verified technology, or emerging technology for—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a bus;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a medium-duty truck or a heavy-duty truck;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) a marine engine;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) a locomotive; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) a nonroad engine or vehicle used in—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) construction;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) handling of cargo (including at a port or airport);

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) agriculture;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) mining; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(V) energy production; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) programs or projects to reduce long-duration idling using verified technology involving a vehicle or equipment described in subparagraph (A).

#### (2) Regulatory programs

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;Notwithstanding paragraph (1), no grant or loan provided under this section shall be used to fund the costs of emissions reductions that are mandated under Federal, State or local law.

#### (B) Mandated

&emsp;&emsp;For purposes of subparagraph (A), voluntary or elective emission reduction measures shall not be considered “mandated”, regardless of whether the reductions are included in the State implementation plan of a State.

(Pub. L. 109–58, title VII, §792, Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 839.)
