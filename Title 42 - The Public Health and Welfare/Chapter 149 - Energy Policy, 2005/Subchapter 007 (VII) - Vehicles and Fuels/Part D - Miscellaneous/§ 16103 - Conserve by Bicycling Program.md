### §16103. Conserve by Bicycling Program

#### (a) Definitions

In this section:

#### (1) Program

&emsp;The term “program” means the Conserve by Bicycling Program established by subsection (b).

#### (2) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Transportation.

#### (b) Establishment

There is established within the Department of Transportation a program to be known as the “Conserve by Bicycling Program”.

#### (c) Projects

#### (1) In general

&emsp;In carrying out the program, the Secretary shall establish not more than 10 pilot projects that are—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) dispersed geographically throughout the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) designed to conserve energy resources by encouraging the use of bicycles in place of motor vehicles.

#### (2) Requirements

&emsp;A pilot project described in paragraph (1) shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) use education and marketing to convert motor vehicle trips to bicycle trips;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) document project results and energy savings (in estimated units of energy conserved);

&emsp;&emsp;(C) facilitate partnerships among interested parties in at least 2 of the fields of—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) transportation;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) law enforcement;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) education;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) public health;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) environment; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) energy;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) maximize bicycle facility investments;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) demonstrate methods that may be used in other regions of the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) facilitate the continuation of ongoing programs that are sustained by local resources.

#### (3) Cost sharing

&emsp;At least 20 percent of the cost of each pilot project described in paragraph (1) shall be provided from non-Federal sources.

#### (d) Energy and bicycling research study

#### (1) In general

&emsp;Not later than 2 years after August 8, 2005, the Secretary shall enter into a contract with the National Academy of Sciences for, and the National Academy of Sciences shall conduct and submit to Congress a report on, a study on the feasibility of converting motor vehicle trips to bicycle trips.

#### (2) Components

&emsp;The study shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) document the results or progress of the pilot projects under subsection (c);

&emsp;&emsp;(B) determine the type and duration of motor vehicle trips that people in the United States may feasibly make by bicycle, taking into consideration factors such as—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) weather;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) land use and traffic patterns;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) the carrying capacity of bicycles; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) bicycle infrastructure;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) determine any energy savings that would result from the conversion of motor vehicle trips to bicycle trips;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) include a cost-benefit analysis of bicycle infrastructure investments; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) include a description of any factors that would encourage more motor vehicle trips to be replaced with bicycle trips.

#### (e) Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this section $6,200,000, to remain available until expended, of which—

&emsp;(1) $5,150,000 shall be used to carry out pilot projects described in subsection (c);

&emsp;(2) $300,000 shall be used by the Secretary to coordinate, publicize, and disseminate the results of the program; and

&emsp;(3) $750,000 shall be used to carry out subsection (d).

(Pub. L. 109–58, title VII, §755, Aug. 8, 2005, 119 Stat. 828.)
