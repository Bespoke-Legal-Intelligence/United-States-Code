### §11211. Establishment

#### (a) Composition

There is established in the Department of Health and Human Services (hereinafter referred to as the “Department”) the Council on Alzheimer's Disease (hereinafter referred to as the “Council”). The Council shall be composed of—

&emsp;(1) the Assistant Secretary for Health;

&emsp;(2) the Surgeon General of the Public Health Service;

&emsp;(3) the Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation;

&emsp;(4) the Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases;

&emsp;(5) the Director of the National Institute of Mental Health;

&emsp;(6) the Director of the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke;

&emsp;(7) the Director of the National Institute on Aging;

&emsp;(8) the Assistant Secretary for Aging;

&emsp;(9) the Administrator of the Health Care Financing Administration (or the designee of such Administrator);

&emsp;(10) the Administrator of the Agency for Health Care Policy and Research;

&emsp;(11) the Administrator of the Health Resources and Services Administration;

&emsp;(12) the Director of the National Center for Nursing Research;

&emsp;(13) the Chief Medical Director of the Department of Veterans Affairs;

&emsp;(14) the Director of the National Center for Health Statistics; and

&emsp;(15) such additional members as the Secretary of Health and Human Services (hereinafter referred to as the “Secretary”) considers appropriate.

#### (b) Chairman

The Assistant Secretary for Health shall serve as the Chairman of the Council.

#### (c) Quorum

A majority of the members of the Council shall constitute a quorum, but a lesser number may hold hearings.

#### (d) Meetings

The Council shall meet periodically at the call of the Chairman, but not less than once each year.

#### (e) Appointment of Executive Secretary; administrative staff and support

The Secretary shall appoint an Executive Secretary for the Council and shall provide the Council with such additional administrative staff and support as may be necessary to enable the Council to carry out its functions.
