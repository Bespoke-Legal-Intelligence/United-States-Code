### §299c–6. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “Administrator” means the Administrator for Health Care Policy and Research.

&emsp;(2) The term “Agency” means the Agency for Health Care Policy and Research.

&emsp;(3) The term “Council” means the National Advisory Council on Health Care Policy, Research, and Evaluation.

&emsp;(4) The term “Director” means the Director of the Office of the Forum for Quality and Effectiveness in Health Care.

(July 1, 1944, ch. 373, title IX, §927, as added Dec. 19, 1989, Pub. L. 101–239, title VI, §6103(c), 103 Stat. 2204.)
