### §1308. Limitation on payments to Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands, Guam, and American Samoa

#### (a) Total amount certified under subchapters I, X, XIV, XVI and parts A and E of subchapter IV

The total amount certified by the Secretary of Health and Human Services under subchapters I, X, XIV, and XVI of this chapter, and under parts A and E of subchapter IV of this chapter (exclusive of any amounts on account of services and items to which subsection (b) of this section or, in the case of part A of subchapter IV of this chapter, section 603(k) of this title applies)—

&emsp;(1) for payment to Puerto Rico shall not exceed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $12,500,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1968,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $15,000,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1969,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $18,000,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1970,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) $21,000,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1971,

&emsp;&emsp;(E) $24,000,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1972 through 1978,

&emsp;&emsp;(F) $72,000,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1979 through 1988, or

&emsp;&emsp;(G) $82,000,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1989 and each fiscal year thereafter;

&emsp;(2) for payment to the Virgin Islands shall not exceed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $425,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1968,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $500,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1969,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $600,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1970,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) $700,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1971,

&emsp;&emsp;(E) $800,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1972 through 1978,

&emsp;&emsp;(F) $2,400,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1979 through 1988, or

&emsp;&emsp;(G) $2,800,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1989 and each fiscal year thereafter;

&emsp;(3) for payment to Guam shall not exceed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $575,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1968,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $690,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1969,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) $825,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1970,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) $960,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1971,

&emsp;&emsp;(E) $1,100,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1972 through 1978,

&emsp;&emsp;(F) $3,300,000 with respect to each of the fiscal years 1979 through 1988, or

&emsp;&emsp;(G) $3,800,000 with respect to the fiscal year 1989 and each fiscal year thereafter.

> Each jurisdiction specified in this subsection may use in its program under subchapter XX of this chapter any sums available to it under this subsection which are not needed to carry out the programs specified in this subsection.

#### (b) Total amount certified for family planning services and manpower services

The total amount certified by the Secretary under part A of subchapter IV of this chapter, on account of family planning services with respect to any fiscal year—

&emsp;(1) for payment to Puerto Rico shall not exceed $2,000,000,

&emsp;(2) for payment to the Virgin Islands shall not exceed $65,000, and

&emsp;(3) for payment to Guam shall not exceed $90,000.

#### (c) Total amount certified under subchapter XIX

The total amount certified by the Secretary under subchapter XIX of this chapter with respect to a fiscal year for payment to—

&emsp;(1) Puerto Rico shall not exceed (A) $116,500,000 for fiscal year 1994 and (B) for each succeeding fiscal year the amount provided in this paragraph for the preceding fiscal year increased by the percentage increase in the medical care component of the consumer price index for all urban consumers (as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics) for the twelve-month period ending in March preceding the beginning of the fiscal year, rounded to the nearest $100,000;

&emsp;(2) the Virgin Islands shall not exceed (A) $3,837,500 for fiscal year 1994, and (B) for each succeeding fiscal year the amount provided in this paragraph for the preceding fiscal year increased by the percentage increase referred to in paragraph (1)(B), rounded to the nearest $10,000;

&emsp;(3) Guam shall not exceed (A) $3,685,000 for fiscal year 1994, and (B) for each succeeding fiscal year the amount provided in this paragraph for the preceding fiscal year increased by the percentage increase referred to in paragraph (1)(B), rounded to the nearest $10,000;

&emsp;(4) Northern Mariana Islands shall not exceed (A) $1,110,000 for fiscal year 1994, and (B) for each succeeding fiscal year the amount provided in this paragraph for the preceding fiscal year increased by the percentage increase referred to in paragraph (1)(B), rounded to the nearest $10,000; and

&emsp;(5) American Samoa shall not exceed (A) $2,140,000 for fiscal year 1994, and (B) for each succeeding fiscal year the amount provided in this paragraph for the preceding fiscal year increased by the percentage increase referred to in paragraph (1)(B), rounded to the nearest $10,000.

#### (d) Limitation on payments to American Samoa

The total amount certified by the Secretary under parts A and E of subchapter IV of this chapter with respect to a fiscal year for payment to American Samoa (exclusive of any amounts on account of services and items to which, in the case of part A of such subchapter, section 603(k) of this title applies) shall not exceed $1,000,000.

#### (e) Allotment of smaller amounts

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 621 of this title, and until such time as the Congress may by appropriation or other law otherwise provide, the Secretary shall, in lieu of the initial allotment specified in such sections, allot such smaller amounts to Guam, American Samoa, and the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands as he may deem appropriate.
