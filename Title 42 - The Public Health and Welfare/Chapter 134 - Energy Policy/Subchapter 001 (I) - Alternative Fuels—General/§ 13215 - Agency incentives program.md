### §13215. Agency incentives program

#### (a) Reduction in rates

To encourage and promote use of alternative fueled vehicles in Federal agencies, the Administrator of General Services may offer a reduction in fees charged to agencies for the lease of alternative fueled vehicles below those feescharged for the lease of comparable conventionally fueled motor vehicles.

#### (b) Sunset provision

This section shall cease to be effective 3 years after October 24, 1992.

(Pub. L. 102–486, title III, §306, Oct. 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 2873.)
