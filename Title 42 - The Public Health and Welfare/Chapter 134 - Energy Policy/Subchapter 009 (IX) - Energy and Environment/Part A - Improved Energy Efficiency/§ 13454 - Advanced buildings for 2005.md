### §13454. Advanced buildings for 2005

#### (a) Program direction

The Secretary shall initiate a 5-year program, in accordance with sections 13541 and 13542 of this title, to increase building energy efficiency, while maintaining affordability, by the year 2005. Such program shall include activities on—

&emsp;(1) building design, design methods, and construction techniques;

&emsp;(2) building materials, including recycled materials, and components;

&emsp;(3) on-site energy supply conversion systems such as photovoltaics;

&emsp;(4) automated energy management systems;

&emsp;(5) methods of evaluating performance; and

&emsp;(6) insulation products manufactured with nonozone depleting materials.

#### (b) Proposals

#### (1) Solicitation

&emsp;Within 1 year after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall solicit proposals for conducting activities under this section.

#### (2) Contents of proposals

&emsp;Proposals submitted under this subsection shall include and be judged upon—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) evidence of knowledge of current building practices in the United States and in other countries;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) an explanation of how the proposal will encourage the commercialization of thetechnologies resulting from activities in subsection (a) of this section;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) evidence of consideration of collaboration with Department of Energy national laboratories;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) evidence of collaboration with relevant industry or other groups or organizations; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) a demonstration of the ability of the proposers to undertake and complete the project proposed.

(Pub. L. 102–486, title XXI, §2104, Oct. 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 3069.)
