### §13553. Energy subsidy study

#### (a) In general

The Secretary shall contract with the National Academy of Sciences to conduct a study of energy subsidies that—

&emsp;(1) are in effect on October 24, 1992; or

&emsp;(2) have been in effect prior to October 24, 1992.

#### (b) Report to Congress

Not later than 18 months after October 24, 1992, the Secretary shall transmit to the Congress,<sup>1</sup> the results of such study to be accompanied by recommendations for legislation, if any.

#### (c) Contents

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The study shall identify and quantify the direct and indirect subsidies and other legal andinstitutional factors that influence decisions in the marketplace concerning fuels and energy technologies.

#### (2) Topics for examination

&emsp;The study shall examine—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) fuel and technology choices that are—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) available on October 24, 1992; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) reasonably foreseeable on October 24, 1992;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) production subsidies for the extraction of raw materials;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) subsidies encouraging investment in large capital projects;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) indemnification;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) fuel cycle subsidies, including waste disposal;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) government research and development support; and

&emsp;&emsp;(G) other relevant incentives and disincentives.

#### (d) Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $500,000 for each of the fiscal years 1993 and 1994.

(Pub. L. 102–486, title XXX, §3015, Oct. 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 3129.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. The comma probably should not appear.</i>
