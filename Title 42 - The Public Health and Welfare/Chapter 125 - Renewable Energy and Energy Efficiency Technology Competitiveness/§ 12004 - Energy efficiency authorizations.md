### §12004. Energy efficiency authorizations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for the following energy efficiency research, development, and demonstration programs: transportation, industrial, buildings and community systems, multi-sector, and policy and management—

&emsp;(1) not to exceed $201,100,000 for fiscal year 1991, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not to exceed $68,300,000 shall be available for the transportation program; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not to exceed $53,500,000 shall be available for the industrial program; and

&emsp;(2) not to exceed $210,600,000 for fiscal year 1992, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not to exceed $71,000,000 shall be available for the transportation program; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not to exceed $54,700,000 shall be available for the industrial program.

(Pub. L. 101–218, §5, Dec. 11, 1989, 103 Stat. 1862; Pub. L. 102–486, title XXI, §2125(4)–(6), Oct. 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 3085.)
