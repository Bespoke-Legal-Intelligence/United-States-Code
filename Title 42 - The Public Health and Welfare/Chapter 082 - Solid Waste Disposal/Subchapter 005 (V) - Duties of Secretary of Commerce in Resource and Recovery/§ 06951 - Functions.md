### §6951. Functions

The Secretary of Commerce shall encourage greater commercialization of proven resource recovery technology by providing—

&emsp;(1) accurate specifications for recovered materials;

&emsp;(2) stimulation of development of markets for recovered materials;

&emsp;(3) promotion of proven technology; and

&emsp;(4) a forum for the exchange of technical and economic data relating to resource recovery facilities.

(Pub. L. 89–272, title II, §5001, as added Pub. L. 94–580, §2, Oct. 21, 1976, 90 Stat. 2820.)
