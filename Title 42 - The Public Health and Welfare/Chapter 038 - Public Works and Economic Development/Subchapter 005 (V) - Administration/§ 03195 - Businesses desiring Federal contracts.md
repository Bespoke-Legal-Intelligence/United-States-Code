### §3195. Businesses desiring Federal contracts

The Secretary may provide the procurement divisions of Federal agencies with a list consisting of—

&emsp;(1) the names and addresses of businesses that are located in areas described in section 3161(a) of this title and that wish to obtain Federal Government contracts for the provision of supplies or services; and

&emsp;(2) the supplies and services that each business provides.

(Pub. L. 89–136, title V, §505, as added Pub. L. 105–393, title I, §102(a), Nov. 13, 1998, 112 Stat. 3610.)
