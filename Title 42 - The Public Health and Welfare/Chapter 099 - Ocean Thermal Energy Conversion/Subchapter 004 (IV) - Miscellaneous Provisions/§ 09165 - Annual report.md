### §9165. Annual report

Within 6 months after the end of each fiscal year after August 3, 1980, the Administrator shall submit to the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives a report on the administration of this chapter during such fiscal year. Such report shall include, with respect to the fiscal year covered by the report—

&emsp;(1) a description of progress in implementing this chapter;

&emsp;(2) a list of all licenses issued, suspended, revoked, relinquished, surrendered, terminated, renewed, or transferred; denials of issuance of licenses; and required suspensions and modifications of activities under licenses;

&emsp;(3) a description of ocean thermal energy conversion activities undertaken pursuant to licenses;

&emsp;(4) the number and description of all civil and criminal proceedings instituted under subchapter III of this chapter, and the current status of such proceedings; and

&emsp;(5) such recommendations as the Administrator deems appropriate for amending this chapter.

(Pub. L. 96–320, title IV, §405, Aug. 3, 1980, 94 Stat. 999; Pub. L. 98–623, title VI, §602(c), Nov. 8, 1984, 98 Stat. 3411.)

#### Amendments

1984—Pub. L. 98–623 substituted “each fiscal year” for “each of the first 3 fiscal years” in provisions preceding par. (1).
