### §1962d–14. Authorization of appropriations for Alaskan water resources investigation

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated not more than $250,000 in any one fiscal year.

(Aug. 9, 1955, ch. 682, §3, 69 Stat. 618.)

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of the Water Resources Planning Act which comprises this chapter.

Section was formerly classified to section 487b of Title 48, Territories and Insular Possessions.
