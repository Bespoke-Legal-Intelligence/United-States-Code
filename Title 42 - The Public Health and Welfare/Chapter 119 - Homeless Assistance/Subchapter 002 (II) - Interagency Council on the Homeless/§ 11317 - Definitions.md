### §11317. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term “Council” means the Interagency Council on the Homeless established in section 11311 of this title.

&emsp;(2) The term “Federal agency” has the meaning given the term “agency” in section 551(1) of title 5.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title II, §207, July 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 489.)
