### §11317. Definitions

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term "Council" means the United States Interagency Council on Homelessness established in section 11311 of this title.

&emsp;(2) The term "Federal agency" has the meaning given the term "agency" in section 551(1) of title 5.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title II, §207, July 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 489; Pub. L. 108–199, div. G, title II, §216(4), Jan. 23, 2004, 118 Stat. 394.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### Amendments
