### §11402. Environmental review

For purposes of environmental review, assistance and projects under this subchapter shall betreated as assistance for special projects that are subject to section 3547 of this title, and shall be subject to the regulations issued by the Secretary to implement such section.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title IV, §443, as added Pub. L. 100–628, title IV, §482(a), Nov. 7, 1988, 102 Stat. 3238; amended Pub. L. 106–377, §1(a)(1) \[title II, §208\], Oct. 27, 2000, 114 Stat. 1441, 1441A–25.)

#### Amendments

2000—Pub. L. 106–377 amended section catchline and text generally. Prior to amendment, text read as follows: “The provisions of, and regulations and procedures applicable under, section 5304(g) of this title shall apply to assistance and projects under this subchapter.”
