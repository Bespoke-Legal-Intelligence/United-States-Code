### §11403h. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

For purposes of the housing programs under this part, there are authorized to be appropriated $266,550,000 for fiscal year 1993 and $277,745,100 for fiscal year 1994. Of any amount appropriated in any fiscal year to carry out this part—

&emsp;(1) not less than 10 percent shall be available only for carrying out subpart II of this part;

&emsp;(2) not less than 10 percent shall be available only for carrying out subpart III of this part;

&emsp;(3) not less than 10 percent shall be available only for carrying out subpart IV of this part; and

&emsp;(4) not less than 10 percent shall be available only for carrying out subpart V of this part.

#### (b) Availability

Sums appropriated under this section shall remain available until expended.
