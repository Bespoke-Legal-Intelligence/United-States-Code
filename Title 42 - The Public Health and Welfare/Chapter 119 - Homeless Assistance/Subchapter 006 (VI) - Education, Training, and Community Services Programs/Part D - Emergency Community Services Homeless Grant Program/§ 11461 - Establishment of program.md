### §11461. Establishment of program

The Secretary of Health and Human Services (in this part referred to as the “Secretary”) shall carry out an emergency community services homeless grant program through the Officeof Community Services of the Department of Health and Human Services.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title VII, §751, July 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 532.)
