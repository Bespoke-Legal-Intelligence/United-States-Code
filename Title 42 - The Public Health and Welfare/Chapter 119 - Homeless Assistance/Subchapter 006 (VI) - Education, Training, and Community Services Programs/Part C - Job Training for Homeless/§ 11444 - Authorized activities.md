### §11444. Authorized activities

Demonstration grants under this part may be used for—

&emsp;(1) basic skills instruction;

&emsp;(2) remedial education activities;

&emsp;(3) basic literacy instruction;

&emsp;(4) job search activities;

&emsp;(5) job counseling;

&emsp;(6) job preparatory training, including resume writing and interviewing skills; and

&emsp;(7) any other activities described in section 1604 of title 29 which the grant recipient determines will contribute to carrying out the objectives of this part;

> for homeless individuals.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title VII, §734, July 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 529.)

#### Termination of Section

<i>For termination of section on Dec. 31, 1999, see section 11450 of this title.</i>
