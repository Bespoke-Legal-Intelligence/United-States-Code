### §11442. State coordination with demonstration grant recipients

A State shall describe in the comprehensive plan required under section 11361 of this title how the State will coordinate projects conducted within a State under this part with other services for homeless individuals assisted under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 100–77, title VII, §732, July 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 528.)

#### Termination of Section

<i>For termination of section on Dec. 31, 1999, see section 11450 of this title.</i>
