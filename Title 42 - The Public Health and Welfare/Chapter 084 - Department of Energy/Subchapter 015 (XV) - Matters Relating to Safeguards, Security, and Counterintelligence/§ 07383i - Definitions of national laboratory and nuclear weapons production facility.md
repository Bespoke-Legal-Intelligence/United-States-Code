### §7383i. Definitions of national laboratory and nuclear weapons production facility

For purposes of this subchapter:

&emsp;(1) The term "national laboratory" means any of the following:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) The Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Livermore, California.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) The Los Alamos National Laboratory, Los Alamos, New Mexico.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) The Sandia National Laboratories, Albuquerque, New Mexico and Livermore, California.

&emsp;(2) The term "nuclear weapons production facility" means any of the following:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) The Kansas City Plant, Kansas City, Missouri.

&emsp;&emsp;(B) The Pantex Plant, Amarillo, Texas.

&emsp;&emsp;(C) The Y–12 Plant, Oak Ridge, Tennessee.

&emsp;&emsp;(D) The tritium operations at the Savannah River Site, Aiken, South Carolina.

&emsp;&emsp;(E) The Nevada Test Site, Nevada.

(Pub. L. 106–65, div. C, title XXXI, §3155, Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 942.)
