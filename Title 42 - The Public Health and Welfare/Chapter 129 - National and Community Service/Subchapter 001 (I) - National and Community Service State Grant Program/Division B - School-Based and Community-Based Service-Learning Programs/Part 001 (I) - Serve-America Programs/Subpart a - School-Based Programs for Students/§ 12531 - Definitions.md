### §12531. Definitions

As used in this subpart:

#### (1) Grantmaking entity

&emsp;The term “grantmaking entity” means an organization described in section 12523(a) of this title.

#### (2) School-based

&emsp;The term “school-based” means based in an elementary school or a secondary school.

#### (3) Student

&emsp;Notwithstanding section 12511(29) of this title, the term “student” means an individual who is enrolled in an elementary or secondary school on a full- or part-time basis.

(Pub. L. 101–610, title I, §116B, as added Pub. L. 103–82, title I, §103(a)(2), Sept. 21, 1993, 107 Stat. 833.)

#### Prior Provisions
