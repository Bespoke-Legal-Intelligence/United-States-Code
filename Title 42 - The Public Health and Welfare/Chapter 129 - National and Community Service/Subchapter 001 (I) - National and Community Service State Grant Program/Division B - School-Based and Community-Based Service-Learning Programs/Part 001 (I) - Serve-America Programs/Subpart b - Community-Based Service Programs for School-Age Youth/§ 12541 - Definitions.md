### §12541. Definitions

As used in this subpart:

#### (1) Community-based service program

&emsp;The term “community-based service program” means a program described in section 12542(b)(1)(A) of this title.

#### (2) Grantmaking entity

&emsp;The term “grantmaking entity” means a qualified organization that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) submits an application under section 12544(a) of this title to make grants to qualified organizations;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) was in existence at least 1 year before the date on which the organization submitted the application; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) meets such other criteria as the Chief Executive Officer shall establish.

#### (3) Qualified organization

&emsp;The term “qualified organization” means a public or private nonprofit organization with experience working with school-age youth that meets such criteria as the Chief Executive Officer may establish.

(Pub. L. 101–610, title I, §117, as added Pub. L. 103–82, title I, §103(a)(2), Sept. 21, 1993, 107 Stat. 833.)

#### Prior Provisions
