### §12653c. Military installation conversion demonstration programs

#### (a) Purposes

The purposes of this section are to—

&emsp;(1) provide meaningful service opportunities for economically disadvantaged youth;

&emsp;(2) fully utilize military installations affected by closures or realignments;

&emsp;(3) encourage communities affected by such closures or realignments to convert the installations to community use; and

&emsp;(4) foster a sense of community pride in the youth in the community.

#### (b) Definitions

As used in this section:

#### (1) Affected military installation

&emsp;The term “affected military installation” means a military installation being closed or realigned under—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Defense Base Closure and Realignment Act of 1990 (part A of title XXIX of division B of Public Law 101–510; 10 U.S.C. 2687 note); and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) title II of the Defense Authorization Amendments and Base Closure and Realignment Act (Public Law 100–526; 10 U.S.C. 2687 note).

#### (2) Community

&emsp;The term “community” includes a county.

#### (3) Convert to community use

&emsp;The term “convert to community use”, used with respect to an affected military installation, includes—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) conversion of the installation or a part of the installation to—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a park;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a community center;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) a recreational facility; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) a facility for a Head Start program under the Head Start Act (42 U.S.C. 9831 et seq.); and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) carrying out, at the installation, a construction or economic development project that is of substantial benefit, as determined by the Chief Executive Officer, to—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the community in which the installation is located; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a community located within such distance of the installation as the Chief Executive Officer may determine by regulation to be appropriate.

#### (4) Demonstration program

&emsp;The term “demonstration program” means a program described in subsection (c) of this section.

#### (c) Demonstration programs

#### (1) Grants

&emsp;The Corporation may make grants to communities and community-based agencies topay for the Federal share of establishing and carrying out military installation conversion demonstration programs, to assist in converting to community use affected military installations located—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) within the community; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) within such distance from the community as the Chief Executive Officer may by regulation determine to be appropriate.

#### (2) Duration

&emsp;In carrying out such a demonstration program, the community or community-based agency may carry out—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a program of not less than 6 months in duration; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a full-time summer program.

#### (d) Use of funds

#### (1) Stipend

&emsp;A community or community-based agency that receives a grant under subsection (c) of this section to establish and carry out a project through a demonstration program may use the funds made available through such grant to pay for a portion of a stipend for the participants in the project.

#### (2) Limitation on amount of stipend

&emsp;The amount of the stipend provided to a participant under paragraph (1) that may be paid using assistance provided under this section and using any other Federal funds shall not exceed the lesser of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 85 percent of the total average annual subsistence allowance provided to VISTA volunteers under section 4955 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) 85 percent of the stipend established by the demonstration program involved.

#### (e) Participants

#### (1) Eligibility

&emsp;A person shall be eligible to be selected as a participant in a project carried out through a demonstration program if the person is—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an economically disadvantaged individual; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B)(i) a person described in section 12613(b) of this title;

&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a youth described in section 12614(a) of this title; or

&emsp;&emsp;(iii) an individual described in section 2884 of title 29.

#### (2) Participation

&emsp;Persons desiring to participate in such a project shall enter into an agreement with the service sponsor of the project to participate—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) on a full-time or a part-time basis; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) for the duration referred to in subsection (f)(2)(C) of this section.

#### (f) Application

#### (1) In general

&emsp;To be eligible to receive a grant under subsection (c) of this section, a community or community-based agency shall submit an application to the Chief Executive Officer at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Chief Executive Officer may require.

#### (2) Contents

&emsp;At a minimum, such application shall contain—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a description of the demonstration program proposed to be conducted by the applicant;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a proposal for carrying out the program that describes the manner in which the applicant will—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) provide preservice and inservice training, for supervisors and participants, that will be conducted by qualified individuals or qualified organizations;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) conduct an appropriate evaluation of the program; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) provide for appropriate community involvement in the program;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) information indicating the duration of the program; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) an assurance that the applicant will comply with the nonduplication and nondisplacement provisions of section 12637 of this title and the grievance procedure requirements of section 12636(f) of this title.

#### (g) Limitation on grant

In making a grant under subsection (c) of this section with respect to a demonstration program to assist in converting an affected military installation, the Corporation shall not make a grant for more than 25 percent of the total cost of the conversion.
