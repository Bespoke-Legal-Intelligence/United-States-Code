### Division E—Civilian Community Corps

#### Codification

Subtitle E of title I of Pub. L. 101–610, comprising this division, was formerly classified to part H (§12653 et seq.) of this subchapter prior to amendment by Pub. L. 103–82, §104(b).

#### Prior Provisions

This division is comprised of subtitle E, §§151–166, of title I of Pub. L. 101–610. A prior part E (§12591 et seq.), comprised of subtitle E, §§155–167, of title I of Pub. L. 101–610, related to innovative and demonstration programs and projects, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 103–82, §104(a).

#### Division Referred to in Other Sections

This division is referred to in sections 12573, 12581, 12681 of this title.
