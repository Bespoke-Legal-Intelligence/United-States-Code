### §3426. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration—

&emsp;(1) for fiscal year 2009—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $13,750,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $5,500,000 for the National Technology Institute;

&emsp;(2) for fiscal year 2010—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $15,125,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $6,050,000 for the National Technology Institute;

&emsp;(3) for fiscal year 2011—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $16,638,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $6,655,000 for the National Technology Institute;

&emsp;(4) for fiscal year 2012—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $18,301,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $7,321,000 for the National Technology Institute;

&emsp;(5) for fiscal year 2013—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $20,131,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $8,053,000 for the National Technology Institute;

&emsp;(6) for fiscal year 2014—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $22,145,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $8,859,000 for the National Technology Institute; and

&emsp;(7) for fiscal year 2015—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $24,359,000 for the regional centers, of which 50 percent shall be for West Coast regional centers and 50 percent shall be for East Coast regional centers; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $9,744,000 for the National Technology Institute.

(Pub. L. 111–11, title XII, §12107, Mar. 30, 2009, 123 Stat. 1420.)
