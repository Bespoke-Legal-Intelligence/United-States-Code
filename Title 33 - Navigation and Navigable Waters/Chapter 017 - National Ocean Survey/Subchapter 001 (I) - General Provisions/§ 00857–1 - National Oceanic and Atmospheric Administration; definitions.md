### §857–1. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration; definitions

Definitions listed in section 101 of title 10 apply to this Act, except as noted below:

&emsp;(1) “active duty” means full-time duty in the active service of a uniformed service;

&emsp;(2) “Administration” means the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration;

&emsp;(3) “grade” means a step or degree, in a graduated scale of office or rank, that is established and designated as a grade by law or regulation;

&emsp;(4) “officer” means a commissioned officer;

&emsp;(5) “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce;

&emsp;(6) “Secretary concerned” is defined in section 101 of title 37;

&emsp;(7) “uniformed services” is defined in section 101 of title 37.

(Pub. L. 91–621, §1, Dec. 31, 1970, 84 Stat. 1863.)
