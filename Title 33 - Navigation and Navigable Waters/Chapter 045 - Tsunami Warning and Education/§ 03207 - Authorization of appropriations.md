### §3207. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator to carry out this chapter—

&emsp;(1) $25,000,000 for fiscal year 2008, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 27 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami hazard mitigation program under section 3204 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 8 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami research program under section 3205 of this title;

&emsp;(2) $26,000,000 for fiscal year 2009, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 27 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami hazard mitigation program under section 3204 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 8 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami research program under section 3205 of this title;

&emsp;(3) $27,000,000 for fiscal year 2010, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 27 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami hazard mitigation program under section 3204 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 8 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami research program under section 3205 of this title;

&emsp;(4) $28,000,000 for fiscal year 2011, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 27 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami hazard mitigation program under section 3204 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 8 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami research program under section 3205 of this title; and

&emsp;(5) $29,000,000 for fiscal year 2012, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) not less than 27 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami hazard mitigation program under section 3204 of this title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not less than 8 percent of the amount appropriated shall be for the tsunami research program under section 3205 of this title.

(Pub. L. 109–424, §8, Dec. 20, 2006, 120 Stat. 2908; Pub. L. 109–479, title VIII, §808, Jan. 12, 2007, 120 Stat. 3660.)

#### Codification

Pub. L. 109–424 and title VIII of Pub. L. 109–479 enacted substantially identical sections. This section is based on the text of section 808 of Pub. L. 109–479.
