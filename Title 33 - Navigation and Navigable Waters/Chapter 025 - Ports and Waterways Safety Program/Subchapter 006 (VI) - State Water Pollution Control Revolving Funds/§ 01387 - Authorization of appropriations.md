### §1387. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of this subchapter the following sums:

&emsp;(1) $1,200,000,000 per fiscal year for each of fiscal years 1989 and 1990;

&emsp;(2) $2,400,000,000 for fiscal year 1991;

&emsp;(3) $1,800,000,000 for fiscal year 1992;

&emsp;(4) $1,200,000,000 for fiscal year 1993; and

&emsp;(5) $600,000,000 for fiscal year 1994.

(June 30, 1948, ch. 758, title VI, §607, as added Feb. 4, 1987, Pub. L. 100–4, title II, §212(a), 101 Stat. 26.)
