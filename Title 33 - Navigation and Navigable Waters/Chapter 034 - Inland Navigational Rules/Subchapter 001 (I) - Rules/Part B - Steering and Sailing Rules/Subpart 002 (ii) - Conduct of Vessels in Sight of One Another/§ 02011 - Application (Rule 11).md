### §2011. Application (Rule 11)

Rules in this subpart apply to vessels in sight of one another.

(Pub. L. 96–591, §2, Dec. 24, 1980, 94 Stat. 3420.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Pub. L. 108–293, title III, §303(a), (c), Aug. 9, 2004, 118 Stat. 1042, provided that this section is</i><i>repealed effective on the effective date of final regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard is operating under section 2071 of this title.</i>

#### References in Text

Rules in this subpart, referred to in text, means sections 2011 to 2018 of this title.

#### Codification

Section was enacted as part of section 2 of Pub. L. 96–591 which enacted the Inland Navigational Rules which comprise this subchapter.
