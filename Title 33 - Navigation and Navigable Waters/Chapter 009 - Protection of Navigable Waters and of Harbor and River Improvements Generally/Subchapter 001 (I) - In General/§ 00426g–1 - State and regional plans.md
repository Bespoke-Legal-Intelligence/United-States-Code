### §426g–1. State and regional plans

The Secretary may—

&emsp;(1) cooperate with any State in the preparation of a comprehensive State or regional plan for the conservation of coastal resources located within the boundaries of the State;

&emsp;(2) encourage State participation in the implementation of the plan; and

&emsp;(3) submit to Congress reports and recommendations with respect to appropriate Federal participation in carrying out the plan.

(Aug. 13, 1946, ch. 960, §4, as added Oct. 12, 1996, Pub. L. 104–303, title II, §227(d)(2), 110 Stat. 3700.)
