### §467i. Statutory construction

Nothing in this subchapter and no action or failure to act under this subchapter shall—

&emsp;(1) create any liability in the United States or its officers or employees for the recovery of damages caused by such action or failure to act;

&emsp;(2) relieve an owner or operator of a dam of the legal duties, obligations, or liabilities incident to the ownership or operation of the dam; or

&emsp;(3) preempt any other Federal or State law.

(Pub. L. 92–367, §11, as added Pub. L. 104–303, title II, §215(c)(8), Oct. 12, 1996, 110 Stat. 3693.)
