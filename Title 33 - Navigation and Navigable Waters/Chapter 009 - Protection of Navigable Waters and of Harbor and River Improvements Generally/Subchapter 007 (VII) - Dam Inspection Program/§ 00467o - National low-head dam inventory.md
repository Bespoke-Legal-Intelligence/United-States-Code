### §467<i>o</i>. National low-head dam inventory

#### (a) Definitions

In this section:

#### (1) Inventory

&emsp;The term "inventory" means the national low-head dam inventory developed under subsection (b)(1)(A).

#### (2) Low-head dam

&emsp;The term "low-head dam" means a river-wide artificial barrier that generally spans a stream channel, blocking the waterway and creating a backup of water behind the barrier, with a drop off over the wall of not less than 6 inches and not more than 25 feet.

#### (b) National low-head dam inventory

#### (1) In general

&emsp;Not later than 18 months after December 23, 2022, the Secretary of the Army, in consultation with the heads of appropriate Federal and State agencies, shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) develop an inventory of low-head dams in the United States that includes—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the location, ownership, description, current use, condition, height, and length of each low-head dam;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) any information on public safety conditions at each low-head dam;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) public safety information on the dangers of low-head dams;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) a directory of financial and technical assistance resources available to reduce safety hazards and fish passage barriers at low-head dams; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) any other relevant information concerning low-head dams; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) submit the inventory to the Committee on Environment and Public Works of the Senate and the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of Representatives.

#### (2) Data

&emsp;In carrying out this subsection, the Secretary shall—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) coordinate with Federal and State agencies and other relevant entities; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) use data provided to the Secretary by those agencies and entities.

#### (3) Public availability

&emsp;The Secretary shall make the inventory publicly available, including on a publicly available website.

#### (4) Updates

&emsp;The Secretary, in consultation with the heads of appropriate Federal and State agencies, shall maintain and periodically publish updates to the inventory.

#### (c) Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this section $30,000,000.

#### (d) Clarification

Nothing in this section provides authority to the Secretary to carry out an activity, with respect to a low-head dam, that is not explicitly authorized under this section.

(Pub. L. 92–367, §15, as added Pub. L. 117–263, div. H, title LXXXI, §8122, Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 3713.)
