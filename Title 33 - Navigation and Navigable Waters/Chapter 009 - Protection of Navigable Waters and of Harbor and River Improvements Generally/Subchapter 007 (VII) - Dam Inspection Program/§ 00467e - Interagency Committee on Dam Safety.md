### §467e. Interagency Committee on Dam Safety

#### (a) Establishment

There is established an Interagency Committee on Dam Safety—

&emsp;(1) comprised of a representative of each of the Department of Agriculture, the Department of Defense, the Department of Energy, the Department of the Interior, the Department of Labor, FEMA, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, the Tennessee Valley Authority, and the United States Section of the International Boundary Commission; and

&emsp;(2) chaired by the Director.

#### (b) Duties

ICODS shall encourage the establishment and maintenance of effective Federal and State programs, policies, and guidelines intended to enhance dam safety for the protection of human life and property through—

&emsp;(1) coordination and information exchange among Federal agencies and State dam safety agencies; and

&emsp;(2) coordination and information exchange among Federal agencies concerning implementation of the Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety.

(Pub. L. 92–367, §7, as added Pub. L. 104–303, title II, §215(c)(8), Oct. 12, 1996, 110 Stat. 3687.)
