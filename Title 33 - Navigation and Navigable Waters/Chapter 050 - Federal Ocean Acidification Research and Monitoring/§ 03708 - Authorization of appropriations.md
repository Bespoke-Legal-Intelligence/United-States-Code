### §3708. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) NOAA

There are authorized to be appropriated to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration to carry out the purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) $8,000,000 for fiscal year 2009;

&emsp;(2) $12,000,000 for fiscal year 2010;

&emsp;(3) $15,000,000 for fiscal year 2011; and

&emsp;(4) $20,000,000 for fiscal year 2012.

#### (b) NSF

There are authorized to be appropriated to the National Science Foundation to carry out the purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) $6,000,000 for fiscal year 2009;

&emsp;(2) $8,000,000 for fiscal year 2010;

&emsp;(3) $12,000,000 for fiscal year 2011; and

&emsp;(4) $15,000,000 for fiscal year 2012.

(Pub. L. 111–11, title XII, §12409, Mar. 30, 2009, 123 Stat. 1441.)
