### §3904. Eligible entities

The following entities are eligible to receive assistance under this chapter:

&emsp;(1) A corporation.

&emsp;(2) A partnership.

&emsp;(3) A joint venture.

&emsp;(4) A trust.

&emsp;(5) A Federal, State, or local governmental entity, agency, or instrumentality.

&emsp;(6) A tribal government or consortium of tribal governments.

&emsp;(7) A State infrastructure financing authority.

(Pub. L. 113–121, title V, §5025, June 10, 2014, 128 Stat. 1334.)
