### §3610. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Commerce to support the integrated oceans observations under this chapter—

&emsp;(1) $48,000,000 for fiscal year 2021;

&emsp;(2) $50,000,000 for fiscal year 2022;

&emsp;(3) $52,000,000 for fiscal year 2023;

&emsp;(4) $54,000,000 for fiscal year 2024; and

&emsp;(5) $56,000,000 for fiscal year 2025.

(Pub. L. 111–11, title XII, §12311, Mar. 30, 2009, 123 Stat. 1436; Pub. L. 116–271, title I, §108, Dec. 31, 2020, 134 Stat. 3341.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### Amendments

<b>2020</b>—Pub. L. 116–271 amended section generally. Prior to amendment, section related to authorization of appropriations for fiscal years 2009 to 2013.
