### §2102. Establishment of standards

Based on the best scientific information available, artificial reefs in waters covered under thischapter shall be sited and constructed, and subsequently monitored and managed in a manner which will—

&emsp;(1) enhance fishery resources to the maximum extent practicable;

&emsp;(2) facilitate access and utilization by United States recreational and commercial fishermen;

&emsp;(3) minimize conflicts among competing uses of waters covered under this chapter and the resources in such waters;

&emsp;(4) minimize environmental risks and risks to personal health and property; and

&emsp;(5) be consistent with generally accepted principles of international law and shall not create any unreasonable obstruction to navigation.

(Pub. L. 98–623, title II, §203, Nov. 8, 1984, 98 Stat. 3395.)
