### §3005. Authorized number for fiscal years 2003 through 2005

There are authorized to be on the lineal list of the commissioned corps of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration—

&emsp;(1) 270 officers for fiscal year 2003;

&emsp;(2) 285 officers for fiscal year 2004; and

&emsp;(3) 299 officers for fiscal year 2005.

(Pub. L. 107–372, title II, §215, Dec. 19, 2002, 116 Stat. 3084.)

#### Prior Provisions

Provisions similar to those in this section were contained in section 853a of this title prior to repeal by Pub. L. 107–372.
