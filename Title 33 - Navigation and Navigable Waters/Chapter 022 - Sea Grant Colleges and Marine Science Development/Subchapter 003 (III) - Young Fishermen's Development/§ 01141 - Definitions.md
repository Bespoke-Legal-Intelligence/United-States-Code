### §1141. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) Sea Grant Institution

&emsp;The term "Sea Grant Institution" means a sea grant college or sea grant institute, as those terms are defined in section 1122 of this title.

#### (2) Tribal organization

&emsp;The term "Tribal organization" has the meaning given the term "tribal organization" in section 5304 of title 25.

#### (3) Young fisherman

&emsp;The term "young fisherman" means an individual who—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) desires to participate in the commercial fisheries of the United States, including the Great Lakes fisheries;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) has worked as a captain, crew member, or deckhand on a commercial fishing vessel for not more than 10 years of cumulative service; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) is a beginning commercial fisherman.

(Pub. L. 116–289, §2, Jan. 5, 2021, 134 Stat. 4886.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### Codification

Section was enacted as part of the Young Fishermen's Development Act, and not as part of titles I and II of Pub. L. 89–454 which comprise this chapter.
