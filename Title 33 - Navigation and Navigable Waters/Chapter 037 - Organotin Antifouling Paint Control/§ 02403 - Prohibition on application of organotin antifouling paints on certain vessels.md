### §2403. Prohibition on application of organotin antifouling paints on certain vessels

#### (a) Prohibition

Subject to section 12(d), and except as provided in subsection (b) of this section, no person in any State may apply to a vessel that is less than 25 meters in length an antifouling paint containing organotin.

#### (b) Exceptions

Subsection (a) of this section shall not prohibit the application of a qualified antifouling paint containing organotin on—

&emsp;(1) the aluminum hull of a vessel that is less than 25 meters in length; or

&emsp;(2) the outboard motor or lower drive unit of a vessel that is less than 25 meters in length.

(Pub. L. 100–333, §4, June 16, 1988, 102 Stat. 606.)

#### References in Text

Section 12(d), referred to in subsec. (a), is section 12(d) of Pub. L. 100–333, which is set out as a note under section 2401 of this title.
