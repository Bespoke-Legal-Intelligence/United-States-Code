### §969. Revalidation

The Secretary, acting through the Commissioner, shall develop and implement—

&emsp;(1) a revalidation process for Tier 2 and Tier 3 participants;

&emsp;(2) a framework based upon objective criteria for identifying participants for periodic revalidation not less frequently than once during each 4-year period following the initial validation; and

&emsp;(3) an annual plan for revalidation that includes—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) performance measures;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) an assessment of the personnel needed to perform the revalidations; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the number of participants that will be revalidated during the following year.

(Pub. L. 109–347, title II, §219, Oct. 13, 2006, 120 Stat. 1913.)
