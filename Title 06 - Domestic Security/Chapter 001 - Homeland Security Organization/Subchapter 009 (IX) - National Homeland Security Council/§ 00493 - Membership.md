### §493. Membership

The members of the Council shall be the following:

&emsp;(1) The President.

&emsp;(2) The Vice President.

&emsp;(3) The Secretary of Homeland Security.

&emsp;(4) The Attorney General.

&emsp;(5) The Secretary of Defense.

&emsp;(6) Such other individuals as may be designated by the President.

(Pub. L. 107–296, title IX, §903, Nov. 25, 2002, 116 Stat. 2258.)
