### §532. Explosives Training and Research Facility

#### (a) Establishment

There is established within the Bureau an Explosives Training and Research Facility at Fort AP Hill, Fredericksburg, Virginia.

#### (b) Purpose

The facility established under subsection (a) shall be utilized to train Federal, State, and local law enforcement officers to—

&emsp;(1) investigate bombings and explosions;

&emsp;(2) properly handle, utilize, and dispose of explosive materials and devices;

&emsp;(3) train canines on explosive detection; and

&emsp;(4) conduct research on explosives.

#### (c) Authorization of appropriations

#### (1) In general

&emsp;There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to establish and maintain the facility established under subsection (a).

#### (2) Availability of funds

&emsp;Any amounts appropriated pursuant to paragraph (1) shall remain available until expended.

(Pub. L. 107–296, title XI, §1114, Nov. 25, 2002, 116 Stat. 2280.)
