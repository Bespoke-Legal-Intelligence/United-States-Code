### §822. Assessment of global catastrophic risk

#### (a) In general

The Secretary and the Administrator shall coordinate an assessment of global catastrophic risk.

#### (b) Coordination

When coordinating the assessment under subsection (a), the Secretary and the Administrator shall coordinate with senior designees of—

&emsp;(1) the Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs;

&emsp;(2) the Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy;

&emsp;(3) the Secretary of State and the Under Secretary of State for Arms Control and International Security;

&emsp;(4) the Attorney General and the Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation;

&emsp;(5) the Secretary of Energy, the Under Secretary of Energy for Nuclear Security, and the Director of Science;

&emsp;(6) the Secretary of Health and Human Services, the Assistant Secretary for Preparedness and Response, and the Assistant Secretary of Global Affairs;

&emsp;(7) the Secretary of Commerce, the Under Secretary of Commerce for Oceans and Atmosphere, and the Under Secretary of Commerce for Standards and Technology;

&emsp;(8) the Secretary of the Interior and the Director of the United States Geological Survey;

&emsp;(9) the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency and the Assistant Administrator for Water;

&emsp;(10) the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration;

&emsp;(11) the Director of the National Science Foundation;

&emsp;(12) the Secretary of the Treasury;

&emsp;(13) the Secretary of Defense, the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Civil Works, and the Chief of Engineers and Commanding General of the Army Corps of Engineers;

&emsp;(14) the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff;

&emsp;(15) the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development;

&emsp;(16) the Secretary of Transportation; and

&emsp;(17) other stakeholders the Secretary and the Administrator determine appropriate.

(Pub. L. 117–263, div. G, title LXXIII, §7303, Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 3685.)
