### §750. Remedial action management program

The Administrator, in coordination with the National Council on Disability and the National Advisory Council, shall establish a remedial action management program to—

&emsp;(1) analyze training, exercises, and real-world events to identify and disseminate lessons learned and best practices;

&emsp;(2) generate and disseminate, as appropriate, after action reports to participants in exercises and real-world events; and

&emsp;(3) conduct remedial action tracking and long-term trend analysis.

(Pub. L. 109–295, title VI, §650, Oct. 4, 2006, 120 Stat. 1428.)
