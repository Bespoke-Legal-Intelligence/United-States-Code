### §1521. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) Agency

&emsp;The term "agency" has the meaning given the term in section 3502 of title 44.

#### (2) Agency information system

&emsp;The term "agency information system" has the meaning given the term in section 149 of this title.

#### (3) Appropriate congressional committees

&emsp;The term "appropriate congressional committees" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Committee on Homeland Security of the House of Representatives.

#### (4) Cybersecurity risk; information system

&emsp;The terms "cybersecurity risk" and "information system" have the meanings given those terms in section 148 of this title.

#### (5) Director

&emsp;The term "Director" means the Director of the Office of Management and Budget.

#### (6) Intelligence community

&emsp;The term "intelligence community" has the meaning given the term in section 3003(4) of title 50.

#### (7) National security system

&emsp;The term "national security system" has the meaning given the term in section 11103 of title 40.

#### (8) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of Homeland Security.

(Pub. L. 114–113, div. N, title II, §222, Dec. 18, 2015, 129 Stat. 2963.)
