### §2152. Intervention in litigation

#### (a) Intervention

The Oversight Board may intervene in any litigation filed against the territorial government.

#### (b) Injunctive relief

#### (1) In general

&emsp;If the Oversight Board intervenes in a litigation under subsection (a), the Oversight Boardmay seek injunctive relief, including a stay of litigation.

#### (2) No independent basis for relief

&emsp;This section does not create an independent basis on which injunctive relief, including a stay of litigation, may be granted.

(Pub. L. 114–187, title II, §212, June 30, 2016, 130 Stat. 577.)
