### §1004. Grant application

#### (a) Application required

A partnership desiring to receive a grant under this part shall submit an application to the Secretary, in such form and providing such information as the Secretary, by regulation, shall require.

#### (b) Contents of application

The application shall include—

&emsp;(1) the partnership agreement described in section 1002 of this title;

&emsp;(2) a listing of the public and private nonprofit secondary school or schools to be involved in the program;

&emsp;(3) a description of the activities and services for which assistance is sought;

&emsp;(4) a description of the programs to be developed and operated by the partnership; and

&emsp;(5) assurances to the Secretary that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the partnership will establish a governing body including one representative of each participant in the partnership;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) Federal funds will provide no more than 70 percent of the cost of the project in the first year, 60 percent of such costs in the second year, and 50 percent of such costs in the third year and any subsequent year;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a local educational agency or institution of higher education receiving funds under this part shall not reduce its combined fiscal effort per student or its aggregate expenditure on education;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) a local educational agency or institution of higher education participating in this partnership shall utilize any Federal funds it shall receive from a grant under this part to supplement, and, to the extent practicable, increase the resources that would, in the absence of such Federal funds, be made available from non-Federal sources for the education of students described in this part; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) in no case shall funds under such a grant be used to supplant non-Federal funds already available.

#### (c) Special rule

The non-Federal share of grants awarded under this part may be in cash or in kind fairly evaluated, including services, supplies or equipment.

#### (d) Waiver

The Secretary may waive the matching requirement described in paragraph (5)(B) for any eligible partnership that demonstrates to the satisfaction of the Secretary a unique hardship that prevents compliance with such matching requirement.
