### §1015c. “Public broadcasting entity” defined

For the purpose of this part, the term “public broadcasting entity” has the same meaning given to such term by section 397(11) of title 47.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title I, §134, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title I, §101, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 466.)
