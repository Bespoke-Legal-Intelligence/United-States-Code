### §1042. Geographical distribution of grants

In making grants under this part, the Secretary shall endeavor to achieve broad and equitable geographical distribution throughout the Nation.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title II, §232, as added Pub. L. 96 374, title II, §201, Oct. 3, 1980, 94 Stat. 1386; amended Pub. L. 102 325, title II, §201, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 471.)
