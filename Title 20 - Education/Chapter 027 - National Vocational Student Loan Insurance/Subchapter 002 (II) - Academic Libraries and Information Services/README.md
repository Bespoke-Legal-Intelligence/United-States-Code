### SUBCHAPTER II—ACADEMIC LIBRARIES AND INFORMATION SERVICES

#### Codification

Title II of the Higher Education Act of 1965, comprising this subchapter, was originally enacted by Pub. L. 89–329, title II, Nov. 8, 1965, 79 Stat. 1224, and amended by Pub. L. 89–752, Nov. 3, 1966, 80 Stat. 1240; Pub. L. 90–575, Oct. 16, 1968, 82 Stat. 1014; Pub. L. 91–230, Apr. 13, 1970, 84 Stat. 121; Pub. L. 92–318, June 23, 1972, 86 Stat. 235; Pub. L. 94–482, Oct. 12, 1976, 90 Stat. 2081; Pub. L. 96–49; Aug. 13, 1979, 93 Stat. 351. Such title is shown herein, however, as having been added by Pub. L. 96–374, title II, §201, Oct. 3, 1980, 94 Stat. 1383, without reference to such intervening amendments because of the extensive revision of title II by Pub. L. 96–374.

#### Subchapter Referred to in Other Sections

This subchapter is referred to in section 1069b of this title.
