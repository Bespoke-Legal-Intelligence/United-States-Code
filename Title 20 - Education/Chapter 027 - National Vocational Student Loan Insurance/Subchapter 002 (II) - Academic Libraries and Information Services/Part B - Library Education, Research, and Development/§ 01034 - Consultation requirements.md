### §1034. Consultation requirements

The Secretary shall consult with the appropriate library and information science professional bodies in the determination of critical needs under section 1032 of this title and in the determination of priorities under section 1033 of this title.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title II, §224, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title II, §201, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 470.)
