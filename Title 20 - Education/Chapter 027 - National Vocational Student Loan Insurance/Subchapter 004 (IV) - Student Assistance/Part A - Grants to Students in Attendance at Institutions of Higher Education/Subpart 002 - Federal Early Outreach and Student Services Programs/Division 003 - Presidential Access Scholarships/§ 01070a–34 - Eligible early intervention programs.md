### §1070a–34. Eligible early intervention programs

#### (a) Participation in trio programs and national early intervention scholarship and partnership programs

Participation in a program authorized under section 1070a–12, 1070a–13, or 1070a–16 of this title, or division 2 of subpart 2 of this part for a 36-month period shall meet the requirement of section 1070a–33(a)(4)(A) of this title.

#### (b) Other eligible early intervention program

Participation in another early intervention program, regardless of sponsorship, for a 36-month period, shall meet the requirements of section 1070a–33(a)(4)(A) of this title if the program—

&emsp;(1) meets the requirements established by the Secretary; and

&emsp;(2) is certified by the Governor as an honors scholars program.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IV, §406D, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title IV, §402(a)(4), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 498.)
