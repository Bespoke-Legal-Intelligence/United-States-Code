### §1132f–8. Use of stock sale proceeds

The proceeds received by the Secretary upon the sale of any shares of the Corporation to the Student Loan Marketing Association or any other person shall be deposited in the general fund of the Treasury.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title VII, §759, as added Pub. L. 99–498, title VII, §701, Oct. 17, 1986, 100 Stat. 1533.)
