### §1132d 2. Apportionment

#### (a) Limitation

Not more than 12.5 percent of the amount of the funds provided for in this part in the form of loans shall be made available to educational institutions within any one State.

#### (b) Priorities

In awarding loans under this part, the Secretary shall give priority—

&emsp;(1) to loans for renovation or reconstruction of undergraduate and graduate academic facilities; and

&emsp;(2) to loans for renovation or reconstruction of older undergraduate and graduate academic facilities that have gone without major renovation or reconstruction for an extended period.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title VII, §733, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title VII, §705, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 750.)
