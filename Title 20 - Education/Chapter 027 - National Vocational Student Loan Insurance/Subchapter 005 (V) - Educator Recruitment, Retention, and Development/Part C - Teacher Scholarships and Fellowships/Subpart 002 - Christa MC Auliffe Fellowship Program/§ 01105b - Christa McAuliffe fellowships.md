### §1105b. Christa McAuliffe fellowships

#### (a) Award distribution and amount

#### (1) Award distribution

&emsp;Each State educational agency receiving a grant under this subpart shall use such funds to award Christa McAuliffe fellowships to public and private school teachers who have been employed as teachers for 8 or more years to enable such teachers to engage in the activities described in subsection (b) of this section.

#### (2) Amount

&emsp;Fellowships shall be in an amount equal to the annual salary the individual would earn in such individual's current place of employment for the award period.

#### (3) Ratable reduction

&emsp;If an individual receives a fellowship award for less than a school year, such fellowship shall be ratably reduced to equal the salary forgone.

#### (4) Duration

&emsp;No Christa McAuliffe fellow may receive an award for 2 consecutive years.

#### (5) Requirement

&emsp;Subject to the repayment provisions of section 1105e of this title, each Christa McAuliffe fellow shall be required to return to a teaching position, in their place of employment prior to receiving the fellowship award, for at least 2 years following such award. The Secretary is authorized, in extraordinary circumstances, to waive or defer all or a portion of the service requirement, or allow fellows to fulfill their service requirement by going into a teaching position in another school or school district within the State or in another State upon approval of the sending and receiving State.

#### (b) Use of fellowships

Each Christa McAuliffe fellowship may be used for—

&emsp;(1) sabbaticals for study, research or academic improvement to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A)(i) improve such teacher's knowledge base in an area of expertise; or

&emsp;&emsp;(ii) learn a new area of expertise;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) increase skills and professional ability; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) enhance the ability of teachers to work with special education populations, including—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) gifted and talented children;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) limited-English proficient children;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) children with disabilities; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) economically and educationally disadvantaged children;

&emsp;(2)(A) consultation with or assistance to other school districts or private school systems; or

&emsp;(B) development of special innovative programs;

&emsp;(3) projects or partnerships that involve the business community and the schools;

&emsp;(4) programs that incorporate the use and the sharing of technologies to help students learn; or

&emsp;(5) expanding or replicating model programs of staff development.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title V, §533, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 684.)
