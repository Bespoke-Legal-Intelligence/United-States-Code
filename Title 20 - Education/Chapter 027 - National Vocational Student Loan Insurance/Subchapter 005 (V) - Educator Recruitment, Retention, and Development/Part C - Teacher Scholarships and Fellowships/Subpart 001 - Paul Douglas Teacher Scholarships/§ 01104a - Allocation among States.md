### §1104a. Allocation among States

#### (a) Allocation

From the sums appropriated for this subpart for any fiscal year, the Secretary shall allocate to any State an amount based on the school-age population in the State compared to the school-age population in all States.

#### (b) Use of census data

For the purpose of this section, the number of persons in a State and in all States shall be determined by the most recently available data from the Bureau of the Census.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title V, §522, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 677.)
