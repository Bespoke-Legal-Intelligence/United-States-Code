### §1104. Purpose; designation

#### (a) Purpose

It is the purpose of this subpart to make available, through grants to the States, scholarships to individuals who are outstanding secondary school graduates and who demonstrate an interest in teaching, in order to enable and encourage those individuals to pursue teaching careers in education at the preschool, elementary or secondary level.

#### (b) Designation

Scholarships awarded under this subpart shall be referred to as the “Paul Douglas Teacher Scholarships”.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §521, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 677.)
