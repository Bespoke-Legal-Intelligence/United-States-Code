### §1104e. Scholarship conditions

Recipients of scholarship assistance under this subpart shall continue to receive such scholarship payments only during such periods that the State agency finds that the recipient is—

&emsp;(1) enrolled as a full-time student in an accredited postsecondary institution;

&emsp;(2) pursuing a course of study leading to teacher certification; and

&emsp;(3) maintaining satisfactory progress as determined by the postsecondary institution the recipient is attending.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §526, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 681.)
