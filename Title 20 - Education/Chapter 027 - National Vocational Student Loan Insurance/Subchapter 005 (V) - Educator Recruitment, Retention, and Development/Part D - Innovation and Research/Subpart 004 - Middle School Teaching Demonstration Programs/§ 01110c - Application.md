### §1110c. Application

#### (a) In general

Each institution of higher education desiring a grant under this subpart shall submit an application to the Secretary at such time, in such manner, and accompanied by such information as the Secretary may reasonably require.

#### (b) Contents

Each application submitted pursuant to subsection (a) of this section shall demonstrate that—

&emsp;(1) the applicant will establish and maintain a program of teacher training or retraining designed to offer specialized preparation for individuals teaching grades 6 through 9;

&emsp;(2) the applicant has designed a program of teacher training or retraining which includes—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a study of adolescent development (including cognitive, social, and emotional) with particular emphasis on early adolescent development;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a study of the influence of institutions such as schools, families, and peer groups in the socialization of adolescents;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) information concerning the organization of schools for students in grades 6 through 9, with particular emphasis on developmentally appropriate school and classroom organization and practices;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) training in at least 2 subject areas and related instructional strategies;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) direct experience through internships in middle grade schools under the guidance of teachers who demonstrate exemplary classroom practices;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) strategies for the prevention and detection of high risk behavior, particularly drug and alcohol abuse, and for the enhancement of self esteem among adolescents;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) a study of effective methods and models of presenting substance abuse information and education to adolescent students; and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) methods of encouraging parental and community involvement with middle schools; and

&emsp;(3) the program will be designed and operated with the active participation of classroom teachers and will include an in-service training component.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §569, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 703.)
