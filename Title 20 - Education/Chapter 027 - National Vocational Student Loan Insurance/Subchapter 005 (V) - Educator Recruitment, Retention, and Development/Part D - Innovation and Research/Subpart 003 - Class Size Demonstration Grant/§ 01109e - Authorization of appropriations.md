### §1109e. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated $3,000,000 for fiscal year 1993, and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years to carry out this subpart.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title V, §565A, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 702.)
