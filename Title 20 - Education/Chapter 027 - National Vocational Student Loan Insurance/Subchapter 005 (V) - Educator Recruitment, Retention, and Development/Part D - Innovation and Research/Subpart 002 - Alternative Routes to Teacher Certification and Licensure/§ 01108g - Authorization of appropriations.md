### §1108g. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subpart $15,000,000 for fiscal year 1993.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §559, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 700.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Section repealed effective July 1, 1995, see Termination Date note set out under section 1108 of this title.</i>

#### Prior Provisions

A prior section 559 of Pub. L. 89 329, title V, as added Pub. L. 99 498, title V, §501(a), Oct. 17, 1986, 100 Stat. 1510, related to Federal administration of State programs and judicial review of Congressional Teacher Scholarship programs and was classified to section 1111h of this title, prior to the general revision of this subchapter by Pub. L. 102 325.
