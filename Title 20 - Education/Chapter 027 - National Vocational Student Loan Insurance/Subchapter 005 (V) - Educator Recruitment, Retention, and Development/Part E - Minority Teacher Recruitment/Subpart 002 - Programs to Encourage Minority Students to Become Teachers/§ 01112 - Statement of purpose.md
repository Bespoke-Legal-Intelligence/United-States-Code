### §1112. Statement of purpose

It is the purpose of the program conducted pursuant to section 1112a of this title to carry out activities designed to—

&emsp;(1) improve recruitment and training opportunities in education for minority individuals, including language minority individuals;

&emsp;(2) increase the number of minority teachers, including language minority teachers, in elementary and secondary schools; and

&emsp;(3) to identify and encourage minority students in the 7th through the 12th grades to aspire to, and to prepare for, careers in elementary and secondary school teaching.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §577, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 708.)
