### §1112a. Partnership grants authorized

#### (a) Authority

The Secretary is authorized to make grants to pay the Federal share of carrying out the purposes of this subpart to a partnership between—

&emsp;(1) one or more institutions of higher education which have a demonstrated record and special expertise in carrying out the purposes of this subpart; and

&emsp;(2)(A) one or more local educational agencies;

&emsp;(B) a State educational agency or a State higher education agency; or

&emsp;(C) community-based organizations.

#### (b) Federal share

The Federal share of each grant awarded under this section shall be 50 percent.

#### (c) Non-Federal share

The non-Federal share of each grant awarded under this section may be in cash or kind fairly evaluated, including planned equipment or services.

#### (d) Administrative costs

Not more than 5 percent of any grant awarded under this section may be used for administrative expenses.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §578, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 708.)
