### §1111e. Special consideration

In awarding grants under this subpart, the State shall give special consideration to—

&emsp;(1) programs designed to identify, recruit, and certify—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) speakers of non-English languages who have been trained as teachers in their home country; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) individuals already employed in a local educational agency; and

&emsp;(2) eligible recipients located in shortage areas as defined in section 1111g of this title.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §576, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 707.)
