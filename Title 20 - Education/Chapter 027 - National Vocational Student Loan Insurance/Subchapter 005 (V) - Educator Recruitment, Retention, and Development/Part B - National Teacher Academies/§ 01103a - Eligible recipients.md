### §1103a. Eligible recipients

#### (a) In general

For the purposes of this part, the term “eligible recipient” means—

&emsp;(1) an institution of higher education;

&emsp;(2) a private nonprofit educational organization of demonstrated effectiveness; or

&emsp;(3) a combination of the institutions or organizations set forth in paragraphs (1) and (2) of this paragraph.

#### (b) Expertise requirements

The Secretary shall only award grants to eligible recipients that have demonstrated expertise in the—

&emsp;(1) subject area of the National Teacher Academy to be established and operated; and

&emsp;(2) in-service training of teachers at the national, State, and local levels.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title V, §512, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 674.)
