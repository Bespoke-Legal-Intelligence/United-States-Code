### SUBCHAPTER VI—INTERNATIONAL EDUCATION PROGRAMS

#### Codification

Title VI of the Higher Education Act of 1965, comprising this subchapter, was originally enacted by Pub. L. 89 329, title VI, Nov. 8, 1965, 79 Stat. 1261; amended Pub. L. 89 752, Nov. 3, 1966, 80 Stat. 1240; Pub. L. 90 575, Oct. 16, 1968, 82 Stat. 1014; Pub. L. 92 318, June 23, 1972, 86 Stat. 235; Pub. L. 94 482, Oct. 12, 1976, 90 Stat. 2081; Pub. L. 95 180, Nov. 15, 1977, 91 Stat. 1372; Pub. L. 96 49, Aug. 13, 1979; 93 Stat. 351. Such title is shown herein, however, as having been added by Pub. L. 96 374, title VI, §601(a), Oct. 3, 1980, 94 Stat. 1464, without reference to such intervening amendments because of the extensive revision of the title's provisions by Pub. L. 96 374.
