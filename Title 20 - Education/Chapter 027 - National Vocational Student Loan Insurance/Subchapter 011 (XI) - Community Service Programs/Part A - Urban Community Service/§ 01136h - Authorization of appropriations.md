### §1136h. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated $20,000,000 for fiscal year 1993 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years to carry out the provisions of this part.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title XI, §1109, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title XI, §1101, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 787.)
