### §1134c. Use of funds

Awards made to institutions or consortia under this part shall be used exclusively to provide direct fellowship aid which may include need-based stipends, room and board costs, transportation costs, and tuition for courses for which credit is given by the institution or consortium as approved by the Secretary.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title IX, §913, as added Pub. L. 102 325, title IX, §901, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 762.)
