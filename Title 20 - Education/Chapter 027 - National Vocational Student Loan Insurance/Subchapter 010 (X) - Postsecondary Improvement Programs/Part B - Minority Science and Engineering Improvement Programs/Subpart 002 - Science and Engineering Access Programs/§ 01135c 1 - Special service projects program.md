### §1135c 1. Special service projects program

The Secretary shall, in accordance with the provisions of this subpart, carry out a program of making grants to institutions of higher education that are designed to provide or improve support to accredited colleges and universities and professional scientific societies for a broad range of activities designed to eliminate or reduce specific barriers to the entry of minorities into science and technology.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title X, §1032, as added Pub. L. 99 498, title X, §1002, Oct. 17, 1986, 100 Stat. 1563.)
