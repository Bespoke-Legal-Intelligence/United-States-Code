### §1135d. Eligibility for grants

Eligibility to receive grants under this part is limited to—

&emsp;(1) public and private nonprofit institutions that are minority institutions (as defined in section 1135d 5(3) of this title);

&emsp;(2) nonprofit science-oriented organizations, professional scientific societies, and all nonprofit, accredited colleges and universities which provide a needed service to a group of eligible minority institutions or which provide in-service training for project directors, scientists, and engineers from eligible minority institutions; and

&emsp;(3) for the purposes of section 1135c 1 of this title, public and private nonprofit institutions that have at least 10 percent minority enrollment.

(Pub. L. 89 329, title X, §1041, as added Pub. L. 99 498, title X, §1002, Oct. 17, 1986, 100 Stat. 1564.)
