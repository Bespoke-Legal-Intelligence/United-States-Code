### §4360. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) Gallaudet University

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1993 through 1997 to carry out the provisions of this chapter, relating to—

&emsp;(1) Gallaudet University,

&emsp;(2) Kendall Demonstration Elementary School, and

&emsp;(3) the model secondary school for individuals who are deaf.

#### (b) National Technical Institute for the Deaf

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1993 through 1997 to carry out the provisions of this chapter relating to the National Technical Institute for the Deaf.

(Pub. L. 99 371, title II, §211, formerly title IV, §411, Aug. 4, 1986, 100 Stat. 794; renumbered title II, §211, and amended Pub. L. 102 421, title I, §§101(b)(5), (6), 140, 151(a)(4), Oct. 16, 1992, 106 Stat. 2151, 2163, 2164; Pub. L. 103 73, title II, §204(i), Aug. 11, 1993, 107 Stat. 735.)

#### Codification

Section is also set out in D.C. Code, §31 1842.11.

#### Prior Provisions

Provisions similar to this section were contained in sections 681, 691g, 693, and 695b of this title prior to repeal by Pub. L. 99 371.
