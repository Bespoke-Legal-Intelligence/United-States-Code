### §2396a. Program authorized

#### (a) In general

The Secretary is authorized to make grants to eligible recipients having applications approved pursuant to section 2396g of this title to establish and operate not more than 10 community education employment centers nationwide.

#### (b) Grant period

Grants awarded under this section may be for a period of 5 years.

(Pub. L. 88 210, title III, §363, as added Pub. L. 101 392, title III, §310, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 794.)
