### §2394e. Definitions

For purposes of this part:

&emsp;(1) The term “articulation agreement” means a commitment to a program designed to provide students with a nonduplicative sequence of progressive achievement leading to competencies in a tech-prep education program.

&emsp;(2) The term “community college”—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) has the meaning provided in section 1141(a) of this title for an institution which provides not less than a 2-year program which is acceptable for full credit toward a bachelor's degree; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) includes tribally controlled community colleges.

&emsp;(3) The term “tech-prep education program” means a combined secondary and postsecondary program which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) leads to an associate degree or 2-year certificate;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) provides technical preparation in at least 1 field of engineering technology, applied science, mechanical, industrial, or practical art or trade, or agriculture, health, or business;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) builds student competence in mathematics, science, and communications (including through applied academics) through a sequential course of study; and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) leads to placement in employment.

&emsp;(4) The terms “institution of higher education” and “higher education” include institutions offering apprenticeship programs of at least 2 years beyond the completion of secondary school.

(Pub. L. 88 210, title III, §347, as added Pub. L. 101 392, title III, §308, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 792.)
