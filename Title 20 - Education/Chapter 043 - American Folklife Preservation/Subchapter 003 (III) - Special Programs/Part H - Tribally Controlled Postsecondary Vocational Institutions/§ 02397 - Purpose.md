### §2397. Purpose

It is the purpose of this part to provide grants for the operation and improvement of tribally controlled postsecondary vocational institutions to ensure continued and expanded educational opportunities for Indian students, and to allow for the improvement and expansion of the physical resources of such institutions.

(Pub. L. 88 210, title III, §382, as added Pub. L. 101 392, title III, §311, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 800.)

#### Short Title

Section 381 of Pub. L. 88 210, as added by Pub. L. 101 392, title III, §311, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 799, provided that: “This part \[part H (§§381 390) of title III of Pub. L. 88 210, enacting this part\] may be cited as the ‘Tribally Controlled Vocational Institutions Support Act of 1990’.”
