### §2395. Statement of purpose

It is the purpose of this part to provide funding to local educational agencies in economically depressed areas for program improvement activities, especially the improvement of facilities and acquisition or leasing of equipment to be used to carry out vocational education programs that receive assistance under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 88–210, title III, §351, as added Pub. L. 101–392, title III, §309, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 792.)

#### Effective Date

Part effective July 1, 1991, see section 702(a) of Pub. L. 101–392, set out as an Effective Date of 1990 Amendment note under section 2301 of this title.
