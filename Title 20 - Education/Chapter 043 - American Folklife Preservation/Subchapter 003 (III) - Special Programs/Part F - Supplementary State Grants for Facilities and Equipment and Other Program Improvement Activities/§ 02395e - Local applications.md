### §2395e. Local applications

Each local educational agency or consortium of such agencies that desires to receive a grant under this part shall submit to the State an application at such time, in such manner, and containing or accompanied by such information as the State may reasonably require.

(Pub. L. 88 210, title III, §356, as added Pub. L. 101 392, title III, §309, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 794.)
