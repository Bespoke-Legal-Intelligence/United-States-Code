### §2419. Model programs of regional training for skilled trades

#### (a) Program authorized

The Secretary is authorized to make grants to regional model centers which provide—

&emsp;(1) training for skilled tradesmen within a region serving several States, and

&emsp;(2) technical assistance for programs which train such tradesmen within a region serving several States.

#### (b) Use of funds

The regional model centers described in subsection (a) of this section shall—

&emsp;(1) provide training and career counseling for skilled tradesmen in areas of skill shortages or projected skilled shortages;

&emsp;(2) provide prejob and apprenticeship training and career counseling in skilled trades;

&emsp;(3) upgrade specialized craft training; and

&emsp;(4) improve the access of women, minorities, economically disadvantaged individuals, individuals with handicaps and ex-criminal offenders to trade occupations and training.

#### (c) Special rule

In awarding grants under this section, and to the extent practicable, the Secretary shall ensure an equitable distribution of funds available under this section to the various skilled trades.

(Pub. L. 88 210, title IV, §419, as added Pub. L. 101 392, title IV, §406(a), Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 819.)
