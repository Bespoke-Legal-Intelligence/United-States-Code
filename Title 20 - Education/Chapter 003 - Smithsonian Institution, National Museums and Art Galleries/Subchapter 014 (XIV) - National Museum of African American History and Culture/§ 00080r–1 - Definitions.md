### §80r–1. Definitions

In this subchapter:

#### (1) Board of Regents

&emsp;The term “Board of Regents” means the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

#### (2) Council

&emsp;The term “Council” means the National Museum of African American History and Culture Council established by section 80r–3 of this title.

#### (3) Museum

&emsp;The term “Museum” means the National Museum of African American History and Culture established by section 80r–2 of this title.

#### (4) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution.

(Pub. L. 108–184, §3, Dec. 16, 2003, 117 Stat. 2676.)
