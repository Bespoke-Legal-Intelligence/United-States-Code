### §9309. Funds for fiscal year 2000

Of the amount authorized to be appropriated by section 301 for operation and maintenance for fiscal year 2000, $3,000,000 shall be available for purposes of carrying out the Troops-to-Teachers Program.

(Pub. L. 106–65, div. A, title XVII, §1709, Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 824.)

#### References in Text

Section 301, referred to in text, is section 301 of Pub. L. 106–65, div. A, title III, Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 556, which is not classified to the Code.
