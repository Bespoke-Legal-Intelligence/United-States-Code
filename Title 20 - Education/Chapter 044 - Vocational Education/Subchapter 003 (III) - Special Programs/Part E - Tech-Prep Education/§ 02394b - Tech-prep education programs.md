### §2394b. Tech-prep education programs

#### (a) General authority

Each grant recipient shall use amounts provided under the grant to develop and operate a 4-year tech-prep education program.

#### (b) Contents of program

Any such program shall—

&emsp;(1) be carried out under an articulation agreement between the participants in the consortium;

&emsp;(2) consist of the 2 years or 4 years of secondary school preceding graduation and 2 years of higher education, or an apprenticeship program of at least 2 years following secondary instruction, with a common core of required proficiency in mathematics, science, communications, and technologies designed to lead to an associate degree or certificate in a specific career field;

&emsp;(3) include the development of tech-prep education program curricula appropriate to the needs of the consortium participants;

&emsp;(4) include in-service training for teachers that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is designed to train teachers to effectively implement tech-prep education curricula;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) provides for joint training for teachers from all participants in the consortium; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) may provide such training in weekend, evening, and summer sessions, institutes or workshops;

&emsp;(5) include training programs for counselors designed to enable counselors to more effectively—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) recruit students for tech-prep education programs;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) ensure that such students successfully complete such programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) ensure that such students are placed in appropriate employment;

&emsp;(6) provide equal access to the full range of technical preparation programs to individuals who are members of special populations, including the development of tech-prep education program services appropriate to the needs of such individuals; and

&emsp;(7) provide for preparatory services which assist all participants in such programs.

#### (c) Additional authorized activities

Each such program may—

&emsp;(1) provide for the acquisition of tech-prep education program equipment; and

&emsp;(2) as part of the program's planning activities, acquire technical assistance from State or local entities that have successfully designed, established and operated tech-prep programs.

(Pub. L. 88–210, title III, §344, as added Pub. L. 101–392, title III, §308, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 790; amended Pub. L. 103–239, title VII, §711(a), May 4, 1994, 108 Stat. 606.)

#### Amendments
