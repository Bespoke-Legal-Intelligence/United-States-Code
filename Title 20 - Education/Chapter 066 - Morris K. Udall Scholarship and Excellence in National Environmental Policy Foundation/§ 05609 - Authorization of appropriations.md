### §5609. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) Trust Fund

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Trust Fund $40,000,000 to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

#### (b) Environmental Dispute Resolution Fund

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Environmental Dispute Resolution Fund established under section 5607a of this title—

&emsp;(1) $4,250,000 for fiscal year 1998, of which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $3,000,000 shall be for capitalization; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) $1,250,000 shall be for operation costs; and

&emsp;(2) $1,250,000 for each of the fiscal years 1999 through 2002 for operation costs.

(Pub. L. 102–259, §13, formerly §11, Mar. 19, 1992, 106 Stat. 84; renumbered §13 and amended Pub. L. 105–156, §§6(a), 8, Feb. 11, 1998, 112 Stat. 9, 12.)

#### Amendments

1998—Pub. L. 105–156, §8, designated existing provisions as subsec. (a), inserted heading, substituted “There is authorized to be appropriated to the Trust Fund” for “There are authorized to be appropriated to the Fund”, and added subsec. (b).

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 5606, 5607a of this title.
