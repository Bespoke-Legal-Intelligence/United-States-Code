### §5609. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Fund<sup>1</sup> $40,000,000 to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

(Pub. L. 102 259, §11, Mar. 19, 1992, 106 Stat. 84.)
