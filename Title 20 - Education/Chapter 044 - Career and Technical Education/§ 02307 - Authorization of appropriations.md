### §2307. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter (other than sections 2324, 2327, and 2328 of this title, and subchapter II) such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2007 through 2012.

(Pub. L. 88–210, §9, as added Pub. L. 109–270, §1(b), Aug. 12, 2006, 120 Stat. 691; amended Pub. L. 115–224, §10, July 31, 2018, 132 Stat. 1576.)

#### Amendment of Section

<i>Pub. L. 115–224, §§4, 10, July 31, 2018, 132 Stat. 1564, 1576, provided that, effective July 1, 2019, this section is amended to read as follows:</i>

#### <i>§2307. Authorization of appropriations</i>

<i>There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter (other than sections 2324 and 2327 of this title)—</i>

&emsp;<i>(1) $1,229,568,538 for fiscal year 2019;</i>

&emsp;<i>(2) $1,246,782,498 for fiscal year 2020;</i>

&emsp;<i>(3) $1,264,237,452 for fiscal year 2021;</i>

&emsp;<i>(4) $1,281,936,777 for fiscal year 2022;</i>

&emsp;<i>(5) $1,299,883,892 for fiscal year 2023; and</i>

&emsp;<i>(6) $1,318,082,266 for fiscal year 2024.</i>

<i>See 2018 Amendment note below.</i>

#### Prior Provisions

A prior section 2307, Pub. L. 88–210, §8, as added Pub. L. 105–332, §1(b), Oct. 31, 1998, 112 Stat. 3083, related to authorization of appropriations, prior to the general amendment of this chapter by Pub. L. 109–270.
