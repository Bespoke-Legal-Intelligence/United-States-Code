### §3902. Definitions

For the purpose of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) The term “area vocational education school” has the same meaning given that term under section 2471(3) <sup>1</sup> of this title..<sup>2</sup>

&emsp;(2) The term “Director” means the Director of the National Science Foundation.

&emsp;(3) The term “elementary school” has the same meaning given that term under section 198(a)(7) <sup>1</sup> of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.

&emsp;(4) The term “Governor” means the chief executive of a State.

&emsp;(5) The term “Foundation” means the National Science Foundation.

&emsp;(6) The term “institution of higher education” has the same meaning given that term by section 1141(a) of this title.

&emsp;(7) The term “local educational agency” has the same meaning given that term under section 198(a)(10) <sup>1</sup> of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.

&emsp;(8) The term “secondary school” has the same meaning given that term under section 198(a)(7) <sup>1</sup> of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.

&emsp;(9) The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Education.

&emsp;(10) The term “State” means each of the several States, the District of Columbia, theCommonwealth of Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands.

&emsp;(11) The term “State agency for higher education” means the State board of higher education or other agency or officer primarily responsible for the State supervision of higher education, or, if there is no such officer or agency, an officer or agency designated for the purpose of this chapter <sup>1</sup> by the Governor or by State law.

&emsp;(12) The term “State educational agency” has the meaning given that term under section 198(a)(17) <sup>1</sup> of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.

(Pub. L. 98–377, §3, Aug. 11, 1984, 98 Stat. 1267; Pub. L. 99–159, title II, §221, Nov. 22, 1985, 99 Stat. 897.)

#### References in Text

Section 2471(3) of this title, referred to in par. (1), was redesignated section 2471(4) of this title by Pub. L. 101–392, title V, §502, Sept. 25, 1990, 104 Stat. 834.

Section 198 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965, referred to in pars. (3), (7), (8), and (12), is section 198 of Pub. L. 89–10, title I, as added by Pub. L. 95–561, title I, §101(a), Nov. 1, 1978, 92 Stat. 2198, which was classified to section 2854 of this title prior to the general amendment of Pub. L. 89–10 by Pub. L. 100–297, Apr. 28, 1988, 102 Stat. 140. For definitions, see section 8801 of this title.

This chapter, referred to in par. (11), was in the original “this title” and has been translated as if the reference was to “this Act” to reflect the probable intent of Congress inasmuch as this section is not part of a title of Pub. L. 98–377.

#### Amendments

1985—Par. (1). Pub. L. 99–159 substituted reference to section 2471(3) of this title for reference to section 195(2) of the Vocational Education Act of 1965.
