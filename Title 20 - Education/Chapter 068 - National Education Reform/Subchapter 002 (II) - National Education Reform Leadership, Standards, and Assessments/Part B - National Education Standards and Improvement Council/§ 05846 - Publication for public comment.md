### §5846. Publication for public comment

#### (a) Transmittal

For the purpose of obtaining public comment through publication in the Federal Register, the Council shall transmit to the Secretary—

&emsp;(1) proposed criteria for certifying voluntary national content standards, voluntary national student performance standards, and State content standards and State performance standards;

&emsp;(2) proposed criteria for certifying voluntary national opportunity-to-learn standards and State opportunity-to-learn standards;

&emsp;(3) proposed criteria for certifying State assessments; and

&emsp;(4) proposed voluntary national content standards, voluntary national student performance standards, and voluntary national opportunity-to-learn standards.

#### (b) Publication

The Secretary shall publish such proposed procedures, standards, and criteria in the Federal Register.

(Pub. L. 103–227, title II, §216, Mar. 31, 1994, 108 Stat. 148.)
