### §6232. Sanctions

#### (a) Termination or suspension of assistance

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Secretaries may terminate or suspend any financial assistance under this chapter, in whole or in part, or not make payments under a grant awarded under this chapter, if the Secretaries determine that a recipient has failed to meet any requirements of this chapter, including—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) reporting requirements under section 6192(c) of this title;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) regulations under this chapter; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) requirements of an approved State plan.

#### (2) Notice and opportunity for hearing

&emsp;If the Secretaries terminate or suspend such financial assistance, or do not make such payments under paragraph (1), with respect to a recipient, then the Secretaries shall provide—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) prompt notice to such recipient; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the opportunity for a hearing to such recipient not later than 30 days after the date on which such notice is provided.

#### (b) Nondelegation

The Secretaries shall not delegate any of the functions or authority specified in this section, other than to an officer whose appointment is required to be made by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.

(Pub. L. 103 239, title VI, §602, May 4, 1994, 108 Stat. 604.)
