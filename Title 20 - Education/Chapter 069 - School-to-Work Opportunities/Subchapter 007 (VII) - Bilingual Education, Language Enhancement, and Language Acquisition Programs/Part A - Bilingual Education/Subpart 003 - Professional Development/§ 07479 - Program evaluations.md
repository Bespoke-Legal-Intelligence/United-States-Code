### §7479. Program evaluations

Each recipient of funds under this subpart shall provide the Secretary with an evaluation of the program assisted under this subpart every two years. Such evaluation shall include data on—

&emsp;(1) post-program placement of persons trained in a program assisted under this subpart;

&emsp;(2) how the training relates to the employment of persons served by the program;

&emsp;(3) program completion; and

&emsp;(4) such other information as the Secretary may require.

(Pub. L. 89 10, title VII, §7149, as added Pub. L. 103 382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3736.)
