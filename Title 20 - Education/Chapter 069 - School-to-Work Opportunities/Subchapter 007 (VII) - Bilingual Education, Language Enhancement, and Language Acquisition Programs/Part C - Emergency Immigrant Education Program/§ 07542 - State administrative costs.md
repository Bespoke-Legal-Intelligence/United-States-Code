### §7542. State administrative costs

For any fiscal year, a State educational agency may reserve not more than 1.5 percent of the amount allocated to such agency under section 7544 of this title to pay the costs of performing such agency's administrative functions under this part.

(Pub. L. 89 10, title VII, §7302, as added Pub. L. 103 382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3739.)
