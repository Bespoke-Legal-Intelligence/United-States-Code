### §7548. Reports

#### (a) Biennial report

Each State educational agency receiving funds under this part shall submit, once every two years, a report to the Secretary concerning the expenditure of funds by local educational agencies under this part. Each local educational agency receiving funds under this part shall submit to the State educational agency such information as may be necessary for such report.

#### (b) Report to Congress

The Secretary shall submit, once every two years, a report to the appropriate committees of the Congress concerning programs assisted under this part in accordance with section 8941 of this title.

(Pub. L. 89 10, title VII, §7308, as added Pub. L. 103 382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3744.)
