### §8503. Purpose

The purpose of this subchapter is to help the Nation meet the National Education Goals through the provision of Federal funds to enable local educational agencies to meet the costs associated with the improvement of schools within their jurisdiction.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title XII, §12003, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3871.)
