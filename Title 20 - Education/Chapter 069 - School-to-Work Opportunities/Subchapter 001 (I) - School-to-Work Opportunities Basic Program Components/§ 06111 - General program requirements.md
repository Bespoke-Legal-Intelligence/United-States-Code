### §6111. General program requirements

A School-to-Work Opportunities program under this chapter shall—

&emsp;(1) integrate school-based learning and work-based learning, as provided for in sections 6112 and 6113 of this title, integrate academic and occupational learning, and establish effective linkages between secondary and postsecondary education;

&emsp;(2) provide participating students with the opportunity to complete career majors;

&emsp;(3) incorporate the program components provided in sections 6112 through 6114 of this title;

&emsp;(4) provide participating students, to the extent practicable, with strong experience in and understanding of all aspects of the industry the students are preparing to enter; and

&emsp;(5) provide all students with equal access to the full range of such program components (including both school-based and work-based learning components) and related activities, such as recruitment, enrollment, and placement activities, except that nothing in this chapter shall be construed to provide any individual with an entitlement to services under this chapter.

(Pub. L. 103 239, title I, §101, May 4, 1994, 108 Stat. 576.)
