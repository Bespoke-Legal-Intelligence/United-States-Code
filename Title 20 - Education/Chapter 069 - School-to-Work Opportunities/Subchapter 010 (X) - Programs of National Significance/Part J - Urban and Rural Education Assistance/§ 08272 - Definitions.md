### §8272. Definitions

Except as otherwise provided, for the purposes of this part:

#### (1) Central city

&emsp;The term “central city” has the same meaning used by the Bureau of the Census.

#### (2) Metropolitan statistical area

&emsp;The term “metropolitan statistical area” has the same meaning used by the Bureau of the Census.

#### (3) Poverty level

&emsp;The term “poverty level” means the criteria of poverty used by the Bureau of the Census in compiling the most recent decennial census.

#### (4) Rural eligible local educational agency

&emsp;The term “rural eligible local educational agency” means a local educational agency—

&emsp;&emsp;(A)(i) in which at least 15 percent of the children enrolled in the schools served by such agency are eligible to be counted under part A of subchapter I of this chapter; and

&emsp;&emsp;(ii) which is not in a metropolitan statistical area; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) in which the total enrollment in the schools served by such agency is less than 2,500 students and that does not serve schools located in a metropolitan statistical area.

#### (5) Urban eligible local educational agency

&emsp;The term “urban eligible local educational agency” means a local educational agency that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) serves the largest central city in a State;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) enrolls more than 30,000 students and serves a central city with a population of atleast 200,000 in a metropolitan statistical area; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) enrolls between 25,000 and 30,000 students and serves a central city with a population of at least 140,000 in a metropolitan statistical area.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10952, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3847.)
