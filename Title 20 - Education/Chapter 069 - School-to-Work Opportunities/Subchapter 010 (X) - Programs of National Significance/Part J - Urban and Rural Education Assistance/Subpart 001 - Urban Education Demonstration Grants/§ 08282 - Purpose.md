### §8282. Purpose

It is the purpose of this subpart to provide financial assistance to—

&emsp;(1) assist urban public schools in meeting the National Education Goals;

&emsp;(2) improve the educational and social well-being of urban public school children;

&emsp;(3) close the achievement gap between urban and nonurban public school children, while improving the achievement level of all children nationally;

&emsp;(4) conduct coordinated research on urban public education problems, solutions, and promising practices;

&emsp;(5) improve the Nation's global economic and educational competitiveness by improving the Nation's urban schools; and

&emsp;(6) encourage community, parental, and business collaboration in the improvement of urban schools.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10962, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3849.)
