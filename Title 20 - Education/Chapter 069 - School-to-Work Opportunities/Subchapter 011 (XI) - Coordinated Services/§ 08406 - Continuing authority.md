### §8406. Continuing authority

The Secretary shall prohibit an eligible entity from using funds made available under section 8826(b) of this title if the Secretary determines that the coordinated services project assisted under this subchapter is not achieving effective coordination after two years of implementation of such project.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title XI, §11006, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3870.)
