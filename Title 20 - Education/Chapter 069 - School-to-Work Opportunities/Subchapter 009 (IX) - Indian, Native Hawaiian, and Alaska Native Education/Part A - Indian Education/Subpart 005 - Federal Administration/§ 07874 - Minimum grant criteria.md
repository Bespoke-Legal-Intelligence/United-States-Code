### §7874. Minimum grant criteria

The Secretary may not approve an application for a grant under subpart 2 or 3 of this part unless the application is for a grant that is—

&emsp;(1) of sufficient size, scope, and quality to achieve the purpose or objectives of such grant; and

&emsp;(2) based on relevant research findings.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title IX, §9154, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3793.)
