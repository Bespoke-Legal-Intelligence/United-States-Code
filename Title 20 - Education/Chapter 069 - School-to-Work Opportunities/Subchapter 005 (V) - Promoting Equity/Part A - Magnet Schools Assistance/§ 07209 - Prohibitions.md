### §7209. Prohibitions

#### (a) Transportation

Grants under this part may not be used for transportation or any activity that does not augment academic improvement.

#### (b) Planning

A local educational agency shall not expend funds under this part after the third year that such agency receives funds under this part for such project.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title V, §5109, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3694.)
