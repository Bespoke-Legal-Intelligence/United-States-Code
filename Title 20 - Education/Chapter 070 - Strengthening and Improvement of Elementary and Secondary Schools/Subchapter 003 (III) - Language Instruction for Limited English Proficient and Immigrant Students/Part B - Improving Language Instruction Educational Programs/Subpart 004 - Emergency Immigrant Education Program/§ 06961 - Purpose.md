### §6961. Purpose

The purpose of this subpart is to assist eligible local educational agencies that experience unexpectedly large increases in their student population due to immigration—

&emsp;(1) to provide high-quality instruction to immigrant children and youth; and

&emsp;(2) to help such children and youth—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) with their transition into American society; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) meet the same challenging State academic content and student academic achievement standards as all children are expected to meet.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title III, §3241, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title III, §301, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1723.)
