### §6369. Evaluation

From funds reserved under section 6362(b)(1) of this title, the Secretary shall provide for an independent evaluation of programs assisted under this part—

&emsp;(1) to determine the performance and effectiveness of programs assisted under this part;

&emsp;(2) to identify effective Even Start programs assisted under this part that can be duplicated and used in providing technical assistance to Federal, State, and local programs; and

&emsp;(3) to provide States and eligible entities receiving a subgrant under this part, directly or through a grant or contract with an organization with experience in the development and operation of successful family literacy services, technical assistance to ensure local evaluations undertaken under section 6365(10) of this title provide accurate information on theeffectiveness of programs assisted under this part.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title I, §1209, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3584; amended Pub. L. 105–277, div. A, §101(f) \[title VIII, §203\], Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2681–337, 2681–408.)
