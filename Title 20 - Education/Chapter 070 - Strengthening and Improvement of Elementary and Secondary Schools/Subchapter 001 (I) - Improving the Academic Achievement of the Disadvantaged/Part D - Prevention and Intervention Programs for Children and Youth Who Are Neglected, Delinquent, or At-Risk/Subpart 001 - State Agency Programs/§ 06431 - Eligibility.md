### §6431. Eligibility

A State agency is eligible for assistance under this subpart if such State agency is responsiblefor providing free public education for children and youth—

&emsp;(1) in institutions for neglected or delinquent children and youth;

&emsp;(2) attending community day programs for neglected or delinquent children and youth; or

&emsp;(3) in adult correctional institutions.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title I, §1411, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title I, §101, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1581.)

#### Prior Provisions
