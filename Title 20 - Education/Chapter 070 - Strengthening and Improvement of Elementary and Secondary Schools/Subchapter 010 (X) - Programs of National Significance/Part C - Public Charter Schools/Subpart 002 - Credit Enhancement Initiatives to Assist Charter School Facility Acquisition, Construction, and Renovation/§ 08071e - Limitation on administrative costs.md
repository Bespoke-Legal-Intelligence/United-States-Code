### §8071e. Limitation on administrative costs

An eligible entity may use not more than 0.25 percent of the funds received under this subpartfor the administrative costs of carrying out its responsibilities under this subpart.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10326, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(1) \[title III, §322(a)(2)\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–59.)
