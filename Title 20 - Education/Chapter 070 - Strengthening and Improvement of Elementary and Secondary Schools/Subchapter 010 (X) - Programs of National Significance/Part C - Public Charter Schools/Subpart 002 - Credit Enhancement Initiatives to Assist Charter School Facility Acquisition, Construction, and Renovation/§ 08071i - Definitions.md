### §8071i. Definitions

In this subpart:

&emsp;(1) The term “charter school” has the meaning given such term in section 8066 of this title.

&emsp;(2) The term “eligible entity” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a public entity, such as a State or local governmental entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a private nonprofit entity; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a consortium of entities described in subparagraphs (A) and (B).

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10330, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(1) \[title III, §322(a)(2)\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–61.)
