### §8371. General assistance for Virgin Islands

There are authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000 for fiscal year 1995 and for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years, for the purpose of providing general assistance to improve public education in the Virgin Islands.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10995, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3867.)
