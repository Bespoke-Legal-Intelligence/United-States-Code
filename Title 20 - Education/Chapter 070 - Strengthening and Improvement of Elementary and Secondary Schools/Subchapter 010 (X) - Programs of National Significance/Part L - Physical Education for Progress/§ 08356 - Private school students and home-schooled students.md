### §8356. Private school students and home-schooled students

An application for funds under this part may provide for the participation, in the activities funded under this part, of—

&emsp;(1) home-schooled children, and their parents and teachers; or

&emsp;(2) children enrolled in private nonprofit elementary schools or secondary schools, and their parents and teachers.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10999G, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(1) \[title VII, §701\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–78.)
