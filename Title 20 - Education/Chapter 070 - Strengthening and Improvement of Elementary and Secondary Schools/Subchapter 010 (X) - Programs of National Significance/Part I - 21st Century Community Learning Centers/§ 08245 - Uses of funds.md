### §8245. Uses of funds

Grants awarded under this part may be used to plan, implement, or expand community learning centers which include not less than four of the following activities:

&emsp;(1) Literacy education programs.

&emsp;(2) Senior citizen programs.

&emsp;(3) Children's day care services.

&emsp;(4) Integrated education, health, social service, recreational, or cultural programs.

&emsp;(5) Summer and weekend school programs in conjunction with recreation programs.

&emsp;(6) Nutrition and health programs.

&emsp;(7) Expanded library service hours to serve community needs.

&emsp;(8) Telecommunications and technology education programs for individuals of all ages.

&emsp;(9) Parenting skills education programs.

&emsp;(10) Support and training for child day care providers.

&emsp;(11) Employment counseling, training, and placement.

&emsp;(12) Services for individuals who leave school before graduating from secondary school, regardless of the age of such individual.

&emsp;(13) Services for individuals with disabilities.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10905, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3846.)
