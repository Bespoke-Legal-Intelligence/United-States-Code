### §8291. Purpose

It is the purpose of this subpart to address the unique needs of rural school districts that frequently—

&emsp;(1) lack the personnel and resources needed to compete for Federal competitive grants; and

&emsp;(2) receive formula allocations in amounts too small to be effective in meeting their intended purposes.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title X, §10972, as added Pub. L. 106–554, §1(a)(1) \[title IX, §901\], Dec. 21, 2000, 114 Stat. 2763, 2763A–89.)
