### §6682. Definitions

In this division:

#### (1) Eligible participant

&emsp;The term “eligible participant” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an individual with substantial, demonstrable career experience, including a highly qualified paraprofessional; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) an individual who is a graduate of an institution of higher education who—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) has graduated not more than 3 years before applying to an eligible entity to teach under this division; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) in the case of an individual wishing to teach in a secondary school, has completed an academic major (or courses totaling an equivalent number of credit hours) in the academic subject that the individual will teach.

#### (2) High-need local educational agency

&emsp;The term “high-need local educational agency” has the meaning given the term in section 6602 of this title.

#### (3) High-need school

&emsp;The term “high-need school” means a school that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is located in an area in which the percentage of students from families with incomes below the poverty line is 30 percent or more; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B)(i) is located in an area with a high percentage of out-of-field teachers, as defined in section 6602 of this title;

&emsp;&emsp;(ii) is within the top quartile of elementary schools and secondary schools statewide, as ranked by the number of unfilled, available teacher positions at the schools;

&emsp;&emsp;(iii) is located in an area in which there is a high teacher turnover rate; or

&emsp;&emsp;(iv) is located in an area in which there is a high percentage of teachers who are not certified or licensed.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title II, §2312, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title II, §201, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1656.)
