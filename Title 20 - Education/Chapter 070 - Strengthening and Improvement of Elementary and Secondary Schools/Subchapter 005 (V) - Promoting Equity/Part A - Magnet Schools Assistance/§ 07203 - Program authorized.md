### §7203. Program authorized

The Secretary, in accordance with this part, is authorized to make grants to eligible local educational agencies, and consortia of such agencies where appropriate, to carry out the purpose of this part for magnet schools that are—

&emsp;(1) part of an approved desegregation plan; and

&emsp;(2) designed to bring students from different social, economic, ethnic, and racial backgrounds together.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title V, §5103, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3691.)
