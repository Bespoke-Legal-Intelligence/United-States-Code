### §7427. Intensified instruction

In carrying out this subpart, each grant recipient may intensify instruction for limited English proficient students by—

&emsp;(1) expanding the educational calendar of the school in which such student is enrolled to include programs before and after school and during the summer months;

&emsp;(2) expanding the use of professional and volunteer aids;

&emsp;(3) applying technology to the course of instruction; and

&emsp;(4) providing intensified instruction through supplementary instruction or activities, including educationally enriching extracurricular activities, during times when school is not routinely in session.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title VII, §7117, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3727.)
