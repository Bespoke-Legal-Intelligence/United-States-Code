### §7903. Purpose

It is the purpose of this part to—

&emsp;(1) authorize and develop supplemental educational programs to assist Native Hawaiians in reaching the National Education Goals;

&emsp;(2) provide direction and guidance to appropriate Federal, State, and local agencies to focus resources, including resources made available under this part, on Native Hawaiian education, through the establishment of a Native Hawaiian Education Council, and five island councils;

&emsp;(3) supplement and expand existing programs and authorities in the area of education to further the purposes of the <sup>1</sup> subchapter; and

&emsp;(4) encourage the maximum participation of Native Hawaiians in planning and management of Native Hawaiian Education Programs.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title IX, §9203, as added Pub. L. 103–382, title I, §101, Oct. 20, 1994, 108 Stat. 3798.)

<i><sup>1</sup></i><i> So in original. Probably should be “this”.</i>
