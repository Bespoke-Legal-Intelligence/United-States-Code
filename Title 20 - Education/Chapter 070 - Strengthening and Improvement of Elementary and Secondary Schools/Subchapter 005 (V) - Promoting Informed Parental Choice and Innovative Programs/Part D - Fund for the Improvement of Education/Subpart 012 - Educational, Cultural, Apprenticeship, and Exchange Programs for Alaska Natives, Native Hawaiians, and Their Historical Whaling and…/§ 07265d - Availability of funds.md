### §7265d. Availability of funds

If sufficient funds are made available under section 7241 of this title to carry out this subpart for a fiscal year, the Secretary shall make available, to support activities described in section 7265b(b) of this title, the following amounts:

&emsp;(1) Not less than $2,000,000 each to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the New Bedford Whaling Museum, in partnership with the New Bedford Oceanarium, in Massachusetts; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Inupiat Heritage Center in Alaska.

&emsp;(2) For the New Trade Winds project, not less than $1,000,000 each to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Alaska Native Heritage Center in Alaska;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Bishop Museum in Hawaii; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Peabody-Essex Museum in Massachusetts.

&emsp;(3) For internship and apprenticeship programs (including the Museum Action Corps of the Peabody-Essex Museum), not less than $1,000,000 each to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Alaska Native Heritage Center in Alaska;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Bishop Museum in Hawaii; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Peabody-Essex Museum in Massachusetts.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title V, §5525, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title V, §501, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1847.)
