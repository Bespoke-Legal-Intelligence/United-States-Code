### §7223i. Definitions

In this subpart:

#### (1) Charter school

&emsp;The term "charter school" has the meaning given such term in section 7221i of this title.

#### (2) Eligible entity

&emsp;The term "eligible entity" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a public entity, such as a State or local governmental entity;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a private nonprofit entity; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a consortium of entities described in subparagraphs (A) and (B).

(Pub. L. 89–10, title V, §5230, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title V, §501, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1803.)
