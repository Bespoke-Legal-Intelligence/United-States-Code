### §6661i. Authorizations of appropriations; reservations from appropriations; sunset

#### (a) Authorizations

#### (1) FY 1999

&emsp;There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part $260,000,000 for fiscal year 1999.

#### (2) FY 2000

&emsp;There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part $260,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.

#### (b) Reservations

From each of the amounts appropriated under subsection (a) of this section for a fiscal year, the Secretary—

&emsp;(1) shall reserve 1.5 percent to carry out section 6661f(a) of this title; and

&emsp;(2) shall reserve $5,000,000 to carry out section 6661g of this title.

#### (c) Sunset

Notwithstanding section 1226a(a) of this title, this part is not subject to extension under such section.
