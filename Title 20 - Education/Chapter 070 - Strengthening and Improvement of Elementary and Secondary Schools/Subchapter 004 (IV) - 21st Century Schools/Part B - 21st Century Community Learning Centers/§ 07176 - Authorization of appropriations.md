### §7176. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated—

&emsp;(1) $1,250,000,000 for fiscal year 2002;

&emsp;(2) $1,500,000,000 for fiscal year 2003;

&emsp;(3) $1,750,000,000 for fiscal year 2004;

&emsp;(4) $2,000,000,000 for fiscal year 2005;

&emsp;(5) $2,250,000,000 for fiscal year 2006; and

&emsp;(6) $2,500,000,000 for fiscal year 2007.

(Pub. L. 89–10, title IV, §4206, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title IV, §401, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1773.)
