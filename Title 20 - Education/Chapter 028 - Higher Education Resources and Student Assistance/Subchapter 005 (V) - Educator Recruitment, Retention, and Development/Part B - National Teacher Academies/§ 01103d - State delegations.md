### §1103d. State delegations

#### (a) In general

Each selection panel established pursuant to section 1103e(b) of this title shall select a State delegation to participate in each National Teacher Academy assisted under this part.

#### (b) Composition

#### (1) In general

&emsp;Except as provided in paragraphs (2) and (3), each State delegation described in subsection (a) of this section shall, at a minimum, be composed of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 1 school administrator with authority to design and conduct in-service teacher training and academic programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) at least 5 teachers, of whom at least 2 shall be elementary school teachers.

#### (2) Special rule

&emsp;The State delegations for the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, the Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Republic of Palau (until the Compact of Free Association is ratified) shall, at a minimum, be composed of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 1 school administrator with authority to design and conduct in-service teacher training and academic programs; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) at least 3 teachers, of whom at least 1 shall be an elementary school teacher.

#### (3) Additional teachers

&emsp;(A) Each State that has obtained the approval of the appropriate National Teacher Academy may send to such National Teacher Academy the number of additional teachers determined in accordance with subparagraph (B).

&emsp;(B) The appropriate National Teacher Academy shall determine the number of additional teachers to attend such National Teacher Academy on the basis of the number of full-time equivalent teachers in the State compared to such number in all States.

#### (c) Duties

Each State delegation shall—

&emsp;(1) attend the appropriate subject area summer institute at the appropriate National Teacher Academy; and

&emsp;(2) after participation in the National Teacher Academy assist in the development and operation of the appropriate National Teacher Academy.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title V, §515, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title V, §501(a), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 676.)
