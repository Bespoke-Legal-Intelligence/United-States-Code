### §1131f. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated $10,000,000 for fiscal year 1993 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years to carry out this part.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title VI, §627, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title VI, §601, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 736.)
