### SUBCHAPTER IX—GRADUATE PROGRAMS

#### Codification

Title IX of the Higher Education Act of 1965, comprising this subchapter, was originally enacted by Pub. L. 89–329, title IX, as added by Pub. L. 90–575, title II, §261, Oct. 16, 1968, 82 Stat. 1043, and amended by Pub. L. 92–318, June 23, 1972, 86 Stat. 235; Pub. L. 93–380, Aug. 21, 1974, 88 Stat. 484; Pub. L. 94–482, Oct. 12, 1976, 90 Stat. 2081; Pub. L. 96–49, Aug. 13, 1979, 93 Stat. 351; Pub. L. 96–374, Oct. 3, 1980, 94 Stat. 1367; Pub. L. 96–470, Oct. 19, 1980, 94 Stat. 2237; Pub. L. 99–159, Nov. 22, 1985, 99 Stat. 887. Such title (except for part A) is shown herein, however, as having been added by Pub. L. 99–498, title IX, §901(a), Oct. 17, 1986, 100 Stat. 1549, without reference to such intervening amendments because of the extensive revision of title IX by Pub. L. 99–498. Part A of such title is shown as having been added by Pub. L. 102–325, title IX, §901, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 761, without reference to intervening amendments because of the extensive revision of part A by Pub. L. 102–325.
