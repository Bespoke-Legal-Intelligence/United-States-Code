### §1134b. Submission and contents of application

#### (a) Required information

Each institution of higher education or consortium desiring assistance under this part shall submit an application to the Secretary in such form and containing such information as the Secretary may by regulation prescribe. Each such application shall provide information regarding—

&emsp;(1) the program of study, to take the form of summer research internships, seminars, and other educational experiences;

&emsp;(2) the institution's or consortium's plan for identifying and recruiting talented women and minority undergraduates, especially those interested in entering fields in which such women and minority undergraduates are underrepresented;

&emsp;(3) the participation of faculty in the program and a detailed description of the research in which students will be involved;

&emsp;(4) a plan for the evaluation of the effectiveness of the program; and

&emsp;(5) such other assurances and information as the Secretary may require by regulation.

#### (b) Selection requirements

In making awards to institutions and consortia—

&emsp;(1) the Secretary shall consider the quality of the research in which students will be involved as well as the recruitment program and program of study; and

&emsp;(2) the Secretary shall ensure an equitable geographic distribution among public and private institutions of higher education and consortia.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IX, §912, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title IX, §901, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 761.)
