### §1070a–51. Database and information line

From the funds available under section 1070a–53 of this title, the Secretary shall award a contract to establish and maintain—

&emsp;(1) a computerized database of all public and private student financial assistance programs, to be accessible to schools and libraries through either modems or toll-free telephone lines; and

&emsp;(2) a toll-free information line, including access by telecommunications devices for the deaf (“TDD's”), to provide individualized financial assistance information to parents, students, and other individuals, including individuals with disabilities, and to refer students with disabilities and their families to the postsecondary clearinghouse that is authorized under section 1433(c) <sup>1</sup> of this title.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IV, §409A, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title IV, §402(a)(4), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 501; amended Pub. L. 103–208, §2(b)(24), Dec. 20, 1993, 107 Stat. 2459.)

#### References in Text

Section 1433(c) of this title, referred to in par. (2), was in the original a reference to section 633(c) of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, Pub. L. 91–230, title VI. Section 633 of Pub. L. 91–230 was omitted in the general amendment of subchapters III and IV of chapter 33 of this title by Pub. L. 105–17, title I, §101, June 4, 1997, 111 Stat. 37. Pub. L. 105–17 enacted a new section 633 of Pub. L. 91–230, which is classified to section 1433 of this title, and which no longer contains a subsec. (c) and no longer relates to clearinghouses.

#### Amendments

1993—Par. (1). Pub. L. 103–208 substituted “private student financial” for “private financial”.
