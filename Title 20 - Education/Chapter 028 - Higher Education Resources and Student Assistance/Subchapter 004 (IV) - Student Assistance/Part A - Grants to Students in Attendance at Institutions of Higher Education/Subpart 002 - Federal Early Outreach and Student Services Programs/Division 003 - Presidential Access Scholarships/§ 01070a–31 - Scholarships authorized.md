### §1070a–31. Scholarships authorized

The Secretary is authorized in accordance with this division to award Presidential Access Scholarships to students who—

&emsp;(1) are eligible to receive a Pell Grant for the year in which the scholarship is awarded;

&emsp;(2) have participated in a preparatory program for postsecondary education; and

&emsp;(3) demonstrate academic achievement.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IV, §406A, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title IV, §402(a)(4), July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 497.)
