### §1070a–28. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this division $200,000,000 for fiscal year1999 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IV, §404H, as added Pub. L. 105–244, title IV, §403, Oct. 7, 1998, 112 Stat. 1663.)
