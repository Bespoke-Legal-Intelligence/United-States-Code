### §1070f–3. Authorized activities

Funds awarded to an eligible partnership under this subpart shall be used to—

&emsp;(1) develop and assess model distance learning programs or innovative educational software;

&emsp;(2) develop methodologies for the identification and measurement of skill competencies;

&emsp;(3) develop and assess innovative student support services; or

&emsp;(4) support other activities that are consistent with the purpose of this subpart.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title IV, §420G, as added Pub. L. 105–244, title IV, §411, Oct. 7, 1998, 112 Stat. 1672.)

#### Codification

Another section 411 of Pub. L. 105–244 amended section 1071 of this title.
