### §1139f. Designation of Urban Grant Institutions

The Secretary shall publish a list of eligible institutions under this part and shall designatethese institutions of higher education as “Urban Grant Institutions”. The Secretary shall establish a national network of Urban Grant Institutions so that the results of individual projects achieved in one metropolitan area can then be generalized, disseminated, replicated, and applied throughout the Nation. The information developed as a result of this section shall be made available to Urban Grant Institutions and to any other interested institution of higher education by any appropriate means.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title VII, §757, as added Pub. L. 105–244, title VII, §701, Oct. 7, 1998, 112 Stat. 1800.)
