### §1135e–3. Amount, duration, and use of funds

#### (a) Amount and duration of grants

Grants under this part shall be provided in an amount which is not less than $500,000 in a single fiscal year, and shall be continued for a period not to exceed 5 fiscal years.

#### (b) Use of grants

Grants provided under this section may be used for—

&emsp;(1) the operation and administration of outreach programs to elementary and secondary school students;

&emsp;(2) faculty development programs in support of outreach programs;

&emsp;(3) curriculum development in support of the outreach programs;

&emsp;(4) disseminating information about the outreach programs to elementary and secondary schools and institutions of higher education;

&emsp;(5) supporting cooperative efforts with elementary and secondary schools, community groups, business and industry, and other education-related groups, to expand the scope of the outreach programs; and

&emsp;(6) establishing infrastructure necessary to operate programs, specifically including telecommunications equipment providing distance learning capabilities.

(Pub. L. 89–329, title X, §1064, as added Pub. L. 102–325, title X, §1003, July 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 782.)
