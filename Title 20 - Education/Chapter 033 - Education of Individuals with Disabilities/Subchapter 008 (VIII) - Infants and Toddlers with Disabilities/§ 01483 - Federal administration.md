### §1483. Federal administration

Sections 1416, 1417, and 1420 of this title shall, to the extent not inconsistent with this subchapter, apply to the program authorized by this subchapter, except that—

&emsp;(1) any reference to a State educational agency shall be deemed to be a reference to the State agency established or designated under section 1476(b)(9) of this title,

&emsp;(2) any reference to the education of children with disabilities and the education of all children with disabilities and the provision of free public education to all children with disabilities shall be deemed to be a reference to the provision of services to infants and toddlers with disabilities in accordance with this subchapter, and

&emsp;(3) any reference to local educational agencies and intermediate educational agencies shall be deemed to be a reference to local service providers under this subchapter.

(Pub. L. 91–230, title VI, §683, as added Pub. L. 99–457, title I, §101(a), Oct. 8, 1986, 100 Stat. 1154; amended Pub. L. 101–476, title IX, §901(b)(183), (184), Oct. 30, 1990, 104 Stat. 1151; Pub. L. 102–119, §25(b), Oct. 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 607.)

#### Amendments

1990—Par. (2). Pub. L. 101–476, as amended by Pub. L. 102–119, substituted “children with disabilities” for “handicapped children” in three places and “infants and toddlers with disabilities” for “handicapped infants and toddlers”.
