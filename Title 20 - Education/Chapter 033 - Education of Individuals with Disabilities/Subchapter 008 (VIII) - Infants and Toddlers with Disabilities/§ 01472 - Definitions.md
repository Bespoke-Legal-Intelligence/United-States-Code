### §1472. Definitions

As used in this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) The term “infants and toddlers with disabilities” means individuals from birth to age 2, inclusive, who need early intervention services because they—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) are experiencing developmental delays, as measured by appropriate diagnostic instruments and procedures in one or more of the following areas: cognitive development, physical development, language and speech development (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as “communication development”), psychosocial development (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as “social or emotional development”), or self-help skills (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as “adaptive development”), or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) have a diagnosed physical or mental condition which has a high probability of resulting in developmental delay.

> &emsp;Such term may also include, at a State's discretion, individuals from birth to age 2, inclusive, who are at risk of having substantial developmental delays if early intervention services are not provided.

&emsp;(2) The term “early intervention services” are developmental services which—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) are provided under public supervision,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) are provided at no cost except where Federal or State law provides for a system of payments by families, including a schedule of sliding fees,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) are designed to meet the developmental needs of an infant or toddler with a disability in any one or more of the following areas:

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) physical development,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) cognitive development,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) communication development,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) social or emotional development, or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) adaptive development,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) meet the standards of the State, including the requirements of this subchapter,

&emsp;&emsp;(E) include—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) family training, counseling, and home visits,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) special instruction,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) speech pathology and audiology,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) occupational therapy,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) physical therapy,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) psychological services,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) case management services (hereafter in this subchapter referred to as “service coordination services”),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) medical services only for diagnostic or evaluation purposes,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ix) early identification, screening, and assessment services,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(x) health services necessary to enable the infant or toddler to benefit from the other early intervention services,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xi) social work services,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xii) vision services,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xiii) assistive technology devices and assistive technology services, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xiv) transportation and related costs that are necessary to enable an infant or toddler and the infant's or toddler's family to receive early intervention services,

&emsp;&emsp;(F) are provided by qualified personnel, including—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) special educators,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) speech and language pathologists and audiologists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) occupational therapists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) physical therapists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) psychologists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) social workers,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) nurses,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) nutritionists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ix) family therapists,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(x) orientation and mobility specialists, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xi) pediatricians and other physicians,

&emsp;&emsp;(G) to the maximum extent appropriate, are provided in natural environments, including the home, and community settings in which children without disabilities participate, and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) are provided in conformity with an individualized family service plan adopted in accordance with section 1477 of this title.

&emsp;(3) The term “developmental delay” has the meaning given such term by a State under section 1476(b)(1) of this title.

&emsp;(4) The term “Council” means the State Interagency Coordinating Council established under section 1482 of this title.
