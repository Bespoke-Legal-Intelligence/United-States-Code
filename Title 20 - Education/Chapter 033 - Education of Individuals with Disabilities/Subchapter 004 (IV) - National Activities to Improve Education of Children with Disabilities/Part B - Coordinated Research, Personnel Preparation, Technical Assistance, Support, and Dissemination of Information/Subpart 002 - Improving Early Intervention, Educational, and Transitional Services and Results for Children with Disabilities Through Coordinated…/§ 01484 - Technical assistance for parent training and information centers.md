### §1484. Technical assistance for parent training and information centers

#### (a) In general

The Secretary may, directly or through awards to eligible entities, provide technical assistance for developing, assisting, and coordinating parent training and information programs carried out by parent training and information centers receiving assistance under sections 1482 and 1483 of this title.

#### (b) Authorized activities

The Secretary may provide technical assistance to a parent training and information center under this section in areas such as—

&emsp;(1) effective coordination of parent training efforts;

&emsp;(2) dissemination of information;

&emsp;(3) evaluation by the center of itself;

&emsp;(4) promotion of the use of technology, including assistive technology devices and assistive technology services;

&emsp;(5) reaching underserved populations;

&emsp;(6) including children with disabilities in general education programs;

&emsp;(7) facilitation of transitions from—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) early intervention services to preschool;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) preschool to school; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) secondary school to postsecondary environments; and

&emsp;(8) promotion of alternative methods of dispute resolution.

(Pub. L. 91–230, title VI, §684, as added Pub. L. 105–17, title I, §101, June 4, 1997, 111 Stat. 152.)
