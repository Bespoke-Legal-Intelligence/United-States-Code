### SUBCHAPTER IV—NATIONAL ACTIVITIES TO IMPROVE EDUCATION OF CHILDREN WITH DISABILITIES

#### Codification

Part D of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (part D of title VI of Pub. L. 91–230), comprising this subchapter, was originally enacted by Pub. L. 91–230, title VI, Apr. 13, 1970, 84 Stat. 175, and amended by Pub. L. 93–380, Aug. 21, 1974, 88 Stat. 484; Pub. L. 95–49, June 17, 1977, 91 Stat. 230; Pub. L. 98–199, Dec. 2, 1983, 97 Stat. 1357; Pub. L. 99–457, Oct. 8, 1986, 100 Stat. 1145; Pub. L. 100–630, Nov. 7, 1988, 102 Stat. 3289; Pub. L. 101–476, Oct. 30, 1990, 104 Stat. 1103; Pub. L. 102–119, Oct. 7, 1991, 105 Stat. 587; Pub. L. 102–421, Oct. 16, 1992, 106 Stat. 2151; Pub. L. 102–569, Oct. 29, 1992, 106 Stat. 4344; Pub. L. 103–73, Aug. 11, 1993, 107 Stat. 718; Pub. L. 103–218, Mar. 9, 1994, 108 Stat. 50. Such part is shownherein, however, as having been added by Pub. L. 105–17, title I, §101, June 4, 1997, 111 Stat. 37, without reference to such intervening amendments because of the extensive revision of part D by Pub. L. 105–17.
