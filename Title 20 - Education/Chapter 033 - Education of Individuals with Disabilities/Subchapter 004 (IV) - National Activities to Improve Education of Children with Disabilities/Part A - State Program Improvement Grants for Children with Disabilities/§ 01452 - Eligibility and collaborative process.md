### §1452. Eligibility and collaborative process

#### (a) Eligible applicants

A State educational agency may apply for a grant under this part for a grant period of not less than 1 year and not more than 5 years.

#### (b) Partners

#### (1) Required partners

#### (A) Contractual partners

&emsp;&emsp;In order to be considered for a grant under this part, a State educational agency shall establish a partnership with local educational agencies and other State agenciesinvolved in, or concerned with, the education of children with disabilities.

#### (B) Other partners

&emsp;&emsp;In order to be considered for a grant under this part, a State educational agency shall work in partnership with other persons and organizations involved in, and concerned with, the education of children with disabilities, including—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the Governor;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) parents of children with disabilities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) parents of nondisabled children;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) individuals with disabilities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) organizations representing individuals with disabilities and their parents, such as parent training and information centers;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) community-based and other nonprofit organizations involved in the education and employment of individuals with disabilities;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) the lead State agency for subchapter III of this chapter;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) general and special education teachers, and early intervention personnel;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ix) the State advisory panel established under subchapter III of this chapter;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(x) the State interagency coordinating council established under subchapter III of this chapter; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xi) institutions of higher education within the State.

#### (2) Optional partners

&emsp;A partnership under subparagraph (A) or (B) of paragraph (1) may also include—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) individuals knowledgeable about vocational education;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the State agency for higher education;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the State vocational rehabilitation agency;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) public agencies with jurisdiction in the areas of health, mental health, social services, and juvenile justice; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) other individuals.

(Pub. L. 91–230, title VI, §652, as added Pub. L. 105–17, title I, §101, June 4, 1997, 111 Stat. 124.)
