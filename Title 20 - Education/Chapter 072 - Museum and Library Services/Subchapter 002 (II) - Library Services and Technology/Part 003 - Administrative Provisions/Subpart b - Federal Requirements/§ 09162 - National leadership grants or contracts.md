### §9162. National leadership grants or contracts

#### (a) In general

From the amounts reserved under section 9131(a)(1)(B) of this title for any fiscal year the Director shall establish and carry out a program awarding national leadership grants or contracts to enhance the quality of library services nationwide and to provide coordination between libraries and museums. Such grants or contracts shall be used for activities that may include—

&emsp;(1) education and training of persons in library and information science, particularly in areas of new technology and other critical needs, including graduate fellowships, traineeships, institutes, or other programs;

&emsp;(2) research and demonstration projects related to the improvement of libraries, education in library and information science, enhancement of library services through effective and efficient use of new technologies, and dissemination of information derived from such projects;

&emsp;(3) preservation of digitization of library materials and resources, giving priority to projects emphasizing coordination, avoidance of duplication, and access by researchers beyond the institution or library entity undertaking the project; and

&emsp;(4) model programs demonstrating cooperative efforts between libraries and museums.

#### (b) Grants or contracts

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Director may carry out the activities described in subsection (a) of this section by awarding grants to, or entering into contracts with, libraries, agencies, institutions of higher education, or museums, where appropriate.

#### (2) Competitive basis

&emsp;Grants and contracts under this section shall be awarded on a competitive basis.

#### (c) Special rule

The Director shall make every effort to ensure that activities assisted under this section areadministered by appropriate library and museum professionals or experts.
