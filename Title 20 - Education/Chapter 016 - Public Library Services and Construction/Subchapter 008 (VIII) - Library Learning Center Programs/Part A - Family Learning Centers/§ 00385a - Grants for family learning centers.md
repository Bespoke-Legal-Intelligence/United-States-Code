### §385a. Grants for family learning centers

The Secretary shall carry out a program of making grants from sums appropriated pursuant to paragraph (7) of section 351b(a) of this title to local public libraries for the purposes of supporting family learning centers.

(June 19, 1956, ch. 407, title VIII, §802, as added Mar. 15, 1990, Pub. L. 101 254, §23(a), 104 Stat. 108.)
