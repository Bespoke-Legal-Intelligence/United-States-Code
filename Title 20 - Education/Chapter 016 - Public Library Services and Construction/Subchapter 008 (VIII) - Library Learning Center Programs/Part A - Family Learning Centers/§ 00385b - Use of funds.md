### §385b. Use of funds

#### (a) Initiation, expansion, and improvement of services

Funds made available to a grantee under this part shall be used to initiate, expand, or improve public library services to families.

#### (b) Acquisition of resources and materials in print and electronic formats

Not less than 25 percent of the funds made available under this part shall be used for the acquisition of resources and materials in print and electronic formats—

&emsp;(1) which are intended for use by and with adults, including materials in such areas as child care, child development, nutrition, parenting skills, and job and career information; and

&emsp;(2) which are intended for use by and with children and adolescents.

#### (c) Acquisition of computer hardware and software

Not less than 10 percent of the funds made available to a grantee under this part shall be used for—

&emsp;(1) the acquisition or leasing of computer hardware for use by library patrons, including services necessary for the operation, installation, and maintenance of such equipment; and

&emsp;(2) the acquisition of computer software and complementary explanatory material for use by library patrons.

(June 19, 1956, ch. 407, title VIII, §803, as added Mar. 15, 1990, Pub. L. 101–254, §23(a), 104 Stat. 108.)
