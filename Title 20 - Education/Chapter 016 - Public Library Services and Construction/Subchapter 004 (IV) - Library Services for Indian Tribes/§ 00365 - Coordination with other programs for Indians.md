### §365. Coordination with other programs for Indians

The Secretary, with the Secretary of the Interior, shall coordinate programs under this subchapter with the programs assisted under the various Acts and programs administered by the Department of the Interior that pertain to Indians.

(June 19, 1956, ch. 407, title IV, §405, as added Oct. 17, 1984, Pub. L. 98–480, title I, §114, 98 Stat. 2244.)
