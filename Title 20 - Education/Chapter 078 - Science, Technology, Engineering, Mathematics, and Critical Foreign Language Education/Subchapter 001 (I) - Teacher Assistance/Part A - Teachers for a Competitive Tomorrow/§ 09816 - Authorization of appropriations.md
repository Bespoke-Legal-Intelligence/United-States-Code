### §9816. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section <sup>1</sup> $276,200,000 for fiscal year 2008, and such sums as may be necessary for each of the 2 succeeding fiscal years, of which—

&emsp;(1) $151,200,000 shall be available to carry out section 9813 of this title for fiscal year 2008 and each succeeding fiscal year; and

&emsp;(2) $125,000,000 shall be available to carry out section 9814 of this title for fiscal year 2008 and each succeeding fiscal year.

(Pub. L. 110–69, title VI, §6116, Aug. 9, 2007, 121 Stat. 634.)
