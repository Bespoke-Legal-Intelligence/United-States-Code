### §4082. Exemptions for diesel fuel

#### (a) In general

The tax imposed by section 4081 shall not apply to diesel fuel—

&emsp;(1) which the Secretary determines is destined for a nontaxable use,

&emsp;(2) which is indelibly dyed in accordance with regulations which the Secretary shall prescribe, and

&emsp;(3) which meets such marking requirements (if any) as may be prescribed by the Secretary in regulations.

> Such regulations shall allow an individual choice of dye color approved by the Secretary orchosen from any list of approved dye colors that the Secretary may publish.

#### (b) Nontaxable use

For purposes of this section, the term “nontaxable use” means—

&emsp;(1) any use which is exempt from the tax imposed by section 4041(a)(1) other than by reason of a prior imposition of tax,

&emsp;(2) any use in a train, and

&emsp;(3) any use described in section 6427(b)(1) (after the application of section 6427(b)(3)).

#### (c) Exception to dyeing requirements

Paragraph (2) of subsection (a) shall not apply with respect to any diesel fuel—

&emsp;(1) removed, entered, or sold in a State for ultimate sale or use in an area of such State during the period such area is exempted from the fuel dyeing requirements under subsection (i) of section 211 of the Clean Air Act (as in effect on the date of the enactment of this subsection) by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency under paragraph (4) of such subsection (i) (as so in effect), and

&emsp;(2) the use of which is certified pursuant to regulations issued by the Secretary.

#### (d) Regulations

The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out this section, including regulations requiring the conspicuous labeling of retail diesel fuel pumps and other delivery facilities to assure that persons are aware of which fuel is available only for nontaxable uses.

#### (e) Cross reference

&emsp;&emsp;<b>For tax on train and certain bus uses of fuel purchased tax-free, see section 4041(a)(1).</b>
