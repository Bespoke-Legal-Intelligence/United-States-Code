### §4081. Imposition of tax

#### (a) Tax imposed

#### (1) Tax on removal, entry, or sale

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;There is hereby imposed a tax at the rate specified in paragraph (2) on—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) the removal of a taxable fuel from any refinery,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the removal of a taxable fuel from any terminal,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) the entry into the United States of any taxable fuel for consumption, use, or warehousing, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) the sale of a taxable fuel to any person who is not registered under section 4101 unless there was a prior taxable removal or entry of such fuel under clause (i), (ii), or (iii).

#### (B) Exemption for bulk transfers to registered terminals or refineries

&emsp;&emsp;The tax imposed by this paragraph shall not apply to any removal or entry of a taxable fuel transferred in bulk to a terminal or refinery if the person removing or entering the taxable fuel and the operator of such terminal or refinery are registered under section 4101.

#### (2) Rates of tax

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The rate of the tax imposed by this section is—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) in the case of gasoline other than aviation gasoline, 18.3 cents per gallon,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) in the case of aviation gasoline, 19.3 cents per gallon, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) in the case of diesel fuel or kerosene, 24.3 cents per gallon.

#### (B) Leaking Underground Storage Tank Trust Fund tax

&emsp;&emsp;The rates of tax specified in subparagraph (A) shall each be increased by 0.1 cent per gallon. The increase in tax under this subparagraph shall in this title be referred to as the Leaking Underground Storage Tank Trust Fund financing rate.

#### (b) Treatment of removal or subsequent sale by blender

#### (1) In general

&emsp;There is hereby imposed a tax at the rate determined under subsection (a) on taxable fuel removed or sold by the blender thereof.

#### (2) Credit for tax previously paid

&emsp;If—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) tax is imposed on the removal or sale of a taxable fuel by reason of paragraph (1), and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the blender establishes the amount of the tax paid with respect to such fuel by reason of subsection (a),

> &emsp;the amount of the tax so paid shall be allowed as a credit against the tax imposed by reason of paragraph (1).

#### (c) Taxable fuels mixed with alcohol

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary—

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The rate of tax under subsection (a) shall be the alcohol mixture rate in the case of the removal or entry of any qualified alcohol mixture.

#### (2) Tax prior to mixing

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;In the case of the removal or entry of any taxable fuel for use in producing at the time of such removal or entry a qualified alcohol mixture, the rate of tax under subsection (a) shall be the applicable fraction of the alcohol mixture rate. Subject to such terms and conditions as the Secretary may prescribe (including the application of section 4101), the treatment under the preceding sentence also shall apply to use in producing a qualified alcohol mixture after the time of such removal or entry.

#### (B) Applicable fraction

&emsp;&emsp;For purposes of subparagraph (A), the applicable fraction is—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) in the case of a qualified alcohol mixture which contains gasoline, the fraction the numerator of which is 10 and the denominator of which is—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) 9 in the case of 10 percent gasohol,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) 9.23 in the case of 7.7 percent gasohol, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) 9.43 in the case of 5.7 percent gasohol, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) in the case of a qualified alcohol mixture which does not contain gasoline, 10/9.

#### (3) Alcohol; qualified alcohol mixture

&emsp;For purposes of this subsection—

#### (A) Alcohol

&emsp;&emsp;The term “alcohol” includes methanol and ethanol but does not include alcohol produced from petroleum, natural gas, or coal (including peat). Such term does not includealcohol with a proof of less than 190 (determined without regard to any added denaturants).

#### (B) Qualified alcohol mixture

&emsp;&emsp;The term “qualified alcohol mixture” means—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) any mixture of gasoline with alcohol if at least 5.7 percent of such mixture is alcohol, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) any mixture of diesel fuel with alcohol if at least 10 percent of such mixture is alcohol.

#### (4) Alcohol mixture rates for gasoline mixtures

&emsp;For purposes of this subsection—

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The alcohol mixture rate for a qualified alcohol mixture which contains gasoline is the excess of the rate which would (but for this paragraph) be determined under subsection (a) over—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) 5.4 cents per gallon for 10 percent gasohol,

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) 4.158 cents per gallon for 7.7 percent gasohol, and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) 3.078 cents per gallon for 5.7 percent gasohol.

> &emsp;&emsp;In the case of a mixture none of the alcohol in which consists of ethanol, clauses (i), (ii), and (iii) shall be applied by substituting “6 cents” for “5.4 cents”, “4.62 cents” for “4.158 cents”, and “3.42 cents” for “3.078 cents”.

#### (B) 10 percent gasohol

&emsp;&emsp;The term “10 percent gasohol” means any mixture of gasoline with alcohol if at least 10 percent of such mixture is alcohol.

#### (C) 7.7 percent gasohol

&emsp;&emsp;The term “7.7 percent gasohol” means any mixture of gasoline with alcohol if at least 7.7 percent, but not 10 percent or more, of such mixture is alcohol.

#### (D) 5.7 percent gasohol

&emsp;&emsp;The term “5.7 percent gasohol” means any mixture of gasoline with alcohol if at least 5.7 percent, but not 7.7 percent or more, of such mixture is alcohol.

#### (5) Alcohol mixture rate for diesel fuel mixtures

&emsp;The alcohol mixture rate for a qualified alcohol mixture which does not contain gasoline is the excess of the rate which would (but for this paragraph) be determined under subsection (a) over 5.4 cents per gallon (6 cents per gallon in the case of a qualified alcohol mixture none of the alcohol in which consists of ethanol).

#### (6) Limitation

&emsp;In no event shall any alcohol mixture rate determined under this subsection be less than 4.3 cents per gallon.

#### (7) Later separation of fuel from qualified alcohol mixture

&emsp;If any person separates the taxable fuel from a qualified alcohol mixture on which tax was imposed under subsection (a) at a rate determined under paragraph (1) or (2) (or with respect to which a credit or payment was allowed or made by reason of section 6427(f)(1)), such person shall be treated as the refiner of such taxable fuel. The amount of tax imposed on any removal of such fuel by such person shall be reduced by the amount of tax imposed (and not credited or refunded) on any prior removal or entry of such fuel.

#### (8) Termination

&emsp;Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall not apply to any removal, entry, or sale after September 30, 2007.

#### (d) Termination

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The rates of tax specified in clauses (i) and (iii) of subsection (a)(2)(A) shall be 4.3 cents per gallon after September 30, 2005.

#### (2) Aviation gasoline

&emsp;The rate of tax specified in subsection (a)(2)(A)(ii) shall be 4.3 cents per gallon—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) after December 31, 1996, and before the date which is 7 days after the date of the enactment of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund Tax Reinstatement Act of 1997, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) after September 30, 2007.

#### (3) Leaking Underground Storage Tank Trust Fund financing rate

&emsp;The Leaking Underground Storage Tank Trust Fund financing rate under subsection (a)(2) shall apply after September 30, 1997, and before April 1, 2005.

#### (e) Refunds in certain cases

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, if any person who paid the tax imposed by this section with respect to any taxable fuel establishes to the satisfaction of the Secretary that a prior tax was paid (and not credited or refunded) with respect to such taxable fuel, then an amount equal to the tax paid by such person shall be allowed as a refund (without interest) to such person in the same manner as if it were an overpayment of tax imposed by this section.
