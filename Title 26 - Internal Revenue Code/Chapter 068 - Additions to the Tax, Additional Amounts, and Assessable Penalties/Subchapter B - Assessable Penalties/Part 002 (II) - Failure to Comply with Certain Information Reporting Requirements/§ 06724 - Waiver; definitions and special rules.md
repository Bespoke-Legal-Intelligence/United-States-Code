### §6724. Waiver; definitions and special rules

#### (a) Reasonable cause waiver

No penalty shall be imposed under this part with respect to any failure if it is shown that such failure is due to reasonable cause and not to willful neglect.

#### (b) Payment of penalty

Any penalty imposed by this part shall be paid on notice and demand by the Secretary and in the same manner as tax.

#### (c) Special rule for failure to meet magnetic media requirements

No penalty shall be imposed under section 6721 solely by reason of any failure to comply with the requirements of the regulations prescribed under section 6011(e)(2), except to the extent that such a failure occurs with respect to more than 250 information returns.

#### (d) Definitions

For purposes of this part—

#### (1) Information return

&emsp;The term “information return” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any statement of the amount of payments to another person required by—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) section 6041(a) or (b) (relating to certain information at source),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) section 6042(a)(1) (relating to payments of dividends),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) section 6044(a)(1) (relating to payments of patronage dividends),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) section 6049(a) (relating to payments of interest),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) section 6050A(a) (relating to reporting requirements of certain fishing boat operators),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) section 6050N(a) (relating to payments of royalties),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) section 6051(d) (relating to information returns with respect to income tax withheld), or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) section 6050R (relating to returns relating to certain purchases of fish),

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any return required by—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) section 6041A(a) or (b) (relating to returns of direct sellers),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) section 6045(a) or (d) (relating to returns of brokers),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) section 6050H(a) (relating to mortgage interest received in trade or business from individuals),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) section 6050I(a) or (g)(1) (relating to cash received in trade or business, etc.),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) section 6050J(a) (relating to foreclosures and abandonments of security),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) section 6050K(a) (relating to exchanges of certain partnership interests),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) section 6050L(a) (relating to returns relating to certain dispositions of donated property),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) section 6050P (relating to returns relating to the cancellation of indebtedness by certain financial entities),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ix) section 6050Q (relating to certain long-term care benefits),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(x) section 6052(a) (relating to reporting payment of wages in the form of group-life insurance),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xi) section 6053(c)(1) (relating to reporting with respect to certain tips),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xii) subsection (b) or (e) of section 1060 (relating to reporting requirements of transferors and transferees in certain asset acquisitions),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xiii) subparagraph (A) or (C) of subsection (c)(4) of section 4093 (relating to information reporting with respect to tax on diesel and aviation fuels),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xiv) section 4101(d) (relating to information reporting with respect to fuels taxes), or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xv) subparagraph (C) of section 338(h)(10) (relating to information required to be furnished to the Secretary in case of elective recognition of gain or loss), and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) any statement of the amount of payments to another person required to be made to the Secretary under—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) section 408(i) (relating to reports with respect to individual retirement accounts or annuities), or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) section 6047(d) (relating to reports by employers, plan administrators, etc.).

> &emsp;&emsp;Such term also includes any form, statement, or schedule required to be filed with the Secretary with respect to any amount from which tax was required to be deducted and withheld under chapter 3 (or from which tax would be required to be so deducted and withheld but for an exemption under this title or any treaty obligation of the United States).

#### (2) Payee statement

&emsp;The term “payee statement” means any statement required to be furnished under—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) section 6031(b) or (c), 6034A, or 6037(b) (relating to statements furnished by certain pass-thru entities),

&emsp;&emsp;(B) section 6039(a) (relating to information required in connection with certain options),

&emsp;&emsp;(C) section 6041(d) (relating to information at source),

&emsp;&emsp;(D) section 6041A(e) (relating to returns regarding payments of remuneration for services and direct sales),

&emsp;&emsp;(E) section 6042(c) (relating to returns regarding payments of dividends and corporate earnings and profits),

&emsp;&emsp;(F) section 6044(e) (relating to returns regarding payments of patronage dividends),

&emsp;&emsp;(G) section 6045(b) or (d) (relating to returns of brokers),

&emsp;&emsp;(H) section 6049(c) (relating to returns regarding payments of interest),

&emsp;&emsp;(I) section 6050A(b) (relating to reporting requirements of certain fishing boat operators),

&emsp;&emsp;(J) section 6050H(d) relating to returns relating to mortgage interest received in trade or business from individuals),

&emsp;&emsp;(K) section 6050I(e) or paragraph (4) or (5) of section 6050I(g) (relating to cash received in trade or business, etc.),

&emsp;&emsp;(L) section 6050J(e) (relating to returns relating to foreclosures and abandonments of security),

&emsp;&emsp;(M) section 6050K(b) (relating to returns relating to exchanges of certain partnership interests),

&emsp;&emsp;(N) section 6050L(c) (relating to returns relating to certain dispositions of donated property),

&emsp;&emsp;(O) section 6050N(b) (relating to returns regarding payments of royalties),

&emsp;&emsp;(P) section 6050P(d) (relating to returns relating to the cancellation of indebtedness by certain financial entities),

&emsp;&emsp;(Q) section 6050Q(b) (relating to certain long-term care benefits),

&emsp;&emsp;(R) section 6051 (relating to receipts for employees),

&emsp;&emsp;(S) section 6050R(c) (relating to returns relating to certain purchases of fish),

&emsp;&emsp;(T) section 6052(b) (relating to returns regarding payment of wages in the form of group-term life insurance),

&emsp;&emsp;(U) <sup>1</sup> section 6053(b) or (c) (relating to reports of tips),

&emsp;&emsp;(U) <sup>1</sup> section 4093(c)(4)(B) (relating to certain purchasers of diesel and aviation fuels),

&emsp;&emsp;(V) section 6048(b)(1)(B) (relating to foreign trust reporting requirements),

&emsp;&emsp;(W) section 408(i) (relating to reports with respect to individual retirement plans) to any person other than the Secretary with respect to the amount of payments made to such person, or

&emsp;&emsp;(X) section 6047(d) (relating to reports by plan administrators) to any person other than the Secretary with respect to the amount of payments made to such person.

> &emsp;Such term also includes any form, statement, or schedule required to be furnished to the recipient of any amount from which tax was required to be deducted and withheld under chapter 3 (or from which tax would be required to be so deducted and withheld but for an exemption under this title or any treaty obligation of the United States).

#### (3) Specified information reporting requirement

&emsp;The term “specified information reporting requirement” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the notice required by section 6050K(c)(1) (relating to requirement that transferor notify partnership of exchange),

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any requirement contained in the regulations prescribed under section 6109 that a person—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) include his TIN on any return, statement, or other document (other than an information return or payee statement),

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) furnish his TIN to another person, or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) include on any return, statement, or other document (other than an information return or payee statement) made with respect to another person the TIN of such person,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) any requirement contained in the regulations prescribed under section 215 that a person—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) furnish his TIN to another person, or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) include on his return the TIN of another person, and

&emsp;&emsp;(D) any requirement under section 6109(h) that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a person include on his return the name, address, and TIN of another person, or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a person furnish his TIN to another person.

#### (4) Required filing date

&emsp;The term “required filing date” means the date prescribed for filing an information return with the Secretary (determined with regard to any extension of time for filing).
