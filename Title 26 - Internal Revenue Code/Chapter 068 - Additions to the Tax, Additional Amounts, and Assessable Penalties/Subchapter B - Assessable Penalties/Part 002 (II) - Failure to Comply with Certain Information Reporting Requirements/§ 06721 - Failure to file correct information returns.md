### §6721. Failure to file correct information returns

#### (a) Imposition of penalty

#### (1) In general

&emsp;In the case of a failure described in paragraph (2) by any person with respect to an information return, such person shall pay a penalty of $50 for each return with respect to which such a failure occurs, but the total amount imposed on such person for all such failures during any calendar year shall not exceed $250,000.

#### (2) Failures subject to penalty

&emsp;For purposes of paragraph (1), the failures described in this paragraph are—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any failure to file an information return with the Secretary on or before the required filing date, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any failure to include all of the information required to be shown on the return or the inclusion of incorrect information.

#### (b) Reduction where correction in specified period

#### (1) Correction within 30 days

&emsp;If any failure described in subsection (a)(2) is corrected on or before the day 30 days after the required filing date—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the penalty imposed by subsection (a) shall be $15 in lieu of $50, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the total amount imposed on the person for all such failures during any calendar year which are so corrected shall not exceed $75,000.

#### (2) Failures corrected on or before August 1

&emsp;If any failure described in subsection (a)(2) is corrected after the 30th day referred to in paragraph (1) but on or before August 1 of the calendar year in which the required filing date occurs—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the penalty imposed by subsection (a) shall be $30 in lieu of $50, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the total amount imposed on the person for all such failures during the calendar year which are so corrected shall not exceed $150,000.

#### (c) Exception for de minimis failures to include all required information

#### (1) In general

&emsp;If—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an information return is filed with the Secretary,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) there is a failure described in subsection (a)(2)(B) (determined after the application of section 6724(a)) with respect to such return, and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) such failure is corrected on or before August 1 of the calendar year in which the required filing date occurs,

> &emsp;for purposes of this section, such return shall be treated as having been filed with all of the correct required information.

#### (2) Limitation

&emsp;The number of information returns to which paragraph (1) applies for any calendar year shall not exceed the greater of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) 10, or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) one-half of 1 percent of the total number of information returns required to be filed by the person during the calendar year.

#### (d) Lower limitations for persons with gross receipts of not more than $5,000,000

#### (1) In general

&emsp;If any person meets the gross receipts test of paragraph (2) with respect to any calendar year, with respect to failures during such taxable year—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) subsection (a)(1) shall be applied by substituting “$100,000” for “$250,000”,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) subsection (b)(1)(B) shall be applied by substituting “$25,000” for “$75,000”, and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) subsection (b)(2)(B) shall be applied by substituting “$50,000” for “$150,000”.

#### (2) Gross receipts test

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;A person meets the gross receipts test of this paragraph for any calendar year if the average annual gross receipts of such person for the most recent 3 taxable years ending before such calendar year do not exceed $5,000,000.

#### (B) Certain rules made applicable

&emsp;&emsp;For purposes of subparagraph (A), the rules of paragraphs (2) and (3) of section 448(c) shall apply.

#### (e) Penalty in case of intentional disregard

If 1 or more failures described in subsection (a)(2) are due to intentional disregard of the filing requirement (or the correct information reporting requirement), then, with respect to each such failure—

&emsp;(1) subsections (b), (c), and (d) shall not apply,

&emsp;(2) the penalty imposed under subsection (a) shall be $100, or, if greater—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) in the case of a return other than a return required under section 6045(a), 6041A(b), 6050H, 6050I, 6050J, 6050K, or 6050L, 10 percent of the aggregate amount of the items required to be reported correctly,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) in the case of a return required to be filed by section 6045(a), 6050K, or 6050L, 5 percent of the aggregate amount of the items required to be reported correctly, or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) in the case of a return required to be filed under section 6050I(a) with respect to any transaction (or related transactions), the greater of—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) $25,000, or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) the amount of cash (within the meaning of section 6050I(d)) received in such transaction (or related transactions) to the extent the amount of such cash does not exceed $100,000, and

&emsp;(3) in the case of any penalty determined under paragraph (2)—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the $250,000 limitation under subsection (a) shall not apply, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) such penalty shall not be taken into account in applying such limitation (or any similar limitation under subsection (b)) to penalties not determined under paragraph (2).
