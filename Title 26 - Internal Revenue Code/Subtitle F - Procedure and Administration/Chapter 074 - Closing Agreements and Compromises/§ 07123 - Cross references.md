### §7123. Cross references

&emsp;&emsp;<b>For criminal penalties for concealment of property, false statement, or falsifying and destroying records, in connection with any closing agreement, compromise, or offer of compromise, see section 7206.</b>

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 850; Sept. 13, 1982, Pub. L. 97–258, §3(f)(12), 96 Stat. 1065.)

#### Amendments

<b>1982</b>—Subsec. (a). Pub. L. 97–258, §3(f)(12)(A), struck out heading “Criminal penalties”.

Subsec. (b). Pub. L. 97–258, §3(f)(12)(B), struck out subsec. (b) which set forth cross reference to R.S. 3469 (31 U.S.C. 194) relating to compromises after judgment.
