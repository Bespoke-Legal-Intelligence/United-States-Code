### §5093. Cross references

&emsp;&emsp;<b>(1) For exemption of brewer from special tax as wholesale and retail dealer, see section 5113(a).</b>

&emsp;&emsp;<b>(2) For provisions relating to liability for special tax for carrying on business in more than one location, see section 5143(c).</b>

&emsp;&emsp;<b>(3) For exemption from special tax in case of sales made on purchaser dealers’ premises, see section 5113(d).</b>

(Added Pub. L. 85–859, title II, §201, Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1339.)

#### Repeal of Section

<i>Pub. L. 109–59, title XI, §11125(a)(1)(B), (c), Aug. 10, 2005, 119 Stat. 1953, 1957, provided that, effective July 1, 2008, but inapplicable to taxes imposed for periods before such date, this section is repealed.</i>

#### Prior Provisions

A prior section 5093, act Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 617, consisted of provisions similar to those comprising this section, prior to the general revision of this chapter by Pub. L. 85–859.
