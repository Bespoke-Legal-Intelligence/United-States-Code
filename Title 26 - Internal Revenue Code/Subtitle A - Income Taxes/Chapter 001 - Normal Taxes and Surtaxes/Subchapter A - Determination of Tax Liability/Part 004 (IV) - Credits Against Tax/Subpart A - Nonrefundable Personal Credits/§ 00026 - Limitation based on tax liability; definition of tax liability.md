### §26. Limitation based on tax liability; definition of tax liability

#### (a) Limitation based on amount of tax

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The aggregate amount of credits allowed by this subpart (other than sections 23, 24, and 25B) for the taxable year shall not exceed the excess (if any) of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the taxpayer's regular tax liability for the taxable year, over

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the tentative minimum tax for the taxable year (determined without regard to the alternative minimum tax foreign tax credit).

> &emsp;For purposes of subparagraph (B), the taxpayer's tentative minimum tax for any taxable year beginning during 1999 shall be treated as being zero.

#### (2) Special rule for 2000 and 2001

&emsp;For purposes of any taxable year beginning during 2000 or 2001, the aggregate amount of credits allowed by this subpart for the taxable year shall not exceed the sum of—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the taxpayer's regular tax liability for the taxable year reduced by the foreign tax credit allowable under section 27(a), and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the tax imposed by section 55(a) for the taxable year.

#### (b) Regular tax liability

For purposes of this part—

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The term “regular tax liability” means the tax imposed by this chapter for the taxable year.

#### (2) Exception for certain taxes

&emsp;For purposes of paragraph (1), any tax imposed by any of the following provisions shall not be treated as tax imposed by this chapter:

&emsp;&emsp;(A) section 55 (relating to minimum tax),

&emsp;&emsp;(B) section 59A (relating to environmental tax),

&emsp;&emsp;(C) subsection (m)(5)(B), (q), (t), or (v) of section 72 (relating to additional taxes on certain distributions),

&emsp;&emsp;(D) section 143(m) (relating to recapture of proration of Federal subsidy from use of mortgage bonds and mortgage credit certificates),

&emsp;&emsp;(E) section 530(d)(3) (relating to additional tax on certain distributions from Coverdell education savings accounts),

&emsp;&emsp;(F) section 531 (relating to accumulated earnings tax),

&emsp;&emsp;(G) section 541 (relating to personal holding company tax),

&emsp;&emsp;(H) section 1351(d)(1) (relating to recoveries of foreign expropriation losses),

&emsp;&emsp;(I) section 1374 (relating to tax on certain built-in gains of S corporations),

&emsp;&emsp;(J) section 1375 (relating to tax imposed when passive investment income of corporation having subchapter C earnings and profits exceeds 25 percent of gross receipts),

&emsp;&emsp;(K) subparagraph (A) of section 7518(g)(6) (relating to nonqualified withdrawals from capital construction funds taxed at highest marginal rate),

&emsp;&emsp;(L) sections 871(a) and 881 (relating to certain income of nonresident aliens and foreign corporations),

&emsp;&emsp;(M) section 860E(e) (relating to taxes with respect to certain residual interests),

&emsp;&emsp;(N) section 884 (relating to branch profits tax),

&emsp;&emsp;(O) sections 453(<i>l</i>)(3) and 453A(c) (relating to interest on certain deferred tax liabilities),

&emsp;&emsp;(P) section 860K (relating to treatment of transfers of high-yield interests to disqualified holders), and

&emsp;&emsp;(Q) section 220(f)(4) (relating to additional tax on Archer MSA distributions not used for qualified medical expenses).

#### (c) Tentative minimum tax

For purposes of this part, the term “tentative minimum tax” means the amount determined under section 55(b)(1).
