L. 94–455, title XIX, §§1901(b)(36)(E), (37)(F), 1951(c)(3)(D), Oct. 4, 1976, 90 Stat. 1802, 1803, 1841, struck out items for parts III and IV "Involuntary liquidation and replacement of LIFO inventories" and "War loss recoveries", respectively, and substituted in item for part VI "Maximum rate on personal service income" for "Other limitations".
