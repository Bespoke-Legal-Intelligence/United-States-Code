### §684. Recognition of gain on certain transfers to certain foreign trusts and estates

#### (a) In general

Except as provided in regulations, in the case of any transfer of property by a United States person to a foreign estate or trust, for purposes of this subtitle, such transfer shall be treated as a sale or exchange for an amount equal to the fair market value of the property transferred, and the transferor shall recognize as gain the excess of—

&emsp;(1) the fair market value of the property so transferred, over

&emsp;(2) the adjusted basis (for purposes of determining gain) of such property in the hands of the transferor.

#### (b) Exception

Subsection (a) shall not apply to a transfer to a trust by a United States person to the extent that any person is treated as the owner of such trust under section 671.

#### (c) Treatment of trusts which become foreign trusts

If a trust which is not a foreign trust becomes a foreign trust, such trust shall be treated forpurposes of this section as having transferred, immediately before becoming a foreign trust, all of its assets to a foreign trust.

(Added Pub. L. 105–34, title XI, §1131(b), Aug. 5, 1997, 111 Stat. 978; amended Pub. L. 107–16, title V, §542(e)(1)(A)–(C), June 7, 2001, 115 Stat. 84, 85.)

#### <i>(b) Exceptions</i>

#### <i>(1) Transfers to certain trusts</i>

&emsp;<i>Subsection (a) shall not apply to a transfer to a trust by a United States person to the extent that any United States person is treated as the owner of such trust under section 671.</i>

#### <i>(2) Lifetime transfers to nonresident aliens</i>

&emsp;<i>Subsection (a) shall not apply to a lifetime transfer to a nonresident alien.</i>
