#### Amendments

<b>2017</b>—Pub. L. 115–97, title I, §13001(b)(5)(A), Dec. 22, 2017, 131 Stat. 2098, which directed amendment of the table of sections for part I of subchapter B of chapter 5 by striking out item 1551 "Disallowance of the benefits of the graduated corporate rates and accumulated earnings credit", was executed to the table of sections for part I of subchapter B of this chapter to reflect the probable intent of Congress.
