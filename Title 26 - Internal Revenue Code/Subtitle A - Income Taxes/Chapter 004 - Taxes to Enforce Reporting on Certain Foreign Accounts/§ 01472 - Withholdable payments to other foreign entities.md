### §1472. Withholdable payments to other foreign entities

#### (a) In general

In the case of any withholdable payment to a non-financial foreign entity, if—

&emsp;(1) the beneficial owner of such payment is such entity or any other non-financial foreign entity, and

&emsp;(2) the requirements of subsection (b) are not met with respect to such beneficial owner,

> then the withholding agent with respect to such payment shall deduct and withhold from such payment a tax equal to 30 percent of the amount of such payment.

#### (b) Requirements for waiver of withholding

The requirements of this subsection are met with respect to the beneficial owner of a payment if—

&emsp;(1) such beneficial owner or the payee provides the withholding agent with either—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a certification that such beneficial owner does not have any substantial United States owners, or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the name, address, and TIN of each substantial United States owner of such beneficial owner,

&emsp;(2) the withholding agent does not know, or have reason to know, that any information provided under paragraph (1) is incorrect, and

&emsp;(3) the withholding agent reports the information provided under paragraph (1)(B) to the Secretary in such manner as the Secretary may provide.

#### (c) Exceptions

Subsection (a) shall not apply to—

&emsp;(1) except as otherwise provided by the Secretary, any payment beneficially owned by—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any corporation the stock of which is regularly traded on an established securities market,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any corporation which is a member of the same expanded affiliated group (as defined in section 1471(e)(2) without regard to the last sentence thereof) as a corporation described in subparagraph (A),

&emsp;&emsp;(C) any entity which is organized under the laws of a possession of the United States and which is wholly owned by one or more bona fide residents (as defined in section 937(a)) of such possession,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) any foreign government, any political subdivision of a foreign government, or any wholly owned agency or instrumentality of any one or more of the foregoing,

&emsp;&emsp;(E) any international organization or any wholly owned agency or instrumentality thereof,

&emsp;&emsp;(F) any foreign central bank of issue, or

&emsp;&emsp;(G) any other class of persons identified by the Secretary for purposes of this subsection, and

&emsp;(2) any class of payments identified by the Secretary for purposes of this subsection as posing a low risk of tax evasion.

#### (d) Non-financial foreign entity

For purposes of this section, the term "non-financial foreign entity" means any foreign entity which is not a financial institution (as defined in section 1471(d)(5)).

(Added Pub. L. 111–147, title V, §501(a), Mar. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 102.)
