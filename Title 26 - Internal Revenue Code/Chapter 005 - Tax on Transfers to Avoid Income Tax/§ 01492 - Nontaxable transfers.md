### §1492. Nontaxable transfers

The tax imposed by section 1491 shall not apply—

&emsp;(1) If the transferee is an organization exempt from income tax under part I of subchapter F of chapter 1 (other than an organization described in section 401(a)); or

&emsp;(2) To a transfer—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) described in section 367, or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) not described in section 367 but with respect to which the taxpayer elects (before the transfer) the application of principles similar to the principles of section 367, or

&emsp;(3) To a transfer for which an election has been made under section 1057.
