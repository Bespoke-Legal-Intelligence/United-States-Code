### §8021. Powers

#### (a) To obtain data and inspect income returns

&emsp;&emsp;<b>For powers of the Joint Committee to obtain and inspect income returns, see section 6103(f).</b>

#### (b) Relating to hearings and sessions

The Joint Committee, or any subcommittee thereof, is authorized—

#### (1) To hold

&emsp;To hold hearings and to sit and act at such places and times;

#### (2) To require attendance of witnesses and production of books

&emsp;To require by subpoena (to be issued under the signature of the chairman or vice chairman) or otherwise the attendance of such witnesses and the production of such books, papers, and documents;

#### (3) To administer oaths

&emsp;To administer such oaths; and

#### (4) To take testimony

&emsp;To take such testimony;

> as it deems advisable.

#### (c) To procure printing and binding

The Joint Committee, or any subcommittee thereof, is authorized to have such printing and binding done as it deems advisable.

#### (d) To make expenditures

The Joint Committee, or any subcommittee thereof, is authorized to make such expenditures as it deems advisable.

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 927; Oct. 4, 1976, Pub. L. 94–455, title XIX, §1907(a)(3), 90 Stat. 1835; Nov. 10, 1988, Pub. L. 100–647, title I, §1018(s)(1), 102 Stat. 3586.)

#### Amendments

<b>1988</b>—Subsec. (a). Pub. L. 100–647 substituted “6103(f)” for “6103(d)”.

<b>1976</b>—Subsec. (d). Pub. L. 94–455 struck out par. (2) relating to limitation on cost of stenographic services in reporting hearings.
