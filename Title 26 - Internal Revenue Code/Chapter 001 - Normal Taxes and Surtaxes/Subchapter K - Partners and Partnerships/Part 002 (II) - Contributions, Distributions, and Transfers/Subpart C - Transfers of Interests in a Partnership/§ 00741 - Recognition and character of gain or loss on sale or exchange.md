### §741. Recognition and character of gain or loss on sale or exchange

In the case of a sale or exchange of an interest in a partnership, gain or loss shall be recognized to the transferor partner. Such gain or loss shall be considered as gain or loss from the sale or exchange of a capital asset, except as otherwiseprovided in section 751 (relating to unrealized receivables and inventory items which have appreciated substantially in value).

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 248.)
