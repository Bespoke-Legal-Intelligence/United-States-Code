### §107. Rental value of parsonages

In the case of a minister of the gospel, gross income does not include—

&emsp;(1) the rental value of a home furnished to him as part of his compensation; or

&emsp;(2) the rental allowance paid to him as part of his compensation, to the extent used by him to rent or provide a home.

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 32.)
