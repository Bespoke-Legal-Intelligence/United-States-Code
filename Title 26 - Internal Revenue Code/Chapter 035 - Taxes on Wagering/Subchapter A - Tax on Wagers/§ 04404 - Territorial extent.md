### §4404. Territorial extent

The tax imposed by this subchapter shall apply only to wagers

&emsp;(1) accepted in the United States, or

&emsp;(2) placed by a person who is in the United States

&emsp;&emsp;(A) with a person who is a citizen or resident of the United States, or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) in a wagering pool or lottery conducted by a person who is a citizen or resident of the United States.

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 525.)
