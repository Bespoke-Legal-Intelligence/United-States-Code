### §6112. Organizers and sellers of potentially abusive tax shelters must keep lists of investors

#### (a) In general

Any person who—

&emsp;(1) organizes any potentially abusive tax shelter, or

&emsp;(2) sells any interest in such a shelter,

> shall maintain (in such manner as the Secretary may by regulations prescribe) a list identifying each person who was sold an interest in such shelter and containing such other information as the Secretary may by regulations require.

#### (b) Potentially abusive tax shelter

For purposes of this section, the term “potentially abusive tax shelter” means—

&emsp;(1) any tax shelter (as defined in section 6111) with respect to which registration is required under section 6111, and

&emsp;(2) any entity, investment plan or arrangement, or other plan or arrangement which is of a type which the Secretary determines by regulations as having a potential for tax avoidance or evasion.

#### (c) Special rules

#### (1) Availability for inspection; retention of information on list

&emsp;Any person who is required to maintain a list under subsection (a)—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) shall make such list available to the Secretary for inspection upon request by the Secretary, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) except as otherwise provided under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, shall retain any information which is required to be included on such list for 7 years.

#### (2) Lists which would be required to be maintained by 2 or more persons

&emsp;The Secretary shall prescribe regulations which provide that, in cases in which 2 or more persons are required under subsection (a) to maintain the same list (or portion thereof), only 1 person shall be required to maintain such list (or portion).

(Added Pub. L. 98–369, div. A, title I, §142(a), July 18, 1984, 98 Stat. 681.)

#### Prior Provisions
