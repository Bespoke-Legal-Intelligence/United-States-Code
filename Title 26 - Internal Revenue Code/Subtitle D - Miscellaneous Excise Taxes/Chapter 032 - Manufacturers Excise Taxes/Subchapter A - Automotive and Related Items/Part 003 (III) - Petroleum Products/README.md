#### Amendments

<b>2004</b>—Pub. L. 108–357, title VIII, §853(d)(2)(R), Oct. 22, 2004, 118 Stat. 1614, amended analysis generally, substituting items for subparts A “Motor and aviation fuels” and B “Special provisions applicable to fuels tax” for former items for subparts A “Gasoline and diesel fuel”, B “Aviation fuel”, and C “Special provisions applicable to petroleum products”.

<b>1993</b>—Pub. L. 103–66, title XIII, §13242(d)(43), Aug. 10, 1993, 107 Stat. 528, substituted “Gasoline and diesel fuel” for “Gasoline” in item for subpart A and “Aviation fuel” for “Diesel fuel and aviation fuel” in item for subpart B.

<b>1987</b>—Pub. L. 100–203, title X, §10502(d)(18), Dec. 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 1330–445, added item relating to subpart B.

<b>1983</b>—Pub. L. 97–424, title V, §515(b)(13), Jan. 6, 1983, 96 Stat. 2182, struck out the item for subpart B “Lubricating oil”.
