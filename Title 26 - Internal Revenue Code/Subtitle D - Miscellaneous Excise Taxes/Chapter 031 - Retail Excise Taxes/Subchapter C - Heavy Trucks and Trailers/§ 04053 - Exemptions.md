### §4053. Exemptions

No tax shall be imposed by section 4051 on any of the following articles:

#### (1) Camper coaches bodies for self-propelled mobile homes

&emsp;Any article designed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to be mounted or placed on automobile trucks, automobile truck chassis, or automobile chassis, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to be used primarily as living quarters or camping accommodations.

#### (2) Feed, seed, and fertilizer equipment

&emsp;Any body primarily designed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to process or prepare seed, feed, or fertilizer for use on farms,

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to haul feed, seed, or fertilizer to and on farms,

&emsp;&emsp;(C) to spread feed, seed, or fertilizer on farms,

&emsp;&emsp;(D) to load or unload feed, seed, or fertilizer on farms, or

&emsp;&emsp;(E) for any combination of the foregoing.

#### (3) House trailers

&emsp;Any house trailer.

#### (4) Ambulances, hearses, etc.

&emsp;Any ambulance, hearse, or combination ambulance-hearse.

#### (5) Concrete mixers

&emsp;Any article designed—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to be placed or mounted on an automobile truck chassis or truck trailer or semitrailer chassis, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to be used to process or prepare concrete.

#### (6) Trash containers, etc.

&emsp;Any box, container, receptacle, bin or other similar article—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) which is designed to be used as a trash container and is not designed for the transportation of freight other than trash, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) which is not designed to be permanently mounted on or permanently affixed to an automobile truck chassis or body.

#### (7) Rail trailers and rail vans

&emsp;Any chassis or body of a trailer or semitrailer which is designed for use both as a highway vehicle and a railroad car. For purposes of the preceding sentence, piggy-back trailer or semitrailer shall not be treated as designed for use as a railroad car.

(Added Pub. L. 97–424, title V, §512(b)(1), Jan. 6, 1983, 96 Stat. 2176; amended Pub. L. 98–369, div. A, title VII, §735(b)(1), July 18, 1984, 98 Stat. 981.)
