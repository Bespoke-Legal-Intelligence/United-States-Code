### §6244. Certain partnership provisions made applicable

The provisions of—

&emsp;(1) subchapter C which relate to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) assessing deficiencies, and filing claims for credit or refund, with respect to partnership items, and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) judicial determination of partnership items, and

&emsp;(2) so much of the other provisions of this subtitle as relate to partnership items,

> are (except to the extent modified or made inapplicable in regulations) hereby extended to and made applicable to subchapter S items.

(Added Pub. L. 97–354, §4(a), Oct. 19, 1982, 96 Stat. 1691.)
