### §20107. Definitions

As used in this chapter, the term—

&emsp;(1) "accessible" means accessible to handicapped and elderly individuals for the purpose of voting or registration, as determined under guidelines established by the chief election officer of the State involved;

&emsp;(2) "elderly" means 65 years of age or older;

&emsp;(3) "Federal election" means a general, special, primary, or runoff election for the office of President or Vice President, or of Senator or Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress;

&emsp;(4) "handicapped" means having a temporary or permanent physical disability; and

&emsp;(5) "State" means a State of the United States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and any territory or possesssion <sup>1</sup> of the United States.

(Pub. L. 98–435, §8, Sept. 28, 1984, 98 Stat. 1679.)
