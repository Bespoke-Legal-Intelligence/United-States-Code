### §1015. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Affected stakeholder

&emsp;The term "affected stakeholder" means an entity that significantly affects, or is significantly affected by, the quality or quantity of water in a watershed, as determined by the Secretary.

#### (2) Grant recipient

&emsp;The term "grant recipient" means a watershed group that the Secretary has selected toreceive a grant under section 1015a(c)(2) of this title.

#### (3) Program

&emsp;The term "program" means the Cooperative Watershed Management Program established by the Secretary under section 1015a(a) of this title.

#### (4) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

#### (5) Watershed group

&emsp;The term "watershed group" means a self-sustaining, cooperative watershed-wide group that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is comprised of representatives of the affected stakeholders of the relevant watershed;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) incorporates the perspectives of a diverse array of stakeholders, including, to the maximum extent practicable—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) representatives of—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) hydroelectric production;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) livestock grazing;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(III) timber production;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IV) land development;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(V) recreation or tourism;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VI) irrigated agricultural production;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VII) the environment;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(VIII) potable water purveyors and industrial water users; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(IX) private property owners within the watershed;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) any Federal agency that has authority with respect to the watershed;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) any State agency that has authority with respect to the watershed;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) any local agency that has authority with respect to the watershed; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) any Indian tribe that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) owns land within the watershed; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) has land in the watershed that is held in trust;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) is a grassroots, nonregulatory entity that addresses water availability and quality issues within the relevant watershed;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) is capable of promoting the sustainable use of the water resources of the relevant watershed and improving the functioning condition of rivers and streams through—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) water conservation;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) improved water quality;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) ecological resiliency; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) the reduction of water conflicts; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) makes decisions on a consensus basis, as defined in the bylaws of the watershed group.

#### (6) Watershed management project

&emsp;The term "watershed management project" means any project (including a demonstration project) that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) enhances water conservation, including alternative water uses;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) improves water quality;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) improves ecological resiliency of a river or stream;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) reduces the potential for water conflicts; or

&emsp;&emsp;(E) advances any other goals associated with water quality or quantity that the Secretary determines to be appropriate.

(Pub. L. 111–11, title VI, §6001, Mar. 30, 2009, 123 Stat. 1165.)
