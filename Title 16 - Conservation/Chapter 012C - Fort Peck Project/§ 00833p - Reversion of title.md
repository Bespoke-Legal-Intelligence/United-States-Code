### §833p. Reversion of title

All designations of Indian lands pursuant to sections 833<i>l</i> to 833p of this title shall be made subject to the condition that in the event any such lands shall no longer be required for the purposes for which they were designated, then the right, title, or interest so acquired in lands so designated shall revert to the United States in trust for the Fort Peck Indian Tribes.

(Apr. 23, 1946, ch. 199, §5, 60 Stat. 118.)

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of act May 18, 1938, which comprises this chapter.

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 833<i>l</i>, 833m, 833n, 833<i>o</i> of this title.
