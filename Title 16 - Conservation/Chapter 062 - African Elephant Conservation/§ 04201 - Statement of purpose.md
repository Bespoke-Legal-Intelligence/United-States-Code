### §4201. Statement of purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to perpetuate healthy populations of African elephants.

(Pub. L. 100–478, title II, §2002, Oct. 7, 1988, 102 Stat. 2315.)

#### Short Title

Section 2001 of title II of Pub. L. 100–478 provided that: “This title \[enacting this chapter and amending section 1538 of this title\] may be cited as the ‘African Elephant Conservation Act’.”
