### §5801. Definitions

In this chapter (unless the context otherwise requires):

#### (1) Board

&emsp;The term “Board” means the Board of Trustees established under section 5803 of this title.

#### (2) Department

&emsp;The term “Department” means the Department of Agriculture.

#### (3) Foundation

&emsp;The term “Foundation” means the National Natural Resources Conservation Foundation established by section 5802(a) of this title.

#### (4) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.

(Pub. L. 104–127, title III, §352, Apr. 4, 1996, 110 Stat. 1010.)

#### Short Title
