### §425<i>o</i>. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of sections 425k to 425<i>o</i> of this title.

(Pub. L. 101–214, §6, Dec. 11, 1989, 103 Stat. 1851.)

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 425k, 425<i>l</i>, 425m of this title.
