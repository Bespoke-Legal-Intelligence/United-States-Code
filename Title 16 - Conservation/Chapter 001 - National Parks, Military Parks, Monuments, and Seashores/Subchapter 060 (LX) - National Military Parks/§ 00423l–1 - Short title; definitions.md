### §423<i>l</i>–1. Short title; definitions

#### (a) Short title

Sections 423l–1 to 423l–6 of this title may be cited as the "Richmond National Battlefield Park Act of 2000".

#### (b) Definitions

In sections 423l–1 to 423l–6 of this title:

#### (1) Battlefield park

&emsp;The term "battlefield park" means the Richmond National Battlefield Park.

#### (2) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 106–511, title V, §501, Nov. 13, 2000, 114 Stat. 2373.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>
