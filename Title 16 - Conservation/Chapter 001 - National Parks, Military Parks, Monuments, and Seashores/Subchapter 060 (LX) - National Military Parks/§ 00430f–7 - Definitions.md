### §430f–7. Definitions

In sections 430f–6 to 430f–12 of this title:

#### (1) Map

&emsp;The term “Map” means the map entitled “Park Boundary-Corinth Unit”, numbered 304/80,007, and dated October 1998.

#### (2) Park

&emsp;The term “Park” means the Shiloh National Military Park.

#### (3) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

#### (4) Unit

&emsp;The term “Unit” means the Corinth Unit of Shiloh National Military Park established under section 430f–8 of this title.

(Pub. L. 106–271, §3, Sept. 22, 2000, 114 Stat. 793.)

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 430f–6, 430f–10, 430f–11, 430f–12 of this title.
