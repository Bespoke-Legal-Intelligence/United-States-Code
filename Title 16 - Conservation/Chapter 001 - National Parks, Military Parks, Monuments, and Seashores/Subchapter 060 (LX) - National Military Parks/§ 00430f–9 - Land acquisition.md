### §430f–9. Land acquisition

#### (a) In general

The Secretary may acquire land and interests in land within the boundary of the Park described in section 430f–8(b) of this title, by—

&emsp;(1) donation;

&emsp;(2) purchase with donated or appropriated funds; or

&emsp;(3) exchange.

#### (b) Exception

Land may be acquired only by donation from—

&emsp;(1) the State of Mississippi (including a political subdivision of the State);

&emsp;(2) the State of Tennessee (including a political subdivision of the State); or

&emsp;(3) the organization known as "Friends of the Siege and Battle of Corinth".

(Pub. L. 106–271, §5, Sept. 22, 2000, 114 Stat. 793; Pub. L. 110–161, div. F, title I, §127(3), Dec. 26, 2007, 121 Stat. 2122.)
