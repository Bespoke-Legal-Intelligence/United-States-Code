### §460hhh . Definitions

As used in this subchapter:

#### (1) National Forest lands

&emsp;The term “National Forest lands” means lands included in the National Forest System (as defined in section 1609(a) of this title).

#### (2) Recreation Area

&emsp;The term “Recreation Area” means the Spring Mountains National Recreation Area established by this subchapter.

#### (3) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.

(Pub. L. 103–63, §2, Aug. 4, 1993, 107 Stat. 297.)

#### Short Title

Section 1 of Pub. L. 103–63 provided that: “This Act \[enacting this subchapter\] may be cited as the ‘Spring Mountains National Recreation Area Act’.”
