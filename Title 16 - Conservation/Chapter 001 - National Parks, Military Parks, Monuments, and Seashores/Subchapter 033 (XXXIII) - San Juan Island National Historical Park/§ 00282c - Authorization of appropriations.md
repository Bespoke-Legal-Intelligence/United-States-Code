### §282c. Authorization of appropriations

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums, but not more than $5,575,000 for the acquisition of lands and interests thereinand for the development of the San Juan National Historical Park.<sup>1</sup>

(Pub. L. 89–565, §4, Sept. 9, 1966, 80 Stat. 737; Pub. L. 95–625, title I, §101(22), Nov. 10, 1978, 92 Stat. 3472.)

#### Amendments

1978—Pub. L. 95–625 substituted “$5,575,000” for “$3,542,000”.
