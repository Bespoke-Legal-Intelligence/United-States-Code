### §284j. Definitions

As used in this subchapter, the term—

&emsp;(1) “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

&emsp;(2) “Park” means the Wolf Trap Farm Park established under this subchapter, including the Center.

&emsp;(3) “Center” means the Filene Center in the Park. Such term includes all real property and fixtures which are within or directly related to the Filene Center.

&emsp;(4) “Foundation” means the Wolf Trap Foundation for the Performing Arts organized pursuant to the District of Columbia Nonprofit Organization Act \[D.C. Code, §29–501 et seq.\].

(Pub. L. 89–671, §11, as added Pub. L. 97–310, Oct. 14, 1982, 96 Stat. 1458.)

#### References in Text

The District of Columbia Nonprofit Corporation Act, referred to in par. (4), is Pub. L. 87–569, Aug. 6, 1962, 76 Stat. 265, as amended, which appears in chapter 5 (§29–501 et seq.) of Title 29, Corporations, of the District of Columbia Code.
