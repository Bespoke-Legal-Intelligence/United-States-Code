### §460zzz–5. Management plan

#### (a) In general

Not later than 3 years after March 30, 2009, the Secretary shall develop a comprehensive management plan for the long-term protection and management of the Conservation Area.

#### (b) Purposes

The management plan shall—

&emsp;(1) describe the appropriate uses and management of the Conservation Area;

&emsp;(2) be developed with extensive public input;

&emsp;(3) take into consideration any information developed in studies of the land within the Conservation Area; and

&emsp;(4) include a comprehensive travel management plan.

(Pub. L. 111–11, title II, §2406, Mar. 30, 2009, 123 Stat. 1107.)
