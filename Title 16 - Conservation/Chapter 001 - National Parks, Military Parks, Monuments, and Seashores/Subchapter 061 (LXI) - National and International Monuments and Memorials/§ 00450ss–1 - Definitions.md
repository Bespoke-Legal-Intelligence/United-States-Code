### §450ss–1. Definitions

In sections 450ss to 450ss–7 of this title:

#### (1) Foundation

&emsp;The term "Foundation" means the Oklahoma City National Memorial Foundation, a not-for-profit corporation that is—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) described in section 501(c)(3) of title 26;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) exempt from taxation under section 501(a) of such title; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) dedicated to the support of the Memorial.

#### (2) Memorial

&emsp;The term "Memorial" means the Oklahoma City National Memorial designated under section 450ss–2(a) of this title.

#### (3) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

#### (4) Trust

&emsp;The term "Trust" means the Oklahoma City National Memorial Trust.

(Pub. L. 105–58, §3, Oct. 9, 1997, 111 Stat. 1262; Pub. L. 108–199, div. F, title V, §544(b), Jan. 23, 2004, 118 Stat. 347.)
