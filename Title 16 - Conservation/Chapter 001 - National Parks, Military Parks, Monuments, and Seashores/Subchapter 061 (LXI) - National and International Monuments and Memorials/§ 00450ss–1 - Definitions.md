### §450ss–1. Definitions

In sections 450ss to 450ss–7 of this title:

#### (1) Memorial

&emsp;The term “Memorial” means the Oklahoma City National Memorial designated under section 450ss–2(a) of this title.

#### (2) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

#### (3) Trust

&emsp;The term “Trust” means the Oklahoma City National Memorial Trust designated under section 450ss–3(a) of this title.

(Pub. L. 105–58, §3, Oct. 9, 1997, 111 Stat. 1262.)

#### Section Referred to in Other Sections

This section is referred to in sections 450ss–2, 450ss–3, 450ss–4, 450ss–5, 450ss–7 of this title.
