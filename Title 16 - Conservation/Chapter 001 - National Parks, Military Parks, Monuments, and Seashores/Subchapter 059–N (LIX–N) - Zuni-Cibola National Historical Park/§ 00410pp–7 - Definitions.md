### §410pp–7. Definitions

As used in this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “Advisory Commission” means the Zuni-Cibola National Historical Park Advisory Commission established under section 410pp–4 of this title;

&emsp;(2) the term “park” means lands constituting a Zuni-Cibola National Historical Park established under section 410pp of this title;

&emsp;(3) the term “park plan” means the general management plan developed pursuant to section 410pp–5 of this title; and

&emsp;(4) the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 100–567, §9, Oct. 31, 1988, 102 Stat. 2851.)
