### §460<i>lll</i>–61. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) Agriculture

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Agriculture such sums as are necessary to—

&emsp;(1) permit the Secretary to exercise administrative jurisdiction over the Recreation Area under this subchapter; and

&emsp;(2) administer the Recreation Area area as a unit of the National Forest System.

#### (b) Interior

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of the Interior such sums as are necessary to carry out activities within the Recreation Area.

(Pub. L. 105–277, div. A, §101(e) \[title V, §551\], Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2681–231, 2681–326.)
