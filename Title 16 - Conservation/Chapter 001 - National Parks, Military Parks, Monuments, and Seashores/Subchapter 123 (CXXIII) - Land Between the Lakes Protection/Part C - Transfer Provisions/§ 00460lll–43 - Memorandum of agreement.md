### §460<i>lll</i>–43. Memorandum of agreement

#### (a) In general

Not later than 30 days after the date of transfer pursuant to section 460<i>lll</i>–41 of this title, theSecretary and the Tennessee Valley Authority shall enter into a memorandum of agreement concerning implementation of this subchapter.

#### (b) Provisions

The memorandum of understanding shall provide procedures for—

&emsp;(1) the orderly withdrawal of officers and employees of the Tennessee Valley Authority;

&emsp;(2) the transfer of property, fixtures, and facilities;

&emsp;(3) the interagency transfer of officers and employees;

&emsp;(4) the transfer of records; and

&emsp;(5) other transfer issues.

#### (c) Transition team

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The memorandum of understanding may provide for a transition team consisting of the Tennessee Valley Authority and Forest Service employees.

#### (2) Duration

&emsp;The team may continue in existence after the date of transfer.

#### (3) Personnel costs

&emsp;The Tennessee Valley Authority and the Forest Service shall pay personnel costs of their respective team members.

(Pub. L. 105–277, div. A, §101(e) \[title V, §543\], Oct. 21, 1998, 112 Stat. 2681–231, 2681–318.)
