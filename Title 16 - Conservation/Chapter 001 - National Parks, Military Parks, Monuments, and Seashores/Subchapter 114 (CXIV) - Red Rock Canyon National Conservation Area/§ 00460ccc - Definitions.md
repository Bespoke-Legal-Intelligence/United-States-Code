### §460ccc. Definitions

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term—

&emsp;(a) “conservation area” means the Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area established pursuant to section 460ccc–1 of this title;

&emsp;(b) “public lands” has the meaning stated in section 1702(e) of title 43; and

&emsp;(c) “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 101–621, §2, Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 3342.)

#### Short Title

Section 1 of Pub. L. 101–621 provided that: “This Act \[enacting this subchapter\] may be cited as the ‘Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area Establishment Act of 1990’.”
