### §6302. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) CITES

&emsp;The term "CITES" means the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, done at Washington March 3, 1973 (27 UST 1087; TIAS 8249), including its appendices.

#### (2) Conservation

&emsp;The term "conservation"—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) means the use of methods and procedures necessary to prevent the diminution of, and to sustain viable populations of, a species; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) includes all activities associated with wildlife management, such as—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) conservation, protection, restoration, acquisition, and management of habitat;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) in-situ research and monitoring of populations and habitats;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) assistance in the development, implementation, and improvement of management plans for managed habitat ranges;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) enforcement and implementation of CITES;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(v) enforcement and implementation of domestic laws relating to resource management;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vi) development and operation of sanctuaries for members of a species rescued from the illegal trade in live animals;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(vii) training of local law enforcement officials in the interdiction and prevention of the illegal killing of great apes;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(viii) programs for the rehabilitation of members of a species in the wild and release of the members into the wild in ways which do not threaten existing wildlife populations by causing displacement or the introduction of disease;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ix) conflict resolution initiatives;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(x) community outreach and education; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(xi) strengthening the capacity of local communities to implement conservation programs.

#### (3) Fund

&emsp;The term "Fund" means the Great Ape Conservation Fund established by section 6304 of this title.

#### (4) Great ape

&emsp;The term "great ape" means a chimpanzee, gorilla, bonobo, orangutan, or gibbon.

#### (5) Multinational Species Conservation Fund

&emsp;The term "Multinational Species Conservation Fund" means such fund as established in title I of the Department of the Interior and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 1999, under the heading "

multinational species conservation fund" \[16 U.S.C. 4246\].

#### (6) Secretary

&emsp;The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 106–411, §3, Nov. 1, 2000, 114 Stat. 1790.)
