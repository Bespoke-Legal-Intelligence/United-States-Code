### §620j. Authority of Export Administration Act of 1979

Nothing in sections 620 to 620j of this title shall be construed to—

&emsp;(1) prejudice the outcome of pending or prospective petitions filed under, or

&emsp;(2) warrant the exercise of the authority contained in,

> section 7 of the Export Administration Act of 1979 \[50 App. U.S.C. 2406\] with respect to the export of unprocessed timber.

(Pub. L. 101–382, title IV, §499, Aug. 20, 1990, 104 Stat. 726.)

This section is referred to in sections 620, 620b, 620c, 620d, 620e, 620f, 620g, 620h of this title.
