### §3344. Construction with other laws

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed—

&emsp;(1) to diminish Federal, State, or tribal jurisdiction, responsibility, or rights in the fieldof resource enhancement and management, or control of water resources, submerged lands, or navigable waters; nor to limit the authority of Congress to authorize and fund projects; or

&emsp;(2) as superseding, modifying, or repealing any existing applicable law, except as provided for in this section.

(Pub. L. 96–561, title I, §143, Dec. 22, 1980, 94 Stat. 3286.)
