### §1504. Authority of Secretary to furnish eligible owners and operators conservation materials, etc.

The Secretary is authorized to make available to eligible owners and operators conservation materials including seeds, seed inoculants, soil conditioning materials, trees, plants, and if he determines it is appropriate to the purposes of this chapter, fertilizer and liming materials.

(Pub. L. 91–524, title X, §1004, as added Pub. L. 93–86, §1(28), Aug. 10, 1973, 87 Stat. 243.)
