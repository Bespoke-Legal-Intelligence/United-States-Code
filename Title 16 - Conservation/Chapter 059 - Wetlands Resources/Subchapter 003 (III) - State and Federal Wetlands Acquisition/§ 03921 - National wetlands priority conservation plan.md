### §3921. National wetlands priority conservation plan

#### (a) In general

The Secretary shall establish, and periodically review and revise, a national wetlands priority conservation plan which shall specify, on a region-by-region basis or other basis considered appropriate by the Secretary, the types of wetlands and interests in wetlands which should be given priority with respect to Federal and State acquisition.

#### (b) Consultation

The Secretary shall establish the plan required by subsection (a) of this section after consultation with—

&emsp;(1) the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency;

&emsp;(2) the Secretary of Commerce;

&emsp;(3) the Secretary of Agriculture; and

&emsp;(4) (the chief executive officer of) each State.

#### (c) Factors to be considered

The Secretary, in establishing the plan required by subsection (a) of this section, shall consider—

&emsp;(1) the estimated proportion remaining of the respective types of wetlands which existed at the time of European settlement;

&emsp;(2) the estimated current rate of loss and the threat of future losses of the respective types of wetlands; and

&emsp;(3) the contributions of the respective types of wetlands to—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) wildlife, including endangered and threatened species, migratory birds, and resident species;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) commercial and sport fisheries;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) surface and ground water quality and quantity, and flood control;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) outdoor recreation; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) other areas or concerns the Secretary considers appropriate.

(Pub. L. 99–645, title III, §301, Nov. 10, 1986, 100 Stat. 3586.)
