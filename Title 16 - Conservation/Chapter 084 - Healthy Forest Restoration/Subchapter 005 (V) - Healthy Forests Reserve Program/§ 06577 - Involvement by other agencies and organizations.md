### §6577. Involvement by other agencies and organizations

In carrying out this subchapter, the Secretary of Agriculture may consult with—

&emsp;(1) nonindustrial private forest landowners;

&emsp;(2) other Federal agencies;

&emsp;(3) State fish and wildlife agencies;

&emsp;(4) State forestry agencies;

&emsp;(5) State environmental quality agencies;

&emsp;(6) other State conservation agencies; and

&emsp;(7) nonprofit conservation organizations.

(Pub. L. 108–148, title V, §507, Dec. 3, 2003, 117 Stat. 1914.)
