### §4902. Statement of purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to promote the conservation of exotic birds by—

&emsp;(1) assisting wild bird conservation and management programs in the countries of origin of wild birds;

&emsp;(2) ensuring that all trade in species of exotic birds involving the United States is biologically sustainable and is not detrimental to the species;

&emsp;(3) limiting or prohibiting imports of exotic birds when necessary to ensure that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) wild exotic bird populations are not harmed by removal of exotic birds from the wild for the trade; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) exotic birds in trade are not subject to inhumane treatment; and

&emsp;(4) encouraging and supporting effective implementation of the Convention.

(Pub. L. 102–440, title I, §103, Oct. 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 2225.)
