### §5603. Authorities of Secretary of State with respect to Convention

The Secretary of State may, on behalf of the Government of the United States—

&emsp;(1) receive and transmit reports, requests, recommendations, proposals, and other communications of and to the Organization and its subsidiary organs;

&emsp;(2) object, or withdraw an objection, to the proposal of the Fisheries Commission;

&emsp;(3) give or withdraw notice of intent not to be bound by a measure of the Fisheries Commission;

&emsp;(4) object or withdraw an objection to an amendment to the Convention; and

&emsp;(5) act upon, or refer to any other appropriate authority, any other communication referred to in paragraph (1).

(Pub. L. 104–43, title II, §204, Nov. 3, 1995, 109 Stat. 379.)
