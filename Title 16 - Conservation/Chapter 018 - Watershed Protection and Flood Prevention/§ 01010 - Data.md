### §1010. Data

The Secretary shall collect and maintain data on a national and State by State basis concerning—

&emsp;(1) expenditures for the individual flood control and conservation measures for which assistance is provided under this chapter; and

&emsp;(2) the expected flood control or environmental (including soil erosion) benefits that will result from the implementation of such measures.

(Aug. 4, 1954, ch. 656, §13, as added Nov. 28, 1990, Pub. L. 101–624, title XIV, §1463, 104 Stat. 3615.)
