### §6102. Purposes

The purposes of this chapter are—

&emsp;(1) to perpetuate healthy populations of neotropical migratory birds;

&emsp;(2) to assist in the conservation of neotropical migratory birds by supporting conservation initiatives in the United States, Latin America, and the Caribbean; and

&emsp;(3) to provide financial resources and to foster international cooperation for those initiatives.

(Pub. L. 106–247, §3, July 20, 2000, 114 Stat. 593.)
