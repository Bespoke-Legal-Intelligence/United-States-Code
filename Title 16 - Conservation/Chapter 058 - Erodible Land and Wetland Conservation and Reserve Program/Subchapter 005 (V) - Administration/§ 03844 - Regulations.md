### §3844. Regulations

Not later than 180 days after December 23, 1985, the Secretary shall issue such regulations as the Secretary determines are necessary to carry out subchapters I through V of this chapter, including regulations that—

&emsp;(1) define the term “person”;

&emsp;(2) govern the determination of persons who shall be ineligible for program benefits under subchapters II and III of this chapter, so as to ensure a fair and reasonable determination of ineligibility; and

&emsp;(3) protect the interests of landlords, tenants, and sharecroppers.

(Pub. L. 99–198, title XII, §1244, Dec. 23, 1985, 99 Stat. 1515.)
