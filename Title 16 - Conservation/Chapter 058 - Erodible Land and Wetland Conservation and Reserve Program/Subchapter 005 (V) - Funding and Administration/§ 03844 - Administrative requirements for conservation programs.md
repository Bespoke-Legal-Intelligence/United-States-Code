### §3844. Administrative requirements for conservation programs

#### (a) Beginning farmers and ranchers and Indian tribes

In carrying out any conservation program administered by the Secretary, the Secretary may provide to beginning farmers and ranchers andIndian tribes (as those terms are defined in section 3838 of this title) and limited resource agricultural producers incentives to participate in the conservation program to—

&emsp;(1) foster new farming and ranching opportunities; and

&emsp;(2) enhance environmental stewardship over the long term.

#### (b) Privacy of personal information relating to natural resources conservation programs

#### (1) Information received for technical and financial assistance

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;In accordance with section 552(b)(3) of title 5, except as provided in subparagraph (C) and paragraph (2), information described in subparagraph (B)—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) shall not be considered to be public information; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) shall not be released to any person or Federal, State, local agency or Indian tribe (as defined by the Secretary) outside the Department of Agriculture.

#### (B) Information

&emsp;&emsp;The information referred to in subparagraph (A) is information—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) provided to the Secretary or a contractor of the Secretary (including information provided under subchapter IV of this chapter) for the purpose of providing technical or financial assistance to an owner, operator, or producer with respect to any natural resources conservation program administered by the Natural Resources Conservation Service or the Farm Service Agency; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) that is proprietary (within the meaning of section 552(b)(4) of title 5) to the agricultural operation or land that is a part of an agricultural operation of the owner, operator, or producer.

#### (C) Exception

&emsp;&emsp;Nothing in this section affects the availability of payment information (including payment amounts and the names and addresses of recipients of payments) under section 552 of title 5.

#### (2) Exceptions

#### (A) Release and disclosure for enforcement

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may release or disclose to the Attorney General information covered by paragraph (1) to the extent necessary to enforce the natural resources conservation programs referred to in paragraph (1)(B)(i).

#### (B) Disclosure to cooperating persons and agencies

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary may release or disclose information covered by paragraph (1) to a person or Federal, State, local, or tribal agency working in cooperation with the Secretary in providing technical and financial assistance described in paragraph (1)(B)(i) or collecting information from data gathering sites.

#### (ii) Use of information

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The person or Federal, State, local, or tribal agency that receives information described in clause (i) may release the information only for the purpose of assisting the Secretary—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) in providing the requested technical or financial assistance; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) in collecting information from data gathering sites.

#### (C) Statistical and aggregate information

&emsp;&emsp;Information covered by paragraph (1) may be disclosed to the public if the information has been transformed into a statistical or aggregate form without naming any—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) individual owner, operator, or producer; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) specific data gathering site.

#### (D) Consent of owner, operator, or producer

#### (i) In general

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;An owner, operator, or producer may consent to the disclosure of information described in paragraph (1).

#### (ii) Condition of other programs

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The participation of the owner, operator, or producer in, and the receipt of any benefit by the owner, operator, or producer under, this chapter or any other program administered by the Secretary may not be conditioned on the owner, operator, or producer providing consent under this paragraph.

#### (3) Violations; penalties

&emsp;Section 2276(c) of title 7 shall apply with respect to the release of information collected in any manner or for any purpose prohibited by this subsection.

#### (4) Data collection, disclosure, and review

&emsp;Nothing in this subsection—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) affects any procedure for data collection or disclosure through the National Resources Inventory; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) limits the authority of Congress or the Government Accountability Office to review information collected or disclosed under this subsection.
