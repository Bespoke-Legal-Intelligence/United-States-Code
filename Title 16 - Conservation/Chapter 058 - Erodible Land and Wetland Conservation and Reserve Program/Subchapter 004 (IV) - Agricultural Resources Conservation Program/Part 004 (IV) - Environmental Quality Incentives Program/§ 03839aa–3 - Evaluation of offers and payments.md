### §3839aa–3. Evaluation of offers and payments

In evaluating applications for cost-share payments and incentive payments, the Secretary shall accord a higher priority to assistance and payments that—

&emsp;(1) encourage the use by producers of cost-effective conservation practices; and

&emsp;(2) address national conservation priorities.

(Pub. L. 99–198, title XII, §1240C, as added Pub. L. 107–171, title II, §2301, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 256.)

#### Prior Provisions
