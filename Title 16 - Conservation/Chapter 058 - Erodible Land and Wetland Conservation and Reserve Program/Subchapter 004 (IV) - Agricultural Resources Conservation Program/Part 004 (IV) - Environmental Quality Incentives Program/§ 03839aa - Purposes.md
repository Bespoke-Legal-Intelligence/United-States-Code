### §3839aa. Purposes

The purposes of the environmental quality incentives program established by this part are to promote agricultural production and environmental quality as compatible goals, and to optimize environmental benefits, by—

&emsp;(1) assisting producers in complying with local, State, and national regulatory requirements concerning—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) soil, water, and air quality;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) wildlife habitat; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) surface and ground water conservation;

&emsp;(2) avoiding, to the maximum extent practicable, the need for resource and regulatory programs by assisting producers in protecting soil, water, air, and related natural resources and meeting environmental quality criteria established by Federal, State, tribal, and local agencies;

&emsp;(3) providing flexible assistance to producers to install and maintain conservation practices that enhance soil, water, related natural resources (including grazing land and wetland), and wildlife while sustaining production of food and fiber;

&emsp;(4) assisting producers to make beneficial, cost effective changes to cropping systems,grazing management, nutrient management associated with livestock, pest or irrigation management, or other practices on agricultural land; and

&emsp;(5) consolidating and streamlining conservation planning and regulatory compliance processes to reduce administrative burdens on producers and the cost of achieving environmental goals.

(Pub. L. 99–198, title XII, §1240, as added Pub. L. 107–171, title II, §2301, May 13, 2002, 116 Stat. 253.)
