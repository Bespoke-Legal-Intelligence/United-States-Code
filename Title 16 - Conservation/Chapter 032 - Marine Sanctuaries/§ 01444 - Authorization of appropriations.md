### §1444. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this chapter the following—

&emsp;(1) $8,000,000 for fiscal year 1993;

&emsp;(2) $12,500,000 for fiscal year 1994;

&emsp;(3) $15,000,000 for fiscal year 1995; and

&emsp;(4) $20,000,000 for fiscal year 1996.

(Pub. L. 92–532, title III, §313, as added Pub. L. 100–627, title II, §208, Nov. 7, 1988, 102 Stat. 3221; amended Pub. L. 101–605, §10(a), Nov. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 3095; Pub. L. 102–587, title II, §2111, Nov. 4, 1992, 106 Stat. 5046.)

#### Amendments

1992—Pub. L. 102–587 amended section generally, substituting provisions relating to authorization of appropriations for fiscal years 1993 to 1996 to carry out this chapter for provisions relating to authorization of appropriations for fiscal years 1989 to 1992 to carry out general administration, management of national marine sanctuaries and site review and analysis of national marine sanctuaries of this chapter.

1990—Par. (2)(C). Pub. L. 101–605 substituted “$4,000,000” for “$3,000,000”.
