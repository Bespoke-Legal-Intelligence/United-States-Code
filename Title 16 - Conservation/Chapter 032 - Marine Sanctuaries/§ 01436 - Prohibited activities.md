### §1436. Prohibited activities

It is unlawful to—

&emsp;(1) destroy, cause the loss of, or injure any sanctuary resource managed under law or regulations for that sanctuary;

&emsp;(2) possess, sell, deliver, carry, transport, or ship by any means any sanctuary resource taken in violation of this section;

&emsp;(3) interfere with the enforcement of this chapter; or

&emsp;(4) violate any provision of this chapter or any regulation or permit issued pursuant to this chapter.

(Pub. L. 92–532, title III, §306, as added Pub. L. 98–498, title I, §102, Oct. 19, 1984, 98 Stat. 2302; amended Pub. L. 102–587, title II, §2106, Nov. 4, 1992, 106 Stat. 5043.)

#### Amendments

1992—Pub. L. 102–587 amended section generally. Prior to amendment, section read as follows: “The Secretary shall conduct research and educational programs as are necessary and reasonable to carry out the purposes and policies of this chapter.”
