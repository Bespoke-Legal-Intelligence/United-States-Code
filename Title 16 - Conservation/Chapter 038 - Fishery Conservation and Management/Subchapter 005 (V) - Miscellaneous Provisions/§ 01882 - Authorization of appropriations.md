### §1882. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary, for purposes of carrying out the provisions of this chapter, not to exceed the following sums:

&emsp;(1) $5,000,000 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1976.

&emsp;(2) $5,000,000 for the transitional fiscal quarter ending September 30, 1976.

&emsp;(3) $25,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1977.

&emsp;(4) $30,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978.

&emsp;(5) $30,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1979.

&emsp;(6) $33,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1980.

&emsp;(7) $40,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1981.

&emsp;(8) $47,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1982.

&emsp;(9) $59,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1983.

&emsp;(10) $64,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1984.

&emsp;(11) $69,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1985.

&emsp;(12) $69,000,000 for fiscal year 1986.

&emsp;(13) $70,800,000 for fiscal year 1987.

&emsp;(14) $72,900,000 for fiscal year 1988.

&emsp;(15) $75,000,000 for fiscal year 1989.

&emsp;(16) $77,200,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1990.

&emsp;(17) $94,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1991, of which $6,500,000 shall be used for enforcement and $5,000,000 shall be used to increase research and assessment efforts.

&emsp;(18) $98,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1992.

&emsp;(19) $102,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1993.

(Pub. L. 94–265, title IV, §406, Apr. 13, 1976, 90 Stat. 361; Pub. L. 95–354, §1, Aug. 28, 1978, 92 Stat. 519; Pub. L. 96–61, §1, Aug. 15, 1979, 93 Stat. 407; Pub. #### References in Text

This chapter, referred to in text, was in the original “this Act”, meaning Pub. L. 94–265, Apr. 13, 1976, 90 Stat. 331, as amended, known as the Magnuson Fishery Conservation and Management Act, which is classified principally to this chapter. For complete classificationof this Act to the Code, see Short Title note set out under section 1801 of this title and Tables.
