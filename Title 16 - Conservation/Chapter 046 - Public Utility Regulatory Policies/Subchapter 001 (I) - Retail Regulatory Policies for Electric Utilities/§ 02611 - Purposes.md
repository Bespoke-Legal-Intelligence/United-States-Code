### §2611. Purposes

The purposes of this chapter are to encourage—

&emsp;(1) conservation of energy supplied by electric utilities;

&emsp;(2) the optimization of the efficiency of use of facilities and resources by electric utilities; and

&emsp;(3) equitable rates to electric consumers.

(Pub. L. 95–617, title I, §101, Nov. 9, 1978, 92 Stat. 3120.)

#### References in Text

This chapter, referred to in text, was in the original “this title”, meaning title I (§101 et seq.) of Pub. L. 95–617, Nov. 9, 1978, 92 Stat. 3120, which enacted this chapter and amended sections 6801 to 6808 of Title 42, The Public Health and Welfare.
