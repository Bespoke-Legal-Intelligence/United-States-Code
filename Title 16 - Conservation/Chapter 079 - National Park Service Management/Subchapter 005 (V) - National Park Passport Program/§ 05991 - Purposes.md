### §5991. Purposes

The purposes of this subchapter are—

&emsp;(1) to develop a national park passport that includes a collectible stamp to be used for admission to units of the National Park System; and

&emsp;(2) to generate revenue for support of the National Park System.

(Pub. L. 105–391, title VI, §601, Nov. 13, 1998, 112 Stat. 3518.)
