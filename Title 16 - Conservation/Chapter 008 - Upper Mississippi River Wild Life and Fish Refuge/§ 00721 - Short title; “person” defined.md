### §721. Short title; “person” defined

This chapter may be cited as “The Upper Mississippi River Wild Life and Fish Refuge Act.”The term “person” as used therein includes an individual, partnership, association, or corporation.

(June 7, 1924, ch. 346, §§1, 12, 43 Stat. 650, 652.)

#### Codification

Section is a combination provision, the first sentence being derived from section 1 and the last from section 12, of act June 7, 1924.
