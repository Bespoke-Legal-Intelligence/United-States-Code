### §3459. Limitation on assistance

The program provided for in this subchapter shall be limited to providing technical and financial assistance to not more than 450 active designated areas.

(Pub. L. 97–98, title XV, §1536, Dec. 22, 1981, 95 Stat. 1340; Pub. L. 101–624, title XIV, §1452(a), Nov. 28, 1990, 104 Stat. 3611.)

#### Amendments

1990—Pub. L. 101–624 substituted “450” for “two hundred and twenty-five”.
