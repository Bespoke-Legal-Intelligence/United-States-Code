### §3460. Supplemental authority of Secretary

The authority of the Secretary under this subchapter to assist States, local units of government, and local nonprofit organizations in the development and implementation of area plans shall be supplemental to, and not in lieu of, any authority of the Secretary under any other provision of law.

(Pub. L. 97–98, title XV, §1537, Dec. 22, 1981, 95 Stat. 1340.)
