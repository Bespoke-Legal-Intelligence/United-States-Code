### §6851. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Federal land

&emsp;The term "Federal land" means any land managed by a Federal land management agency.

#### (2) Federal land management agency

&emsp;The term "Federal land management agency" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Bureau of Reclamation;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the National Park Service;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Bureau of Land Management;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the United States Fish and Wildlife Service;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) the Forest Service; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) the Corps of Engineers.

#### (3) Road or trail

&emsp;The term "road or trail" means a road or trail designated by 1 or more of the Secretaries for public use.

#### (4) Secretaries

&emsp;The term "Secretaries" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Secretary of the Interior; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Civil Works.

(Pub. L. 117–114, §2, Apr. 29, 2022, 136 Stat. 1175.)

#### <b>Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries</b>

#### Short Title

Pub. L. 117–114, §1, Apr. 29, 2022, 136 Stat. 1175, provided that: "This Act \[enacting this chapter\] may be cited as the 'Modernizing Access to Our Public Land Act'."
