### §6856. Reports

Not later than 1 year after April 29, 2022, and annually thereafter through March 30, 2031, the Secretaries shall submit a report on the progress made by the Secretaries with respect to meeting the requirements of this chapter to—

&emsp;(1) the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the Senate;

&emsp;(2) the Committee on Environment and Public Works of the Senate;

&emsp;(3) the Committee on Natural Resources of the House of Representatives; and

&emsp;(4) the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of Representatives.

(Pub. L. 117–114, §7, Apr. 29, 2022, 136 Stat. 1177.)
