### §3736. Certiorari—(Rule)

#### See Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure

Petition to Supreme Court, time, Rule 37(b).

(June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 845.)

#### References in Text

Rule 37 of the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure was abrogated Dec. 4, 1967, eff. July 1, 1968. Provisionsof such former rule for certiorari are covered by rule 19 et seq. of the Rules of the United States Supreme Court, set out in the Appendix to Title 28, Judiciary and Judicial Procedure.
