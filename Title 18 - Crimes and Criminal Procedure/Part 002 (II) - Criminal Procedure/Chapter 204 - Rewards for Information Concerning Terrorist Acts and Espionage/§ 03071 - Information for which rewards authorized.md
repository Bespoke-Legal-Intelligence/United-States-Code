### §3071. Information for which rewards authorized

(a) With respect to acts of terrorism primarily within the territorial jurisdiction of the UnitedStates, the Attorney General may reward any individual who furnishes information—

&emsp;(1) leading to the arrest or conviction, in any country, of any individual or individuals for the commission of an act of terrorism against a United States person or United States property; or

&emsp;(2) leading to the arrest or conviction, in any country, of any individual or individuals for conspiring or attempting to commit an act of terrorism against a United States person or property; or

&emsp;(3) leading to the prevention, frustration, or favorable resolution of an act of terrorism against a United States person or property.

(b) With respect to acts of espionage involving or directed at the United States, the Attorney General may reward any individual who furnishes information—

&emsp;(1) leading to the arrest or conviction, in any country, of any individual or individuals for commission of an act of espionage against the United States;

&emsp;(2) leading to the arrest or conviction, in any country, of any individual or individuals for conspiring or attempting to commit an act of espionage against the United States; or

&emsp;(3) leading to the prevention or frustration of an act of espionage against the United States.

(Added Pub. L. 98–533, title I, §101(a), Oct. 19, 1984, 98 Stat. 2706; amended Pub. L. 103–359, title VIII, §803(a), Oct. 14, 1994, 108 Stat. 3438.)

#### Amendments

1994—Pub. L. 103–359 designated existing provisions as subsec. (a) and added subsec. (b).
