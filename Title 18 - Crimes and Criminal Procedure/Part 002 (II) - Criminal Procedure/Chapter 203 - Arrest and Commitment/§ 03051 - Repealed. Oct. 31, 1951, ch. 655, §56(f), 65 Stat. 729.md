### \[§3051. Repealed. Oct. 31, 1951, ch. 655, §56(f), 65 Stat. 729\]

Section, act June 25, 1948, ch. 645, §1, 62 Stat. 817, related to powers of extradition agents. Substantially identical provisions are contained in section 3193 of this title.

#### Savings Provision

Subsec. (<i>l</i>) of section 56 of act Oct. 31, 1951, ch. 655, 65 Stat. 730, provided that the repeal of this section should not affect any rights or liabilities existing hereunder on the effective date of the repeal (Oct. 31, 1951).
