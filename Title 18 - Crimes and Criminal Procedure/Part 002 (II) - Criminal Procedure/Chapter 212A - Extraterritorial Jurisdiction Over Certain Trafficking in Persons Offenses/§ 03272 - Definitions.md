### §3272. Definitions

As used in this chapter:

&emsp;(1) The term "employed by the Federal Government outside the United States" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) employed as a civilian employee of the Federal Government, as a Federal contractor (including a subcontractor at any tier), or as an employee of a Federal contractor (including a subcontractor at any tier);

&emsp;&emsp;(B) present or residing outside the United States in connection with such employment; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) not a national of or ordinarily resident in the host nation.

&emsp;(2) The term "accompanying the Federal Government outside the United States" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a dependant of—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a civilian employee of the Federal Government; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a Federal contractor (including a subcontractor at any tier) or an employee of a Federal contractor (including a subcontractor at any tier);

&emsp;&emsp;(B) residing with such civilian employee, contractor, or contractor employee outside the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) not a national of or ordinarily resident in the host nation.

(Added Pub. L. 109–164, title I, §103(a)(1), Jan. 10, 2006, 119 Stat. 3562.)
