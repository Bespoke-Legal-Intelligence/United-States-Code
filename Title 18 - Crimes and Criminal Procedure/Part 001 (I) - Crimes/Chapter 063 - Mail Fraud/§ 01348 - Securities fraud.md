### §1348. Securities fraud

Whoever knowingly executes, or attempts to execute, a scheme or artifice—

&emsp;(1) to defraud any person in connection with any security of an issuer with a class of securities registered under section 12 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78<i>l</i>) or that is required to file reports under section 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78<i>o</i>(d)); or

&emsp;(2) to obtain, by means of false or fraudulent pretenses, representations, or promises, any money or property in connection with the purchase or sale of any security of an issuer with a class of securities registered under section 12 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78<i>l</i>) or that is required to file reports under section 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78<i>o</i>(d));

> shall be fined under this title, or imprisoned not more than 25 years, or both.

(Added Pub. L. 107–204, title VIII, §807(a), July 30, 2002, 116 Stat. 804.)
