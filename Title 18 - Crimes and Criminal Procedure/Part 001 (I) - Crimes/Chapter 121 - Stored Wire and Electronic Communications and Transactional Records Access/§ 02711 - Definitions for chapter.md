### §2711. Definitions for chapter

As used in this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the terms defined in section 2510 of this title have, respectively, the definitions given such terms in that section; and

&emsp;(2) the term “remote computing service” means the provision to the public of computer storage or processing services by means of an electronic communications system.

(Added Pub. L. 99–508, title II, §201\[(a)\], Oct. 21, 1986, 100 Stat. 1868, §2710; renumbered §2711, Pub. L. 100–618, §2(a)(1), Nov. 5, 1988, 102 Stat. 3195.)

#### Amendments
