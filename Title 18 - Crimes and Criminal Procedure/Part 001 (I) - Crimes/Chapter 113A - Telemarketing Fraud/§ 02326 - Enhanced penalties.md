### §2326. Enhanced penalties

A person who is convicted of an offense under section 1028, 1029, 1341, 1342, 1343, or 1344 in connection with the conduct of telemarketing—

&emsp;(1) may be imprisoned for a term of up to 5 years in addition to any term of imprisonment imposed under any of those sections, respectively; and

&emsp;(2) in the case of an offense under any of those sections that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) victimized ten or more persons over the age of 55; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) targeted persons over the age of 55,

> &emsp;may be imprisoned for a term of up to 10 years in addition to any term of imprisonment imposed under any of those sections, respectively.

(Added Pub. L. 103–322, title XXV, §250002(a)(2), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2082.)
