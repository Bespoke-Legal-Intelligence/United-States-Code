### §178. Definitions

As used in this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “biological agent” means any micro-organism, virus, or infectious substance, capable of causing—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) death, disease, or other biological malfunction in a human, an animal, a plant, or another living organism;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) deterioration of food, water, equipment, supplies, or material of any kind; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) deleterious alteration of the environment;

&emsp;(2) the term “toxin” means, whatever its origin or method of production—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any poisonous substance produced by a living organism; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any poisonous isomer, homolog, or derivative of such a substance;

&emsp;(3) the term “delivery system” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) any apparatus, equipment, device, or means of delivery specifically designed to deliver or disseminate a biological agent, toxin, or vector; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) any vector; and

&emsp;(4) the term “vector” means a living organism capable of carrying a biological agent or toxin to a host.

(Added Pub. L. 101–298, §3(a), May 22, 1990, 104 Stat. 202.)
