### §926B. Carrying of concealed firearms by qualified law enforcement officers

(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of the law of any State or any political subdivision thereof, an individual who is a qualified law enforcement officer and who is carrying the identification required by subsection (d) may carry a concealed firearm that has been shipped or transported in interstate or foreign commerce, subject to subsection (b).

(b) This section shall not be construed to supersede or limit the laws of any State that—

&emsp;(1) permit private persons or entities to prohibit or restrict the possession of concealed firearms on their property; or

&emsp;(2) prohibit or restrict the possession of firearms on any State or local government property, installation, building, base, or park.

(c) As used in this section, the term “qualified law enforcement officer” means an employee of a governmental agency who—

&emsp;(1) is authorized by law to engage in or supervise the prevention, detection, investigation, or prosecution of, or the incarceration of any person for, any violation of law, and has statutory powers of arrest;

&emsp;(2) is authorized by the agency to carry a firearm;

&emsp;(3) is not the subject of any disciplinary action by the agency;

&emsp;(4) meets standards, if any, established by the agency which require the employee to regularly qualify in the use of a firearm;

&emsp;(5) is not under the influence of alcohol or another intoxicating or hallucinatory drug or substance; and

&emsp;(6) is not prohibited by Federal law from receiving a firearm.

(d) The identification required by this subsection is the photographic identification issued by the governmental agency for which the individual is employed as a law enforcement officer.

(e) As used in this section, the term “firearm” does not include—

&emsp;(1) any machinegun (as defined in section 5845 of the National Firearms Act);

&emsp;(2) any firearm silencer (as defined in section 921 of this title); and

&emsp;(3) any destructive device (as defined in section 921 of this title).

(Added Pub. L. 108–277, §2(a), July 22, 2004, 118 Stat. 865.)

#### References in Text

Section 5845 of the National Firearms Act, referred to in subsec. (e)(1), is classified to section 5845 of Title 26, Internal Revenue Code.
