### §1120. Murder by escaped prisoners

(a)

Definition.—In this section, “Federal prison” and “term of life imprisonment” have the meanings stated in section 1118.<sup>1</sup>

(b)

Offense and Penalty.—A person, having escaped from a Federal prison where the person was confined under a sentence for a term of life imprisonment, kills another shall be punished as provided in sections 1111 and 1112.

(Added Pub. L. 103–322, title VI, §60012(a), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1973.)

#### References in Text

Section 1118, referred to in subsec. (a), probably means the section 1118 of this title which was added by section 60005(a) of Pub. L. 103–322.
