### §1602. Definitions

As used in this chapter:

#### (1) Biomaterials supplier

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “biomaterials supplier” means an entity that directly or indirectly supplies a component part or raw material for use in the manufacture of an implant.

#### (B) Persons included

&emsp;&emsp;Such term includes any person who—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) has submitted master files to the Secretary for purposes of premarket approval of a medical device; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) licenses a biomaterials supplier to produce component parts or raw materials.

#### (2) Claimant

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “claimant” means any person who brings a civil action, or on whose behalf a civil action is brought, arising from harm allegedly caused directly or indirectly by an implant, including a person other than the individual into whose body, or in contact with whose blood or tissue, the implant is placed, who claims to have suffered harm as a result of the implant.

#### (B) Action brought on behalf of an estate

&emsp;&emsp;With respect to an action brought on behalf of or through the estate of a deceased individual into whose body, or in contact with whose blood or tissue the implant was placed, such term includes the decedent that is the subject of the action.

#### (C) Action brought on behalf of a minor or incompetent

&emsp;&emsp;With respect to an action brought on behalf of or through a minor or incompetent, such term includes the parent or guardian of the minor or incompetent.

#### (D) Exclusions

&emsp;&emsp;Such term does not include—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a provider of professional health care services in any case in which—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) the sale or use of an implant is incidental to such services; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) the essence of the professional health care services provided is the furnishing of judgment, skill, or services;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a person acting in the capacity of a manufacturer, seller, or biomaterials supplier; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) a person alleging harm caused by either the silicone gel or the silicone envelope utilized in a breast implant containing silicone gel, except that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) neither the exclusion provided by this clause nor any other provision of this chapter may be construed as a finding that silicone gel (or any other form of silicone) may or may not cause harm; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) the existence of the exclusion under this clause may not—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(aa) be disclosed to a jury in any civil action or other proceeding; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(bb) except as necessary to establish the applicability of this chapter, otherwise be presented in any civil action or other proceeding.

#### (3) Component part

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “component part” means a manufactured piece of an implant.

#### (B) Certain components

&emsp;&emsp;Such term includes a manufactured piece of an implant that—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) has significant non-implant applications; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) alone, has no implant value or purpose, but when combined with other component parts and materials, constitutes an implant.

#### (4) Harm

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “harm” means—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) any injury to or damage suffered by an individual;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) any illness, disease, or death of that individual resulting from that injury or damage; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) any loss to that individual or any other individual resulting from that injury or damage.

#### (B) Exclusion

&emsp;&emsp;The term does not include any commercial loss or loss of or damage to an implant.

#### (5) Implant

&emsp;The term “implant” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a medical device that is intended by the manufacturer of the device—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) to be placed into a surgically or naturally formed or existing cavity of the body for a period of at least 30 days; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) to remain in contact with bodily fluids or internal human tissue through a surgically produced opening for a period of less than 30 days; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) suture materials used in implant procedures.

#### (6) Manufacturer

&emsp;The term “manufacturer” means any person who, with respect to an implant—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) is engaged in the manufacture, preparation, propagation, compounding, or processing (as defined in section 360(a)(1) of this title) of the implant; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) is required—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) to register with the Secretary pursuant to section 360 of this title and the regulations issued under such section; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) to include the implant on a list of devices filed with the Secretary pursuant to section 360(j) of this title and the regulations issued under such section.

#### (7) Medical device

&emsp;The term “medical device” means a device, as defined in section 321(h) of this title, and includes any device component of any combination product as that term is used in section 353(g) of this title.

#### (8) Raw material

&emsp;The term “raw material” means a substance or product that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) has a generic use; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) may be used in an application other than an implant.

#### (9) Secretary

&emsp;The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.

#### (10) Seller

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term “seller” means a person who, in the course of a business conducted for that purpose, sells, distributes, leases, packages, labels, or otherwise places an implant in the stream of commerce.

#### (B) Exclusions

&emsp;&emsp;The term does not include—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) a seller or lessor of real property;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) a provider of professional health care services in any case in which—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(I) the sale or use of the implant is incidental to such services; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(II) the essence of the professional health care services provided is the furnishing of judgment, skill, or services; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) any person who acts in only a financial capacity with respect to the sale of an implant.

(Pub. L. 105–230, §3, Aug. 13, 1998, 112 Stat. 1520.)
