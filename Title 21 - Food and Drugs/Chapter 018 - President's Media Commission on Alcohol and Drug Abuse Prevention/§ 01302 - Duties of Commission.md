### §1302. Duties of Commission

The Commission shall—

&emsp;(1) examine public education programs in effect on October 27, 1986, which are—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) implemented through various segments of mass media; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) intended to prevent alcohol and drug abuse;

&emsp;(2) act as an administrative and coordinating body for the voluntary donation of resources from—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) television, radio, motion picture, cable communications, and print media;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the recording industry;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the advertising industry;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the business sector of the United States; and

&emsp;&emsp;(E) professional sports organizations and associations;

> &emsp;to assist the implementation of new programs and national strategies for dissemination of information intended to prevent alcohol and drug abuse;

&emsp;(3) encourage media outlets throughout the country to provide information aimed at preventing alcohol and drug abuse, including public service announcements, documentary films, and advertisements; and

&emsp;(4) evaluate the effectiveness and assist in the update of programs and national strategies formulated with the assistance of the Commission.

(Pub. L. 99–570, title VIII, §8003, Oct. 27, 1986, 100 Stat. 3207–161.)
