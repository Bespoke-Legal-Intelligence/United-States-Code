### §114e. Control and eradication of cattle grubs; research and investigations

In order to protect, promote, and conserve livestock and livestock products and to minimize losses, the Secretary of Agriculture, either independently or in cooperation with States or subdivisions thereof, farmers’ associations, and other organizations and individuals, it <sup>1</sup> is authorized to increase and intensify research and investigations into problems and methods relating to the eradication of cattle grubs and to undertake measures to eradicate these parasites.

(June 16, 1948, ch. 477, §1, 62 Stat. 458.)
