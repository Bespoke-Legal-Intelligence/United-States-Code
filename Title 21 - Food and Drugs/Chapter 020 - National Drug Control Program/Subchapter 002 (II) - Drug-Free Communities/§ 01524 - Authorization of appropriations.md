### §1524. Authorization of appropriations

#### (a) In general

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Office of National Drug Control Policy to carry out this subchapter—

&emsp;(1) $10,000,000 for fiscal year 1998;

&emsp;(2) $20,000,000 for fiscal year 1999;

&emsp;(3) $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2000;

&emsp;(4) $40,000,000 for fiscal year 2001; and

&emsp;(5) $43,500,000 for fiscal year 2002.

#### (b) Administrative costs

Not more than the following percentages of the amounts authorized under subsection (a) of this section may be used to pay administrative costs:

&emsp;(1) 10 percent for fiscal year 1998.

&emsp;(2) 6 percent for fiscal year 1999.

&emsp;(3) 4 percent for fiscal year 2000.

&emsp;(4) 3 percent for fiscal year 2001.

&emsp;(5) 3 percent for fiscal year 2002.

(Pub. L. 100–690, title I, §1024, as added Pub. L. 105–20, §2(a)(2), June 27, 1997, 111 Stat. 226.)
