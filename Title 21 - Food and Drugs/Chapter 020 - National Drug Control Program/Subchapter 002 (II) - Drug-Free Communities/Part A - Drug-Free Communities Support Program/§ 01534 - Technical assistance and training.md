### §1534. Technical assistance and training

#### (a) In general

#### (1) Technical assistance and agreements

&emsp;With respect to any grant recipient or other organization, the Administrator may—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) offer technical assistance and training; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) enter into contracts and cooperative agreements.

#### (2) Coordination of programs

&emsp;The Administrator may facilitate the coordination of programs between a grant recipient and other organizations and entities.

#### (b) Training

The Administrator may provide training to any representative designated by a grant recipient in—

&emsp;(1) coalition building;

&emsp;(2) task force development;

&emsp;(3) mediation and facilitation, direct service, assessment and evaluation; or

&emsp;(4) any other activity related to the purposes of the Program.

(Pub. L. 100–690, title I, §1034, as added Pub. L. 105–20, §2(a)(2), June 27, 1997, 111 Stat. 231.)
