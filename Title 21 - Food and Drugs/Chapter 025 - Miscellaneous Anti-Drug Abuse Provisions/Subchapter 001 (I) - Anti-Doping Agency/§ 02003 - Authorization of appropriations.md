### §2003. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated to the United States Anti-Doping Agency—

&emsp;(1) for fiscal year 2023, $15,500,000;

&emsp;(2) for fiscal year 2024, $16,200,000;

&emsp;(3) for fiscal year 2025, $16,900,000;

&emsp;(4) for fiscal year 2026, $17,700,000;

&emsp;(5) for fiscal year 2027, $18,500,000;

&emsp;(6) for fiscal year 2028, $19,800,000;

&emsp;(7) for fiscal year 2029, $22,100,000;

&emsp;(8) for fiscal year 2030, $24,900,000; and

&emsp;(9) for fiscal year 2031, $23,700,000.

(Pub. L. 109–469, title VII, §703, Dec. 29, 2006, 120 Stat. 3534; Pub. L. 113–280, §3, Dec. 18, 2014, 128 Stat. 3020; Pub. L. 117–103, div. Q, title III, §304, Mar. 15, 2022, 136 Stat. 817.)

#### <b>Editorial Notes</b>

#### Amendments

<b>2022</b>—Pub. L. 117–103 amended section generally. Prior to amendment, section related to appropriations for fiscal years 2014 to 2020.

<b>2014</b>—Pub. L. 113–280 amended section generally. Prior to amendment, section related to authorization of appropriations for fiscal years 2007 to 2011.
