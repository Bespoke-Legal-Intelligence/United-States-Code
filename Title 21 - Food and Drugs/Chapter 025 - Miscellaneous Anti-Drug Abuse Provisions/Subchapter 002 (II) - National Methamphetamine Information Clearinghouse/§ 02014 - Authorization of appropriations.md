### §2014. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated—

&emsp;(1) for fiscal year 2007—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) $500,000 to establish the NMIC and Council; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) such sums as are necessary for the operation of the NMIC and Council; and

&emsp;(2) for each of fiscal years 2008 and 2009, such sums as are necessary for the operation of the NMIC and Council.

(Pub. L. 109–469, title X, §1005, Dec. 29, 2006, 120 Stat. 3539.)
