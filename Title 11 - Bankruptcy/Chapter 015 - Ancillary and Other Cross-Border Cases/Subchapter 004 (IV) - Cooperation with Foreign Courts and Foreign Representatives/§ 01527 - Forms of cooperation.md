### §1527. Forms of cooperation

Cooperation referred to in sections 1525 and 1526 may be implemented by any appropriate means, including—

&emsp;(1) appointment of a person or body, including an examiner, to act at the direction of the court;

&emsp;(2) communication of information by any means considered appropriate by the court;

&emsp;(3) coordination of the administration and supervision of the debtor's assets and affairs;

&emsp;(4) approval or implementation of agreements concerning the coordination of proceedings; and

&emsp;(5) coordination of concurrent proceedings regarding the same debtor.

(Added Pub. L. 109–8, title VIII, §801(a), Apr. 20, 2005, 119 Stat. 143.)
