### §1207. Property of the estate

(a) Property of the estate includes, in addition to the property specified in section 541 of this title—

&emsp;(1) all property of the kind specified in such section that the debtor acquires after the commencement of the case but before the case is closed, dismissed, or converted to a case under chapter 7 of this title, whichever occurs first; and

&emsp;(2) earnings from services performed by the debtor after the commencement of the case but before the case is closed, dismissed, or converted to a case under chapter 7 of this title, whichever occurs first.

(b) Except as provided in section 1204, a confirmed plan, or an order confirming a plan, the debtor shall remain in possession of all property of the estate.

(Added Pub. L. 99–554, title II, §255, Oct. 27, 1986, 100 Stat. 3108.)
