### §1202. Trustee

(a) If the United States trustee has appointed an individual under section 586(b) of title 28 toserve as standing trustee in cases under this chapter and if such individual qualifies as a trustee under section 322 of this title, then such individual shall serve as trustee in any case filed under this chapter. Otherwise, the United States trustee shall appoint one disinterested person to serve as trustee in the case or the United States trustee may serve as trustee in the case if necessary.

(b) The trustee shall—

&emsp;(1) perform the duties specified in sections 704(2), 704(3), 704(5), 704(6), 704(7), and 704(9) of this title;

&emsp;(2) perform the duties specified in section 1106(a)(3) and 1106(a)(4) of this title if the court, for cause and on request of a party in interest, the trustee, or the United States trustee, so orders;

&emsp;(3) appear and be heard at any hearing that concerns—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the value of property subject to a lien;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) confirmation of a plan;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) modification of the plan after confirmation; or

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the sale of property of the estate;

&emsp;(4) ensure that the debtor commences making timely payments required by a confirmed plan; and

&emsp;(5) if the debtor ceases to be a debtor in possession, perform the duties specified in sections 704(8), 1106(a)(1), 1106(a)(2), 1106(a)(6), 1106(a)(7), and 1203.

#### Amendments

1986—Subsecs. (c), (d). Pub. L. 99–554, §227, struck out subsecs. (c) and (d) which read as follows:

“(c) If the number of cases under this chapter commenced in a particular judicial district so warrants, the court may appoint one or more individuals to serve as standing trustee for such district in cases under this chapter.

“(d)(1) A court that has appointed an individual under subsection (a) of this section to serve as standing trustee in cases under this chapter shall set for such individual—

&emsp;“(A) a maximum annual compensation not to exceed the lowest annual rate of basic pay in effect for grade GS–16 of the General Schedule prescribed under section 5332 of title 5; and

&emsp;“(B) a percentage fee not to exceed the sum of—

&emsp;&emsp;“(i) not to exceed ten percent of the payments made under the plan of such debtor, with respect to payments in an aggregate amount not to exceed $450,000; and

&emsp;&emsp;“(ii) three percent of payments made under the plan of such debtor, with respect to payments made after the aggregate amount of payments made under the plan exceeds $450,000;

> &emsp;based on such maximum annual compensation and the actual, necessary expenses incurred by such individual as standing trustee.

“(2) Such individual shall collect such percentage fee from all payments under plans in the cases under this chapter for which such individual serves as standing trustee. Such individual shall pay annually to the Treasury—

&emsp;“(A) any amount by which the actual compensation received by such individual exceeds five percent of all such payments made under plans in cases under this chapter for which such individual serves as standing trustee; and

&emsp;“(B) any amount by which the percentage fee fixed under paragraph (1)(B) of this subsection for all such cases exceeds—

&emsp;&emsp;“(i) such individual's actual compensation for such cases, as adjusted under subparagraph (A) of this paragraph; plus

&emsp;&emsp;“(ii) the actual, necessary expenses incurred by such individual as standing trustee in such cases.”

> See section 586(b) and (e) of Title 28, Judiciary and Judicial Procedure.
