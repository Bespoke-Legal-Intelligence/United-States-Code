### §102502. Definition of museum object

In this chapter:

&emsp;(1)

In general.—The term "museum object" means an object that—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) typically is movable; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) is eligible to be, or is made part of, a museum, library, or archive collection through a formal procedure, such as accessioning.

&emsp;(2)

Inclusions.—The term "museum object" includes a prehistoric or historic artifact, work of art, book, document, photograph, or natural history specimen.

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3157.)
