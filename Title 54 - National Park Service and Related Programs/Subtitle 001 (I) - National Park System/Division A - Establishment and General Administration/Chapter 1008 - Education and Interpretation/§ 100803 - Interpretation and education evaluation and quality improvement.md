### §100803. Interpretation and education evaluation and quality improvement

The Secretary may undertake a program of regular evaluation of interpretation and education programs to ensure that they—

&emsp;(1) adjust to how people learn and engage with the natural world and shared heritage as embodied in the System;

&emsp;(2) reflect different cultural backgrounds, ages, education, gender, abilities, ethnicity, and needs;

&emsp;(3) demonstrate innovative approaches to management and appropriately incorporate emerging learning and communications technology; and

&emsp;(4) reflect current scientific and academic research, content, methods, and audience analysis.

(Added Pub. L. 114–289, title III, §301(a), Dec. 16, 2016, 130 Stat. 1487.)
