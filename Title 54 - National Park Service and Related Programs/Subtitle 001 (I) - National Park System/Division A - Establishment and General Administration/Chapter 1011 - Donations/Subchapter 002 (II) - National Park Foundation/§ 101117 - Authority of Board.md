### §101117. Authority of Board

In carrying out this chapter, the Board may—

&emsp;(1) adopt bylaws and regulations necessary for the administration of its functions; and

&emsp;(2) contract for any necessary services.

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3121.)
