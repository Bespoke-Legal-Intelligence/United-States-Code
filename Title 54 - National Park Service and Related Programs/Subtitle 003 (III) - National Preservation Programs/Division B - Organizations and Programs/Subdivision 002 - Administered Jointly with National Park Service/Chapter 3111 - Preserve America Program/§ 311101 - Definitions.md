### §311101. Definitions

In this chapter:

&emsp;(1)

Council.—The term "Council" means the Advisory Council on Historic Preservation.

&emsp;(2)

Heritage tourism.—The term "heritage tourism" means the conduct of activities to attract and accommodate visitors to a site or area based on the unique or special aspects of the history, landscape (including trail systems), and culture of the site or area.

&emsp;(3)

Program.—The term "program" means the Preserve America Program established under section 311102(a).

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3247.)
