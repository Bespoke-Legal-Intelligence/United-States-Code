### §308401. Definition of network <sup>1</sup>

In this chapter, the term "Network" means the African American Civil Rights Network established under section 308402(a).

(Added Pub. L. 115–104, §3(a), Jan. 8, 2018, 131 Stat. 2260.)

#### Purposes

Pub. L. 115–104, §2, Jan. 8, 2018, 131 Stat. 2260, provided that: "The purposes of this Act \[enacting this chapter\] are—

&emsp;"(1) to recognize—

&emsp;&emsp;"(A) the importance of the African American civil rights movement; and

&emsp;&emsp;"(B) the sacrifices made by the people who fought against discrimination and segregation; and

&emsp;"(2) to authorize the National Park Service to coordinate and facilitate Federal and non-Federal activities to commemorate, honor, and interpret—

&emsp;&emsp;"(A) the history of the African American civil rights movement;

&emsp;&emsp;"(B) the significance of the civil rights movement as a crucial element in the evolution of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2000a et seq.); and

&emsp;&emsp;"(C) the relevance of the African American civil rights movement in fostering the spirit of social justice and national reconciliation."
