### §300305. Historic conservation district

In this division, the term "historic conservation district" means an area that contains—

&emsp;(1) historic property;

&emsp;(2) buildings having similar or related architectural characteristics;

&emsp;(3) cultural cohesiveness; or

&emsp;(4) any combination of features described in paragraphs (1) to (3).

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3188.)
