### §300317. State

In this division, the term "State" means—

&emsp;(1) a State, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands; and

&emsp;(2) the Republic of the Marshall Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, and the Republic of Palau.

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3190.)
