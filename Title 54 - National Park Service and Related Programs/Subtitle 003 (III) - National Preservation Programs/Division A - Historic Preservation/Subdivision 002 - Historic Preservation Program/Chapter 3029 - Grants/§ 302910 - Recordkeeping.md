### §302910. Recordkeeping

A recipient of assistance under this division shall keep—

&emsp;(1) such records as the Secretary shall prescribe, including records that fully disclose—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the disposition by the recipient of the proceeds of the assistance;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the total cost of the project or undertaking in connection with which the assistance is given or used; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the amount and nature of that portion of the cost of the project or undertaking supplied by other sources; and

&emsp;(2) such other records as will facilitate an effective audit.

(Pub. L. 113–287, §3, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3205.)
