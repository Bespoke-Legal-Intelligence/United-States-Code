### §103. Government corporation

For the purpose of this title—

&emsp;(1) "Government corporation" means a corporation owned or controlled by the Government of the United States; and

&emsp;(2) "Government controlled corporation" does not include a corporation owned by the Government of the United States.

(Pub. L. 89–554, Sept. 6, 1966, 80 Stat. 378.)

#### Historical and Revision Notes

The section is supplied to avoid the necessity for defining "Government corporation" and "Government controlled corporation" each time it is used in this title.
