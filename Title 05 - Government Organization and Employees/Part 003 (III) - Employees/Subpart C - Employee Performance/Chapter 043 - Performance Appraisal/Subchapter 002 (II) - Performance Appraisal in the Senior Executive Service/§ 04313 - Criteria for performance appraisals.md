### §4313. Criteria for performance appraisals

Appraisals of performance in the Senior Executive Service shall be based on both individual and organizational performance, taking into account such factors as—

&emsp;(1) improvements in efficiency, productivity, and quality of work or service, including any significant reduction in paperwork;

&emsp;(2) cost efficiency;

&emsp;(3) timeliness of performance;

&emsp;(4) other indications of the effectiveness, productivity, and performance quality of the employees for whom the senior executive is responsible;

&emsp;(5) meeting affirmative action goals, achievement of equal employment opportunity requirements, and compliance with the merit systems principles set forth under section 2301 of this title; and

&emsp;(6) protecting whistleblowers, as described in section 4302(b)(2).

(Added Pub. L. 95–454, title IV, §405(a), Oct. 13, 1978, 92 Stat. 1168; amended Pub. L. 103–424, §6, Oct. 29, 1994, 108 Stat. 4364; Pub. L. 115–91, div. A, title X, §1097(d)(2), Dec. 12, 2017, 131 Stat. 1620.)
