### §4509. Prohibition of cash award to Executive Schedule officers

No officer may receive a cash award under the provisions of this subchapter, if such officer—

&emsp;(1) serves in—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) an Executive Schedule position under subchapter II of chapter 53; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) a position for which the compensation is set in statute by reference to a section or level under subchapter II of chapter 53; and

&emsp;(2) was appointed to such position by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.

(Added Pub. L. 103–425, §2(a), Oct. 31, 1994, 108 Stat. 4370.)
