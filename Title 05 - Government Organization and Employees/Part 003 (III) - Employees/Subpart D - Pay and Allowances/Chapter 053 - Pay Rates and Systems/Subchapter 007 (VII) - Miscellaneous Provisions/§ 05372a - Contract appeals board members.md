### §5372a. Contract appeals board members

(a) For the purpose of this section—

&emsp;(1) the term “contract appeals board member” means a member of an agency board of contract appeals appointed under section 8 of the Contract Disputes Act of 1978 or a member of the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals appointed under section 42 of the Office of Federal Procurement Policy Act; and

&emsp;(2) the term “appeals board” means an agency board of contract appeals established pursuant to section 8 of the Contract Disputes Act of 1978.

(b) Rates of basic pay for contract appeals board members shall be as follows:

&emsp;(1) Chairman of an appeals board—the rate of basic pay payable for level IV of the Executive Schedule.

&emsp;(2) Vice chairman of an appeals board—97 percent of the rate under paragraph (1).

&emsp;(3) Other members of an appeals board—94 percent of the rate under paragraph (1).

(c) Rates of pay taking effect under this section shall be printed in the Federal Register and the Code of Federal Regulations.

(Added Pub. L. 101–509, title V, §529 \[title I, §104(a)(2)\], Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1427, 1446; amended Pub. L. 109–163, div. A, title VIII, §847(f)(1), Jan. 6, 2006, 119 Stat. 3395.)

#### References in Text

Section 8 of the Contract Disputes Act of 1978, referred to in subsec. (a), is classified to section 607 of Title 41, Public Contracts.

Section 42 of the Office of Federal Procurement Policy Act, referred to in subsec. (a)(1), is classified to section 438 of Title 41, Public Contracts.

Level IV of the Executive Schedule, referred to in subsec. (b)(1), is set out in section 5315 of this title.
