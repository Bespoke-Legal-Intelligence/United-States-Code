### §9601. Definitions

For purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the term "land management agency" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Forest Service of the Department of Agriculture;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Bureau of Land Management of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) the National Park Service of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) the Fish and Wildlife Service of the Department of the Interior;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) the Bureau of Indian Affairs of the Department of the Interior; and

&emsp;&emsp;(F) the Bureau of Reclamation of the Department of the Interior; and

&emsp;(2) the term "time-limited appointment" includes a temporary appointment and a term appointment, as defined by the Office of Personnel Management.

(Added Pub. L. 114–47, §2(a), Aug. 7, 2015, 129 Stat. 485.)
