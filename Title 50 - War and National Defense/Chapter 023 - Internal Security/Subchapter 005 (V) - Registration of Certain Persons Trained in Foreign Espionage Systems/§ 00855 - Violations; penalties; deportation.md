### §855. Violations; penalties; deportation

(a) Whoever willfully violates any provision of this subchapter or any regulation thereunder, or in any registration statement willfully make <sup>1</sup> a false statement of a material fact or willfully omits any material fact, shall be fined not more than $10,000 or imprisoned for not more than five years, or both.

(b) Any alien convicted of a violation of this subchapter or any regulation thereunder is subject to deportation in the manner provided by part V of subchapter II of chapter 12 of title 8.

(Aug. 1, 1956, ch. 849, §6, 70 Stat. 900.)

#### References in Text

Part V (§1251 et seq.) of subchapter II of chapter 12 of title 8, referred to in text, was in the original a reference to chapter 5, title II, of the Immigration and Nationality Act (66 Stat. 163).

#### Codification

Section was not enacted as part of the Internal Security Act of 1950 which comprises subchapters I to III of this chapter.
