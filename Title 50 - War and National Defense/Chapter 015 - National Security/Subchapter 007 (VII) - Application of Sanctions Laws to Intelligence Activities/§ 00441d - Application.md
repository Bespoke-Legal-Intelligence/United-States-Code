### §441d. Application

This subchapter shall cease to be effective on January 6, 1999.

(July 26, 1947, ch. 343, title IX, §905, as added Pub. L. 104–93, title III, §303(a), Jan. 6, 1996, 109 Stat. 965; amended Pub. L. 104–293, title III, §304, Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3464; Pub. L. 105–107, title III, §304, Nov. 20, 1997, 111 Stat. 2252.)

#### Amendments

1997—Pub. L. 105–107 substituted “January 6, 1999” for “January 6, 1998”.

1996—Pub. L. 104–293 substituted “on January 6, 1998” for “on the date which is one year after January 6, 1996”.
