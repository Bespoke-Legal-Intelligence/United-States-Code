### §2561. International cooperative stockpile stewardship

#### (a) Funding prohibition

No funds authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available to the Department of Energy for any fiscal year may be obligated or expended to conduct any activities associated with international cooperative stockpile stewardship.

#### (b) Exceptions

Subsection (a) does not apply to the following:

&emsp;(1) Activities conducted between the United States and the United Kingdom.

&emsp;(2) Activities conducted between the United States and France.

&emsp;(3) Activities carried out under title XIV of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1998 (Public Law 105–85) relating to cooperative threat reduction with states of the former Soviet Union.
