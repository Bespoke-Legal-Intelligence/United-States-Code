### §7803. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Appropriate congressional committees

&emsp;The term “appropriate congressional committees” means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Committee on International Relations of the House of Representatives; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate.

#### (2) China

&emsp;The term “China” means the People's Republic of China.

#### (3) Humanitarian assistance

&emsp;The term “humanitarian assistance” means assistance to meet humanitarian needs, including needs for food, medicine, medical supplies, clothing, and shelter.

#### (4) North Korea

&emsp;The term “North Korea” means the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.

#### (5) North Koreans

&emsp;The term “North Koreans” means persons who are citizens or nationals of North Korea.

#### (6) South Korea

&emsp;The term “South Korea” means the Republic of Korea.

(Pub. L. 108–333, §5, Oct. 18, 2004, 118 Stat. 1290.)
