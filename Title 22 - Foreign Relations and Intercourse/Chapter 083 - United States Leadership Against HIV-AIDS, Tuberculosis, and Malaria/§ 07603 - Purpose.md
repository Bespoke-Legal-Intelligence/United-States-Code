### §7603. Purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to strengthen United States leadership and the effectiveness of the United States response to certain global infectious diseases by—

&emsp;(1) establishing a comprehensive, integrated five-year, global strategy to fight HIV/AIDS that encompasses a plan for phased expansion of critical programs and improved coordination among relevant executive branch agencies and between the United States and foreign governments and international organizations;

&emsp;(2) providing increased resources for multilateral efforts to fight HIV/AIDS;

&emsp;(3) providing increased resources for United States bilateral efforts, particularly for technical assistance and training, to combat HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria;

&emsp;(4) encouraging the expansion of private sector efforts and expanding public-private sector partnerships to combat HIV/AIDS; and

&emsp;(5) intensifying efforts to support the development of vaccines and treatment for HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria.

(Pub. L. 108–25, §4, May 27, 2003, 117 Stat. 717.)
