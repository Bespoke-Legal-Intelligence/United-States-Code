### §1465dd. Assistance from other Government agencies

In order to assist the United States Information Agency in carrying out the provisions of this subchapter, any agency or instrumentality of the United States may sell, loan, lease, or grant property (including interests therein) and may perform administrative and technical support and services at the request of the Agency.

(Pub. L. 101–246, title II, §246, Feb. 16, 1990, 104 Stat. 62.)

#### Codification

Section was enacted as part of the Television Broadcasting to Cuba Act which comprises this subchapter, and not as part of the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948 which comprises this chapter.
