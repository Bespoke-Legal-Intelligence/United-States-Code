### §1464. Voice of America/Europe

As part of its duties and programs under this subchapter, Voice of America/Europe shall—

&emsp;(1) target news and features in accordance with the findings and recommendations of the Young European Survey;

&emsp;(2) conduct periodic audience evaluations and measurements; and

&emsp;(3) promote and advertise Voice of America/Europe.

(Jan. 27, 1948, ch. 36, title V, §504, as added Dec. 22, 1987, Pub. L. 100–204, title IV, §402, 101 Stat. 1381.)
