### §2736c. Professional development opportunities and tools

#### (a) Expand provision of professional development and career advancement opportunities

#### (1) In general

&emsp;The Secretary is authorized to expand professional development opportunities that support the mission needs of the Department, such as—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) academic programs;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) private-public exchanges; and

&emsp;&emsp;(C) detail assignments to relevant positions in—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) private or international organizations;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) State, local, and Tribal governments;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) other branches of the Federal Government; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) professional schools of international affairs.

#### (2) Training for senior positions

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The Secretary shall offer, or sponsor members of the workforce to participate in, a Senior Executive Service candidate development program or other program that trains members on the skills required for appointment to senior positions in the Department.

#### (B) Requirements

&emsp;&emsp;In determining which members of the workforce are granted professional development or career advancement opportunities under subparagraph (A), the Secretary shall—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) ensure any program offered or sponsored by the Department under such subparagraph comports with the requirements of subpart C of part 412 of title 5, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor thereto, including merit staffing and assessment requirements;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) consider the number of expected vacancies in senior positions as a factor in determining the number of candidates to select for such programs;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) understand how participation in any program offered or sponsored by the Department under such subparagraph differs by gender, race, national origin, disability status, or other demographic categories; and

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iv) actively encourage participation from a range of demographic categories, especially from categories with consistently low participation.

(Pub. L. 117–81, div. E, title LIV, §5405, Dec. 27, 2021, 135 Stat. 2373.)
