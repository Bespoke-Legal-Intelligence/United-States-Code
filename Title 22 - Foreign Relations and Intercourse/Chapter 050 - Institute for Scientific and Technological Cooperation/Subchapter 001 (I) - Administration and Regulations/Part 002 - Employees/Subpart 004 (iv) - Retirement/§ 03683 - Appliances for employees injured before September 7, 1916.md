### §3683. Appliances for employees injured before September 7, 1916

Artificial limbs or other appliances may be purchased by the Commission, out of any funds available to the Commission, for persons who were injured in the service of the Isthmian Canal Commission or of the Panama Canal before September 7, 1916.

(Pub. L. 96–70, title I, §1246, Sept. 27, 1979, 93 Stat. 476; Pub. L. 100–203, title V, §5428(a), Dec. 22, 1987, 101 Stat. 1330–274.)

#### Amendments

1987—Pub. L. 100–203 substituted “available” for “appropriated”.
