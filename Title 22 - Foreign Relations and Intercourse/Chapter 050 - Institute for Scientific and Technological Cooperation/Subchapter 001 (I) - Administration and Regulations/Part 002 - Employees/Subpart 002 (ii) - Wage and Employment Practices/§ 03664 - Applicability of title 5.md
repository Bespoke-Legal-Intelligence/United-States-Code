### §3664. Applicability of title 5

The following provisions of title 5 apply to the Panama Canal Commission:

&emsp;(1) Part I of title 5 (relating to agencies generally).

&emsp;(2) Chapter 21 (relating to employee definitions).

&emsp;(3) Section 2302(b)(8) (relating to whistleblower protection) and all provisions of title 5 relating to the administration or enforcement or any other aspect thereof, as identified in regulations prescribed by the Commission in consultation with the Office of Personnel Management.

&emsp;(4) All provisions relating to preference eligibles.

&emsp;(5) Section 5514 (relating to offset from salary).

&emsp;(6) Section 5520a (relating to garnishments).

&emsp;(7) Sections 5531–5535 (relating to dual pay and employment).

&emsp;(8) Subchapter VI of chapter 55 (relating to accumulated and accrued leave).

&emsp;(9) Subchapter IX of chapter 55 (relating to severance and back pay).

&emsp;(10) Chapter 57 (relating to travel, transportation, and subsistence).

&emsp;(11) Chapter 59 (relating to allowances).

&emsp;(12) Chapter 63 (relating to leave for CONUS employees).

&emsp;(13) Section 6323 (relating to military leave; Reserves and National Guardsmen).

&emsp;(14) Chapter 71 (relating to labor relations).

&emsp;(15) Subchapters II and III of chapter 73 (relating to employment limitations and political activities, respectively) and all provisions of title 5 relating to the administration or enforcement or any other aspect thereof, as identified in regulations prescribed by the Commission in consultation with the Office of Personnel Management.

&emsp;(16) Chapter 81 (relating to compensation for work injuries).

&emsp;(17) Chapters 83 and 84 (relating to retirement).

&emsp;(18) Chapter 85 (relating to unemployment compensation).

&emsp;(19) Chapter 87 (relating to life insurance).

&emsp;(20) Chapter 89 (relating to health insurance).

(Pub. L. 96–70, title I, §1224, Sept. 27, 1979, 93 Stat. 467; (As amended Pub. L. 104–201, div. C, title XXXV, §3536, Sept. 23, 1996, 110 Stat. 2864.)

#### Amendments

1996—Pub. L. 104–201 substituted “title 5” for “certain laws” in section catchline and amended text generally. Prior to amendment, text read as follows: “This part does not affect the applicability of—

&emsp;“(1) the provisions of title 5 which relate to preference eligibles;

&emsp;“(2) the provisions of title 5 which relate to removal or suspension from the competitive service; and

&emsp;“(3) the provisions of section 5544(a) of title 5 which relate to wage-board overtime and Sunday rates, with respect to classes of employees who were covered by those provisions on September 27, 1979.”
