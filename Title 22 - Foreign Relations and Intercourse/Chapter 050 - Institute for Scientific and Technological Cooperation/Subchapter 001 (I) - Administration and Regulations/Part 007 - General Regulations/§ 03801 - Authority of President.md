### §3801. Authority of President

The President may prescribe, and from time to time amend, regulations applicable within the areas and installations made available to the United States for the operation and protection of the Panama Canal pursuant to the Panama Canal Treaty of 1977 and related agreements concerning—

&emsp;(1) the use of aircraft;

&emsp;(2) the possession and use of alcoholic beverages;

&emsp;(3) exclusion and removal of persons; and

&emsp;(4) health and sanitation.

(Pub. L. 96–70, title I, §1701, Sept. 27, 1979, 93 Stat. 492.)

#### Delegation of Functions

Functions vested in President by this section with respect to regulations applicable to United States pursuant to Agreement in Implementation of Article IV of the Panama Canal Treaty of 1977 and authority of President under this section with respect to regulations applicable within areas and installations made available to United States pursuant to Agreement in Implementation of Article III of Panama Canal Treaty of 1977 delegated to Secretary of Defense, see sections 1–104 and 1–304 of Ex. Ord. No. 12215, May 27, 1980, 45 F.R. 36043, 36044, set out as a note under section 3601 of this title.
