### §8143. Specific enforcement

#### (a) Jurisdiction

The district courts of the United States shall have jurisdiction over civil actions brought by the head of an executive agency designated under section 8111(a) of this title—

&emsp;(1) to restrain any conduct in violation of section 8124 of this title or section 8141 of this title; or

&emsp;(2) to compel the taking of any action required by or under this chapter or the Additional Protocol.

#### (b) Civil actions

#### (1) In general

&emsp;A civil action described in subsection (a) may be brought—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) in the case of a civil action described in paragraph (1) of such subsection, in the United States district court for the judicial district in which any act, omission, or transaction constituting a violation of section 8124 of this title or section 8141 of this title occurred or in which the defendant is found or transacts business; or

&emsp;&emsp;(B) in the case of a civil action described in paragraph (2) of such subsection, in theUnited States district court for the judicial district in which the defendant is found or transacts business.

#### (2) Service of process

&emsp;In any such civil action, process shall be served on a defendant wherever the defendant may reside or may be found.

(Pub. L. 109–401, title II, §243, Dec. 18, 2006, 120 Stat. 2749.)
