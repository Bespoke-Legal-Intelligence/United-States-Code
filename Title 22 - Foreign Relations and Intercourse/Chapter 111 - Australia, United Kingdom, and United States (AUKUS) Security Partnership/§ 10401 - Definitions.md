### §10401. Definitions

In this chapter:

#### (1) Appropriate congressional committees

&emsp;Except as otherwise provided, the term "appropriate congressional committees" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) the Committee on Foreign Relations and the Committee on Armed Services of the Senate; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) the Committee on Foreign Affairs and the Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives.

#### (2) AUKUS partnership

#### (A) In general

&emsp;&emsp;The term "AUKUS partnership" means the enhanced trilateral security partnership between Australia, the United Kingdom, and the United States announced in September 2021.

#### (B) Pillars

&emsp;&emsp;The AUKUS partnership includes the following two pillars:

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) Pillar One is focused on developing a pathway for Australia to acquire conventionally armed, nuclear-powered submarines.

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) Pillar Two is focused on enhancing trilateral collaboration on advanced defense capabilities, including hypersonic and counter hypersonic capabilities, quantum technologies, undersea technologies, and artificial intelligence.

#### (3) International Traffic in Arms Regulations

&emsp;The term "International Traffic in Arms Regulations" means subchapter M of chapter I of title 22, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulations).

(Pub. L. 118–31, div. A, title XIII, §1321, Dec. 22, 2023, 137 Stat. 501.)
