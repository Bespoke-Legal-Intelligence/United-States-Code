### §3382. Purposes

The purposes of this subchapter are—

&emsp;(1) to further strengthen the United States-Taiwan strategic partnership and broaden understanding of the Indo-Pacific region by temporarily assigning officials of any agencies of the United States Government to Taiwan for intensive study in Mandarin and placement as Fellows in a Taiwanese civic institution;

&emsp;(2) to provide for eligible United States personnel—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) to learn or strengthen Mandarin Chinese language skills; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) to expand their understanding of the political economy of Taiwan and the Indo-Pacific region; and

&emsp;(3) to better position the United States to advance its economic, security, and human rights interests and values in the Indo-Pacific region.

(Pub. L. 117–263, div. E, title LV, §5528, Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 3325.)
