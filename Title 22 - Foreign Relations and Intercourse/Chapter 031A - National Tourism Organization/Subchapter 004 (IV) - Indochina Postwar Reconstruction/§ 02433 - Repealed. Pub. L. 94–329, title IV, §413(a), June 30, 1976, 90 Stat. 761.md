### §2433. Repealed. Pub. L. 94–329, title IV, §413(a), June 30, 1976, 90 Stat. 761

Section, Pub. L. 87–195, pt. V, §803, as added Pub. L. 93–189, §24, Dec. 17, 1973, 87 Stat. 728, provided for assistance to South Vietnamese children.

#### Funds Available From Appropriations Authorization for Fiscal Year 1975

Pub. L. 93–559, §37, Dec. 30, 1974, 88 Stat. 1808, made provision for day care centers, orphanages, hostels, school feeding programs, health and welfare programs, and training related thereto for the benefit of Vietnamese children, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 94–329, title IV, §413(a), June 30, 1976, 90 Stat. 761.
