### §2141f. Definitions

For purposes of this chapter—

&emsp;(1) the term “Organization” means the United States National Tourism Organization established under section 2141a of this title; and

&emsp;(2) the term “Board” means the United States National Tourism Organization Board established under section 2141b of this title.

(Pub. L. 104–288, §12, Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3410.)
