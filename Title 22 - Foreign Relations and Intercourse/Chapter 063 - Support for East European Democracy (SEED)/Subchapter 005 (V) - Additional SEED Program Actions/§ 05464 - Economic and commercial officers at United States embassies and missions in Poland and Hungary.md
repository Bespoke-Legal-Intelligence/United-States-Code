### §5464. Economic and commercial officers at United States embassies and missions in Poland and Hungary

It is the sense of the Congress that, to the extent practicable—

&emsp;(1) the United States Embassy in Budapest, Hungary, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer;

&emsp;(2) the United States Embassy in Warsaw, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic officer and one additional commercial officer;

&emsp;(3) the United States Trade Center in Warsaw, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer; and

&emsp;(4) the United States mission in Krakow, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer.

(Pub. L. 101–179, title VI, §604, Nov. 28, 1989, 103 Stat. 1320.)
