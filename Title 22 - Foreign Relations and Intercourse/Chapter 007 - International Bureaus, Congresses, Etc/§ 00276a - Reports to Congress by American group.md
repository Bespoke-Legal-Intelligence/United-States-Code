### §276a. Reports to Congress by American group

The American group of the Interparliamentary Union shall submit to the Congress a report for each fiscal year for which an appropriation is made, including its expenditures under such appropriation.

(June 28, 1935, ch. 322, §2, 49 Stat. 426.)
