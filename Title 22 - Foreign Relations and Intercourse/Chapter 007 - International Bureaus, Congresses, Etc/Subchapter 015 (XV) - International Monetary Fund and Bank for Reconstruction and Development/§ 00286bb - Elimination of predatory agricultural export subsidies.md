### §286bb. Elimination of predatory agricultural export subsidies

The Secretary of the Treasury shall instruct the United States Executive Director of the Fund to propose and work for the adoption of apolicy encouraging Fund members to eliminate all predatory agricultural export subsidies which might result in the reduction of other member countries’ exports.

(July 31, 1945, ch. 339, §44, as added Pub. L. 98–181, title VIII, §805, Nov. 30, 1983, 97 Stat. 1271.)
