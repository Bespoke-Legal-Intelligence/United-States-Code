### §4108. Functions of General Counsel

The General Counsel may—

&emsp;(1) investigate alleged unfair labor practices under this subchapter,

&emsp;(2) file and prosecute complaints under this subchapter, and

&emsp;(3) exercise such other powers of the Board as the Board may prescribe.

(Pub. L. 96–465, title I, §1008, Oct. 17, 1980, 94 Stat. 2133.)
