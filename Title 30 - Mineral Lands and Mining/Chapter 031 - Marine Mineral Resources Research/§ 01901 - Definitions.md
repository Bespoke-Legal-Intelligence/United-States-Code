### §1901. Definitions

In this chapter:

&emsp;(1) The term "contract" has the same meaning as "procurement contract" in section 6303 of title 31.

&emsp;(2) The term "cooperative agreement" has the same meaning as in section 6305 of title 31.

&emsp;(3) The term "eligible entity" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a research or educational entity chartered or incorporated under Federal or State law;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) an individual who is a United States citizen; or

&emsp;&emsp;(C) a State or regional agency.

&emsp;(4) The term "grant" has the same meaning as "grant agreement" in section 6304 of title 31.

&emsp;(5) The term "in-kind contribution" means a noncash contribution provided by a non-Federal entity that directly benefits and is related to a specific project or program. An in-kind contribution may include real property, equipment, supplies, other expendable property, goods, and services.

&emsp;(6) The term "marine mineral resource" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) sand and aggregates;

&emsp;&emsp;(B) placers;

&emsp;&emsp;(C) phosphates;

&emsp;&emsp;(D) manganese nodules;

&emsp;&emsp;(E) cobalt crusts;

&emsp;&emsp;(F) metal sulfides;

&emsp;&emsp;(G) for purposes of this section and sections 1902 through 1905 of this title only, methane hydrate; and

&emsp;&emsp;(H) other marine resources that are not—

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(i) oil and gas;

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(ii) fisheries; or

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;(iii) marine mammals.

&emsp;(7) The term "methane hydrate" means—

&emsp;&emsp;(A) a methane clathrate that is in the form of a methane-water ice-like crystalline material and is stable and occurs naturally in deep-ocean and permafrost areas; and

&emsp;&emsp;(B) other natural gas hydrates found in association with deep-ocean and permafrost deposits of methane hydrate.

&emsp;(8) The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of the Interior.

(Pub. L. 91–631, title II, §201, as added Pub. L. 104–325, §2(3), Oct. 19, 1996, 110 Stat. 3994; amended Pub. L. 106–193, §4, May 2, 2000, 114 Stat. 236.)
